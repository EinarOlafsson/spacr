# annotate -- 10 candidate concepts

White artwork on transparent background, 1024x1024 RGBA, house style of `plaque.png` / `measure.png`.
Candidates for review only; nothing here is installed.

1. **annotate_01** - A grid of crops being scored one by one.
2. **annotate_02** - Pencil writing a label on a crop.
3. **annotate_03** - Pointer clicking through crops.
4. **annotate_04** - A label tag tied to a cell.
5. **annotate_05** - A scoring checklist beside the object.
6. **annotate_06** - Bounding box with grab handles around a cell.
7. **annotate_07** - A stamp pressing a verdict onto a crop.
8. **annotate_08** - A comment attached to an object.
9. **annotate_09** - Sorting crops into two bins.
10. **annotate_10** - Tracing an outline by hand with a stylus.

Contact sheets: `_sheet_dark.png` (white ink on #14161a) and `_sheet_light.png` (the same alpha masks tinted dark on #f5f6f8, which is how they would have to be drawn in a light theme -- as shipped they are pure white and invisible there).
