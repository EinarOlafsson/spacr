# umap -- candidate concepts

1. **umap_01** - the embedding plot: axes with separated dot clusters
2. **umap_02** - cells become points: outlines in, scatter out
3. **umap_03** - high-dimensional cube projected onto a 2-D plane
4. **umap_04** - cluster hulls drawn around the embedded points
5. **umap_05** - density contours over the embedding
6. **umap_06** - feature matrix reduced to a scatter
7. **umap_07** - the k-nearest-neighbour graph the embedding is built from
8. **umap_08** - swiss roll manifold unrolled into a flat strip
9. **umap_09** - many feature dimensions funnelled down to two axes
10. **umap_10** - point cloud focused through a lens onto a plane

_All 1024x1024 RGBA, white on transparent, house style (flat, thin strokes + solid fills), matching `plaque.png` / `measure.png`._

_`_sheet_dark.png` shows the PNGs as they are. `_sheet_light.png` re-inks the same alpha masks in dark grey, because pure-white artwork is invisible on a light background -- that is the point of the second sheet._
