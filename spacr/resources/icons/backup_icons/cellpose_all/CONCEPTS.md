# cellpose_all -- 10 candidate concepts

White artwork on transparent background, 1024x1024 RGBA, house style of `plaque.png` / `measure.png`.
Candidates for review only; nothing here is installed.

1. **cellpose_all_01** - A whole batch of frames, the front one segmented.
2. **cellpose_all_02** - Every well of the plate segmented.
3. **cellpose_all_03** - End to end: image, then masks, then measurements.
4. **cellpose_all_04** - Frames streaming past the segmenter on a belt.
5. **cellpose_all_05** - Point it at a folder and it does the lot.
6. **cellpose_all_06** - A contact sheet: every field, every shape.
7. **cellpose_all_07** - All channels folded into one segmentation.
8. **cellpose_all_08** - Masks and the measurements that come with them.
9. **cellpose_all_09** - Everything in, uniform masks out (funnel).
10. **cellpose_all_10** - A queue of jobs, all of them running.

Contact sheets: `_sheet_dark.png` (white ink on #14161a) and `_sheet_light.png` (the same alpha masks tinted dark on #f5f6f8, which is how they would have to be drawn in a light theme -- as shipped they are pure white and invisible there).
