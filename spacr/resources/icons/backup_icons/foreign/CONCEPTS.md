# foreign - candidate concepts

White-on-transparent, 1024x1024 RGBA, spaCR house style
(flat, thin outlines + solid fills, no colour).

1. **foreign_01** - Their column names on the left wired across to ours on the right.
2. **foreign_02** - A foreign table with its header row lifted off and ours dropped in.
3. **foreign_03** - A foreign-shaped plug machined to fit spaCR's socket.
4. **foreign_04** - A crosswalk grid, ticked where their column answers to ours.
5. **foreign_05** - Their column heads dropping into the holes whose shape they match.
6. **foreign_06** - Ragged foreign headings funnelled into evenly cut spaCR columns.
7. **foreign_07** - One column lifted out of their table and dropped in our empty slot.
8. **foreign_08** - A rename tag tied onto the one column it re-labels.
9. **foreign_09** - An unclaimed column, with the spaCR names it could be renamed to.
10. **foreign_10** - A stamp pressing spaCR's headings onto someone else's sheet.

See `_sheet_dark.png` / `_sheet_light.png` for a numbered contact sheet;
each cell also shows the icon at 48 px.

`_sheet_light.png` recolours the artwork through its alpha channel to dark ink.
The PNGs themselves are pure white, so on a light background they are invisible
(the known light-theme bug) - the tinted sheet lets the *shape* be judged there.

Regenerate with:
`QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen python3 _generators/group_data_io.py`
