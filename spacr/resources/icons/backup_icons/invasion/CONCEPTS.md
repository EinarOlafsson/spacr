# invasion -- 8 candidate concepts

White artwork on transparent background, 1024x1024 RGBA,
house style of `plaque.png` / `measure.png`.
Candidates for review only; the installed icon is whichever
one was copied to `spacr/resources/icons/`.

1. **invasion_01** - Host cell: outlined parasites on the membrane, solid ones within.
2. **invasion_02** - One parasite caught halfway through the membrane.
3. **invasion_03** - Two panels: attached on the left, invaded on the right.
4. **invasion_04** - The moving junction: a parasite pinching through the membrane.
5. **invasion_05** - Outside stain vs inside stain: a ring around the attached ones.
6. **invasion_06** - A doorway in the membrane, with a parasite going through it.
7. **invasion_07** - Counted: three attached outside, two inside, one host nucleus.
8. **invasion_08** - Two-colour stain read as two outlines: solid in, hollow out.

Contact sheets: `_sheet_dark.png` (white ink on #14161a)
and `_sheet_light.png` (the same alpha masks tinted dark on
#f5f6f8, which is how a light theme re-inks them).

Regenerate with:
`python _generators/group_queue_batch_invasion_replication.py`
