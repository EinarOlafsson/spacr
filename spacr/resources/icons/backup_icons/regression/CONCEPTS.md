# regression - candidate concepts

White-on-transparent flat vector, 1024x1024 RGBA, house style of
`plaque.png` / `measure.png`.  Ten conceptually distinct metaphors.

1. Scatter with a least-squares line through it - the classic fit.
2. Fit plus its dashed confidence band - uncertainty on the slope.
3. Residuals: every observation dropped onto the fit.
4. Literal least squares: a square raised on each residual.
5. Volcano plot: effect size against significance, hits called out.
6. sgRNA read-count bars with the trend fitted through their tops.
7. The coefficient itself: a slope triangle (dy/dx) on the fitted line.
8. Dose-response: a sigmoid fitted to the points.
9. Forest plot: per-gene coefficients with confidence intervals around zero.
10. Ranked effect sizes: genes as stems either side of zero, dashed fit over them.
