# activation -- 10 candidate concepts

White artwork on transparent background, 1024x1024 RGBA, house style of `plaque.png` / `measure.png`.
Candidates for review only; nothing here is installed.

1. **activation_01** - Cell with concentric Grad-CAM contours around a hot spot.
2. **activation_02** - Saliency rendered as a block heat map inside the cell.
3. **activation_03** - Spotlight beam picking out one cell.
4. **activation_04** - An eye attending to a cell.
5. **activation_05** - Network activations fanning into a cell.
6. **activation_06** - Hot spot radiating inside the cell.
7. **activation_07** - Saliency map as a topographic contour plot.
8. **activation_08** - One cell, half plain and half saturated with attention.
9. **activation_09** - Neuron firing an action-potential spike train.
10. **activation_10** - Attention brackets locking onto a sub-region of the cell.

Contact sheets: `_sheet_dark.png` (white ink on #14161a) and `_sheet_light.png` (the same alpha masks tinted dark on #f5f6f8, which is how they would have to be drawn in a light theme -- as shipped they are pure white and invisible there).
