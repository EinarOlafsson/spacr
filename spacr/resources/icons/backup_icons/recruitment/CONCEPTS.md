# recruitment - candidate concepts

White-on-transparent flat vector, 1024x1024 RGBA, house style of
`plaque.png` / `measure.png`.  Ten conceptually distinct metaphors.

1. Cytosolic protein beaded densely onto the vacuole membrane.
2. Recruitment as inward flux: arrows converging, cargo carried at the tips.
3. Tachyzoite inside a vacuole whose membrane is coated.
4. Line scan across the vacuole; the intensity trace peaks at both membranes.
5. Density gradient: dots crowding progressively toward the membrane.
6. Protein trafficking in along curved tracks and landing on the rim.
7. Partial recruitment: only one face of the vacuole is decorated.
8. Whole infected host cell - nucleus plus a haloed vacuole beside it.
9. Magnified rim: the coat resolved into discrete puncta under a lens.
10. Effector proteins docking on the membrane as Y-shaped ligands.
