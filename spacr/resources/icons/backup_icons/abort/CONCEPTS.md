# abort -- 10 candidate concepts

White artwork on transparent background, 1024x1024 RGBA, house style of `plaque.png` / `measure.png`.
Candidates for review only; nothing here is installed.

1. **abort_01** - Octagonal stop sign with a solid square core.
2. **abort_02** - Raised open palm (halt) in front of a petri dish.
3. **abort_03** - Emergency-stop mushroom button seen from above.
4. **abort_04** - Power plug yanked out of its socket.
5. **abort_05** - Pipeline arrow slamming into a wall.
6. **abort_06** - Prohibition sign struck over a cell.
7. **abort_07** - Progress bar terminated mid-run.
8. **abort_08** - Scissors cutting the run line.
9. **abort_09** - Power / shutdown symbol.
10. **abort_10** - Traffic signal with the stop lamp lit.

Contact sheets: `_sheet_dark.png` (white ink on #14161a) and `_sheet_light.png` (the same alpha masks tinted dark on #f5f6f8, which is how they would have to be drawn in a light theme -- as shipped they are pure white and invisible there).
