# plaque - candidate concepts

White-on-transparent flat vector, 1024x1024 RGBA, house style of
`plaque.png` / `measure.png`.  Ten conceptually distinct metaphors.

1. Petri dish full of lens-shaped plaques (the existing idea, redrawn).
2. Six-well plate, each well carrying a different plaque load.
3. Side view of the dish: a hole in the monolayer, measured, with parasites at the front.
4. One plaque magnified: cleared centre ringed by the advancing infection front.
5. Dish with a counting grid laid over it - plaques as countable events.
6. Time course: the same plaque widening across three dishes.
7. Confluent monolayer texture with the plaques as clearings in it.
8. Stained tissue-culture flask with plaques on its growth surface.
9. Dish plus the bar chart of counts it produces - plaque assay as readout.
10. A single plaque measured: diameter callipers and a scale bar.
