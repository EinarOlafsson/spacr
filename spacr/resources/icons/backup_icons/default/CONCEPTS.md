# default - candidate concepts

_fallback icon: generic-but-deliberate, never a broken image_

Ten conceptually different metaphors for the same idea.

The PNGs are pure white on transparent, matching `plaque.png` and
`measure.png`. `_sheet_light.png` shows the same artwork tinted dark so
the shapes can be judged on a light background - the shipped white files
would still be invisible there, which is the existing open bug, not
something these candidates fix on their own.

1. **Cell in a frame - the plainest spaCR object, deliberately framed**
2. **Empty dish - a prepared but unassigned experiment**
3. **Well plate - the unit of a spaCR run, nothing selected**
4. **Hexagon module - a plug-in slot with an unnamed payload**
5. **Objective iris - looking at something, subject unspecified**
6. **Puzzle piece - a tool that fits, purpose not yet declared**
7. **Pipeline node - a step in a graph, contents unspecified**
8. **Gear with a nucleus - a generic engine, biology at the hub**
9. **Target - focus on the field, no particular assay**
10. **Layer stack - some content of an unknown kind**
