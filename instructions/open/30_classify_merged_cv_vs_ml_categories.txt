================================================================================
CLASSIFY (MERGED): SAY WHICH SETTINGS ARE COMPUTER VISION
================================================================================

Status:    not started
Requested: 2026-08-06 - "for the new classify module, the settings that are specifically for computer vision need to be under a settings category that states that. this module should make it obvious to the user what they are doing, computer vision or machine learning like XGBoost"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE STATE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The merged Classify module exists and greys the settings the unselected
family does not read (spacr/classify.py FAMILY_SETTINGS). But the CATEGORY
NAMES do not say which family they belong to: "Model Architecture",
"Optimization & Loss", "Validation" are all computer-vision groups and none
of them says so, while "ML Classifier & Validation" does.

Greying tells the user a control is inactive. It does not tell them WHAT
THEY ARE DOING, which is what was asked for.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The whole point of merging the two modules was to stop "which one do I
use?" being a question. A merged screen whose headings do not name the two
families just moves the question inside.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Rename the CV-only groups so each says so, e.g. "Computer Vision - Model
   Architecture". Same for the ML-only ones. Leave the SHARED groups
   (Labels & Classes, Plate Sources & Workflow, Runtime) unprefixed -
   prefixing those would imply they belong to one family.
2. The family switch should read as the top-level choice it is, not as one
   setting among ninety. Consider moving `classifier_family` out of "Model
   Architecture" into its own group at the top.
3. Only the MERGED module changes. Classify (CV) and Classify (ML) are one
   family each and a prefix there is noise.
4. tests/test_settings_categories.py pins category names; update the
   expectation and say why in the docstring.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Open the merged module: the headings say Computer Vision and Machine
Learning, the family switch is the first thing in the panel, and the two
original modules are unchanged.
