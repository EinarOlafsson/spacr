--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE STATE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

spaCR has two ways to turn an image into numbers, and both require you to
already know what you are looking for.

  * MEASURED FEATURES. `spacr/measure.py` and the `spacr/measurement_*`
    family compute a fixed, hand-designed panel -- intensity, shape, texture,
    and the distances in `spacr/object_distances.py`. Excellent, auditable,
    and closed: it measures what somebody thought to name.

  * SUPERVISED CLASSIFICATION. `spacr/deep_spacr.py`, `spacr/classify.py` and
    `spacr/flowview/classify_blueprint.py` fine-tune ImageNet backbones --
    resnet18/34/50/101/152, convnext_tiny..large, efficientnet_b0..b7 and
    _v2_s/m/l, vit_b_16/b_32/l_16/l_32, maxvit_t. Every one of them needs
    labels, and labels need a phenotype somebody can already describe.

WHAT DOES NOT EXIST: any self-supervised or foundation-model embedding.
Grepped 2026-09-09 across the package -- no DINOv2, no OpenPhenom, no
SubCell, no scDINO, no CLIP-style image encoder used as a feature extractor.
Every backbone in the tree is a supervised ImageNet classifier being
fine-tuned.

WHAT ALREADY EXISTS AND WOULD CONSUME IT, unchanged: `spacr/umap.py` and
`spacr/umap_search.py`, `spacr/regression_backends.py` and the whole
regression family, `spacr/hits.py` and `spacr/hit_attribution.py`,
`spacr/batch_correction.py`, `spacr/anndata_export/`. The downstream is
already built for a wide numeric matrix keyed by object. It does not care
where the columns came from.

AND THE PLUMBING EXISTS TOO: `spacr/model_zoo.py` (published checkpoints with
checksums, per instruction 370), `spacr/torch_artifacts.py`,
`spacr/checkpoint.py`, `spacr/mixed_gpu.py`, `spacr/accelerator.py`.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A pooled CRISPR screen exists to find a phenotype nobody has named yet. Both
current paths make that hard in the same way: the measured panel finds only
what was designed in, and the supervised path finds only what was annotated.
A screen whose real hit is "the vacuole is subtly mislocalised in a way we
have no column for" returns nothing, and returns it confidently.

Self-supervised embeddings are the standard answer, and on Cell Painting
benchmarks they beat hand-crafted panels at retrieving known mechanism
without a single label. For this lab specifically they are the difference
between a screen that confirms a hypothesis and one that generates one.

The cost of the gap is invisible, which is what makes it worth writing down:
you do not see the hits you had no column for.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. `spacr/embeddings.py` -- the engine. One entry point that takes the same
   crops the classifier path already produces (`spacr/crops.py`,
   `spacr/crop_source.py`) and returns a float32 matrix keyed by object id.
   No new cropping, no new I/O.

2. THE CHANNEL PROBLEM IS THE DESIGN PROBLEM, and it must be settled first.
   These encoders expect three channels; spaCR images routinely have four or
   five with no RGB meaning. Decide and record: per-channel encoding then
   concatenation (keeps channel identity, costs N forward passes), or a
   learned/fixed projection to three (one pass, mixes channels). Do not let
   this be decided implicitly by whatever the first backbone wants.

3. Register the encoder in `spacr/model_zoo.py` like any other published
   model, with its checksum. Instruction 370's scorecard applies: an
   embedding that ships without one is a black box twice over.

4. The column family in `spacr/measure.py` -- a documented prefix so
   embedding dimensions never collide with measured features, and so
   `spacr/columns.py` and `spacr/column_groups.py` can group them for the UI
   and for `spacr/attribution_columns.py`.

5. Only then the consumers, none of which should need changing: UMAP,
   regression, hit calling, anndata export.

6. A GUI surface last, if at all. The engine is useful from the API on day
   one; the screen can follow the way Dose-Response's did.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE MEASUREMENT IS RETRIEVAL, NOT LOSS. Take a plate where the answer is
already known -- a positive control gene with a phenotype the measured panel
already detects. Embed it, then ask whether the control wells retrieve each
other: k-nearest-neighbour accuracy on gene identity, embeddings versus the
measured panel, same wells, same split.

  * SUCCESS: embeddings match or beat the measured panel on that retrieval.
  * THE INTERESTING RESULT: embeddings separate something the panel does not.
    That is the whole point and it needs a human to look at the crops before
    it is believed.
  * FAILURE THAT LOOKS LIKE SUCCESS: separation that tracks plate, batch or
    illumination rather than gene. Check against `spacr/batch_correction.py`
    and `spacr/plate_qc.py` BEFORE reporting any result. An embedding is an
    excellent batch detector and will happily hand you one.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DELIBERATELY NOT DONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

TRAINING AN ENCODER FROM SCRATCH. Use published weights. A lab-trained
encoder is months of compute to arrive somewhere near the public checkpoint,
and it cannot be compared to anybody else's result.

REPLACING THE MEASURED PANEL. Embeddings are added alongside, never instead.
The panel is interpretable and a reviewer can be told what a column means;
an embedding dimension has no such sentence. Instruction 364's question --
is this setting needed, is its name honest -- cannot even be asked of a
column called `emb_0347`.

PSEUDOTIME AND TRAJECTORY INFERENCE on top of the embedding. Considered and
rejected: these are fixed-cell endpoint screens, and a trajectory through
fixed cells is an ordering the data cannot support.
