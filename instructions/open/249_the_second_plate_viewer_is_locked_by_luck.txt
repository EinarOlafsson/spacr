================================================================================
THE SECOND PLATE VIEWER IS LOCKED BY LUCK, AND DUPLICATES THE FIRST
================================================================================

Status:    OPEN. Found while fixing the plate map picker's wells.
Owner:     spacr/qt/screens/experiment_design.py, and whatever it should
           import from spacr/qt/widgets/plate_map_picker.py.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT HAPPENED TO THE FIRST ONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every well on the plate map picker measured 22 wide by 40 tall instead of
square, and the reason is a trap worth writing down because it catches any
widget that sets a fixed size.

`spacr/qt/theme.py` carries `QPushButton { min-height: 22px; padding: 8px
12px }`. `QStyleSheetStyle` does not merely draw that rule -- it polishes it
into a REAL `setMinimumHeight` on the widget: 22 plus twice the 8px padding
plus twice the 1px rim is 40. That call OVERWRITES the minimum that
`setFixedSize(22, 22)` wrote. The maximum it wrote survives, so the well is
left holding `min = (22, 40)` against `max = (22, 22)`, and Qt resolves a
minimum taller than its maximum in favour of the minimum.

The cure is for the cell to state its square in its OWN style sheet, which
outranks the application's, in CONTENT-box numbers -- Qt adds border,
padding and margin back on, so asking for 22 with a 1px rim polishes to 24.

THE TEST DID NOT CATCH IT because it measured the plate against a bare
QApplication, and it is the THEME that takes the square away. A geometry
guard that runs without the application stylesheet is measuring an
application nobody runs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THE SECOND ONE IS NOT BROKEN, WHICH IS NOT THE SAME AS BEING SAFE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

`spacr/qt/screens/experiment_design.py` is a second plate viewer with its
own `WELL_SIDE = 22` and the same `setFixedSize` pattern. It is not broken
today, and the reason is luck rather than design: its `_Well` is a `QLabel`,
and every `QLabel` rule in the generated stylesheet is object-name scoped
(`QLabel#CardTitle`, `QLabel#HintBar`), so there is no blanket `QLabel`
geometry rule for `QStyleSheetStyle` to polish in.

The day anyone adds `QLabel { min-height: ... }`, or a padding to
`#spacrPlateMap QLabel`, that plate unlocks exactly the way this one did --
silently, with no test to say so.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * give the second viewer's cells the same self-stated square, so it is
    locked by construction rather than by the absence of a rule;
  * it duplicates `_Well`, `WELL_SIDE` and the whole drag selection rather
    than importing them. One plate widget, imported twice, is the answer --
    the two have already drifted once;
  * every geometry guard on either plate runs THEMED. A guard that does not
    apply the application stylesheet cannot see this class of defect at all.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * with a hostile blanket rule installed -- `QLabel { min-height: 60px;
    padding: 9px }` -- every cell and both header strips on BOTH plates
    still measure `WELL_SIDE` square;
  * the two viewers import one `_Well` and one `WELL_SIDE` rather than
    declaring their own;
  * a press with no movement selects exactly one well, and a one-pixel
    wobble keeps the wells already chosen.
