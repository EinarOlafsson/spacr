WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN THE COUNT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Instruction 288 is counting on the suite being green to mean something, and a
failure that only appears in company is invisible to anyone running one file.
It is also the second such family found in two days -- 345 records three
annotate tests with the same shape -- so the shared mechanism is worth finding
once rather than twice.

TWO CANDIDATE MECHANISMS, and they need opposite fixes:

  * SHARED PROCESS STATE. `spacr.measure` and `spacr.settings` both hold
    module-level registries, and `register_defaults` refuses redefinition, so
    an earlier import can decide what a later one sees. `spawn` and `3d` both
    appear here, which fits.
  * THE 288 WIDGET-TEARDOWN FAMILY. Less likely -- these are not Qt tests --
    but 288 records that the same pairing can produce a plain FAILURE rather
    than a crash, so it should be ruled out rather than assumed away.
