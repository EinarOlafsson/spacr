================================================================================
"COLOUR BY" TAKES MORE THAN ONE COLUMN
================================================================================

Status:    filed 2026-08-21, not started.
Requested: 2026-08-21 -- "when choosing color by i should be able to choose a
           selection of options not only one".
Owner:     Qt GUI (results panel, graph builder)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS ASKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

One column at a time answers "which of these is a control"; several answer
"which of these is a control THAT ALSO failed QC", and the second is the
question a screen actually raises. A user who can only pick one has to keep
the other in their head and re-colour to check it.

HOW SEVERAL COLUMNS BECOME ONE COLOUR is the whole design problem, and it
has to be decided rather than left to whatever the plotting call does:

  * COMBINE them into a compound level -- "control & failed", "control &
    passed" -- which is honest and multiplies the legend. Two columns of
    three levels is nine entries, and 122 already records that a
    twenty-seven entry legend costs 40 ms of every redraw.
  * or LAYER them -- hue from the first, marker from the second, alpha from
    the third -- which keeps the legend small and only works for two or
    three.

The second is what a reader can actually decode, and the layering has a
fixed order so the same choice always produces the same picture.

A CAP, AND SAY WHY. Beyond about three channels a point carries more
encodings than anyone can read back. Refusing the fourth with a sentence is
better than drawing something nobody can interpret.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Several columns can be chosen and each maps to a distinct visual channel.
* The legend says which channel carries which column.
* One column behaves exactly as it does now -- this must not change the
  common case.
* A selection beyond the cap is refused with the reason, not silently
  truncated.
* Clearing the selection returns to unlcoloured, and that is reachable.
