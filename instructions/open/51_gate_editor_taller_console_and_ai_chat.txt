--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A console showing three lines is a console the user has to scroll to read
a traceback in, which is exactly when they least want to. Same for a chat
reply longer than its box.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Find who sets the heights -- a splitter size, a minimumHeight, or a
   stretch factor.
2. Give both more room. If it is a splitter, this is an initial-sizes
   change and the user can still drag it; that is the better fix than a
   larger fixed minimum, which takes the choice away.
3. Beware the neighbouring failure mode just fixed in instruction 46: a
   minimum height that cannot be shrunk starves whatever shares the axis
   with it. Prefer stretch and initial sizes over hard minimums.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Open the gate editor at an ordinary window size: the console shows
appreciably more lines than now without the graph losing usable area, and
both panes can still be dragged.
