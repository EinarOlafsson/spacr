Status:    not started

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE STATE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Home screen shows module descriptions TWICE: a hover popup on each
module tile, and a line at the bottom of the screen. The bottom line is the
one to keep.

Not yet located precisely. `spacr/qt/widgets/home.py` builds the tiles, and
the descriptions come from `spacr.settings.descriptions` (13 module blurbs,
which instruction 61 measured at 340-1022 characters). Find which of the
two mechanisms feeds each.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A popup that repeats what is already on screen is noise, and it appears
over the grid the user is trying to read -- so it hides the thing they are
choosing between at the moment they are choosing.

The blurbs are also long: several hundred characters is a paragraph, which
is fine in a fixed line at the bottom that the eye can ignore, and wrong in
a popup that covers the tiles.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Find both mechanisms in `spacr/qt/widgets/home.py`. The popup is likely
   a `setToolTip` on the tile or a HoverTooltip install; the bottom line is
   likely a label updated on hover.
2. Remove the popup only. The bottom line must keep updating on hover --
   that is the behaviour being kept, not merely tolerated.
3. Check the tile still has an accessible name for screen readers if the
   tooltip was providing it. Removing a tooltip can remove the only text an
   assistive tool could read.
4. Leave every other screen alone. This is the Home grid specifically.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Hovering a module tile updates the line at the bottom of the Home screen
and shows no popup over the grid.
