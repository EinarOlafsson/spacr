Measured on that machine: Cellpose on one 256x256 image, 444.5 s on the

ENGLISH SOURCE to translate, from README.rst lines 76-78 and 295-298:

  "NVIDIA (CUDA), AMD (ROCm on Linux, Metal on macOS), Apple Silicon
  (Metal) and Intel Arc/Xe (XPU). spaCR picks the device for you"

  "Linux is recommended for the heaviest CUDA and ROCm work, macOS
  through Metal, which covers Apple Silicon and the AMD cards in Intel
  Macs, and Windows through CUDA or DirectML."

WATCH, and this is the whole difficulty:

  CUDA, ROCm, Metal, XPU and DirectML are PRODUCT NAMES and stay as they
  are in all nine. So do NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, Apple and Arc/Xe.

  "Metal on macOS" is the load-bearing clause and the one a translator
  will be tempted to smooth. It is not a synonym for Apple Silicon: on
  an Intel Mac, Metal is what drives the AMD card. 319's own backend
  table got this wrong -- it filed Metal under "Apple Silicon" and AMD
  under ROCm, and since ROCm has no macOS build at all, the combination
  that actually works was named nowhere. A translation that collapses
  the two tells an Intel-Mac owner their card is unsupported.

  "spaCR picks the device for you" must not become "spaCR uses the GPU".
  The resolver reports DETECTED and USABLE separately on purpose, and a
  translation that promises use where only detection happened is the
  failure 319 spends a section warning about.

2026-09-04 -- THE MISSING HEADINGS ARE A LOCALIZER BOUND TO A PREVIOUS README
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The earlier note today said the four band headings "never reach the
translator" and blamed block extraction. That was the right symptom and the
wrong mechanism, and the difference matters because it changes the fix.

`translatable_blocks` is CORRECT to withhold them. The module grid sits inside
`.. spacr-workflow-begin`, which the function deliberately keeps byte-for-byte
-- its own comment says the block is generated from the live Home registry and
that sending its rows through a model corrupts the RST delimiters and links.
The headings are meant to be localized DETERMINISTICALLY afterwards, by
`tools/readme_i18n.py::localize_workflow_markup`.

That localizer is what is broken, in two ways at once, and both are the same
root cause: IT IS BOUND TO A README STRUCTURE THAT NO LONGER EXISTS.

  1. THE LABEL SET IS THE OLD VOCABULARY. `WORKFLOW_SECTION_LABELS['sv']` is

         Data, Segmentation models, Results & QC, Explore, Assays, Design

     The current bands are Core, Data, Tools, Assays. So `Core` and `Tools`
     have no entry at all, and three of the six entries name sections that
     were removed. Instruction 318 folded the tiles and 330/376 rebuilt the
     dock; this table was not moved with them.

  2. THE MARKUP FORM IS WRONG. The localizer rewrites `**Bold**` labels:

         re.sub(rf"(?m)^\*\*{re.escape(source)}\*\*$", ...)

     The current README writes the bands as `^`-underlined HEADINGS:

         Core
         ^^^^

     A bold-label pattern cannot match an underlined heading, so even `Data`
     and `Assays` -- which DO have entries -- are not rewritten either.

MEASURED, and this is the number that shows it is structure and not wording:

    canonical README.rst                     4 `^`-underlined band headings
    every one of the nine localized READMEs  0

The block is present in all nine; the four headings inside it are gone from all
nine. A reader in any language loses the entire grouping the module reference
is built on -- the structure 318, 330 and 376 exist to give it -- and no test
sees it, because the only gate here counts ``**`` pairs and a heading that
vanishes takes its markup with it.

WHAT THE FIX IS, now that the mechanism is named:

  a. Extend `WORKFLOW_SECTION_LABELS` to the CURRENT bands, taking Core, Data,
     Tools and Assays VERBATIM from `spacr/qt/i18n.py::_ROWS`. This file
     already records why: they must match what `_ROWS` gives those four words
     or the Home caption names bands the screen does not show. Retire the three
     entries naming removed sections rather than leaving them to rot.
  b. Teach `localize_workflow_markup` the heading form as well as the bold
     form, and RESIZE THE UNDERLINE for the translated text -- `^^^^` under
     `Core` must become as wide as `Kärna`/`核心`. `build_documentation_i18n.py`
     already has `_rst_display_width` for exactly this, and it counts East
     Asian wide characters as two columns, which a naive `len()` would get
     wrong for zh_CN, ko and hi.
  c. Rebuild the nine READMEs and assert 35 headings in each, against the 30
     they carry today.

THE "Other resources" HEADING IS A SEPARATE, SMALLER CASE and is unchanged
from the earlier note: it IS a translatable block, it IS being translated, and
it comes back as `**Sonstige Ressourcen**` -- a bold paragraph where a heading
was. That is the one the ``**`` gate catches, and it needs the registry entry
plus the rebuild in one pass, because that gate also requires the reviewed
target to already be present in the localized file.
