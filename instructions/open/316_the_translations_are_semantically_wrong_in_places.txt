is the mechanical fixed point 306 was driving at, and it has been

It is not evidence of correctness. Five defects found by READING, all of
which pass every gate this repository has -- present, source-hash-

  Icelandic   "Allir notendur"     = "all users"         (English: all apps)

              -- the bird-of-prey sense of "hover", conjugated to agree
              with "aplicaciones"

  Chinese     leaves "hover to reveal" in English

Icelandic is the worst of the nine and is the one the maintainer can
check fastest without help.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THREE MECHANICAL PROXIES THAT DO NOT WORK, SO NOBODY REBUILDS THEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tried across all 134 x 9 candidates. Every one is dominated by false
positives:

  LENGTH RATIO          flagged 64. Chinese legitimately runs at 0.36 of
                        the English length -- measured from the catalogs
                        themselves, not assumed.
  UNTRANSLATED FRAGMENT flagged 41. Nearly all are legitimate identifiers
                        that must stay English: `mean-field ADVI`,
                        `well_abundance_factor_mu`, and the settings keys.
  TRANSLATION == SOURCE flagged "FlowView", which is a product name.

The five real defects were found by reading, not by any of these. That
is the finding: a mechanical fixed point is not a correctness claim, and
the remaining criterion needs a reader per locale.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Fix the five above. They are known, named, and each is one string.
  2. Decide what "translated" may be claimed in the README, the paper and
     the Zenodo record. "Nine languages, machine-drafted and
     technically reviewed" is defensible today; "translated into nine
     languages" is not, until a reader per locale has passed over them.
  3. If a per-locale reader is not available, say so in the record rather
     than leaving the claim unqualified.

HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED

  * a native or fluent reader has passed over each locale's runtime
    strings and signed the evidence file for it;
  * the five named defects are gone;
  * the README's claim matches what was actually done.

   correctness bug, not a style one.

   with a clause each. It was cut to 253 before being offered for
   translation, because nobody reads a 667-character caption in any
   language and the length would have been paid nine times over in text
   no reviewer could check at a glance. If a caption here ever grows past

ADDED 2026-08-31 (later) — THE NINE READMEs STILL SAY CUDA-ONLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Instruction 319 landed and the ENGLISH README now names every backend.
The nine under `docs/i18n/readme/` do not: each still describes CUDA as
the only way to get a GPU.

This is not a small wrong. A reader of the Swedish or Chinese README owns
a machine spaCR now runs 139x faster on -- the Radeon in an Intel iMac,
through Metal -- and their page tells them they need an NVIDIA card.
Measured on that machine: Cellpose on one 256x256 image, 444.5 s on the
CPU against 3.2 s on the Radeon.

DO NOT HAND-EDIT THEM. They are generated by
`tools/build_documentation_i18n.py`; an edit is overwritten by the next
run and puts unreviewed text in the repo in the meantime.

ENGLISH SOURCE to translate, from README.rst lines 76-78 and 295-298:

  "NVIDIA (CUDA), AMD (ROCm on Linux, Metal on macOS), Apple Silicon
  (Metal) and Intel Arc/Xe (XPU). spaCR picks the device for you"

  "Linux is recommended for the heaviest CUDA and ROCm work, macOS
  through Metal, which covers Apple Silicon and the AMD cards in Intel
  Macs, and Windows through CUDA or DirectML."

WATCH, and this is the whole difficulty:

  CUDA, ROCm, Metal, XPU and DirectML are PRODUCT NAMES and stay as they
  are in all nine. So do NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, Apple and Arc/Xe.

  "Metal on macOS" is the load-bearing clause and the one a translator
  will be tempted to smooth. It is not a synonym for Apple Silicon: on
  an Intel Mac, Metal is what drives the AMD card. 319's own backend
  table got this wrong -- it filed Metal under "Apple Silicon" and AMD
  under ROCm, and since ROCm has no macOS build at all, the combination
  that actually works was named nowhere. A translation that collapses
  the two tells an Intel-Mac owner their card is unsupported.

  "spaCR picks the device for you" must not become "spaCR uses the GPU".
  The resolver reports DETECTED and USABLE separately on purpose, and a
  translation that promises use where only detection happened is the
  failure 319 spends a section warning about.
