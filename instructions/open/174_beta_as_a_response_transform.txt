================================================================================
BETA AS A RESPONSE TRANSFORM FOR THE REGRESSION
================================================================================

Status:    filed 2026-08-19.
Requested: 2026-08-19 -- "i should have beta transform as an option for the
           regression as well."

`transform` offers log and its siblings today. A classification score is a
PROPORTION in [0, 1], and the well aggregate of one is too, so the natural
model is beta rather than a log of something that can be zero.

WHAT TO BUILD

A. `transform='beta'` alongside the existing options, applying the standard
   logit link with the Smithson-Verkuilen squeeze for the endpoints -- a
   proportion of exactly 0 or 1 has no logit, and a screen produces both.
   The squeeze is stated in the run summary, because a transform that
   silently moved a user's 0 to 0.001 changed their data.

B. IT MUST AGREE WITH `regression_type='beta'`, which already exists and
   fits a beta GLM. Two routes to "beta" that mean different things is the
   confusion 145 is about: one is a TRANSFORM of the response, the other is
   the FAMILY of the fit, and the settings panel has to say which is which
   rather than offering two controls with one word.

C. And the summary says which was used, in the sentence that already names
   the transform.

RELATED
  173 -- keeps beta as an option for the attribution likelihood, for the same
         reason: a classification score lives at 0 and 1 and a log does not.
