--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. COMPARE ANY MEASUREMENT IN THE DATABASE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    "compare any measurement from the database tables (requires them to be
    joined)"

The comparison today reads the columns on the OBJECT ROWS the tab happens to
hold. Every measurement in the attached databases should be reachable --
cell, nucleus, pathogen, cytoplasm -- which means the join is a precondition,
exactly as the sweep's is (186 A). Say so and offer to do it, rather than
offering a shorter list of measurements and no reason.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. THE THREE COMPARISONS THAT MATTER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    "compare the annotated cells in the well, compare to the controlls
    (controlls would need to be specified.), compare to all other wells. and
    i whould be able to choose which wells to include from the gene
    annotation."

Three contrasts for one set of annotated cells, and they answer different
questions:

    within the well      annotated against the rest of the SAME well --
                         removes plate and well batch entirely. This is the
                         contrast instruction 173's validation used, and the
                         only one the well-level fraction can never make.
    against controls     annotated against the control wells. Needs the
                         controls named -- and `spacr.control_names` is now
                         the one reader for that (184), so it takes a gene or
                         a guide in any of the four spellings.
    against every other  annotated against all other wells on the plate.
    well

AND THE WELLS ARE CHOSEN, not all-or-nothing: "i whould be able to choose
which wells to include from the gene annotation". A gene's wells are listed
and each can be included or left out, because a well that failed for an
unrelated reason should not have to poison the contrast.

THE PLATE-MAP PICKER OF 185 IS THE OBVIOUS CONTROL FOR THIS, and the two
instructions should land together or at least agree on the vocabulary.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. AN OPENED SECTION FILLS ITS SPACE                            [REPORTED]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    "in the measure tab the subsections now snap to the top which is good.
    but when opened they just open a tiny bit. have them fill the container
    to the next subsection."

The fold-upward fix (186 C) added a zero-minimum filler that absorbs what
folded sections give up. It absorbs too well: an opened section now takes its
minimum and the filler keeps the rest. Opening a section should give it the
space up to the next section, i.e. the filler should yield first.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D. THE EXPORT BUTTON GOES; SAVE STYLED IS THE ONE DOOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    "the export button dosnt cause any errors but the exported figure is
    broken, with massive text and so on. uppon clicking export the user
    should be prompted with a window like in save styled. actually remove the
    export button, save styled is enough."

TWO DOORS, ONE OF THEM BROKEN. Export writes with no preview and no styling
pass, which is where the massive text comes from -- the page is sized in
millimetres and the text is not scaled to it. Save styled already previews
what it will write.

Remove Export. One door, and it shows you what you are getting.

D2. AND SAVE STYLED GETS REAL SETTINGS.

    "in save styled there should be comprehensive figure setting that change
    the figure live."

Today: ink, background, grid, and -- for a pyqtgraph plot -- a size and
resolution that are greyed with reasons (186 D2). LIVE is the operative word:
every control redraws the preview, which is what makes it a preview rather
than a form. Font size is the first candidate, since the reported fault is
text that is wrong for the page.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Every numeric column in the attached databases is offered, and the join is
  offered when it is what is missing.
* The three contrasts are selectable and each names what it removes.
* A gene's wells can be included and excluded individually.
* Opening a section fills the space to the next one.
* There is no Export button; Save styled writes what its preview showed.
* Changing a setting in Save styled redraws the preview.
