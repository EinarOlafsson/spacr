--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Not because three tests are red. Because a suite with known-red tests in it
trains everybody to read a failure list rather than a pass/fail, and the next
genuinely new failure in those files will be assumed to be one of these. Two
of the three concern the OUTLINE MODEL card, which is what tells a user
whether the outlines they are annotating against came from a model at all --
so if they are failing for a real reason, the thing being untested is a
correctness claim, not a cosmetic one.

Instruction 288 counts on `tests/qt` being green to mean something.
