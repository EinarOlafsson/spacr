================================================================================
THE NONPARAMETRIC REGRESSIONS ARE OFFERED
================================================================================

Status:    filed 2026-08-21, not started.
Requested: 2026-08-21 -- "there are nonparametric versions of regression these
           should be included!"
Owner:     Regression

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS SCREEN NEEDS THEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The data this module fits breaks the assumptions the current models make.
Fractions are bounded in [0, 1] and pile up at both ends; guide counts per
well are wildly unequal; and a screen's interesting genes are by definition
the outliers, which is precisely where a least-squares fit is least reliable
and where a single bright well can carry a coefficient on its own.

A nonparametric fit answers the question that follows every result on this
screen: IS THIS EFFECT THE DATA, OR IS IT THE MODEL? Two fits agreeing is
worth more than either alone, and where they disagree the disagreement is
itself the finding.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO OFFER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Candidates, to be chosen from with the maintainer rather than all built:

  * QUANTILE REGRESSION -- the median (or any quantile) rather than the mean.
    The natural first one: statsmodels already provides it, it takes the same
    formula, and the median of a bounded skewed response is the estimate the
    mean was standing in for. It also asks a question no current model can:
    does the guide move the TOP of the distribution rather than its centre?
  * RANK-BASED / ROBUST FITS -- Theil-Sen and Huber, for a coefficient that
    a handful of extreme wells cannot set on their own.
  * A PERMUTATION TEST on the existing coefficients, which makes no
    distributional assumption about the null at all. Expensive, and the
    honest answer when a p-value has to survive scrutiny.
  * A RANK TEST per guide (Mann-Whitney / Kruskal-Wallis) for the simple
    comparisons, where a full model is more machinery than the question.

EACH ONE MUST FIT THE EXISTING PIPELINE, not sit beside it: the same formula,
the same coefficient table with the same column names, the same volcano, the
same multiple-testing correction. A model that produces a table the rest of
the screen cannot read is a model nobody will use twice.

WHERE A METHOD HAS NO p-VALUE, SAY SO rather than substituting a
normal-theory one -- that would put the assumption back in through the door
the method was chosen to close.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* At least one nonparametric fit is selectable beside the current models and
  produces a table the volcano, the correction and the export all accept.
* Its assumptions are stated where the user chooses it.
* On data that satisfies the parametric assumptions the two broadly agree --
  checked on generated data where the answer is known.
* On data with a planted outlier they DISAGREE, and the robust one is right.
* Nothing silently substitutes a parametric p-value for a method that has
  none.
