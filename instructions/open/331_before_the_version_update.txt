THE TENSION WORTH STATING
-------------------------
1.5.0.5 is itself the escape hatch for the Windows self-update trap in
01: until it is published, a Windows user cannot take ANY later fix,
including a fix for 314. So holding the release to finish 314 has a cost
of its own, and it is not obviously smaller than the cost of shipping
with 314 open.

That is a maintainer's call and not an engineering one. What engineering
can say is: 01 is ready either way, 314 is characterised well enough for
someone to finish, and 288's gate is genuinely unmet rather than nearly
met.
