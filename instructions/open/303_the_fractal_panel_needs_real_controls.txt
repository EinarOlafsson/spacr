303 — The fractal settings need real controls, and real ranges

Reported 2026-08-28, from using the Mandelbrot theme.

  a) MANDELBROT HAS NO CPU PATH. It draws on the GPU and nothing on CPU.
     The pasted source has one -- `render_cpu_perturbation`, long-double
     perturbation in row tiles -- and every other pattern has both, so a
     machine without a usable GL context gets nothing here.

  b) THE MOUSE DOES NOT STEER THE DIVE. It shifts the sample coordinate,
     which moves the picture but not the point the zoom descends INTO: the
     camera centre is fixed and the offset is re-applied every frame, so
     the effect washes out as the scale shrinks. To steer, the pointer has
     to move the CENTRE the zoom is converging on -- `u_center_offset` --
     and that offset has to persist between frames rather than being
     recomputed from the current cursor position.

  c) UP AND DOWN DO NOT DO WHAT WAS WANTED. In the source they change the
     zoom rate. Say what they should do here and bind them.

  d) THE PANEL IS TOO SMALL AND ITS RANGES TOO TIGHT:
       * SCALE is capped at 2.0 and should go far higher;
       * SUPERSAMPLING is not exposed at all and is called out as "a super
         important setting" -- it is the one that decides whether the
         picture is smooth or aliased, and it costs four samples a pixel;
       * more of the renderer's settings belong on the panel. The
         Mandelbrot ones with published defaults are: seconds-per-decade,
         base-iterations, iterations-per-decade, max-iterations,
         precision-digits, initial-scale, tile-rows, render-scale, fps,
         zoom-rate, path (fixed/guided) and the four steering numbers.

     A capped setting a user has asked to exceed is a setting that does not
     work, and the caps here were chosen for the two fold patterns rather
     than for a renderer that can usefully take far more.
