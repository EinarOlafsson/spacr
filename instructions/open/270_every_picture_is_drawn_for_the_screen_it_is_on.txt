================================================================================
EVERY PICTURE IS DRAWN FOR THE SCREEN IT IS ON
================================================================================

Status:    OPEN. Requested 2026-08-26 -- "the spacr logo looks super low res
           on the mac".
Owner:     spacr/qt/widgets/home.py and the 24 scaling sites the sweep finds.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IT IS NOT THE ARTWORK, IT IS THE SCALING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

`logo_spacr.png` is 3334 x 3334. There is nothing low-resolution about it.

`home.py:1545`:

    label.setPixmap(logo.scaled(logo_px, logo_px,
                                Qt.KeepAspectRatio,
                                Qt.SmoothTransformation))

`logo_px` is a LOGICAL size. On a display with a device pixel ratio of 2 --
every retina Mac, and plenty of Windows and Linux machines -- Qt then
stretches that logical bitmap across twice as many real pixels in each
direction. A 200 px render is blown up to 400 px. From a 3334 px source.

THE SWEEP, measured:

    24  calls to .scaled() under spacr/qt/
     2  places anywhere read devicePixelRatio -- and BOTH are the ambient
        buffer, neither is a pixmap

So this is not one blurry logo. Every scaled picture in the application is
drawn at a fraction of the resolution the panel can show, on every HiDPI
display. It has simply been most obvious on the biggest one.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FIX, AND WHY IT IS TWO LINES RATHER THAN ONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Render at DEVICE pixels, then tell the pixmap what ratio it was rendered
at, so Qt lays it out at the logical size:

    ratio = widget.devicePixelRatioF()
    picture = source.scaled(round(px * ratio), round(px * ratio),
                            Qt.KeepAspectRatio, Qt.SmoothTransformation)
    picture.setDevicePixelRatio(ratio)

MISSING THE SECOND LINE IS WORSE THAN MISSING BOTH: the picture comes out
correct in pixels and twice the intended SIZE, because Qt has no way to
know those pixels are dense ones.

ONE HELPER, NOT TWENTY-FOUR. This is the same lesson as the close mark and
the dialogs: a rule applied by hand at each site holds until the next site
is written. A helper that takes a source, a widget and a logical size --
and every call site asking it -- is what makes the next picture right
without its author knowing this item existed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT ELSE TO CHECK WHILE THERE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * THE RATIO IS PER SCREEN, and a window can be dragged between a retina
    laptop and an ordinary external monitor. A picture rendered once at 2x
    and moved to a 1x screen is merely wasteful; the reverse is blurry.
    Qt sends a change when it happens -- decide whether re-rendering is
    worth it, and say which you chose;
  * ICONS. `iconset` renders vector glyphs, which are resolution-free, but
    the BUNDLED PNGs go through the same scaling path as the logo;
  * anything drawn into a QImage or QPixmap by hand -- the ambient buffer
    already handles this correctly and is the model to copy.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * with a simulated device pixel ratio of 2, the logo pixmap's own
    dimensions are twice its logical size and its devicePixelRatio is 2 --
    asserted on the pixmap, not on the file;
  * the label it sits in is still the logical size, so nothing moved;
  * every .scaled() site under spacr/qt/ either goes through the helper or
    is named in the report with a reason it does not need to;
  * at a ratio of 1 nothing changes -- the same pixel dimensions as today,
    so no display regresses.
