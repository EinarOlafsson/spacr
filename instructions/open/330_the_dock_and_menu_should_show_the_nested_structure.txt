330 — THE DOCK AND THE MENU BAR SHOULD SHOW THE NESTED STRUCTURE
================================================================

Status:    OPEN, filed 2026-09-01. AFTER THE VERSION UPDATE, by the
           maintainer's own split: this is a feature, and the items
           before the update are defects and the release itself.

ASKED FOR (2026-09-01, verbatim)
--------------------------------
"the dock and the spacr menu bar dont reflect the new module structure.
best would be if the new nested structure is reflected in these two. so
adding a level to each where the butons are represented one level down
from the new main modules"

WHAT THE STATE IS
-----------------
The dock already has ONE level: a collapsible header per section
(Core/Data/Tools/Assays) with the modules of that section under it. What
it does not show is the SECOND level -- instruction 318 folded 33
modules onto the mastheads of 11 hosts, and none of them appears in the
dock or the menu at all. A user who wants Volcano Explorer has to know
it lives on Regression.

The groundwork is already in: `spacr.qt.app.folded_children()` returns
`host key -> the module keys folded onto its masthead`, collected from
each host's own `FOLDED_APPS` (or `FOLD_ORDER`, which make_masks spells
differently). Measured 2026-09-01: 11 hosts, 33 folded modules.

    classify_merged 5   make_masks 6   regression 6   measure 3
    qc_dashboard 3      db_browser 2   foreign 2      graph_builder 2
    umap 2              map_barcodes 1 mask 1

ONE MAPPING ON PURPOSE, so the dock, the menu bar and the fold strips
cannot disagree about what belongs where. Anything added here must read
that function rather than growing a second list -- two copies of the
pattern list caused a bug on this very date, and two copies of the
section map caused another.

WHAT TO DO
----------
1. The dock: under each module row that has folded children, add an
   indented row per child. They must be collapsible with their parent,
   or the dock becomes 60 rows long.

2. The spaCR menu bar: the same, as a submenu per host.

3. Pressing a child must open the host AND raise that module's tab --
   which is what the fold-strip button already does, so reuse its
   opener rather than reimplementing the route.

DONE MEANS
----------
* Every one of the 33 folded modules is reachable from the dock and from
  the menu without knowing which host it belongs to.
* The counts come from `folded_children()`, so a module folded later
  appears without anyone editing a list.
* The dock still fits: sections collapsed by default except Core, as
  now, and children collapsed with their parent.
