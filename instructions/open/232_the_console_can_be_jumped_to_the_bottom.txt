================================================================================
THE BOTTOM OF A CONSOLE IS ONE GESTURE AWAY
================================================================================

Status:    filed 2026-08-21, not started.
Requested: 2026-08-21 -- "it should be possible to go to the bottom of a
           console section without having to scroll through everything".
Owner:     Qt GUI (console widget, every module that has one)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS ASKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A long run writes thousands of lines, and the line that matters is the last
one. Getting to it must not be a scroll through everything above it.

WHAT IS ENOUGH: Ctrl+End, and a visible affordance for people who do not
know it -- a jump-to-bottom control that appears when the view is scrolled
away from the end and does nothing when it is already there.

AND THE OTHER DIRECTION. If a console follows the tail by default, reading
something above must not be yanked back down by the next line written. The
two behaviours are one decision: FOLLOW WHILE AT THE BOTTOM, STOP WHEN THE
USER SCROLLS UP, and offer the way back. A console that always follows
cannot be read; one that never follows has to be chased.

EVERY MODULE THAT HAS A CONSOLE, and the rule belongs where the console is
built rather than on each screen -- see instruction 228, which is about the
same three panels and has the same reason.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Ctrl+End reaches the last line from anywhere in the scrollback.
* The jump control is visible only when it would do something.
* Appending while scrolled up does NOT move the view; appending while at the
  bottom does.
* A test drives the scrollbar and the append, not the internal flag.
* It is true of every module's console, asserted by walking them rather than
  by naming them.
