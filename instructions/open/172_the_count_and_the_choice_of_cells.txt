================================================================================
HOW MANY CELLS, AND WHICH ONES
================================================================================

Status:    filed 2026-08-19. Supersedes the montage's selection arithmetic.
Requested: 2026-08-19 -- "now i see that each tab here is a well. then we need
           to work on the math for desiding which images get chosen. the
           number of images should be equivelent to the fraction. so first
           calculate what all the fractions in that well add up to if not 1
           then normalize to 1 and save the normalization factor then take the
           chosen grna fraction and apply that normalization factor. that
           number multiplied with the number of classified cells in that well
           is what should be shown, x cells. then for determining which cells
           to show rank all cells by classefication score and take the top x
           cells and show them or encircle them if the setting for show all is
           chosen."

THE RULE, IN TWO HALVES

HOW MANY -- x

    total   = sum of every guide's fraction in this well
    factor  = 1 / total          (1 when the fractions already sum to 1)
    share   = this guide's fraction x factor
    x       = round(share x number of CLASSIFIED cells in the well)

WHICH ONES

    rank every cell in the well by classification score
    take the top x
    show those -- or show the whole well and ENCIRCLE those, when
    `show_all_in_well` is on.

WHY THE NORMALISATION IS NOT A NO-OP, measured on the maintainer's screen:

    the raw count tables      1,536 wells, fraction sum EXACTLY 1.000000
    after fraction_threshold  1,536 wells, sum median 0.5526, min 0.1515
                              -- a normalisation factor of up to 6.60x

`fraction_threshold` defaults to 0.02, so the filtered table is the ordinary
case and the un-normalised share understates every count by roughly half.

WHAT THIS REPLACES. The montage chose cells by a SCORE WINDOW -- baseline plus
the coefficient's effect, plus or minus a robust scale -- and took the objects
closest to that target. That rule made the count and the choice independent:
a well could expect 23 objects and contribute 0 because none of its cells fell
inside the window, which is what the maintainer saw. Ranking by score and
taking the top x cannot do that: x cells are shown whenever the well has x
cells.

THE DIRECTION OF THE RANK, and it is an assumption until it is confirmed. "the
top x" is read here as the HIGHEST classification scores for a positive
coefficient and the LOWEST for a negative one, because the cells a
gene-knockout coefficient points at are the ones whose phenotype moved the way
the coefficient says. Ranking always-descending would, for a negative
coefficient, show the cells least consistent with the effect.

HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED

* A well with a guide at fraction 0.2 among fractions summing to 0.5, holding
  100 classified cells, shows round(0.2 / 0.5 x 100) = 40 cells.
* No well that has cells contributes zero because of a window.
* The caption states total, factor, share and x, so a reader can redo the
  arithmetic from what is on screen -- which the window version already did
  and must not lose.
* With `show_all_in_well`, every cell in the well is drawn and exactly x of
  them are encircled.
