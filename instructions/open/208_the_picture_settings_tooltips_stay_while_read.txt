--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. THE HOVER RULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  APPEARS: the pointer is over the setting's TITLE text.
  STAYS:   while the pointer is over the title OR over the tooltip box.
  LEAVES:  when the pointer leaves both.

Today it vanishes when the pointer moves onto it, which means a tooltip
longer than a glance cannot be read at all -- the reader moves toward the
text they are reading and it disappears, and a tooltip that punishes reading
is a tooltip nobody reads.

THE BOX IS PART OF THE TARGET. That is the whole change: the hover region is
the union of the title and the popup, not the title alone.

Qt's own `QToolTip` cannot do this -- it is not a widget the pointer can
enter -- so this is a popup with its own enter and leave handling and its own
small grace period on the way between the two rectangles.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. THE HELP TEXT ITSELF DOES NOT ARRIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    "i dont think the api is working for these tooltips"

Settle whether it is reached, whether it answers, and whether the answer is
being shown -- three different failures with one appearance. If the text is
fetched, the failure path matters as much as the success one: a tooltip that
is empty because a lookup failed must say that, not show nothing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. WHAT THE ANNOTATION HELP MUST SAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    "The API should be verry specific and evplain exactly how cells are
     cjhoosen for annotation."

For each annotation method the help states, in this order: what it is given,
what it computes, which objects end up annotated, which end up unannotated
and why, and the one assumption that makes it wrong. Not a sentence naming
the method -- the method's name is already on the control.

The same text is what instruction 207 needs settled about `Non_annotated`,
and what the maintainer asked to be explained in the chat. ONE SOURCE: the
tooltip, the documentation and the explanation are the same words, or two of
them will be wrong.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* The tooltip appears on the title, survives the pointer moving onto it, and
  closes when the pointer leaves both rectangles.
* A test drives enter and leave across the gap between title and box.
* Every annotation method's help says what it does with objects it cannot
  annotate.
* A failed lookup shows the failure, not an empty box.
