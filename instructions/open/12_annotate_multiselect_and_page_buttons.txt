click per image is the current cost.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Rubber band: press on the grid background (not on a _Thumbnail) starts a
   QRubberBand; on release, hit-test thumbnail geometries against the band,
   prompt for the class number, and apply through the existing
   _set_annotation / _push_undo path so undo still works.
2. "Annotate page" and "Clear page" beside Clear column, both scoped to the
   slots currently loaded. Same prompt for the class number.
3. Both must go through _push_undo — a bulk action that cannot be undone is
   worse than no bulk action.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

range and assert the annotations written; and that Clear page leaves other
