================================================================================
EXPLAIN CV MODELS, THEN RESOLVE REGRESSION HITS BACK TO LIKELY CELLS
================================================================================

Status:    not started, 2026-08-14.
Requested: 2026-08-14 — "there should be a module that can generate suragate
           xgboost or simmilar models to explain what computer vision models
           are focusing on, whrough using human interpretable features, and
           feature importance, shap importance and so on."
           2026-08-14 — after regression identifies a hit such as EAF1, use
           each hit-containing well's EAF1 guide-read proportion and its
           single-cell classifier-score distribution to identify and annotate
           the cells most likely to carry the EAF1-KO phenotype, compare them
           with non-hit wells in UMAP, and add evidence that inspires
           confidence in the method.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE STATE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE SURROGATE BACKEND ALREADY EXISTS. Instruction 17 added
`spacr/surrogate.py`: it joins CV predictions to measured object features,
holds wells intact between training and testing, fits a Random Forest
surrogate, reports held-out fidelity against the majority baseline, and ranks
features by tree gain, held-out permutation importance, and optional SHAP.
It deliberately stopped before adding a GUI module. The new request should
surface and extend that tested implementation, not create a second explainer
with different join, leakage, or fidelity rules.

THE HIT-TO-CELL BRIDGE DOES NOT EXIST. Regression and guide permutation can
produce gene/gRNA effects; `spacr.hits` adds FDR, effect, support and agreement
and the Hit List screen displays them. The database holds per-object
measurements and classifier scores, while sequencing supplies one guide-read
fraction per well/guide. Image UMAP can visualize and annotate exact objects.
Nothing presently combines those pieces into a probabilistic cell attribution
for a selected hit.

THE CENTRAL LIMIT IS IDENTIFIABILITY. An EAF1 read fraction is observed for a
whole well. It is not a cell barcode, and it is not automatically the fraction
of cells infected by an EAF1-KO parasite: amplification efficiency, guide
abundance, parasite number, mixed infection and selection can separate those
quantities. Sorting the cells by the same classifier score that created the
well phenotype and labelling the top EAF1 fraction would be a useful review
queue, but it would not independently prove which cells are EAF1-KO.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Activation maps answer WHERE a CV model attended. A faithful surrogate answers
WHAT measurable morphology, intensity or texture reproduces its decisions.
Returning a gene-level hit to individual images adds the biological question:
WHAT CELLULAR SUBPOPULATION appears to carry the hit phenotype? Putting all
three views together can turn a ranked hit into an inspectable mechanism.

This also creates a serious circularity risk. A visually separated UMAP of
cells selected by the original score demonstrates that the selection is
coherent; by itself it does not validate either the classifier or the guide
identity. The feature must distinguish exploratory evidence, internal
validation and orthogonal validation in both the UI and exported report.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. MAKE THE EXISTING SURROGATE A FIRST-CLASS MODULE

1. Register one visible `Explain CV Model` module through the normal settings,
   validation, dispatch, screen, tooltip and documentation seams. It chooses a
   measurements database and an existing CV prediction output; it must not
   silently rerun or substitute the CV model.
2. Keep `build_surrogate_frame` as the one join and keep its one-to-one and
   leakage guards. Prediction score/probability, class, identifiers, metadata
   labels and annotations derived from the answer must never enter the
   explanatory feature matrix.
3. Offer a fixed dropdown of supported surrogate families. Keep Random Forest
   for backward compatibility; add XGBoost when installed and a maintained
   sklearn histogram-gradient-boosting alternative. Record the actual backend
   and its version. A missing optional XGBoost dependency must disable that
   choice clearly, not fall back under the same label.
4. Fidelity comes before importance. Report grouped held-out fidelity, the
   majority baseline, improvement over baseline, class-wise metrics and a
   confusion matrix. Default grouping is well; plate-held-out evaluation is
   offered where enough plates exist. Refuse to present importances as an
   explanation when the surrogate does not meaningfully reproduce the CV
   model.
5. Show global gain, held-out permutation importance and mean absolute SHAP,
   plus per-cell signed SHAP and dependence plots. Correlated features must be
   disclosed and optionally grouped by biological family; disagreement among
   importance methods is shown rather than averaged away.
6. Link selected importance rows back to their feature distributions and link
   selected cells to crop/image inspection and activation/occlusion maps. Save
   the exact feature list, excluded leakage columns, split units, random seed,
   model parameters, fidelity and sampled SHAP object IDs.

B. ADD `INVESTIGATE HIT` AFTER REGRESSION / HIT LIST

1. A selected hit such as EAF1 opens an investigation screen carrying the
   exact regression run, phenotype, effect direction, FDR, included guides,
   guide agreement, well support and guide-read fractions. Never infer the hit
   again from whichever result file happens to be newest.
2. Build one cell frame through explicit object/well keys. It contains stable
   object identity, plate/well, measured features, the existing classifier
   score, target-guide fractions, other-guide fractions, QC/batch covariates
   and control status. Assert one cell row and one fraction per well/guide;
   duplicates or cross-field joins are errors.
3. Start with an honest review ranking: cells in target-containing wells,
   ordered in the regression's effect direction by classifier score. Label it
   `candidate ranking`, not inferred infection, and show the well's target
   fraction beside every cell.
4. Add a calibrated weak-supervision model only when the design identifies it.
   Treat wells as bags and cells as instances. Learn the target-like and
   background score/feature distributions across MANY wells while guide
   fraction supplies a noisy well-level covariate or prior. Estimate
   `P(target-hit phenotype | cell, well, run)` with a hierarchical mixture,
   multiple-instance-learning, or learning-from-label-proportions model. Do
   not force the mean cell probability to equal the sequencing fraction unless
   an explicit calibration experiment establishes that relationship.
5. Cross-fit the probabilities: every displayed cell is scored by a model that
   did not train on its well, and preferably not its plate. Fit nuisance/batch
   effects only on training folds. Multiple guides targeting the same gene are
   separate evidence streams before a gene-level consensus is formed.
6. Store results without overwriting hand annotations. Use a provenance table
   keyed by object identity plus attribution-run ID, with target gene/guide,
   probability, uncertainty, threshold/call, source regression run, model and
   feature versions, split fold and timestamps. Provide an explicit, reversible
   `promote selected calls to annotation column` action for users who want the
   normal annotation/classification workflow.

C. MAKE THE COMPARISON QUANTITATIVE, NOT ONLY A PERSUASIVE UMAP

1. Fit the reduction on morphology/features that exclude guide identity,
   target-well status and the classifier output being validated. Learn the
   embedding without the held-out comparison wells, then transform those wells
   where the reducer supports it. Clearly mark exploratory embeddings that
   cannot satisfy that contract.
2. Overlay high-probability EAF1-like cells, the remaining cells from the same
   wells, matched non-hit wells, non-targeting controls, and positive controls.
   Show wells/plates, density contours and sample counts so one large well
   cannot masquerade as reproducibility.
3. The useful claim is not merely "a cluster exists." Quantify whether the
   target-like population is enriched in EAF1 wells and absent or depleted in
   matched no-hit wells with per-well prevalence, effect size, bootstrap
   confidence interval and a plate-aware permutation test. Define the region
   or classifier on training wells and evaluate prevalence on untouched wells.
4. Export an object gallery spanning high, borderline and low probabilities,
   including false-looking examples from controls. Let a blinded reviewer
   annotate that stratified sample and compare review with probability using
   calibration, precision/recall and inter-annotator agreement.

D. EVIDENCE THAT SHOULD INSPIRE CONFIDENCE

The report should show an evidence ladder rather than one confidence badge:

  * negative/non-targeting controls and positive controls processed identically;
  * leave-one-well-out and, where possible, leave-one-plate-out performance;
  * target-guide dose response across wells, without assuming read fraction is
    an exact infection fraction;
  * concordant effects and morphology from independent EAF1 guides;
  * replicate-plate reproducibility and stability to cell/field resampling;
  * guide-label and well-label permutation nulls, with the whole selection and
    fitting pipeline repeated inside each permutation;
  * calibration/reliability plots, threshold sensitivity, bootstrap intervals
    and the number of independent wells rather than only the number of cells;
  * a held-out morphology classifier that separates target candidates from
    matched controls without using the original phenotype score;
  * agreement between human-interpretable surrogate features, pixel-level
    attribution/occlusion, and biological expectations, while preserving
    disagreements as warnings;
  * blinded manual review of a predeclared sample; and
  * strongest of all, a small orthogonal experiment that assigns perturbation
    identity per cell (arrayed EAF1 perturbation, rescue, or direct/in-situ guide
    barcode readout) and measures the same phenotype.

The last item is the distinction between inference and ground truth. Optical
pooled screening methods can directly link image phenotype and perturbation
barcode per cell (Feldman et al., Cell 2019, doi:10.1016/j.cell.2019.09.016;
CRISPRmap, Nature Biotechnology 2024, doi:10.1038/s41587-024-02386-x). spaCR's
well-proportion method can prioritize likely cells before such validation, but
the UI and manuscript export must not describe those probabilities as directly
observed guide identities.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

On a synthetic multi-plate screen with planted guide mixtures, batch effects,
multiple guides, null wells and known cell identities:

  * the Explain module reproduces the CV decisions only from allowed measured
    features, holds wells out, reports fidelity before importance, and recovers
    the planted interpretable drivers with gain/permutation/SHAP;
  * leakage columns, duplicate joins, missing provenance and unsupported model
    choices fail clearly;
  * hit investigation recovers calibrated probabilities on wells and plates
    excluded from fitting, while shuffled-guide and null-well tests stay null;
  * independent guides agree at the gene level and a deliberately discordant
    guide remains visible rather than being averaged away;
  * the database writes one versioned attribution per exact object without
    altering existing annotations, and promotion is explicit and reversible;
  * UMAP and galleries select the exact scored objects, and matched controls
    report per-well enrichment with uncertainty instead of a visual-only claim;
  * removing the guide-fraction signal or reversing the phenotype direction
    destroys recovery, proving the test is exercising the intended evidence;
  * focused core/database/Qt tests, a real miniature regression-to-hit-to-cell
    pipeline, documentation/i18n gates and the release selection all pass.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DO NOT CLAIM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Do not call a candidate cell "EAF1-KO" merely because it lies in an EAF1 well
and has an extreme score. Call it `EAF1-hit-like` or `high posterior under the
well-level model` until a cell-resolved barcode or arrayed perturbation verifies
identity. Do not use the same CV score to select, embed and validate the cells;
do not split cells from one well across training and testing; and do not count
thousands of correlated cells as thousands of independent experimental units.
