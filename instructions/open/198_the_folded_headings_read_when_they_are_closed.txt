================================================================================
THE MEASUREMENTS TAB'S SUB-HEADINGS READ WHEN THEY ARE NOT HIGHLIGHTED
================================================================================

Status:    filed 2026-08-21, not started.
Requested: 2026-08-21 -- "the text in the measurements tab for the sub
           categories should be white when they are not highlighted (in dark
           mode oposite in bright mode)."
Owner:     Qt GUI (Measurements tab, theme)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS WRONG
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A sub-category heading is legible when it is highlighted and grey-on-grey
when it is not -- which is backwards. THE UNHIGHLIGHTED STATE IS THE ONE A
USER READS: on a tab with four folded sections, at most one is highlighted
and the rest are what they are scanning to decide where to go.

A heading is not a hint. It is the label of a control the user is looking
for, and dimming it says "this is secondary" about the only thing on screen
that is not.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. THE RULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The resting heading takes the theme's FOREGROUND -- white on the dark theme,
near-black on the light one -- and the highlight is expressed some other way:
a background, a rule, an accent on the fold arrow. Anything but taking the
text away.

FROM THE THEME, NEVER A LITERAL. `#FFFFFF` in a stylesheet is the same fault
as the eleven hard-coded ink colours instruction 178 removed from the
figures: it reads on one theme and vanishes on the other, and the author sees
only the one they use.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. AND IT IS NOT ONLY THE MEASUREMENTS TAB
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The folding sections are `CollapsibleSection`, which every module screen
uses. If the heading is dim there it is dim everywhere, and fixing the one
tab it was reported on would leave the same fault on twenty-seven others.

Check the built screens rather than the stylesheet: a rule can be right and
overridden.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* An unhighlighted sub-heading is the theme's foreground colour, on both
  themes, measured off the rendered widget rather than read off the sheet.
* The highlighted state is still distinguishable -- by something that is not
  the text colour.
* No literal white or black in the rule.
* It holds on every screen that folds, not only Measurements.
