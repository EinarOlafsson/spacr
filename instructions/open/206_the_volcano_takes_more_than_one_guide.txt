================================================================================
THE VOLCANO TAKES MORE THAN ONE GUIDE AT A TIME
================================================================================

Status:    filed 2026-08-21. THE PLOT AND THE FUNNEL DONE 2026-08-21; the montage shows one
           of the selection at a time -- see WHAT IS LEFT.
Requested: 2026-08-21 -- "the uesr should be able to select multiple grnas on
           the volcano plo at once."
Owner:     Qt GUI (volcano plot, selection model)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS ASKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

One guide at a time is a plot you interrogate; several at once is a plot you
compare on. Every downstream screen that reads the selection -- the gene
tile, the image tabs, the cell table graphs, the well set instruction 205
derives -- already has to answer "which guides", and today the answer can
only ever be one.

WHAT MULTI-SELECT NEEDS TO BE:

  * add and remove: click to select, modifier-click to add, click away to
    clear -- the platform gestures, not a bespoke mode;
  * a rubber band over a region of the plot, which is the gesture the shape
    of a volcano invites;
  * visible: every selected point marked, and the count stated somewhere,
    because a selection you cannot count is one you cannot trust;
  * one selection, one source of truth. The consumers named above must all
    read the same list, or the image tab shows one guide's cells while the
    graph shows another's.

THE SINGLE-SELECT CONSUMERS ARE THE WORK. Broadening the plot is the easy
half; every place that says "the selected guide" has to mean "the selected
guides" and decide what it does with three of them.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Several points can be selected, by modifier-click and by dragging a band.
* All of them are marked, and the count is visible.
* The gene tile, the image tabs and the cell table graphs all reflect the
  full selection, not its first member.
* Clearing works, and an empty selection is a state the consumers handle.
* A test drives the mouse gestures, not the selection model directly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DONE 2026-08-21
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ON `FastPlot`, NOT ON THE VOLCANO. The funnel every plot emits through is on
the base class, so a band that only some plots had would be a gesture the
user cannot rely on -- the same mistake as the plate-map button and the
tooltip rule, each of which was applied by hand to a handful of places and
then fixed once centrally.

THE GESTURES ARE THE PLATFORM'S. Ctrl or Shift adds and removes, a plain
click replaces, and a modified left-drag draws pyqtgraph's OWN scale box and
selects what it encloses. The drag handler WRAPS the original rather than
replacing it, so pan and rectangle-zoom still work: a plot that lost those
to gain a selection would be a worse plot.

A KEY THAT IS NOT PLOTTED STAYS SELECTED. It can be missing because its
p-value was unusable or because it is a nuisance term the plot leaves off,
and dropping it would make the count on screen disagree with what the
consumers receive. The RING is what is conditional, never the membership.

TWO NAMES FOR ONE STATE WOULD DRIFT, so `_selected_key` is derived from the
list and never set beside it, and single-select `highlight_key` REPLACES the
list rather than leaving it alone.

ONE SELECTION, ONE SOURCE OF TRUTH. Every plot's `keys_selected` reaches
`ResultsTable.select_keys`, which emits both signals together -- so the gene
tile, the Cells tab and the graphs cannot be looking at different guides.
The two histograms are deliberately NOT on this route: their `keys_selected`
means "the rows behind this bar", which NARROWS the table, and a band means
"these guides", which SELECTS. Narrowing on a band would hide the guides the
user is comparing against.

The count is stated on the plot and in the Cells tab whenever more than one
is selected. A selection you cannot count is one you cannot trust.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS LEFT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE MONTAGE SHOWS ONE COEFFICIENT AT A TIME. `MontageRequest` carries one
name and one effect, and the crops are chosen by how well each cell agrees
with THAT coefficient -- so a grid built from three at once would be three
different questions in one picture with nothing saying which cell answered
which. The selection is held whole (`selected_coefficients`), the caption
says how many there are, and `show_next_coefficient` steps through them.

Composing a genuinely multi-coefficient montage -- one request per key,
tiles labelled by guide, and a caption that survives it -- is real work on
the montage rather than wiring, and is the remaining piece of this item.
