================================================================================
THE LOADING SCREEN BACKGROUND IS TEAL; MAKE IT BLACK OR DARK GREY
================================================================================

Status:    not started, 2026-08-11.
Requested: 2026-08-11 — "add to instructions the background is teal, and i
           want it to be black or dark gray"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE STATE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

`spacr/qt/widgets/loading_screen.py:50` sets

    INSTALLER_GREEN = "#003737"

and paints the full-window preload cover with it. That colour was SAMPLED
FROM THE INSTALLER ICON on purpose, so the splash and the installer would
look like the same product. It reads as teal, because it is teal -- a very
dark cyan-green, hue 180.

The name is also wrong and should go with the colour: it is called
INSTALLER_GREEN and it is not green.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

It is the first thing the application shows, full-window, before anything
else is on screen. A saturated hue at that size reads as a brand statement
rather than as a neutral surface, and it does not match the rest of the app:
the Qt theme's own surfaces are neutral darks, so the splash is the one
screen with a colour cast.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Replace `#003737` with black or a dark grey. Prefer taking the value from
   the Qt theme's existing surface tokens rather than inventing a new hex, so
   the splash and the first painted window agree -- the point of the loading
   screen is that the transition is not visible, and a splash one shade off
   the window behind it makes the handover flash.
2. Rename the constant. `INSTALLER_GREEN` describes neither the old colour
   nor the new one; something like `SPLASH_BACKGROUND` says what it is for.
3. Check the foreground still passes contrast on the new background — the
   strap line and any progress text were chosen against a dark teal.
4. The installer icon keeps its own colour. This is about the splash only;
   nothing here says the icon has to change.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Launch `spacr-qt` on a cold start: the cover is black or dark grey, the strap
line is readable, and there is no visible colour change when the real window
takes over.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RELATED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Instruction 74 owns the loading screen itself (it covers the preload). This
is only its colour, and can land independently.
