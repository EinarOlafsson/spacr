WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A reader of the localized API pages meets English prose in 358 places. That
is not a defect a user can be hurt by -- English is readable, and the pages
say what they say -- which is exactly why it is filed rather than fixed in a
release window.

What it costs is the CLAIM. "spaCR's API documentation is available in nine
languages" is true of the pages and not of 358 of their paragraphs. The
README's qualified wording is what makes that honest today.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2026-09-08, LATER — `--audit` IS NOT HUNG. IT IS SLOW, AND HERE IS WHERE.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The note above said the flag "stalled at 0% CPU and produced nothing" and
was worth diagnosing. Diagnosed, with `faulthandler.dump_traceback_later`
rather than by inference:

    audit()                     build_documentation_i18n.py:6272
      syntax_contract()                                    :6039
        _syntax_preserved()     build_i18n_catalogs.py     :4509
          matches()                                        :4496
            Counter(<genexpr>)                             :4496

It is computing, not blocked. `_syntax_preserved` builds a `Counter` per
PROTECT pattern per block, and the audit runs it over every symbol in every
locale -- roughly 10,243 x 9, each against ~20 patterns, on prose. There is
no deadlock, no missing model, and no I/O wait to find.

WHY IT LOOKED HUNG, and this is the transferable part: `ps -o pcpu=` reports
the average over the process's LIFETIME, not what it is doing now. A process
that spent its first minutes importing and reading before settling into a
tight loop can read low, and I twice took "0.0" as evidence of a block when
the process was busy. Two sessions spent 2026-09-08 finding instruments that
hid the result they were built to show; this is another, and it is in a tool
everyone reaches for.

CONSEQUENCE FOR WHOEVER PICKS THIS UP: budget for it rather than killing it.
The numbers in this file came from the per-locale logs of
`--repair-api-blocks`, which prints the same audit per locale as it goes, so
there is a cheaper route to the same figures. Prefer it.

NOT OPTIMISED HERE. The obvious fix -- hoist the per-pattern Counter, or
short-circuit once a contract is known broken -- touches the gate that every
translation decision goes through, and it is not the sort of change to make
in a release window on a tool whose only symptom is that it is slow.
