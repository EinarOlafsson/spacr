---------------------

   or three mutually exclusive choices where a choice is possible, and accept
   a free-text replacement when wording or evidence is required.
2. Questions may be grouped into one compact answer sheet, but keep the IDs
   below verbatim so an answer such as ``316-A: A1`` is unambiguous.
3. Before acting, append the exact answer, date, and what it authorises to the
   affected instruction. An answer for one item never authorises the release,
   a history rewrite, or a different wording decision.

   cannot make someone fluent in a language. Record the reviewer, the locale,
   the exact source and target, and the review date. Never claim fluent/native
   sign-off for a locale the reviewer did not say they can evaluate.
5. Show the current candidate text when asking for a translation review. Ask a
   fluent reviewer to approve or replace it; do not ask Einar to approve text
   in a language he says he cannot read, and do not silently substitute a new
   machine draft for the human pass required by instruction 316.
6. After an answer unlocks code or catalog work, prove the named acceptance
   test, commit only the owned files, pull with rebase, push immediately, and
   write the commit and evidence into the affected instruction.
7. Keep the explicit 1.5.0.5 hold. Do not ask 05/304's publication questions
   until the pre-update gates are ready, and never treat another answer in this
   file as release approval.
