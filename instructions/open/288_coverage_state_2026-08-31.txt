THE PROBE. covscratch has a per-module probe: run one module against only
the test files that MENTION it. A 100% result is conclusive -- nothing left
to write. A gap is a candidate only, because a test elsewhere may cover it.
That asymmetry is the whole value: it clears modules cheaply and never
falsely condemns one. It cleared model_zoo, methods_export, hit_list and
timelapse_preview outright.

TWO OF THOSE ATTEMPTS FIRST REPORTED A PASS, and the reason is the part

confirmed to have applied -- check the diff, not the exit code.

THE PINS ARE SOURCE-TEXT ASSERTIONS, and that is a deliberate trade.
They are brittle to reformatting -- a line rewrapped will fail one --
and that is the cost of having them fail LOUDLY when the premise moves
rather than silently leaving a newly-live guard untested. Where the
premise is arithmetic (a floor, a padding, a rank tolerance) the
arithmetic is RUN instead, which is not brittle at all; where it is a
library guarantee (QSplitter.handle, pandas' agg return shape,
QDialogButtonBox.button) the guarantee is exercised against the library.
