THE PROBE. covscratch has a per-module probe: run one module against only
the test files that MENTION it. A 100% result is conclusive -- nothing left
to write. A gap is a candidate only, because a test elsewhere may cover it.
That asymmetry is the whole value: it clears modules cheaply and never
falsely condemns one. It cleared model_zoo, methods_export, hit_list and
timelapse_preview outright.
