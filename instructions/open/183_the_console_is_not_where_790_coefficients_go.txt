================================================================================
790 COEFFICIENTS DO NOT BELONG IN THE CONSOLE
================================================================================

Status:    filed 2026-08-20, not started.
Found:     2026-08-19, from a live glm run -- the pasted console output was
           truncated at 50 000 characters and was still inside the coefficient
           table.
Owner:     regression module / console

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT HAPPENS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

`perform_regression` prints `model.summary()` to stdout, which the Qt console
captures. On the reference screen that is 790 coefficient rows plus the header
-- roughly 80 000 characters -- emitted in one burst.

Three costs, in order of how much they matter:

1. IT BURIES EVERYTHING ELSE. The not-identifiable warning, the fraction
   filter's "1.0% retained", the pairing summary and the family sentence are
   all above it and all scrolled away. Those are the lines a user has to read;
   the coefficient table is the one they will read in the Coefficients tab,
   sorted, filtered and searchable.

2. IT IS ALREADY SAVED. The same text goes to `model_summary.txt` in the run
   folder, and the Summary tab reads it back. The console copy is a third
   rendering of something two better ones exist for.

3. IT COSTS. 80 000 characters through a QTextEdit, block by block, during a
   fit -- and the console keeps them.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The console prints the summary HEADER -- everything above the coefficient
table: observations, model, family, link, df, log-likelihood, deviance,
pseudo-R² -- and then one line saying where the rest is:

    790 coefficients written to <run>/model_summary.txt — open the
    Coefficients tab to sort and filter them.

NOT A TRUNCATION AT N ROWS. "first 20 coefficients" is a sample of a table
whose interesting rows are wherever they happen to be, and a reader who sees
twenty has no way to know which twenty. The header is complete and the
pointer is honest; twenty rows is neither.

Verbose mode still prints everything, because a user who asked for verbose
asked for exactly this.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* A 790-guide fit puts under 40 lines on the console and the run's warnings
  are still visible above them.
* The file still holds the whole summary, and the Summary tab still shows it.
* `verbose=True` prints the whole thing.
