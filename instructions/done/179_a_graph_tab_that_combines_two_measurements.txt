The tab is at index 1, beside Summary and ahead of the wells, and appears
only once a montage exists. A re-run RE-POINTS it, so the measurement, the
level, the operator and the plot type survive.

`combine()` gives the four operators and NAMES the column for the expression
(`pathogen_area / cell_area`), so the table, the legend and the saved
settings agree without a key. Division by zero drops the row and COUNTS it in
the note -- infinity would invent an extreme observation and zero an ordinary
one, and a comparison computed on fewer rows than the user believes is a
result that survives review and is wrong.

The dialog and the tab are one implementation; the dialog is the panel in a
QDialog. `save()` writes figure, data, statistics, settings and cells to one
folder.
