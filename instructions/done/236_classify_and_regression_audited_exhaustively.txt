WHAT WAS FOUND, PER ITEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A1  Nine models fit and score. Four of them -- gradient_boosting,
    logistic_regression, svm, mlp -- had NO feature-importance panel at
    all; they get the permutation importance, and the panel names which
    quantity it drew.

A2  THREE OF THE FOUR DOCUMENTED FEATURE SPACES WERE UNREACHABLE.
    `filter_dataframe_features` has always taken a list, 'morphology' and a
    free-text fragment; `expected_types` declared `int`, so the panel could
    only draw a spin box and `check_settings` refused the rest. One
    `feature_selection` reads every shape a user can mean; the panel draws
    the multi-select it already had for train_channels. Driven on plate1:
    194 / 53 / 43 / 85 / 179 / 15 / 68 / 11 features.

A3  THREE OF THE NINE COULD NOT PRODUCE SHAP, one of them the default.
    xgboost raised "Categorical split is not yet supported" -- when CALLED,
    not when built, so a fallback chosen at construction never ran. svm and
    mlp are neither trees nor linear and the model-agnostic explainer takes
    a function.

B4  A misspelled backbone printed and returned None, and the run failed
    three frames later on something else. It raises now, with the nearest
    spellings. The names it offered began "AlexNet, AlexNet_Weights,
    ConvNeXt, ConvNeXt_Base_Weights" -- twenty entries, not one of them a
    name that works.

B5  Dataset generation at 2, 3 and 4 classes; the training loop and 3-fold
    CV, both on CPU, three classes. THE K-FOLD SUMMARY SAID NOTHING ABOUT
    THE CLASSES -- three of its five metrics are NaN in multiclass. It
    carries f1_macro and the per-class accuracies now; plate1's three
    classes show a fold-to-fold sigma of 0.47 on one of them.
    INFERENCE COUNTED THE CHARACTERS IN THE PATH and called them images,
    twice, then returned an empty frame.
    Live monitoring already handled N classes; nothing said so.

B6  TWELVE OF FOURTEEN attribution methods run on resnet18. ATTENTION
    ROLLOUT COULD NOT RUN ON ANY BACKBONE spaCR BUILDS: it watched the
    attention blocks without asking them for their weights, and
    torchvision's ViT calls them with need_weights=False.
    THE SURROGATE REFUSED EVERY DATABASE spaCR HAS EVER WRITTEN -- `prcfo`
    is in png_list and nowhere else. On 2,000 real objects the three
    families reach 0.883, 0.893 and 0.890 against a 0.688 baseline.
    torchcam is an extra, not a core dependency, and deliberately so: both
    of its releases declare a spurious numpy<2 pin.

C7  TWELVE OF THE EIGHTEEN regression types could not be fitted from the
    screen's OWN saved settings. A blank cell reads back as '' and is not
    equal to a default of None. All eighteen fit now.
    Every nonparametric route was down too: `int('')` on a blank
    `guide_primary_min_wells`.

C8  Runs / Results / Measurements / Cells all open and fill on a real
    results table.

C9  All five pickers -- rank, attributed, assigned, multivariate, sudoku --
    run, and each says which one produced the montage and what it did.
    Two came back empty on plate1 for reasons they stated in full.

C10 THE RESULTS HEADER OVERLAPPED ITSELF. At a 577 px panel the second
    combo began 48 px inside the first, the third 27 px inside the second,
    and the third ran 32 px off the edge. Measured at six widths now.

C11 An ols run writes 50 PDFs, 18 CSVs, 2 JSON and 6 TXT. One real gap: a
    `guide_min_wells` rung nothing reached killed the whole plotting loop
    AFTER the analysis had finished.

D12 The dead-setting policy already forbids the strongest form of this: a
    declared setting is a read setting, enforced by
    tests/test_dead_settings.py. A name-similarity sweep over the 131
    classify and regression settings found one confusable pair and no
    redundant one:

        min_cell_count      (Regression: Quality Filters) -- wells with
                            fewer scored objects are dropped before the fit
        minimum_cell_count  (Machine Learning Model and Features) -- wells
                            with fewer measured cells are dropped from the
                            ML plate heatmap

    They are different filters in different modules and NEVER APPEAR ON THE
    SAME SCREEN -- `regression` offers the first, `ml_analyze` and
    `classify_merged` the second -- so this is confusable rather than
    redundant. LEFT ALONE for now: renaming either breaks every settings
    CSV that names it, including the maintainer's own, and the dead-setting
    policy has no migration path any more. Worth a sentence in each tooltip
    naming the other.

D13 Dataset generation and the CV trainer take an arbitrary N. The tabular
    classifier is BINARY BY CONSTRUCTION -- one arm each control -- and a
    third class silently sat out the fit while being scored by a model that
