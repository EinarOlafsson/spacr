================================================================================
BETA AS A RESPONSE TRANSFORM FOR THE REGRESSION
================================================================================

Status:    filed 2026-08-19.
Requested: 2026-08-19 -- "i should have beta transform as an option for the
           regression as well."

`transform` offers log and its siblings today. A classification score is a
PROPORTION in [0, 1], and the well aggregate of one is too, so the natural
model is beta rather than a log of something that can be zero.

WHAT TO BUILD

A. `transform='beta'` alongside the existing options, applying the standard
   logit link with the Smithson-Verkuilen squeeze for the endpoints -- a
   proportion of exactly 0 or 1 has no logit, and a screen produces both.
   The squeeze is stated in the run summary, because a transform that
   silently moved a user's 0 to 0.001 changed their data.

B. IT MUST AGREE WITH `regression_type='beta'`, which already exists and
   fits a beta GLM. Two routes to "beta" that mean different things is the
   confusion 145 is about: one is a TRANSFORM of the response, the other is
   the FAMILY of the fit, and the settings panel has to say which is which
   rather than offering two controls with one word.

C. And the summary says which was used, in the sentence that already names
   the transform.

RELATED
  173 -- keeps beta as an option for the attribution likelihood, for the same
         reason: a classification score lives at 0 and 1 and a log does not.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2026-08-19 -- DONE. A, B AND C.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. `transform='beta'` puts the response on the logit scale.
   `spacr.ml.beta_logit`, reachable through `apply_transformation` and
   offered in both option lists (`settings_model._APP_COMBO_OPTIONS` and
   `settings_spec`), so it can be chosen in the GUI rather than only in a
   settings CSV.

   The whole risk is the endpoint. A screen produces wells at exactly 0 and
   exactly 1 and logit(0) is -inf, so the scale is squeezed off its ends by
   Smithson and Verkuilen's (y*(n-1)+0.5)/n first. The squeeze SHRINKS as the
   screen grows, so the extreme wells of a 1,536-well plate do not say the
   same thing as those of a 96-well plate. A response already inside (0, 1)
   is left exactly as measured, missing stays missing, and `n` counts the
   wells that are there rather than the length of the column.

   AND IT REFUSES A RESPONSE THAT IS NOT A PROPORTION. A logit of an
   intensity is still a plausible-looking number with a sign, a magnitude
   and a p-value, and nothing downstream can tell the coefficients are
   meaningless -- so the run stops at the transform or not at all.

B. IT IS DISTINGUISHED FROM `regression_type='beta'`.
   Both tooltips now say which is which: the family of the fit, versus a
   transform of the response that any model can be fitted on. They can be
   used together.

C. THE SUMMARY SAYS SO.
   `ml.BETA_SQUEEZE_NOTE` is what `regression_summary`'s transform field
   carries when beta was used, rather than only naming the transform. A
   transform that silently moved a user's 0 to 0.001 changed their data.

TESTS
  tests/test_the_beta_transform.py -- 20 of them, most about the endpoints.
