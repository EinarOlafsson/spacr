================================================================================
INVESTIGATE HITS WEARS THE HIT ICON
================================================================================

Status:    DONE. `SHARED_ICON_ASSETS` maps `investigate_hit` to
           `hit_list.png` and `hit_list` still resolves to it under its own
           name, so the fold button is unchanged -- two keys, one asset, on
           purpose. Both are non-null in light and dark at 512x512, driven
           through `app_icon` rather than read off the table.
           tests/qt/test_investigate_hits_wears_the_hit_mark.py, 43 tests.
Owner:     spacr/qt/iconset.py.

"the investigate hits module should get the old hit icon"

`spacr/resources/icons/hit_list.png` is the bundled mark the Hit List tile
wore. The Hit List has since folded onto Regression, so what a user now
associates that mark with is the hits themselves rather than a tile that no
longer exists -- which is exactly why it fits the module that investigates
one.

`investigate_hit` currently resolves to a glyph rather than that PNG.

WHAT TO CHECK WHILE THERE: the Hit List's own fold button still needs a
mark. If both keys are pointed at one asset that is fine -- they are the
same subject -- but it should be deliberate rather than a collision, and
the fold button must not end up with no icon.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * the Investigate Hits tile draws the bundled hit mark, not the fallback
    puzzle piece and not a generic glyph;
  * the Hit List fold button on Regression still draws a mark;
  * both resolve in light and dark, since the bundled PNGs are recoloured
    per theme.
