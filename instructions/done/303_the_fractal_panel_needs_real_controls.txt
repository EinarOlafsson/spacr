303 — The fractal settings need real controls, and real ranges

Reported 2026-08-28, from using the Mandelbrot theme.

  a) MANDELBROT HAS NO CPU PATH. It draws on the GPU and nothing on CPU.
     The pasted source has one -- `render_cpu_perturbation`, long-double
     perturbation in row tiles -- and every other pattern has both, so a
     machine without a usable GL context gets nothing here.

  b) THE MOUSE DOES NOT STEER THE DIVE. It shifts the sample coordinate,
     which moves the picture but not the point the zoom descends INTO: the
     camera centre is fixed and the offset is re-applied every frame, so
     the effect washes out as the scale shrinks. To steer, the pointer has
     to move the CENTRE the zoom is converging on -- `u_center_offset` --
     and that offset has to persist between frames rather than being
     recomputed from the current cursor position.

  c) UP AND DOWN DO NOT DO WHAT WAS WANTED. In the source they change the
     zoom rate. Say what they should do here and bind them.

  d) THE PANEL IS TOO SMALL AND ITS RANGES TOO TIGHT:
       * SCALE is capped at 2.0 and should go far higher;
       * SUPERSAMPLING is not exposed at all and is called out as "a super
         important setting" -- it is the one that decides whether the
         picture is smooth or aliased, and it costs four samples a pixel;
       * more of the renderer's settings belong on the panel. The
         Mandelbrot ones with published defaults are: seconds-per-decade,
         base-iterations, iterations-per-decade, max-iterations,
         precision-digits, initial-scale, tile-rows, render-scale, fps,
         zoom-rate, path (fixed/guided) and the four steering numbers.

     A capped setting a user has asked to exceed is a setting that does not
     work, and the caps here were chosen for the two fold patterns rather
     than for a renderer that can usefully take far more.


PARTLY DONE 2026-08-28.

  (c) DONE. Up, Down and the wheel change the zoom rate by 1.12 a notch, as
      in the source. Handled by the WINDOW and not the backdrop: the
      backdrop must not accept events, or it would eat the clicks meant for
      the interface in front of it -- the same reason the pointer is
      sampled rather than received. Reaching the window at all means
      nothing else wanted the key, so an arrow pressed in a table still
      moves the selection. The depth is INTEGRATED rather than recomputed
      from elapsed time, or lowering the rate would rewind the whole
      descent.

  (d) DONE. The caps are gone: the fields take any number, and
      `explain_a_fractal_number` says in words when a value cannot be used
      and why. A bound exists only where a value CANNOT work -- zero
      samples a pixel, a scale of zero, a 4096 shader loop -- and merely
      extravagant numbers are the user's business. Scale 40 is kept; it
      used to become 2 silently. Supersampling is a setting, and so are the
      twelve Mandelbrot numbers, read from the module that publishes them
      so the panel and the renderer cannot drift.

  ALSO: Mandelbrot on GPU is the default pattern, falling back to the orbit
  fold where there is no usable context -- it is the only pattern with no
  CPU renderer, and the fold has one.

  (a) NOT WANTED. "its fine Mandelbrot dosnt need a cpu its fine as it
      is" -- 2026-08-28. The pattern falls back to the orbit fold where
      there is no usable context, which is what a machine without a GPU
      needs; a second renderer for it would be work nobody asked for.

  (e) THE DIVE HAS AN END, and it reached it as a black screen. Fixed: the
      per-pixel offset is a float32 whatever precision the reference orbit
      has, and measured at the default starting scale the step between
      neighbouring pixels goes denormal at 38 decades and reaches ZERO at
      45 -- one sample of one point, filling the frame. It restarts at 34,
      chosen before the denormals rather than at the cliff because
      precision degrades through that range rather than failing at a line.
      Modulo rather than a reset on a comparison, so a frame arriving after
      the machine slept lands where it should instead of skipping a whole
      descent. `max_depth` is a setting, and the only fractal number with a
      real ceiling.

  (f) A SETTINGS CHANGE RETURNS TO THE SURFACE. It used to resume at the
      depth it had reached, where a changed starting scale or iteration
      count has nothing recognisable to act on -- so the change looked as
      though it had done nothing.

  (b) THE MOUSE DOES NOT STEER THE DIVE. It shifts the sample coordinate,
      which moves the picture but not the point the zoom converges into,
      and being recomputed from the cursor each frame it washes out as the

DONE 2026-08-28, all of it.

  (a) not wanted -- Mandelbrot falls back to the orbit fold without a GPU.
  (b) the pointer moves the CENTRE the zoom converges into, and dragging
      with a button held steers the dive.
  (c) Up, Down and the wheel change the zoom rate, as in the source, and
      Ctrl+R restarts -- both in the hotkey menu.
  (d) the caps are gone: the fields take any number and
      `explain_a_fractal_number` says when one cannot work and why.

AND THE PANEL WAS CUT from twenty-one controls to eight. The first attempt
put the detail behind an "Advanced" heading, which was the same panel with
one more row on it and still let a hand-set combination contradict the
control above. The derived numbers are settings the renderer reads; they
are not offered beside the control that sets them.
