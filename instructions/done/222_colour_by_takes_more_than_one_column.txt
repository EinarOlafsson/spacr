================================================================================
"COLOUR BY" TAKES MORE THAN ONE COLUMN
================================================================================

Status:    DONE 2026-08-21.
Requested: 2026-08-21 -- "when choosing color by i should be able to choose a
           selection of options not only one".
Owner:     Qt GUI (results panel, graph builder)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS ASKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

One column at a time answers "which of these is a control"; several answer
"which of these is a control THAT ALSO failed QC", and the second is the
question a screen actually raises. A user who can only pick one has to keep
the other in their head and re-colour to check it.

HOW SEVERAL COLUMNS BECOME ONE COLOUR is the whole design problem, and it
has to be decided rather than left to whatever the plotting call does:

  * COMBINE them into a compound level -- "control & failed", "control &
    passed" -- which is honest and multiplies the legend. Two columns of
    three levels is nine entries, and 122 already records that a
    twenty-seven entry legend costs 40 ms of every redraw.
  * or LAYER them -- hue from the first, marker from the second, alpha from
    the third -- which keeps the legend small and only works for two or
    three.

The second is what a reader can actually decode, and the layering has a
fixed order so the same choice always produces the same picture.

A CAP, AND SAY WHY. Beyond about three channels a point carries more
encodings than anyone can read back. Refusing the fourth with a sentence is
better than drawing something nobody can interpret.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Several columns can be chosen and each maps to a distinct visual channel.
* The legend says which channel carries which column.
* One column behaves exactly as it does now -- this must not change the
  common case.
* A selection beyond the cap is refused with the reason, not silently
  truncated.
* Clearing the selection returns to unlcoloured, and that is reachable.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DONE 2026-08-21
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

LAYERED, which is the design decision the item said had to be made rather
than left to the plotting call. Hue, then marker SHAPE, then OPACITY, and
ALWAYS IN THAT ORDER so the same choice always produces the same picture.

COMBINING WAS THE ALTERNATIVE AND IT LOSES. Two columns of three levels is
nine legend entries and three is twenty-seven -- and 122 already measured a
twenty-seven entry legend at 40 ms of every redraw, before anybody tries to
read it.

THREE ORDERED COMBOS, NOT A CHECKLIST. The order IS the encoding, and a
checklist has no way to say which column is the colour and which is the
shape. Adding a channel is filling in the next box.

THREE REFUSALS, EACH A REAL ANSWER RATHER THAN AN OVERSIGHT: no first
channel turns the others off, because a shape meaning one thing beside a
colour meaning the q-value is two claims on one dot with nothing saying
which; a column repeated on a second channel draws the same fact twice; and
there is no fourth box at all.

FOUR SHAPES, because four is how many pyqtgraph symbols a reader can tell
apart at eight pixels -- a star and a pentagon are the same blob at that
size, and a legend distinguishing them lies about what is on screen. Past
four they repeat, and the legend hands back what each level ACTUALLY got so
a collision is visible rather than silent.

OPACITY NEVER REACHES ZERO. A fully transparent point is an absent point,
and a channel that can delete a datapoint is not an encoding.

THE CONTROL IS VISIBLE AGAIN. It was hidden because it duplicated the
volcano's own "Colour by a column…"; it now does something that menu cannot,
and a feature nobody can reach is not a feature.
