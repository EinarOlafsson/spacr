================================================================================
THE CELLPOSE WORKBENCH DREW A DUMBBELL, AND FIVE MODULES WERE BORROWING ART
THEY NO LONGER NEEDED TO
================================================================================

Status:    DONE 2026-09-02, same day. Filed as a record rather than as work
           to do: the cause was found while measuring the request and the fix
           was two lines plus a table edit.
Requested: 2026-09-02 — "the cellpose workbench icon should be the cellpose
           white ico ni made, not the train icon."
Owner:     spacr/qt/app.py, spacr/qt/widgets/{fold_strip,section}.py,
           spacr/qt/screens/map_barcodes.py, tests/qt.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WRONG, AND IT WAS NOT THE ICON
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

`_ICON_OVERRIDES` in `spacr/qt/app.py` is the table for a module that BORROWS
another module's picture, and `app._icon_for_app` passes it to
`iconset.app_icon`. THREE OTHER SURFACES CALLED `iconset.app_icon` DIRECTLY:

    spacr/qt/widgets/fold_strip.py      the fold strip button
    spacr/qt/widgets/section.py         the settings-heading mark
    spacr/qt/screens/map_barcodes.py    the same strip on that host

`iconset.app_icon` cannot look the table up itself, and deliberately so --
the table lives beside the app registry it describes and that module "only
knows how to render what it's pointed at". So those three resolved a key by
FILENAME ALONE.

The Cellpose Workbench is the key `train_cellpose`. `train_cellpose.png` is a
DUMBBELL, the training glyph. Its override sends it to `cellpose_masks.png`,
the white cell outline. So the tile drew the outline and the fold button drew
a dumbbell, from one key, on one screen — which is exactly what was reported.

  IT WAS NEVER ONLY CELLPOSE. Every borrowing key was wrong on those three
  surfaces: `analyze_plaques`, `agreement`, `plate_view`, `model_compare` and
  `model_zoo`. Nobody had looked at those particular buttons.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AND FIXING IT EXPOSED THE OTHER HALF
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

With the override finally taking effect everywhere at once, ALL SIX entries
fired — and five of them made things WORSE, because each of those modules has
since been given artwork of its own:

    key                own art                        was borrowing
    ---------------------------------------------------------------------
    agreement          two overlapping circles        Annotate's crops
    model_zoo          a grid of model cards          a download arrow
    plate_view         a plate with wells             a row of barcodes
    analyze_plaques    a well full of plaques         the plaque glyph
    model_compare      two overlapping discs          the split-field mask

The table's own rule, written when four earlier entries were removed for the
same reason: "An override is for an app that BORROWS another app's picture;
it is not the place to record 'this app has an icon'." These five joined
them.

`train_cellpose` KEEPS its override. It is the one real borrow left, its own
file is the dumbbell the request asked to be rid of, and the dumbbell stays
on disk for anything that genuinely means "train".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS WAS INVISIBLE, WHICH IS THE PART WORTH KEEPING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The two halves hid each other. The three surfaces ignored the overrides, so
the five stale entries never drew anything and nobody noticed they were
stale; and because the entries looked live, nobody questioned the surfaces.
Each bug was the other's cover.

`tests/qt/test_a_folded_module_wears_its_own_picture.py` is written against
the RESOLVER rather than against one button: it walks the package for any
`iconset.app_icon` call with no `override=`, because the next module to
borrow a picture will be broken by the same omission and nobody will be
looking at its icon that day.

  IT COMPARES BY PATH, NOT BY QIcon. A QIcon is re-inked for the active theme
  and two of them compare unequal even when drawn from the same file. An
  image-equality test would have passed while the wrong picture was on
  screen, which is close to how this survived.

`test_the_mark_is_the_one_the_registry_ships` failed on the way through and
passes now. It compared the heading mark against the UN-overridden resolver,
so it had been asserting the bug; removing the `agreement` override made both
resolvers agree.

190 tests pass across the icon, fold, dock, home and README suites.
