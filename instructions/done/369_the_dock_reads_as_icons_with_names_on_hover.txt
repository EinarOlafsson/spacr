WHAT IS ALREADY TRUE, MEASURED ON A 1440x900 WINDOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Two asks are done, and both were done by earlier items rather than by luck:

  * NESTED MODULES ARE NESTED. 71 dock rows: 37 top-level and 34 indented
    fold children, each carrying `isFoldChild` and a `foldParent`. That is
    instruction 330's work, and `tests/qt/test_the_dock_and_menu_show_the_
    folded_modules.py` holds it.
  * HELP IS A SECTION AT THE BOTTOM. The headings are Core, Data, Tools,
    Assays, Help -- in that order, Help last. That is 348 part 1.

So the five that remain are the appearance of a row and the mark on one of
them.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE HOME ROW WEARS THE WRONG MARK, AND IT IS THE ONLY ROW THAT DOES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Measured: for 39 of the 40 rows checked, the dock's icon is pixel-identical
to what `spacr.qt.app._icon_for_app` gives the Home tile. Exactly one
differs, and it is `__home__`.

  SO ASKS 1 AND 5 ARE ONE DEFECT. "The home icon should be the spacr icon"
  and "the icons should match the modules on the home screen" are the same
  row, and the second is otherwise already satisfied. Say so when it is
  fixed rather than claiming a sweep.

`spacr/resources/icons/app_icon.png` ships and is the application's own mark.
`iconset.bundled_icon_path("home")` answers None today, which is why the row
falls back to something else.

  WATCH: `app_icon.png` is one of the four assets
  `tests/qt/test_space_theme.py::test_the_bundled_set_is_a_monochrome_alpha_
  mask` exempts, because it carries tonal structure in RGB rather than being
  an alpha mask. Every other dock icon is re-inked per theme; this one cannot
  be, and dropping it in unchanged will either ignore the theme or come out
  as a silhouette. Decide which is wanted BEFORE wiring it: the mark as
  drawn, or a mask cut from it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE NAME ON HOVER, IN BLUE, TO THE RIGHT OF THE ICON
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The names came off the rows in 348 -- deliberately, and the request that
drove it was the maintainer's -- and the row's name currently appears in the
STATUS STRIP at the bottom of the window when the row is hovered.

This asks for something different and closer to hand: the name beside the
icon it belongs to, vertically centred, left-aligned against the icon's right
edge, in blue, only while hovered.

  THE WIDTH IS THE HARD PART AND IS NOT A DETAIL. The dock is 220 px wide
  (`WIDTH_MIN`, pinned by `test_sidebar_caps_its_width_and_elides_a_patho-
  logical_name`). A name that appears INSIDE that width has to fit next to a
  26 px icon; a name that appears OUTSIDE it -- floating over the page like a
  tooltip -- does not, but is then a second tooltip beside the one the row
  already has. Choose deliberately and record which:
      (a) inside the dock, elided when it does not fit;
      (b) an overlay that is allowed to exceed the dock's width.
  Whichever is chosen, THE ROW MUST NOT RESIZE ON HOVER: 348 fixed the row
  height for exactly this reason -- a target that moves under the pointer is
  the flicker this dock has already been reported for once.

  AND THE ACCESSIBLE NAME STAYS. Three things read `text()` back
  (`clipped_items`, `fitting_width`, and the tutorial scripts' label
  fallback) and all three fail silently on an empty string, which is why 348
  cleared only the PAINT. Nothing here may clear the property.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHITE TO BLUE, AND THE TRAY GOES BUT THE HIGHLIGHT STAYS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * The icon is re-inked per theme by `iconset` already -- it is an alpha
    mask and the ink colour is chosen by the theme -- so "white to blue on
    hover" is a second ink colour on the hovered row, not new artwork. That
    is the mechanism to use; painting a blue copy of every PNG is not.
  * The dark grey container behind the rows goes. The HOVER HIGHLIGHT STAYS,
    which means the highlight can no longer be "the tray showing through" and
    has to be drawn by the row.
  * `_DockRow` already paints its own plate and icon (348), so both changes
    belong in its `paintEvent` rather than in a stylesheet that would have to
    guess at the same geometry.

  ONE COLOUR, NAMED ONCE. The blue is the theme's accent, not a literal:
  `spacr/qt/theme.py` owns the palette and the dock reads it. A hard-coded
  #RRGGBB here is a dock that stops matching the application the day the
  palette moves, and there are four themes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW IT WILL BE CHECKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* The `__home__` row's icon is the application mark, and all 71 rows'
  icons equal what the Home screen draws for the same key.
* Hovering a row shows its name to the right of its icon in the accent
  colour, and leaving hides it again -- driven with a real `QEnterEvent` and
  `Leave`, not by calling the handler.
* The row's geometry is IDENTICAL hovered and unhovered. Measured, both
  ways, at the same window size.
* The icon's ink differs hovered and unhovered, and both differ from the
  fallback -- in all four themes.
* No row paints its name when it is not hovered, and every row still
  answers `accessibleName()`, `toolTip()` and `text()`.
* The grey tray is gone from the painted result and the hover highlight is
  still visible against the page behind it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2026-09-02: ALL FIVE REMAINING ASKS DONE. THE MARK HAD TO BE SIMPLIFIED.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1 AND 5, THE HOME MARK. `home.png` is new and is cut from `app_icon.png` by
`tools/icon_generators/home_from_the_app_mark.py`. Both call sites now use
`iconset.app_icon("home")` rather than `iconset.icon("home")`, which was
answering a Font Awesome house.

  THE MARK DOES NOT SURVIVE 26 px AS DRAWN, and this was measured rather
  than assumed. `app_icon.png` is a teal tile carrying a cell outline, a
  nucleus, an interior grid, three parasites and four dots -- ten parts,
  strokes about 8 px wide in a 1024 px image. Downscaled to the dock's
  26 px, NO PIXEL REACHED ALPHA 200. The first version shipped it anyway
  and every object-level check passed while the row on screen was
  unchanged, which is exactly the failure a QIcon-equality test cannot
  see.

  Dilating the whole drawing was tried at radius 8, 16, 24 and 32 and
  produced a blob at every one. What worked is SIMPLIFICATION, which is
  what an app glyph normally is: keep the two largest parts -- the cell
  outline at 20,068 px and its nucleus at 7,574 px -- and drop the detail
  that only exists at launcher size. At 26 px that reads as a cell with a
  nucleus and matches the line weight of every other dock icon. The tile
  itself is untouched and stays the launcher icon.

2, THE NAME ON HOVER. Option (a) of the two this file offered: INSIDE the
dock, elided when it does not fit. The overlay was rejected because the row
already has a status-strip hint and a native tooltip, and a third floating
label for the same string is not more help.

  `text()` is neither cleared nor set -- 348's three silent readers are
  unaffected. Only painting was added.

3, WHITE TO BLUE. A `SourceIn` fill over the icon's own pixmap, in
`active_palette()["accent"]`, resolved at paint time. No second artwork and
no hex literal.

4, THE TRAY. This is TWO MAINTAINER REQUESTS THAT DISAGREE and the answer is
a split, recorded in `theme.py` where it is made. #16j said the dock must
never be transparent, having seen the app list as a ghost over a galaxy;
this file says the dark grey container can go. Over `cell` and `glass` there
IS a picture and #16j's complaint applies verbatim, so the dock stays
opaque; on `dark`, `light` and `space` there is no wallpaper and the
container comes off. THE RIGHT BORDER IS KEPT IN ALL FOUR, so the page still
ends at a line. One line reverses this if the maintainer wants it gone
everywhere -- named in the comment.

GEOMETRY IS IDENTICAL HOVERED AND NOT, asserted on the real widget. 348
pinned the row height so a target does not move under the pointer, and
nothing here was allowed to undo it to make room for a word.

ONE FRAGILITY FIXED ON THE WAY. The dock's `leaveEvent` reset every icon's
SIZE and nothing reset the new ink, so a pointer leaving the column downward
left one row blue with its name showing. Both are reset together now: two
things that mean "the pointer is no longer here" cannot disagree.

15 new tests, 144 pass across the dock, fold, theme and icon suites.
