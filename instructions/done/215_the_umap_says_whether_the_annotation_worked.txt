================================================================================
THE UMAP SAYS WHETHER THE ANNOTATION WORKED
================================================================================

Status:    DONE. The verdict, and the picture it is read off.
Requested: 2026-08-21 -- "generate a umap, perform a hyperparamiter search
           find stucture that separates the positive and negative controlls
           and see where the annotated cells land, do they cluster with PC or
           NC ... this would be a quality controll and a proof of sorts that
           the annotation is working provided that the cells with a positive
           effect size cluster with the positive controll and the cells that
           do not have a positive coeffisient (or equivelent) do not cluster
           with the positive controll and cluster with the negative controll.
           this would be implemented as yet another tab and would draw
           heavily from the image UMAP module."
Owner:     Qt GUI (new tab), annotation

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT IS WORTH DOING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

IT IS INDEPENDENT OF THE ANNOTATION. The annotation is made from sequencing
fractions plus a phenotype call. This asks a different question of a
different space -- where does the cell sit among the controls, using every
measurement at once -- and the controls supply a frame of reference that owes
nothing to the annotation. Agreement between two such different routes is
worth more than either alone.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT MAKES IT A TEST RATHER THAN A PICTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Three things, and without them it will agree with whatever it is shown.
`spacr/annotation_umap_qc.py` implements all three.

  1. THE HYPERPARAMETERS ARE NOT CHOSEN ON THE CELLS BEING JUDGED. Searching
     until PC and NC separate is FITTING THE PROJECTION TO THE LABELS, and
     with enough measurements some setting always separates two groups.
     `fit_on_controls` splits the control wells, tunes on one half and
     reports the separation achieved on the OTHER. The held-out number is
     the only one worth reading, and the gap between the two says whether
     the search found biology or found the split.

  2. THE READOUT IS A NUMBER. `neighbour_purity` gives the share of a cell's
     nearest CONTROL neighbours that are positive controls. A UMAP's cluster
     sizes and the distances between them are artefacts of the layout; who
     is near whom is all it preserves, so a statistic built on anything else
     reads the projection rather than the data.

  3. THERE IS A NULL. Cells land somewhere whatever the annotation says.
     `effect_agreement` asks whether purity tracks the guides' effect signs
     and compares that to the same statistic with the effects SHUFFLED
     BETWEEN GUIDES -- which preserves the number of guides, the cells per
     guide and the purity distribution, and destroys only the
     correspondence being claimed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE TRAP IT CANNOT ESCAPE, WHICH DECIDES WHAT MAY BE JUDGED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A METHOD THAT PICKS CELLS BY THE PHENOTYPE SCORE WILL PRODUCE CELLS NEAR THE
POSITIVE CONTROLS WHATEVER ELSE IS TRUE -- that is what it selected for.
`rank` takes the top-scoring cells in the well, so its cells clustering with
PC is a restatement of how it chose them.

The check is informative exactly for methods whose cell choice did not use
the score: `sudoku` with the score out of the graph, `assigned`, and
`attributed` where the effect comes from the regression. `circularity_
warning` refuses to be quiet about it, and the tab must show that warning
where it applies rather than drawing the plot as though it meant the same
thing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE TAB HAS TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Draws on `generate_image_umap`, `hyperparam.umap_objective_scores` and
`umap_search` -- the search, the scoring and the recipes already exist and
must not be reimplemented.

  * pick the annotation method and the run;
  * embed the annotated cells together with the control cells;
  * tune on half the controls, report the held-out separation, and REFUSE
    to draw conclusions when it is poor;
  * colour by purity, and show the per-guide table beside the plot;
  * state the correlation, its permutation p-value, and the two group means;
  * show the circularity warning where it applies.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* The tab reports a held-out separation, not only a tuned one, and says so
  when the two disagree.
* Purity is measured off neighbours, never off cluster membership.
* The verdict carries a permutation p-value.
* A score-picked method shows the circularity warning.
* On a run where the annotation is known to be good the correlation is
  positive and significant; with the effects shuffled it is not.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WAS DONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

`spacr/annotation_umap_qc.py` is the engine and
`spacr/qt/widgets/annotation_umap_tab.py` is the tab, added to the results
panel as "Annotation check" and reachable by key like every other panel --
so a grid tile can open it, which is instruction 199's door.

THE GUARDS ARE HONOURED WHERE THE USER MEETS THEM, which is the only place
they matter:

  * a method that PICKED cells by the phenotype score is refused with the
    reason INSTEAD of the plot. Drawing it under a caption saying it means
    nothing would still leave a picture somebody screenshots.
  * an embedding whose controls did not separate on cells the search never
    saw stops the verdict rather than decorating it, and prints the tuned
    score, the held-out score and the gap so the refusal can be argued
    with.
  * the readout is neighbour purity; cluster membership is never consulted.

NOTHING IS COMPUTED UNTIL ASKED. A UMAP over a screen's cells is seconds of
work, and a tab that embedded on construction would charge that to every
user who never opens it.

Tests: `tests/test_annotation_umap_qc.py`, 24 passing -- the engine's three
guards, and the tab refusing the same things in the same order.

THE EMBEDDING IS DRAWN, AND IT GOES THROUGH THE FIGURE SYSTEM.

The verdict used to be a paragraph of numbers with the embedding computed,
held, and never shown. A paragraph is not where anyone looks first, and
"where do the annotated cells land" is a question about a picture.

`PurityScatter` is a `fast_plots.FastPlot` and the table beside it is
`fast_plots.ResultsTable`, in a splitter the reader owns. That is the whole
reason it is not a canvas of its own: a scatter here needs hovering a point
to see its guide, the right-click restyle menu, the export and a sortable
table -- all of which those two already carry, and a second canvas would be
a second set of them, drifting from the first.

COLOUR IS THE READING AND POSITION IS ONLY THE LAYOUT. Purity goes on the
colour scale through `colour_by_column`, so a cell with no purity is drawn
grey rather than dark -- painting a missing value at the bottom of viridis
invents a measurement. The GROUP is the shape instead: triangles positive
controls, diamonds negative, circles the annotated cells. A second colour
scale would have fought the one carrying the number.

THE PER-GUIDE TABLE CARRIES THE EFFECT BESIDE THE PURITY, because agreeing
is the claim being made and two columns a reader can put side by side are
what let the correlation be checked rather than believed. A guide with no
effect gets an empty cell; zero is a coefficient somebody measured.

EVERY REFUSAL CLEARS THE PLOT AND THE TABLE. That is the rule the text
already followed and the drawing had to inherit: a scatter left on screen
under a message saying this run means nothing is still a picture somebody
will screenshot. Score-picked method, no cells, too few controls, a tuning
error, an embedding whose controls did not separate on held-out cells --
each one leaves nothing drawn and says why the panel is empty.

`MINIMUM_SEPARATION` now decides, rather than being a documented constant
nothing read. It is named so it can be argued with, and raising it refuses an
embedding the engine was willing to call trustworthy.

Tests: `tests/qt/test_annotation_umap_plot.py`, 18 -- the scatter driven
directly so the drawing is covered without paying for a UMAP, the guide
table, every refusal leaving nothing behind, and one whole run on a screen
whose controls separate.
