================================================================================
CLASSIFY (CV): ONE GRAPH THAT GROWS, NOT ONE GRAPH PER EPOCH
================================================================================

Status:    done - 2026-08-06
Requested: 2026-08-06 - "the graphs keep updating for each epoch and i get a new graph for each epoch. I want one new datapoint in the graph at each epoch, not a completely new graph. the graphs have a black background when they should be dark gray like the container. best would be if they had a completely transparent background with white lines and white text in dark mode (teal, blue, purple and gray for the lines in the graph.)"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE STATE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Not investigated. Two separate defects in one report:

  1. A NEW FIGURE PER EPOCH. Something in the training loop is creating a
     figure and emitting it rather than appending a point to one that
     already exists. Find the emit; it is probably in deep_spacr's training
     callback or in whatever renders to the screen's figure panel.
  2. A BLACK background. Almost certainly matplotlib's default figure
     facecolor rather than the theme's - the same class of bug as the
     segmentation-QC black box (INVARIANTS 3), arrived at through a
     different library. `bg` is the WINDOW colour, #000000 on dark; the
     container is a SURFACE.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Twenty epochs currently means twenty figures to scroll through instead of
one curve to read, which is the thing the curve exists for - you cannot see
whether the loss is still falling if each epoch is a separate picture.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WAS DONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

See the commit message for the full
reasoning; the short version is below.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Emit DATA per epoch, not a figure. The panel owns the figure and appends;
   the training loop appends a point. That also removes the per-epoch
   figure-encoding cost from the training thread.
2. Transparent figure and axes facecolor, so the container shows through and
   the page opacity applies - do not hard-code a grey, or it freezes one
   opacity into the plot while everything around it follows the preference.
3. Text, ticks and spines in the theme's fg; series colours teal, blue,
   purple, grey as asked. Take them from the palette rather than literals,
   so the light theme is not left with white-on-white.
4. Check the light theme explicitly. "White text" is right for dark and
   invisible on light.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

(Each numbered step above was carried out.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Train for several epochs: ONE figure, gaining a point per epoch, on a
transparent background, readable in both themes.
