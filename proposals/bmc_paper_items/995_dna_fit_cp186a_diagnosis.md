# CP186A DNA-fit diagnosis

Read-only assessment, 2026-11-22. **Keep the 0.90 criterion and report CP186A G1/S/G2 as not judged.** The saved fit fails S6 under both tested DNA classifiers. The supplied summaries establish that failure but cannot identify its physical cause. The first diagnostic priority is the difference between the populations used to fit and score the model, followed by field centring and measurement provenance. Neither is yet a demonstrated cause.

## What the DNA path actually does

1. **Feature.** The run selects `dapi` and copies `nucleus_dapi_integrated_intensity` into `_dna_integrated` (`p990_run.py:49,112`; `ops_cell_cycle.py:1080–1085`). Integrated intensity sums masked, non-NaN pixel intensities in the shared measurement implementation (`spacr/measure.py:2255–2274`; OPS caller `spacr/ops_sample.py:479–500`). It is not mean intensity or a robust-z DNA value. Classifier features undergo separate normalization, which can affect the exclusion mask.

2. **Normalization and population.** OPS excludes every model class other than interphase from field-mode estimation and fitting (`ops_cell_cycle.py:1114–1116`). It explicitly fits by well and centres by site using the shared Measure adapter (`:494–499`; `measure.py:7398–7405`). The transform is `log2(max(raw, 1))`; a field mode is shifted to the unweighted median of eligible field modes, then transformed back (`measure.py:7243–7258,7291–7304`). The run requires at least 50 eligible cells per field; smaller fields retain zero offset (`p990_run.py:49`; `measure.py:7243–7258`). This corrects a field-wide multiplicative shift. It does not itself subtract additive background or remove within-field gain variation.

3. **Background contract.** This OPS route does not call the separate `_nucleus_dna` adapter, whose optional correction subtracts outside-ring median/mean × nucleus area (`measure.py:7145–7171`). Therefore the fitter docstring saying “background-subtracted” does not establish that these inputs received that correction. Upstream correction may already exist; its actual provenance is not bound by the supplied summaries. Funk feature values come from `spacr_equiv_plateA/run`; `analysis_plateA_flatv2` supplies alignment keys (`p990_prep.py:33–35,77–87`). The earlier field-centering proposal used a different raw reference path and does not prove identity of these acceptance values.

4. **Fit model.** The shared implementation uses Gaussian G1/G2 peaks, a Gaussian-softened uniform S component between them, and a constant outlier component (`measure.py:6850–6874`). It seeds from a smoothed log-DNA histogram, trims to 0.35–3.2 times the initial G1, and iterates on individual values using log likelihood (`:6877–6901,6944–7047`). G2/G1 is clipped to 1.6–2.4; the peaks share a CV clipped to 0.02–0.15 (`:7008–7018`). These are model restrictions, not measured CP peak widths. The outlier weight is clipped before renormalization (`:7006–7007`), so its final weight need not obey that nominal cap. No final CV, weights, residuals or iteration diagnostics are supplied.

5. **Score and collapse.** S6 uses **all finite centred cells in each well**, including cells excluded from fitting. It creates 100 linear-intensity bins between the 0.5th and 99.5th percentiles of that scored population, predicts bin counts as the total fitted density at each centre × all-finite count × bin width, and computes `R² = 1 − SSE/SST` (`ops_cell_cycle.py:502–514,679–695`). Thus fit and score differ in population, range and objective. Even the density scaling counts finite cells outside the histogram edges. S6 requires R² ≥ 0.90 and the ratio range; low R² collapses G1/S/G2 (`:44–45,732–737`). `final_stages` maps other DNA outcomes to interphase before collapse (`:760–782`). R² is histogram goodness of fit, not phase accuracy or a calibrated confidence probability.

Acquisition provenance deserves care: the [primary PERISCOPE repository](https://github.com/broadinstitute/2022_PERISCOPE/blob/main/README.md#L70-L145), read on the assessment date, describes CP186A examples with **20X Cell Painting** and **10X sequencing** sessions, and separately describes corrected image products. This does not identify which image variant or resampled measurement entered this acceptance run. Do not attribute its low R² to “10X” or absent flat-field correction without that link.

There is also a reporting inconsistency worth resolving before interpreting tails. `run_cp186a_base_DNA.json` records 484,008 interphase cells, 350,037 unresolved flags and 136,112 auto flags across 486,149 cells. `REPORT.md:94` calls all interphase cells unresolved, which those totals cannot support. They imply **133,971–136,112 auto-interphase cells**: lower bound `136112 − (486149 − 484008)`, upper bound `min(136112, 484008)`. They do not identify the actual cross-tab or tail/NaN counts. Pre-existing interphase outcomes can remain auto after collapse (`ops_cell_cycle.py:760–782`); that does not make them validated G1/S/G2 calls.
