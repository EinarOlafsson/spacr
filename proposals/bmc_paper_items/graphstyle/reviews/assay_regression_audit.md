Exposure is an estimate from the role of the entry point and its callers, not measured usage telemetry: high means normal assay/regression reports or previews; medium means optional analysis families or manuscript packages; low means the specialized literature-comparison route. This is not a ranking of scientific validity.

Static evidence establishes explicit font sizes, colors, alpha settings, chart types and layout formulas. A fixed nonsquare canvas alone does not prove the final axes are nonsquare; affected rows say that no square contract exists unless measured render evidence is available. Text clipping, overlap, legend occlusion and dark-theme contrast are render-dependent. Dataset-name titles can be allowed by the authority, whereas statistics, descriptive claims and sample counts require legend placement; a row mentioning a title does not imply that every dataset title is forbidden. Annotation-box opacity is chrome and is not itself a data-alpha violation. Reference *conditions* use grey; reference *lines* have the authority's separate black/dotted/0.6-alpha convention. Do not conflate those roles.
