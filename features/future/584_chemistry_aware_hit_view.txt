A CHEMISTRY-AWARE HIT VIEW: SMILES, RDKIT CLUSTERING, SAR TABLES

Status: DONE (alpha) 2026-10-01 — Control Charts' hit scoring takes a
compound table with SMILES, draws the hits' structures grouped by cluster
(RDKit Morgan fingerprints, Butina), and exports SAR tables linking potency,
phenotype and available host toxicity (item 540). Verified on a real CPJUMP1
compound-screen plate and in the live GUI. Hidden with Alpha features.

2026-09-27 -- Implemented as Alpha
    Where: the hit-scoring option of Control Charts (item 570). Its controls
    gain "Compounds..." (a CSV/Excel/Parquet table read by spacr.tabular:
    a SMILES column, a compound name, and a well column with an optional
    plate column, or no wells and names matching the Treatment column) and
    "Cluster similarity" (Tanimoto, default 0.6). A new output section,
    "Structures and SAR", draws the hit compounds' structures (up to 24,
    framed by cluster, labelled with potency and cytotoxicity) and lists
    the SAR table. "Export hits..." also writes sar_table.csv (one row per
    compound: SMILES, cluster, nearest hit and its similarity, n wells, hit
    wells, potency = median of the ranking statistic, best rank, phenotype =
    median of the scored measurement, host viability and cytotoxicity
    index, selectivity_* when fitted), sar_wells.csv, sar_clusters.csv and
    hit_structures (through plot.save_figure).
    Code: spacr/sp_stats.py private helpers only (_read_compound_map,
    _attach_compounds, _fingerprints, _butina, _structure_activity,
    _cluster_sar, _draw_hit_structures, _write_sar_report, _host_toxicity,
    _host_by_well); screen code in spacr/qt/screens/control_chart.py. Hits
    are clustered by Butina on Morgan radius-2 2048-bit fingerprints;
    every non-hit compound joins the cluster of its most similar hit at the
    same similarity, so a cluster lists its inactive analogues (the SAR).
    Host toxicity is read from the viability_well and viability_selectivity
    tables of the loaded measurements.db (Measure's viability step, 540).
    RDKit is optional and imported only when used: without it the SAR
    tables still export (no clusters or structures) and the screen and the
    report say "pip install rdkit". MAINTAINER: add rdkit as an optional
    dependency (e.g. a "chemistry" extra); not added to setup.py here.
    Checked (CPU, RDKit 2026.3.6 installed into
    /mnt/firecuda2/codex/scratch-docs/584/pylib, not the env): a two-plate
    screen with two planted active series (2-phenylbenzimidazoles,
    tert-butylbenzamides), five unrelated inactives and one inactive
    fluoro analogue: all five hits called, clustered exactly into the two
    series, the analogue joined series A as an inactive member (Tanimoto
    >= 0.6), toxicity of one compound carried to its SAR row, tables and
    the structure sheet exported. Tests: tests/test_chemistry_aware_hits.py
    and tests/qt/test_chemistry_hit_view_alpha_gate.py (the RDKit tests
    skip where RDKit is absent).
    Still open: DONE WHEN on a real small compound screen; structures are
    drawn as RDKit bitmaps (not vector) in the exported sheet.
ALPHA: widgets ControlChartChemistry (the compound picker and cluster

2026-10-01 — REAL COMPOUND-SCREEN ACCEPTANCE; ITEM DONE AS ALPHA
A pinned public CPJUMP1 BR00117010 augmented profile, its published plate
map, and JUMP-Target-1 SMILES metadata supplied 384 real wells (64 negative
controls and 320 sample wells) and 302 named compounds. The profile's
Cells_Number_Object_Number and Nuclei_AreaShape_Area were passed without
normalization or fabricated values. The actual threaded Control Charts
screen loaded the table, scored robust z against the negative controls,
attached compounds through its picker, clustered hits with RDKit, rendered
24 molecular structures in light and dark themes, and exported through the
real Export hits button. Independent calculations matched every robust-z
score and hit call: 88 hit wells, 77 hit compounds, 74 clusters. The visible
SAR table intentionally caps at 200 rows; the export contains all 302.
All four primary CSV exports match the in-memory tables, and the SVG holds
vector paths/text with no raster image nodes. The two invalid published
SMILES remain reported/unclustered. Host viability was absent from these
sources and was not invented; the earlier measured-toxicity synthetic check
still verifies conditional host-toxicity columns when supplied. Original
public input bytes remained unchanged. Input URLs, exact hashes, export
hashes and limits are in features/data/584_real_compound_screen_2026-10-01.json.

The real GUI exposed two display defects: after resize, one-shot tight_layout
left adjacent molecule tiles overlapping; light-theme labels were too pale.
The structure renderer now uses constrained layout on draw/resize and the
resolved figure text colour in screen mode while preserving explicit print
ink. A resize regression reproduces the old overlap and passes with the
fix; 25 relevant chemistry/vector checks and the final 3 layout/theme cases
pass under bounded CPU/memory scopes. Real final light/dark captures were
visually checked for readable, separated tiles. This meets the feature's
small real compound-screen DONE WHEN at alpha scope. It is not a claim that
the source plate measured host toxicity or that clustering validates biology.
