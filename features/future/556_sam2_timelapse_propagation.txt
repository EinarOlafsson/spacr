WHAT TO BUILD
    YES, THIS CONNECTS OBJECTS OVER FRAMES: SAM2's video predictor takes
    masks (or clicks) on one frame and propagates each object through the
    movie with a memory of its appearance, so every object keeps its
    identity across frames (segmentation and tracking in one step). Offer it
    as a timelapse mode next to trackastra / the IoU stitcher / Timeflows:
    seed from spaCR's first-frame masks or user clicks, propagate forward
    and backward, let the user correct a frame and re-propagate. Its outputs
    also make training data for Timeflows (426). SAM2 in its own
    environment.

DONE WHEN
    On a real timelapse, identities from SAM2 propagation are scored against
    ground-truth tracks (e.g. CTC metrics) next to trackastra and the
    stitcher.
