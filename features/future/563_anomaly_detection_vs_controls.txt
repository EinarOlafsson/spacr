EVIDENCE

WHAT TO BUILD
    Model the negative-control cell population in embedding space (kNN
    distance, normalising flow or density model) and score every cell and
    well for how unlike controls it is, to find phenotypes nobody named;
    rank wells and show example outlier cells.

DONE WHEN
    On a screen with known hits, anomaly scores rank the hits above controls
    (AUROC), and top outlier cells are shown for review.

ALPHA: 2026-09-27
    Built as an alpha option of Control Charts (hidden unless Preferences ->
    Show alpha features; ALPHA_FEATURES[563] widgets ControlChartAnomaly,
    ControlChartAnomalySection).
    - spacr/sp_stats.py: _score_anomalies models the negative-control
      objects as normal. Features (emb_ embedding columns when present,
      measurement features otherwise) are centred on each plate's controls,
      MAD-scaled and projected on the controls' principal components.
      Detectors, all classical and CPU only: robust Mahalanobis
      (MinCovDet), kNN distance to controls, isolation forest, and
      Gaussian-mixture density. Controls are scored cross-fitted (two
      halves), so the outlier threshold (control quantile, default 0.99)
      is honest. Wells are ranked by outlier fraction, and the enrichment
      over the control rate and a robust z are reported. Known hits (positive
      control, or listed wells/prc/treatments) give well and object AUROC.
      _draw_anomaly_review shows the top outlier objects (crop tiles when
      png_path points at files, else marked on control PC1/PC2), and
      _write_anomaly_report writes CSVs plus the figure through save_figure.
    - Control Charts: toggle, detector, quantile, known hits and export in
      the controls; an "Anomalies" output section with ranked wells and the
      review figure.
    - Done-when check (tests/test_anomaly_detection_vs_controls.py): on a
      synthetic 2-plate screen with 4 hit wells per plate whose phenotype
      is in half the cells, every detector gives well AUROC >= 0.95 (1.0
      observed) and ranks the 8 hit wells first. It holds under a plate offset.
      CPU only, small data; no GPU run.
    LEFT: no deep density model (normalising flow). The brief lists kNN,
    flow or density; kNN and GMM cover it on the CPU. A flow would be an
    optional extra. Not yet checked on a real screen with known hits.
    Object crops show only when the loaded table carries png_path (a
    png_list join is not done automatically). Captions await a catalog
    rebuild.
