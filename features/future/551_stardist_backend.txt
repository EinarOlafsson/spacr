THE DIAMETER. StarDist has no diameter, but its scale argument rescales the
plane, and it matters: on plate1_E01_1_1 (40x nuclei, median 86 px, 49 in
spaCR's Cellpose-SAM reference) native scale cut them into 111 objects of
median 14 px. Scaled so the nuclei are about 50, 30 and 22 px it found 58,
44 and 39 objects of median 73, 85 and 91 px. So a set diameter rescales by
_STARDIST_DIAMETER / diameter = 30 / diameter; a blank diameter keeps the
plane's own scale. That field is then left out of the nuclei benchmark.
