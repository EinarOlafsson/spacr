SCRATCH / WOUND-CLOSURE ASSAY

Status: IMPLEMENTED AS ALPHA 2026-09-26. Measure finds the scratch wound in
every frame (texture, intensity or cell masks), writes area, mean/min width,
closure rate and half-closure time per field, well and condition, with
closure curves and a plate map; the Measure preview draws the wound edge.
Hidden with Alpha features. DONE WHEN not met on the public data (below);
the 2026-09-30 accuracy pass narrowed the gap but did not close it.
Owner: unassigned. Proposed from image.sc research on 2026-09-26 (what users
ask for in other applications).

2026-09-26 — Implemented as Alpha
    Measure runs it at the end of a run when `wound_closure` is on. Frames
    are grouped by plate/well/field from their merged names (schema
    .parse_field_stem, _merged_names.parse_merged_filename) and ordered by
    timepoint; `wound_hours_per_frame` turns frames into hours and
    voxel_size_xy_um widths into um.
    Wound: `wound_source` texture (541's local-SD idea, on the frame divided
    by its own heavy blur so vignetting drops out, normalised by its median;
    cut by Otsu on the first frame, placed a fifth of the way up from the
    open level in log texture, then the edge grown back by the
    window-straddling offset), intensity (541's quarter-way rule, frame
    normalised by its 95th percentile) or masks. The first frame's largest
    open region is the wound; its principal axis and a band around it are
    kept, and later frames count open regions of at least window^2 px
    whose centre is in the band, clipped to it. Cells inside the wound are
    filled (as the Wound Healing Size Tool and hand tracing count them).
    The cut decided on frame 0 is reused for later frames.
    Width: per one-pixel position along the axis, counted only where the
    whole cross-section is in the field (tilted scratches); mean, min
    (0 = bridged), max, SD.
    Closure: relative open area, half-closure time (linear interpolation),
    closure rate (LSQ slope of the closed fraction up to 90 % closed, with
    R2), width rate and front speed (half of it), reopened flag.
    QC status per field: ok, no_wound (<1 % open at t0), not_a_scratch
    (open across <50 % of the axis), missing_start (first frame later than
    the run's); wound_ok per well.
    Tables in measurements.db: wound (field x frame), wound_well (fields
    pooled by summed area), wound_closure (per well, with condition),
    wound_condition (mean curve and per-well t50/rate mean, SD, n). CSVs and
    figures (closure_curves, half_closure_time, plate_<plate>_half_closure
    via plate_qc.plate_layout/layout_matrix, per-field wound_edges when plot
    is on) in results/wound_closure, all through spacr.tabular and
    spacr.plot.save_figure. `wound_conditions` maps wells to conditions in
    the well_spec vocabulary (r2, c3, B03); unmapped wells are their own.
    Preview: a Wound toggle in the Measure preview draws the edge on the
    loaded field and reports open %, mean and narrowest width.
    Code: spacr/measure.py (_wound_* helpers, _closure_metrics,
    _run_wound_closure; all private, no new module), measure_preview.py.
    Accuracy, synthetic (ragged bands of known area, 0/20/90 deg,
    brightfield/fluorescent/masks): open area within 2 % of the first
    frame's area, half-closure time within 0.1 frame, rate within 6 %.
    A gap narrower than about one texture window reads as closed.
    Accuracy, public data: Zenodo 21779854 (Cytometry A, CC BY 4.0), the 24
    HUVEC/SKOV-3 series whose every frame has a hand-corrected (ImageJ)
    wound mask, 86 frames at 0/8/12/24 h or 0/24/48 h; frames read at half
    resolution, wound_window 15. First frames: median IoU 0.90, median open
    area error 0.8 % of the field. Later frames: closure within 5 points of
    the hand value in 45 %, within 10 in 61 %; median error 5.6, mean 13.9
    points; 9 of 24 series have a mean closure error within 5 points; t50
    in both for 14 series, median difference 1.2 h (mean 2.6 h). For
    scale, the Wound Healing Size Tool on these frames was 25 points of
    field area off (the paper's correction table); ours 3.2. The 5 %
    DONE WHEN is therefore NOT met on this dataset: its time points are
    separately re-imaged (focus, exposure and in one series zoom differ
    from t0), and the frames were hand-corrected because automatic
    measurement failed on them, so they are the hard ones. Scripts and
    per-frame results: /mnt/firecuda2/codex/scratch-docs/536 (tools/,
    real_final_15.csv).
    Tests: tests/test_scratch_wound_closure.py (synthetic sources and
    angles, widths, bridging, gaps beside the wound, cells in the wound,
    no-wound/not-a-scratch QC, closure metrics, conditions, pooling,
    measure_crop end to end with four tables, CSVs and figures, alpha
    registration) and tests/qt/test_measure_wound_preview.py (overlay,
    masks slice, failure reporting, button and Measure form hidden/visible).
    Also: 541's confluency overlay used a bare .scaled(); it now goes
    through hidpi.scaled_for like the wound overlay.
    Left: the 5 % agreement on real time-lapses (a true same-position
    time-lapse with hand measurements would be the fair test; per-frame
    re-thresholding for re-imaged time points was tried and was worse);
    a brightfield-only entry that does not need Measure's masks pipeline
    (Measure still needs merged arrays); drift registration between
    frames; a manual threshold override; condition curves across plates
    are pooled by condition name only.
2026-09-30 — Accuracy pass on the public data (DONE WHEN still not met)
    Two changes shipped in spacr/measure.py (private helpers, no new
    settings): _wound_fronts redraws a scratch's first frame (texture and
    intensity sources, after the QC checks) between two fronts smoothed by
    a running median over 30 % of its length, so floating debris or a
    smooth patch of monolayer at one edge no longer cuts into or bulges
    the starting wound; _wound_relevel recalibrates the texture cut on
    every later frame from that frame's own open level (in the first
    frame's band, seeded by an Otsu split of the band when it separates)
    and covered level (outside the band), unless under 20 % of the first
    area is open, so re-imaged time points with other focus or exposure
    keep their wound. Two synthetic tests pin both (debris over a quarter
    of a fresh wound; later frames at 1/3 contrast, 2.5x noise, 2.5x
    contrast).
    Held-out evaluation: the 24 series were split by experiment into two
    folds of 12 (A: LUIS RAW, Originais (1), P1, Controle+Saudavel; B:
    Migracao n2/n3, P2, Pre-Eclampsia, originais); each design choice was
    picked on one fold by mean error and scored on the other, and both
    folds picked the same configuration, so the pooled numbers are
    out-of-fold. The shipped code then changed four details for the
    synthetic cases (QC before the fronts, a running median of good fronts
    only, the Otsu seed, the covered level from everything outside the
    band) and was scored once more; those are the numbers below. 62 later
    frames, P2|CARBO|F9 (still QC not_a_scratch)
    counted as misses:
                       within 5   within 10   median   mean
        before (09-26)   45 %       61 %       6.7     16.7 points
        now              47 %       65 %       5.9     12.0 points
        fold A / B now  53/40 %    72/57 %   4.6/7.6  7.8/16.4
    (Median over measured frames only, as reported on 09-26: 5.6 -> 5.3.)
    First-frame IoU median 0.90 -> 0.91; t50 in both for 17 series (was
    14), median difference 0.9 h (was 1.2), mean 1.8 h (was 2.6); 9 of 24
    series with mean closure error within 5 points (unchanged).
    Tried and not shipped (all scored the same way): a per-pixel logistic
    or gradient-boosted edge model (texture at 3 scales, gradient energy,
    4-window context, position across the band) trained on one fold:
    92-95 % pixel accuracy but 16 % / 23 % within 5 / 10 points held out,
    because each fold's calibration is biased by +-18 points on the other;
    frame-to-frame drift registration: phase correlation of the monolayer
    never locks (peak error ~1.0), the time points are separate fields of
    view, and the hand wound moves by up to 160 px across the scratch;
    re-centring the band on each frame's own wound, nearest-run tracking
    of the fronts per cross-section, morphological opening of later open
    masks, a lower later-frame cut, and a percentile-of-gradient texture:
    each better on one fold and worse on the other.
    What limits it: the hand masks call a sparse leading edge covered and
    three LUIS/Pre-Eclampsia frames closed where a band of low texture is
    still visible; several later frames are at another zoom. A true
    same-position time-lapse with hand measurements is still the fair
    test. Scripts and per-frame CSVs:
    /mnt/firecuda2/codex/scratch-docs/536/improve (tools/, final.csv,
    base.csv); cached arrays in /mnt/wd4tb/scratch/536.
    Left: the 5 % DONE WHEN; relevelling for the intensity source; a
    manual threshold override; the other items listed above.
