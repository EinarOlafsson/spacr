CELL-CYCLE PHASE CLASSIFICATION: A TORCH, AN XGBOOST AND A MEASUREMENTS VERSION

Status: DONE (alpha) 2026-09-26 (evidence 02cbf2ff9) — Measure calls G1, S, G2 or M for
every nucleus three interchangeable ways -- DNA-content gates, xgboost on the
nucleus measurements, torch on nucleus crops through Classify's training --
into one cell_cycle_phase column, with per-well phase fractions among
infected and uninfected cells. Scored on synthetic nuclei of known phase;
hand-labelled real nuclei are still to come. Hidden with Alpha features.
Owner: unassigned. Proposed from image.sc research on 2026-09-26 (what users
ask for in other applications).

EVIDENCE
    https://forum.image.sc/t/how-to-analyse-cell-cycle-with-dapi-intensity/19168

WHAT TO BUILD
    Call each cell's cycle phase (G1 / S / G2-M, plus mitotic sub-stages
    where the stain allows, and FUCCI states when a FUCCI reporter is
    imaged) in three interchangeable ways, all writing the same per-cell
    phase column: (1) MEASUREMENTS: gating on measured nuclear DNA content
    (integrated DAPI/Hoechst, fitted 2N/4N peaks per plate) and FUCCI
    channel intensities, with the fitted histogram shown and editable gates;
    (2) XGBOOST: a tabular classifier on spaCR's per-nucleus feature table,
    trained from Annotate labels or from the measurement gates as weak
    labels; (3) TORCH: an image classifier on nuclear crops (CNN/ViT through
    the existing Classify training), for morphology the intensity gates miss
    (prophase, metaphase, anaphase). Report the phase fractions per well and
    their relation to infection.

DONE WHEN
    All three versions label the same test set; each is scored against hand-
    labelled nuclei (accuracy / per-phase F1) and the phase fractions per
    well agree between versions within stated tolerances.

2026-09-26 -- Implemented as Alpha
    Measure gains a "Cell Cycle (Alpha)" heading. With `cell_cycle` on, the
    end of a run reads the nucleus table it wrote and calls each nucleus's
    phase by `cell_cycle_method`; a failure is printed and does not fail the
    run. Code: spacr/measure.py only, no new module. The helpers are
    underscored, as 541's are, so the API catalogs stay the docs lane's:
    _classify_cell_cycle is the entry (it takes any measurements.db),
    _run_cell_cycle_step the Measure hook.

    measurements -- per plate (and time point) the background-subtracted
    integrated DNA intensity (integrated minus the median of Measure's ring
    just outside the nucleus times its area) is fitted by EM as a
    Dean-Jett-Fox style mixture (_fit_dna_content): Gaussian G1 and G2 peaks
    with one shared CV (at most 15 %), G2 held at 1.6-2.4x G1, a uniform S
    between them and a flat debris component (at most 15 %); nuclei below
    0.35x or above 3.2x the G1 seed are left out of the fit. The gates are
    the fitted G1/S and S/G2 crossings, or `cell_cycle_gates` [G1/S, S/G2]
    in C units (G1 peak = 2) to set them by hand. Content more than 4 peak
    widths below G1 is subG1, above G2 is >4N. A nucleus past the G1/S gate
    whose mean DNA intensity is at least `cell_cycle_mitotic_ratio` (1.8)
    times the plate's G2 median is M (condensed chromatin). Each nucleus
    keeps its fitted posteriors, and the per-well table also carries
    posterior-summed fractions (fit_fraction_*), the way a flow histogram is
    read when the peaks are wide. With plot on,
    results/cell_cycle/dna_content_<plate> shows the histogram, the fitted
    components and the gates. `cell_cycle_fucci_channels` [G1 reporter,
    S/G2/M reporter] adds fucci_state (early G1, G1, G1/S, S/G2/M) from a
    per-plate Otsu cut of each reporter.

    xgboost -- a gradient-boosted classifier on the nucleus morphology and
    DNA-channel intensity and texture columns (position, neighbour and
    distance columns left out), trained on the Annotate labels in the
    png_list column named by `cell_cycle_labels` (1-4 = G1, S, G2, M, or the
    names; joined through the nucleus's cell), or, when that is blank, on the
    gate calls whose posterior is at least 0.6 plus every M (weak labels).
    Whole fields are held out first for a held-out score; the booster is
    saved as measurements/cell_cycle_xgboost.json.

    torch -- one fixed-size crop per nucleus (twice the 95th-percentile
    major axis, 32-128 px, never resampled; DNA on R and G scaled per plate
    so brightness keeps DNA content, the nucleus's own mask on B), a
    train/<phase> and test/<phase> dataset split by field, then
    spacr.deep_spacr.train_test_model (ResNet-18 from scratch,
    `cell_cycle_epochs`, weighted loss) and apply_model: Classify's own
    training, leakage audit and model card. The phase order is written
    beside the model as cell_cycle_phases.json; `cell_cycle_model` applies a
    model trained earlier (on another plate) instead of training one.

    all -- runs the three; cell_cycle_phase is their majority, the
    measurement call on a tie. The learned methods take subG1 and >4N from
    DNA content, so every method reports the same out-of-peak nuclei.

    Database: measurements.db:cell_cycle, one row per nucleus (prcfo, DNA
    content, C value, condensation, gates, posteriors, phase_<method> and
    confidence_<method>, fucci_state, infected from its cell's pathogen
    count, cell_cycle_phase, cell_cycle_method); cell_cycle_well, per well
    and method (n and the fraction in each phase and in G2M, and the same
    among infected and uninfected cells); cell_cycle_agreement, per well the
    largest phase-fraction difference between the methods, on the nuclei
    they all called.

    Validation (scripts in /mnt/firecuda2/codex/scratch-docs/535): two
    synthetic plates, each 4 wells x 4 fields of 512 px and 1120 nuclei,
    with Poisson noise and a blurred PSF, run through Measure itself. Nuclei
    are drawn with a known phase: G1 at 2C; S uniform over 2.2-3.8C and
    growing; G2 at 4C with 1.6x the G1 area; M at 4C as a compact bright
    bar. Plate B draws half of its M nuclei as prophase instead: G2-sized,
    4C, with condensing threads but no extra brightness -- the morphology
    gates cannot see. Well fractions differ (G1 25-65 %, M 4-18 %). Labels
    are the gates (weak) or 25 % of the nuclei annotated with their true
    phase, scored on the other 75 %. torch trained 30 epochs on the GPU.

    The gates alone are 94-98 % right. Trained on the gates, the learned
    methods can only agree with them (prophase stays G2). Trained on a
    quarter of the nuclei labelled, xgboost finds prophase (M F1 .72 ->
    .97), and torch, from scratch on 277 crops, finds most of it (.90) but
    loses S accuracy: it needs more labels or epochs. Per-well fractions
    against the truth (gate-label runs): within 1.4 points for
    measurements, xgboost and the consensus and 2.9 for torch on plate A;
    5.0, 3.9, 4.3 and 5.4 on plate B, where prophase counted as G2 is most
    of the error. Between methods, the largest per-well phase-fraction
    difference is 2.5 points (A) and 3.2 (B) with gate labels, and 6.4 and
    8.6 with the 25 % annotation, all of it torch's S. Stated tolerance:
    the methods agree within 5 points per phase per well when trained on
    the gates; the annotated torch route does not meet it yet.

    Real data, the plate1 example (2682 Hoechst nuclei, no phase labels):
    G2 at 1.98x G1, one CV of 15 % (the cap; widefield nuclei), fitted
    weights G1 55 %, S 29 %, G2 11 %, debris 5 %. Gated calls are G1 54 %,
    S 6 %, G2 21 %, M 0.7 % and subG1 18 % (fragments and edge-cut nuclei):
    with peaks this wide the gates hand most of S to G1 and G2, which is why
    the per-well table also carries the posterior fractions (S 30-32 % per
    well). All three methods on gate labels (torch 30 epochs on the GPU,
    128 px crops, on the 726 nuclei of the 16 fields whose arrays are in the
    example download): per nucleus, gates and xgboost agree on 91 %, gates
    and torch on 83 %, xgboost and torch on 87 %. Per well, on the nuclei
    all three called, the phase fractions differ by 10-12 points, and gates
    against xgboost alone by 8-9 points, nearly all of it S against G2: the
    learned methods call 11-14 % S where the hard gates call 6 % (and the
    posteriors 30 %). So on real widefield nuclei the 5-point tolerance the
    synthetic plates meet is not met; the S/G2 split of broad peaks is where
    the methods disagree, and hand labels are what would settle which is
    right. In every well infected cells are more often in G2/M than uninfected ones
    (gated 25-31 % against 15-18 %), in line with the host G2 arrest
    Toxoplasma is reported to cause.

    Not done: hand-labelled real nuclei, which DONE WHEN asks for -- the
    example data has no labelled cell-cycle set, so the accuracy above rests
    on synthetic plates; next is about 300 plate1 nuclei annotated 1-4 in
    Annotate and scored through cell_cycle_labels. Gates are edited by
    number in cell_cycle_gates, not by dragging them on the histogram in the
    GUI. M is one class: prophase, metaphase and anaphase are not separate,
    and an anaphase whose two chromatin masses are segmented as two nuclei
    becomes two G1-like objects. FUCCI is not validated on FUCCI images and
    is reported beside the phase rather than folded into it. The torch
    backbone is fixed (ResNet-18 from scratch) and slow on a busy CPU.
    Phases are called at the end of a Measure run; calling them on an
    existing database without re-measuring has no button (the helper takes a
    db path). No Model Zoo entry for a trained cell-cycle model.
