EVIDENCE
    https://forum.image.sc/t/how-to-analyse-cell-cycle-with-dapi-intensity/19168

WHAT TO BUILD
    Call each cell's cycle phase (G1 / S / G2-M, plus mitotic sub-stages
    where the stain allows, and FUCCI states when a FUCCI reporter is
    imaged) in three interchangeable ways, all writing the same per-cell
    phase column: (1) MEASUREMENTS: gating on measured nuclear DNA content
    (integrated DAPI/Hoechst, fitted 2N/4N peaks per plate) and FUCCI
    channel intensities, with the fitted histogram shown and editable gates;
    (2) XGBOOST: a tabular classifier on spaCR's per-nucleus feature table,
    trained from Annotate labels or from the measurement gates as weak
    labels; (3) TORCH: an image classifier on nuclear crops (CNN/ViT through
    the existing Classify training), for morphology the intensity gates miss
    (prophase, metaphase, anaphase). Report the phase fractions per well and
    their relation to infection.

DONE WHEN
    All three versions label the same test set; each is scored against hand-
    labelled nuclei (accuracy / per-phase F1) and the phase fractions per
    well agree between versions within stated tolerances.
