WHAT WAS BUILT (2026-09-27)
    spacr/embeddings.py: _FOUNDATION_MODELS / _foundation_names, and
    _backbone_encoder dispatching a backbone name to _foundation_encoder
    (else timm). Loaders, weights pinned and downloaded once to the HF /
    torch hub cache:
      openphenom  recursionpharma/OpenPhenom @0f92333 (channel-agnostic MAE
                  ViT-S/16, 256 px, 384 dims per pass; HF remote code)
      chada_vit   nicoboou/chadavit16-moyen @91a4112 (channel-adaptive
                  ViT-T/16, 224 px, 192-dim CLS; HF remote code)
      subcell     CZI DNA-Protein_ViT-ProtS-Pool.pth (ViT-B/16 + gated
                  attention pool, 448 px, 1536 dims; two planes, DNA then
                  protein; rebuilt on transformers.ViTModel, strict load)
      cell_dino   refused with a reason: Meta has not released the weights
                  (dinov2 README_CELL_DINO.md).
    embed_array honours an encoder's in_channels: None (channel-adaptive)
    gets one plane per channel under per_channel and every channel in one
    pass under project (the >3 projection limit is lifted for these); 2
    gets the first two planes, zero-padded. Plain encoders unchanged.
    _retrieval_scorecard: kNN accuracy (majority of k=10), mAP and
    precision@k vs chance, over the same standardised cosine space as the
    "find cells like this" index (active_learning._SimilarityIndex).
    No new dependency: transformers (already used) + torch. The two HF
    models run trust_remote_code at a pinned revision. None of the four
    needed its own env here (torch 2.6, transformers 4.48, timm 1.0.17).
