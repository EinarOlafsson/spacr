WHAT TO BUILD
    Estimate the bleed-through matrix from single-stain control wells and
    unmix every field before segmentation and measurement, as a step of the
    enhancement chain (Make Masks and Mask generation) and of Measure;
    record the matrix in provenance.

DONE WHEN
    On single-stain controls, off-target signal falls to background after
    unmixing; a double-stain field's measured intensities move toward the
    known values.

ALPHA: 2026-09-27. Settings unmix, unmix_controls and
    unmix_background_percentile, registered under 538 in ALPHA_FEATURES
    (spacr/settings.py) under a "Spectral Unmixing (Alpha)" heading on Make
    Masks and Measure; hidden unless Preferences -> Show alpha features is
    on. A value saved while hidden still reaches the run.

WHAT WAS BUILT (2026-09-27, CPU only)
    - spacr/psf_pipeline.py: _parse_unmix_controls reads
      "channel:well[,well]; ..." (the dye's own channel and its single-stain
      wells). _prepare_unmixing reads up to 24 fields per dye from those
      wells and _estimate_bleed_through regresses every channel on the dye's
      own, per field centred (so a background offset never reads as
      bleed-through), pooled, negative spill clipped to 0, unit diagonal,
      refusing a near-singular matrix. _unmix solves each pixel with the
      inverse after setting aside a per-plane background percentile and adds
      it back, so an unstained channel stays at background.
    - Make Masks (V1 io._correct_v1_segmentation_batch and V2
      pipeline_v2.stream_masks_from_stack): the PSF session carries the plan
      and unmixes each whole raw field (all channels) before illumination
      correction, the PSF and the enhancement chain. The matrix joins
      psf/segmentation_application.json, so resume refuses archives made
      with another matrix; processing_requested counts unmix.
    - Measure: measure_crop estimates once over the measured channels of
      merged/, stores the record under the private key _unmix_record (so it
      is in the saved measure_crop_settings) and in
      measurements/bleed_through.json, and _measure_crop_core unmixes the
      channel arrays in their integer dtype before preprocessing hooks.
    - The matrix is printed in both runs.
    - Tests: tests/test_spectral_unmixing.py (known two-dye matrix is
      recovered; controls' off-target signal falls to background; a double
      stain's object intensities land within 2% of the known amounts;
      provenance; Measure dtype) and
      tests/qt/test_spectral_unmixing_alpha_gate.py.

REAL-DATA VALIDATION 2026-09-30 (CPU; no code change)
    Data: LUMoS (McRae et al. 2019, PLoS One; figshare
    10.6084/m9.figshare.c.4537607). Scripts and outputs outside the repo in
    /mnt/wd4tb/scratch/538 (val538_fp.py, val538_beads.py); receipt
    features/data/538_541_580_real_data_validation_2026-09-30.json.
    - Single-FP controls (Cerulean-only and YFP-only T cells, confocal z-
      stacks, CFP and YFP channels). The matrix was estimated through
      _prepare_unmixing from the left half of every slice and checked on
      the right half, which it never saw. Estimated Cerulean -> YFP-channel
      spill 2.6 %, YFP -> CFP 0.09 %. Held-out Cerulean cells: off-target
      excess 3.07 % of own signal (1.6 background SD) raw, 0.42 % (0.22 SD)
      unmixed. YFP cells: 0.02 % raw, -0.06 % unmixed. DONE WHEN holds:
      off-target falls to background.
    - Five-bead field (4 channels; each lone bead is a real single-stain
      control; types found by clustering spectra; half the beads per type
      estimate, half evaluate). Channels 1-3 follow the linear model: a
      far-red bead reads 83 % of its signal in the neighbouring channel.
      Estimate 0.880 vs 0.843 measured on held-out beads. After unmixing,
      the off-target residual is 6.8 % (from 83 %). The other spills go from
      <1 % to <0.25 %. Doubles made by summing real bead crops of two dyes
      (background once): the bled-into channel's error is +86 % raw and
      -5.5 % unmixed; the other channel stays within 0.3 %.
      The 6.8 % residual comes from pixel-level PSF mismatch between the
      channels. About 55 % of bead pixels solve below zero and are clipped
      (integer output), which biases object means upward; unclipped means
      over-correct by about 5 %. So strong bleed-through is cut about 12x
      but does not reach background.
    - Channel 0 of the bead image is not linear: its per-bead signal is a
      roughly constant amount plus a small proportional part, and its
      values are quantised. Linear unmixing cannot model such a channel;
      it was left out of the 3-channel check and is noted as a limitation.
    - Estimators tried: smoothing before regression (sigma 2 and 6) and a
      background-subtracted integral ratio. Neither beat the shipped
      per-field centred regression on both datasets, so the code is
      unchanged.
    Still left: no real double-stained sample with known amounts (the
    doubles above are sums of real singles); per-channel registration or
    PSF matching before unmixing; the clipping bias on strong spill.
