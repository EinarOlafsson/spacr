LIVE/DEAD VIABILITY AND HOST CYTOTOXICITY INDEX

Status: IMPLEMENTED AS ALPHA 2026-09-27. Measure calls every nucleus live
or dead -- from a dead stain, a live stain, both, or nuclear morphology --
with thresholds fitted per plate or set by hand, and reports per well
viability, a live-cell index and a cytotoxicity index scaled to the control
wells, each plate's Z', and with a plate map the dose-response of viability,
cytotoxicity and infection with a selectivity index. Scored on synthetic
plates of known viability and (2026-09-30) on one public real stained
plate: per-cell calls agree with blind hand calls on 95-97 % of random
cells after a fix, but Z' > 0.5 is NOT met on the real plate (see
2026-09-30 below). Hidden with Alpha features.
Owner: unassigned. Proposed from image.sc research on 2026-09-26 (what users
ask for in other applications).

EVIDENCE
    https://forum.image.sc/t/live-dead-cell-identification-and-counting/112723

WHAT TO BUILD
    Classify cells live/dead from viability stains (e.g. PI/calcein, SYTOX)
    or morphology, report viability and a cytotoxicity index per well, and
    show it next to dose-response so antiparasitic hits can be separated
    from host toxicity.

DONE WHEN
    On a control plate (untreated vs a cytotoxic compound) viability and the
    index separate the conditions with Z' > 0.5.

2026-09-27 -- Implemented as Alpha
    Measure gains a "Viability (Alpha)" heading. With `viability` on, the
    end of a run reads the nucleus table it wrote (the cell table when there
    are no nuclei) and calls every object live or dead; a failure is printed
    and does not fail the run. Code: spacr/measure.py only, no new module,
    private helpers; _classify_viability is the entry (it takes any
    measurements.db), _run_viability_step the Measure hook.

    Calls. `viability_dead_channel` (propidium iodide, SYTOX, DAPI on
    unfixed cells) and/or `viability_live_channel` (calcein) name measured
    channels; each nucleus's mean intensity minus the median of Measure's
    ring just outside it is the signal. Per plate (and time point) the
    signal is put on an arcsinh scale (cofactor three robust SDs, so the
    unstained population stays Gaussian) and two Gaussian populations are
    fitted by EM from Otsu's split; they count as two only when Ashman's D
    >= 2, the smaller holds >= 3 objects and both the fit and the data dip
    between them. The cut is where the two cross. A plate with one
    population is cut 5 robust SDs from its median (above it for a dead
    stain, below it for a live stain); a plate with fewer than 20 objects
    borrows the cut fitted on all plates. `viability_thresholds` [dead,
    live] overrides either cut by hand (None keeps it automatic). Dead-stain
    positive = dead; live-stain positive = live; with both, positive for
    neither = "unstained" (not counted live). With no stain channel, dead is
    called from nuclear morphology: condensation = (mean DNA intensity /
    plate median) / (area / plate median area), cut the same way on a log
    scale -- pyknotic nuclei score ~4, interphase ~1.

    Per well (measurements.db:viability_well): counts of live, dead and
    unstained, viability (live / objects), dead_fraction, the infection of
    live cells when pathogens were segmented, live_cell_index (live objects
    / mean of the plate's negative-control wells, so detached cells count
    against viability), and cytotoxicity_index: the live-cell index scaled
    to the plate's controls, negative 0 and positive 100, by
    dose_response.normalise_to_controls; with negative wells only it is
    100 x (1 - live-cell index), with no controls 100 x (1 - viability);
    cytotoxicity_basis says which. Controls are named in the well_spec
    notation (`viability_negative_wells`, `viability_positive_wells`, both
    with the Plate... picker). A well among the run's fields whose cells
    were all lost is reported with 0 objects, not dropped.
    viability_qc, per plate: the cuts, how each was found, their separation
    and positive fraction, the control means and SDs, and Z' of viability,
    live-cell index and cytotoxicity index from dose_response.plate_reports
    (i.e. control_chart.zprime_frame, the number the Control Chart and
    Dose-Response screens show); assay_ok = 1 when viability and the index
    both reach Z' 0.5.

    Dose-response: `viability_plate_map` (CSV/Excel with a well column,
    compound/treatment and concentration/dose, optional plateID) joins a
    compound and concentration onto each well; viability, the cytotoxicity
    index and the infection of live cells are fitted per compound with
    dose_response.fit_frame (which refuses non-monotone and unbounded
    curves rather than inventing an EC50) into viability_dose_response, and
    dose_response.selectivity_index divides the host CC50 (EC50 of the
    cytotoxicity index) by the parasite EC50 into viability_selectivity.
    Figures (plot on, results/viability, through plot.save_figure): per
    plate the stain histograms with their cuts and a live-vs-dead scatter,
    plate heatmaps of viability and the cytotoxicity index
    (figures.plates.build_plates), control and sample wells per plate with
    their Z', and the three dose-response curves per compound side by side.

    Validation (CPU; scripts and outputs in
    /mnt/firecuda2/codex/scratch-docs/540). Synthetic fields generated by
    tests/test_live_dead_viability.py and run through Measure itself:
    cells as 9 px disks, Hoechst in every nucleus, dead cells with a
    propidium-iodide nucleus, live cells filled with calcein, some live
    cells infected; background, blur and Poisson noise. A killed cell
    detaches with p 0.5, otherwise stays as a stained dead cell. Accuracy is
    the per-object live/dead call against the drawn state.
      1. Dose plate (80 wells x 2 fields, 12198 cells; untreated and
         cytotoxic control columns, a selective compound CC50 60 / parasite
         EC50 0.3, a toxic one CC50 1 / EC50 1, an inactive one; clean
         stains): accuracy 100 % with both stains, dead stain only and
         morphology, 99.98 % with calcein only. Z' 0.92 on viability and on
         the index. Fits: toxic CC50 0.96 (0.91-1.02; truth 1.0), viability
         EC50 1.44, parasite EC50 0.98, SI 0.98 (0.73-1.33); selective
         parasite EC50 0.29 (0.26-0.33; truth 0.3), host CC50 unbounded
         ("not bounded by 0.03-100"), so its SI is reported one-sided; the
         inactive compound refused as non-monotone (flat).
      2. Hard control plate, two plates (the second at 0.4x stain gain),
         48 untreated + 48 cytotoxic wells x 2 fields, 12960 cells; PI and
         calcein log-SD 0.8 and 0.6, 15 % of dead cells dim (PI 10x lower,
         keeping a fifth of the calcein), only 60 % of dead nuclei
         pyknotic. Accuracy / dead sensitivity / live specificity:
             both stains      99.81 % / 99.59 % / 99.94 %
             dead stain only  99.61 % / 98.95 % / 99.99 %
             live stain only  97.16 % / 92.17 % / 99.95 %
             morphology       86.26 % / 61.68 % / 100 %
         (morphology finds the pyknotic 60 % and nothing else, as designed).
         Per-well viability error mean 0.25 points (max 3.3) with both
         stains. Z' per plate, viability / cytotoxicity index: both stains
         0.94 / 0.95 and 0.92 / 0.94; dead only 0.93 / 0.95 and 0.91 / 0.93;
         live only 0.88 / 0.92 and 0.87 / 0.92; morphology 0.67 / 0.86 and
         0.71 / 0.87. The automatic cut was the fitted mixture on every plate
         and stain; the 0.4x plate was cut at its own lower level.
      3. Depth: the hard plate subsampled (20 draws) to fewer cells per
         well. Both stains, median (min) Z' viability / index: 180 cells
         0.93 / 0.94; 92 cells 0.90 / 0.74 (0.63); 44 cells 0.86 / 0.57
         (0.47); 21 cells 0.79 / 0.36; 10 cells 0.78 / 0.10. Morphology
         falls below 0.5 under ~90 cells. Viability is a fraction and keeps
         its Z' at low depth; the live-cell index counts cells, so its Z'
         depends on how many cells, and how evenly seeded, a well holds.
      4. Uneven seeding: the hard two-plate control design again with the
         cells seeded per well spread log-normally (CV 15 %), 13210 cells.
         Calls are unchanged (both stains 99.86 %, dead only 99.76 %, live
         only 96.96 %, morphology 84.94 %), and viability keeps Z' 0.93 /
         0.93 (both stains; 0.85 / 0.86 live only; 0.72 / 0.69 morphology),
         but the cytotoxicity index, which counts live cells against the
         untreated wells, falls to Z' 0.52 / 0.56 with both stains or the
         dead stain, 0.45 / 0.51 with calcein only and 0.23 / 0.32 from
         morphology. So on a plate seeded this unevenly the index passes
         0.5 only barely with a dead stain; viability passes easily. Read
         the index with viability_qc's Z' beside it.
    DONE WHEN (control plate, untreated vs cytotoxic, Z' > 0.5 for
    viability and the index): met on the synthetic control plates with a
    dead stain (viability 0.92-0.95, index 0.52-0.95 depending on seeding
    evenness and depth), with the limits above: the index needs about 50+
    cells per well and even seeding; morphology alone does not meet it on
    an unevenly seeded plate. On a real stained plate it is not met:
    see 2026-09-30.

2026-09-30 -- Real-data validation (CPU; worktree val-540)
    Dataset: Trizna et al. 2023, Sci Data 10:160, "VirtualStaining
    Dataset" (figshare 10.6084/m9.figshare.21971558, CC BY 4.0, 506 MB).
    Caco-2 cells, untreated vs camptothecin 6 uM 24 h, 40x, 1388x1040
    JPEG; four series (plates here): sets 1/3 acridine orange + PI, sets
    2/4 DiOC6 + PI; sets 1-2 imaged 2019-02-16, sets 3-4 2019-11-15; 252
    fields (20-41 per condition per set). The files are the other way
    round from the paper's text: <k>.1.jpg is green, <k>.2.jpg is red
    (PI). Ground truth given with it: the condition of every field and
    flow-cytometry dead fractions per series (ctrl 1.8 +- 0.2 %, cam
    8.0 +- 1.0 % for sets 1-2; 2.4 +- 0.8 % and 10.1 +- 1.2 % for 3-4).
    No per-cell labels and no field-to-well map are published.
    Pipeline: images binned 2x, cells segmented classically (Otsu on the
    smoothed green+red sum with a low-background floor, watershed from
    intensity peaks; not validated, merged/edge objects occur), written
    as spaCR merged stacks (plate = set, control fields rows A-D, cam
    fields rows E-H, one field per well), measured by measure_crop with
    the mask as nuclei (so the ring background exists), then
    _classify_viability with negative wells r1-r4, positive r5-r8.
    Per-cell reference: 300 objects (per set 50 random + 25 from the top
    fifth of the PI signal), shuffled and hand-called BLIND to spaCR's
    call by the validating agent (Claude, not a biologist) from red/green
    crops: D = a PI-bright body inside the object, L = none, U =
    unscoreable (fragment, edge sliver, a neighbour's PI, diffuse
    textured red); 65 U excluded, 235 scored (171 random; 20 D).
    Before (the 2026-09-27 method): the two-population fit was never
    accepted on a real plate (Ashman's D 1.7-2.0, no dip: the PI-negative
    population has a long bright tail, not a Gaussian), so every plate
    used the one-population cut, median + 5 robust SD, which falls inside
    that tail. PI only, random cells: accuracy 88.3 % (sets 1-2 86.7 %,
    sets 3-4 90.1 %), dead sensitivity 0.75, live specificity 0.90;
    both stains 87.7 %. Dead % ctrl/cam per set: 16.2/35.1, 7.6/28.4,
    1.5/11.4, 6.6/32.2 (flow 1.8/8.0, 1.8/8.0, 2.4/10.1, 2.4/10.1).
    Live stain only: one population on sets 1, 2 and 4, cut 5 robust SD
    below the median, so nothing was called dead (accuracy 88 % = the
    share of live cells, dead sensitivity 0.20).
    The PI signal (mean minus ring median) ranks the hand calls well
    within a plate (AUC 0.98-1.00); the cut was the problem. Rules
    compared leave-one-plate-out on the hand calls (mean held-out
    accuracy all / random): median + 5 robust SD 0.81 / 0.88; 95th-99th
    percentile of the negative-control objects 0.88 / 0.95 (but it fixes
    the untreated dead fraction by construction, and a plate whose
    untreated wells hold dead cells, set 4, collapses at the 99th);
    object mean >= F x its ring median 0.94 / 0.97 (F chosen 1.55-1.65
    in every fold); one fixed manual cut 0.95 / 0.98 (same microscope).
    Change (spacr/measure.py, _split_by_plate + _object_background): a
    DEAD stain on a plate the fit calls one population is now cut per
    object against its own ring: dead when its mean is at least
    _VIABILITY_BACKGROUND_FOLD = 1.65 x the ring median (source
    'background' in viability_qc; the plate threshold shown is 0.65 x the
    plate's median ring). F = 1.65 was fitted on sets 1-2 only (first
    value of the plateau of their accuracy) and then scored on sets 3-4.
    Unchanged: the mixture cut when two populations are found (all
    synthetic plates), manual cuts, plates under 20 objects (pooled),
    tables without ring columns or with a ring at or below zero
    (background-subtracted images), live stains and morphology.
    After, per-cell vs hand calls (random cells; top-fifth stratum):
        PI only, sets 1-2 (tuning)   98.9 %  (86.5 %)
        PI only, sets 3-4 (held out) 95.1 %  (85.2 %); was 90.1 % (77.8 %)
                                     dead sens 0.83 (was 0.58), live
                                     spec 0.97 (was 0.96)
        both stains, held out        91.4 %  (88.9 %); was 88.9 % (85.2 %)
        PI only, all 171 random      97.1 %; dead sens 0.90, live spec 0.98
    Dead % ctrl/cam per set: 1.0/25.6, 0.8/16.1, 0.7/20.0, 4.2/27.6. The
    untreated wells now sit near flow cytometry (1.8-2.4 %); the
    camptothecin wells read 2-3x flow's 8-10 %, as did the hand calls
    on random cam cells (7-30 %): attached, imaged cells are not the
    trypsinised suspension flow counted, as the paper notes.
    Z' (DONE WHEN) -- NOT MET on this plate:
      each 40x field as a well (the only unit the data defines), PI only,
      viability: -0.91 / -1.35 / -2.11 / -1.68 per set (before -7.26 /
      -4.69 / -1.97 / -1.83). No single cut does better than -1.07 (cut
      searched for the best Z' directly). The live-cell and cytotoxicity
      indices are worse (-2 to -10): objects per field range 18-267
      (10th-90th percentile 39-155).
      Consecutive fields pooled into three pseudo-wells per condition
      (the paper imaged three wells per condition, map unpublished):
      viability Z' 0.59 / 0.66 on sets 1-2 but -2.06 / -0.50 on held-out
      sets 3-4 (before -3.66 / -1.34 / -1.20 / -0.19). So the fix sharpens
      per-cell calls but does not deliver Z' > 0.5 here.
      Ceiling: the dataset's own flow-cytometry dead fractions give Z'
      0.42 (sets 1-2) and 0.22 (sets 3-4) from their 3-well SDs; a 2 %
      vs 8-10 % dead contrast on ~40-150 cells per field cannot reach 0.5.
      The DONE WHEN needs a stronger cytotoxic control (near-total kill)
      and replicate wells, which this public set does not have.
