WHAT TO BUILD
    Estimate per-object and per-field segmentation uncertainty from test-
    time augmentation or model ensembles (disagreement between
    flips/rotations/models), show it as a map, and route the most uncertain
    fields to the top of Make Masks' curation queue.

DONE WHEN
    Uncertainty correlates with actual segmentation error on fields with
    ground truth (item 532 set), and the queue ordering finds the worst
    fields first.

ALPHA: 2026-09-27 -- shipped behind Preferences -> "Show alpha features"
    (ALPHA_FEATURES[568]: widget MakeMasksUncertaintyButton).

WHAT WAS BUILT (2026-09-27)
    * spacr/active_learning.py: _tta_label_sets segments a field under
      identity, flip_lr, flip_ud and rot90 with any segmenter and maps each
      result back; _segmentation_uncertainty scores two or more label sets
      (TTA passes or a model ensemble alike): a per-pixel map (foreground
      disagreement 4p(1-p), and each reference object's own score), per
      object 1 - mean best IoU with the other passes, per field 1 - mean
      panoptic quality of the other passes against the reference.
    * spacr/curation_queue.py: order "uncertain" (most uncertain first,
      unscored last, falls back to value with a notice), read from
      <folder>/curate_uncertainty.csv, written by _write_uncertainty
      (merge, sorted, atomic). spacr-make-masks --order uncertain.
    * Make Masks: "Uncertainty..." button (alpha) with "Map this field's
      uncertainty" (four detection runs with the Object detection
      settings on a worker thread; heat map on a new Uncertainty tab,
      least certain object named) and "Rank the fields, most uncertain
      first" (scores every open field, writes curate_uncertainty.csv in
      the queue/open folder, reorders the fields; refused while blind).
    * tools/validate_segmentation_uncertainty.py; result in
      features/data/568_segmentation_uncertainty_2026-09-27.json.

    Reading: uncertainty tracks errors on the objects the model drew, well

IMPROVED SCORING (2026-09-30)
    Built:
    * _segmentation_uncertainty takes optional cell-probability logit maps
      and the reference pass's Cellpose flow vectors. New readings: "area"
      (fraction of the drawn foreground whose disagreement is >= 0.5),
      "missed" (pixels with mean logit above the threshold less 2 lying
      more than 2 px from anything any pass drew, as a fraction of the
      foreground: the consistent misses TTA cannot see), "flow_errors"
      (Cellpose's own flow error per object). "field" is now area +
      missed; the old 1 - mean PQ is kept as "spread". A reference
      object's score is the larger of its TTA disagreement and its flow
      error / 0.4, capped at 1. The map also shows near misses.
    * _tta_passes keeps each pass's cell probability (turned back) and the
      identity pass's flow vectors; Make Masks' Uncertainty button now
      segments through the private _cellpose_detect_with_vectors, so both
      "Map" and "Rank" use them. No new settings or captions.
    * tools/segment_uncertainty_crops.py caches r5 x 4 TTA (labels, cell
      probability, flows) plus stock Cellpose-SAM, r2 and Cellpose 3 cyto3
      (its own env); tools/validate_segmentation_uncertainty.py scores
      eleven field readings and four object readings with a held-out split.
      Result: features/data/568_segmentation_uncertainty_2026-09-30.json.

    Validation (CPU, loaded machine, ~70 min): the ten toxo_pv test fields,
    6 x 256 px crops each (60 crops, 469 truth vacuoles, 335 r5 objects,
    37 wrong at IoU 0.5). Held out = two halves of five fields, all of a

    Honest reading: both held-out pair choices included near misses
    (paired with ensemble area, then TTA area), so the shipped score

    cell probability, and is not wired into Make Masks. The five-field
    half with plate1/N04/I10/G12/D05 stays hard for every score (<= 0.40).
    r5 missed 171 of 469 truth vacuoles here, and these ten fields were in
    r5's training pool (README), so errors are training-set errors.
    Field-level "done when" is now moderately met, not strongly.
