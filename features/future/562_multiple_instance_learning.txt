WHAT TO BUILD
    Train attention-based MIL models from well-level labels only (treated vs
    control, gene knockout vs control) so arrayed screens without cell
    labels get a classifier and a per-cell attention score showing which
    cells carry the phenotype.

DONE WHEN
    On a screen with known responder fractions, attention ranks true
    responders above non-responders (AUROC) and well-level prediction beats
    mean-feature baselines.

ALPHA: 2026-09-27 -- the Embeddings screen's "Learn from well labels..."
    button (ALPHA_FEATURES[562], widget EmbeddingsWellMilButton), hidden
    until Preferences -> Show alpha features is on. The action still runs on
    a table given while the button is hidden.

WHAT WAS BUILT (2026-09-27)
    spacr/embeddings.py (private helpers, no new module, no new dependency;
    torch + scikit-learn):
      _mil_bags        per-cell table -> one bag per well (wellID, or well;
                       well_label 0/1 or a named positive label; features =
                       emb_* columns, else every numeric column)
      _mil_fit         gated-attention MIL (Ilse et al. 2018): one 32-wide
                       cell layer, gated attention, linear well head; CPU,
                       60 epochs, Adam 5e-3, features standardised on the
                       training cells
      _mil_predict     well probability; per-cell attention (weight x cells
                       in the well, 1 = equal share) and per-cell evidence
                       (the linear head on the cell alone; the well score is
                       the attention-weighted sum of evidence)
      _mil_scorecard   stratified k-fold over wells: MIL well AUROC vs L2
                       logistic regression on the well mean and on mean+SD;
                       with known responders, held-out attention and
                       evidence AUROC over the cells of positive wells
      _synthetic_mil_bags  Gaussian wells, per-well offsets, responders
                       planted along one direction in positive wells
      _mil_from_table  scorecard + fit on all wells -> cells table
                       (mil_attention, mil_evidence) and wells table
                       (mil_probability)
    The screen button reads any table spacr.tabular reads and writes
    <name>_mil_cells.csv and <name>_mil_wells.csv beside it.

MEASURED (CPU only; the GPU was held for days)
    Synthetic (tests/test_multiple_instance_learning.py; 32 wells x 50
    cells, 8 dims, 10% planted responders): attention and evidence AUROC
    > 0.85, MIL well AUROC > 0.8 and above the mean baseline.
    Real embeddings reused from item 560: OpenPhenom (project, 384 dims) on
    1,530 plate1 single-nucleus crops. Responders = multiple_pathogens
    crops planted into positive wells; background = uninfected +
    single_pathogen. 34 wells x 32 cells, 4-fold CV, 3 partitions each.
    Scripts: /mnt/firecuda2/codex/scratch-docs/562/embed.py, bench.py
    (+ bench.csv).
      responders   MIL well  mean  mean+SD  attention  evidence  s/run
      10%          0.720    0.649  0.615    0.657      0.780     3.2
      25%          0.982    0.977  0.939    0.691      0.878     2.8
      5-30% mixed  0.912    0.858  0.897    0.701      0.880     2.3
    Reading: MIL beats the mean-feature baseline where it matters (low and
    mixed responder fractions) and ties it at 25%. Responders are ranked
    above non-responders in every setting; the signed evidence ranks them
    well (0.78-0.88), raw attention only moderately (0.66-0.70) at 34 wells.
