      "cannot honour"; no probability map.

  Zoo rows and cards, live_preview and the zoo buttons pick InstanSeg up
  from its spec (model_zoo.PREFIXED_KINDS).

THE DIAMETER. InstanSeg rescales by pixel size, and spaCR's mask settings
carry none. On plate1_E01_1_1 (40x nuclei, median 86 px, 49 in spaCR's
Cellpose-SAM reference) at the model's own scale the nuclei output found 35
objects of median 20 px; with pixel sizes that bring the nuclei to about
43, 26 and 17 px it found 34, 39 and 40 objects of median 51, 81 and 77 px.
So a set diameter is passed as pixel_size = model pixel size (0.5 um) x 26
/ diameter; blank runs the plane at the model's scale. That field is left
out of the nuclei benchmark.
