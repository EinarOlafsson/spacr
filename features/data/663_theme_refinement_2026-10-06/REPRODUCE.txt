source hashes recorded in each file. The Python probes are the exact scripts
used for those measurements; their original scratch/worktree paths are kept
for provenance. Do not run them against the existing evidence directory.

set its ROOT/REPO constants in that copy to new dedicated scratch/worktree

load and OS differences in the new receipt rather than overwriting originals.
