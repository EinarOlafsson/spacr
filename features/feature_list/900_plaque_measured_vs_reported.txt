WHAT THE STATE IS
Scoring plaque effects measured from published figures against the effects
the papers report, and merging the measured wells into one meta-analysis
table, were custom scripts that imported private spaCR functions and ran
from an origin/main worktree.

WHAT WAS BUILT
- Public entries: resolve_plaque_model(name, settings) and
  segment_wells(images, model, settings) (n, areas, cleared_frac per crop,
  plaque_model and plaque_well_resize honoured).
- Scoring: measured_by_condition (area, number, cleared_fraction),
  figure_mode_regions (Figure-mode regions matched to detector boxes at
  IoU >= 0.5 and their stored segmentation), compare_reported and
  score_figure_folder (results_measured_vs_reported.csv and summary.json).
- Merge: merge_figure_wells (flags rejected_not_plaque, low_confidence,
  zoom_duplicate, organism_*, snap_*, reader_second_look; within-panel
  reported effects) and run_figure_scoring writing results/figure_merge.
- Settings (Plaque > Figure Scoring α): figure_condition_labels,
  figure_model_labels, figure_detections, figure_mode_folder,
  figure_segmentation_folder, figure_score_folder, figure_metric,
  compare_permutations (1000), compare_seed (0), figure_low_confidence (0.7),
  figure_second_look; plaque_mode gains figure_score and figure_merge.
- The stored model readings stay data: score_figure_folder reads them, it
  never re-reads a figure, and no LLM provider is involved.
