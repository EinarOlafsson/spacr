Problem (item 960 proof): on wells of 2-3 tiles the per-well tuning picked
the strict read-quality floor 0.25 (39.7 % genotyped) where 0.1 gives 64.3 %
at the same decoy FDR. Cause: tune_thresholds needs 2 tuning sites; at
tune_fraction 0.5 a well of fewer than 4 sites has at most 1, so it does not
tune and takes the strictest value of every axis.
Build: ops_prefix_tuning_min_sites (int, default 0 = off, ALPHA_FEATURES[982]).
A tuned well with fewer sites keeps the floor at SMALL_WELL_FLOOR = 0.1 (the
floor axis holds 0.1 only; the other axes are tuned as before); one log line
per such well; the tuning json and tuning entry gain small_well_floor and
small_well_sites; quality_classes.json records the setting only when on.
Tested value 4, chosen from data: Funk A1, 50 tiles, 200 random n-tile wells
per n, tuned with spaCR's tuner and scored on the held-out tiles. n = 2-3: the
free tuning always picks 0.25 and the guard gains +22.4 points; n >= 4: the
free tuning never picks 0.25 and beats the guard by 1.3 points, so the guard
must not apply there. Proof: item966_guard_proof/REPORT.md.
