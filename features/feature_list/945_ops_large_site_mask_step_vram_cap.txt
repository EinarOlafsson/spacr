BUG
Item 921 runs Cellpose's mask step (resize_and_compute_masks: flow
following, seed finding and the flow check, remove_bad_flow_masks ->
flow_error -> masks_to_flows_gpu) once on the whole stitched 5,500 px site,
on the model's device, outside the GPU governor. Under a 6 GB per-process cap
site 15 (6,328 cells) stopped the run with torch.OutOfMemoryError (1.47 GiB
in _extend_centers_gpu).

FIX
- _cellpose_masks(model, flows, cellprob, *, settings, record, label): a
  model off CUDA keeps item 921's call unchanged (no governor). On a CUDA
  card (_governed_mask_step):
  - seg_mask_device='cpu': the whole step on the CPU.
  - 'gpu', or 'auto' when the estimate (mask_step_bytes) fits the budget
    (_mask_step_budget): item 921's call whole on the card through the
    governor (_guarded), so an out-of-memory error is retried and then run
    on the CPU instead of stopping the run. Labels byte-identical to before
    whenever it does not run out of memory.
  - otherwise: the flow following on the card when it fits, else the CPU;
    the flow check in full-width row bands sized to the budget
    (flow_check_bands, _band_flow_errors, _banded_flow_check), each band
    through the governor; a band that does not fit runs on the CPU, and
    after a band falls back on an out-of-memory error the rest run on the
    CPU too.
- Budget: the governor's (free memory less gpu_memory_margin_gb and other
  spaCR jobs' claims, under gpu_memory_fraction), and no more than
  seg_mask_vram_gb less what the job already holds (its allocator's reserved
  memory plus a CUDA context).
- Estimates: dynamics 32 B per site pixel + 64 B per cell pixel; flow check
  24 B per band pixel + 320 B per mask pixel (the whole-site step measured
  342 B per cell pixel on CP186A Well1 site 76).
- Banded = whole on one device: a band holds its masks plus, as context,
  every mask they touch (an edge pixel's flow reads its neighbour mask's
  diffusion; without the context 17 of 40 synthetic masks got other errors),
  each mask's diffusion reads only its own pixels, its centre comes from its
  own bounding box, niter is the whole image's (2 x the largest extent), and
  above Cellpose's float32 switch (4e7 padded pixels) a band is padded with
  empty rows past it, so it diffuses in the same precision. Flow errors then
  equal the whole step's up to PyTorch's summation order (at most 1 ulp,
  1.2e-16 relative, measured on CPU), so labels are identical unless a
  mask's error lies that close to 0.4. Card vs CPU differ only by float
  order (921 tolerance: every object at IoU >= 0.9).
- Recorded: sites.csv gains mask_step_device ('gpu', 'gpu_banded', 'cpu',
  'mixed', or 'in_eval' when the site fitted one tile and Cellpose's eval
  made the masks under the governor), mask_step_reason ('fits', 'setting',
  'cap', 'oom', 'model_on_cpu'; joined by ';') and mask_step_bands;
  <seg_dst>/mask_step.json holds every step's note (objects, device, reason,
  estimate_gb, budget_gb, bands) per site, for the survey and for the
  control; the run's result gains mask_steps. A 'cap' or 'oom' step is
  logged as a warning on spacr.ops_large_sites.

SETTINGS (alpha, ALPHA_FEATURES[945] module_settings 'ops_large_sites')
- seg_mask_vram_gb (float, default 6.0; 0 = no cap of its own).
- seg_mask_device ('auto' | 'gpu' | 'cpu', default 'auto').
Tooltips and the "Mask step GPU memory" heading are in
spacr.ops_large_sites.LARGE_SITE_TOOLTIPS / LARGE_SITE_CATEGORIES (the module
is CLI-only and does not register a GUI form; the other 921 keys still have
no tooltips).

DEFAULT CHANGE
DEFAULT_CHANGES[938] = {'seg_mask_vram_gb': 0} (938, the next free
number after item 941's 937; SETTINGS_SCHEMA_VERSION 937 -> 938): an unstamped or older settings
file runs without the 6 GB cap, under the governor's budget only. Labels are
unchanged by the cap unless the flow following itself does not fit (sites of
roughly 8,000 px and more under 6 GB), which then runs on the CPU. A
PERISCOPE settings file without a stamp should set seg_mask_vram_gb: 6.

TESTS (CPU; tests/test_ops_large_site_mask_vram_945.py)
Banded flow check = Cellpose's whole step at four budgets (and it drops
masks); every mask in one band, wholly; the float32 padding rows; a fitting
site runs the whole call on the card; a binding cap bands with identical
labels; an unmeetable cap runs on the CPU and logs why; out-of-memory on the
whole step, the bands, or both falls back with identical labels; 'cpu' never
touches the card; a CPU model skips the governor; settings, tooltips,
category, validation and the DEFAULT_CHANGES row; the sites.csv summary; a
settings run with a fake out-of-memory, 'cpu' and a binding cap gives the
same cell labels and records each. gpu-marked: on a real card, banded =
uncapped exactly and the CPU within IoU >= 0.9 per object (skips without
CUDA).
