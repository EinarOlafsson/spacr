DEFINITIONS
- A copy (a segmented cell in one field) is in an OVERLAP STRIP when its
  centroid, mapped through the field transforms (refined when refined == 1),
  lies inside [0, H) x [0, W) of at least one other acquired field; else CORE.
- A copy is matched when ops_merge links it. A physical cell (an
  ops_cross_field component) is matched when any copy is; its zone is the
  owner copy's zone.
- Edge distance = min(row, H - 1 - row, col, W - 1 - col), binned by
  floor(d / ops_report_edge_bin_px).
- External cells are assigned per field to the nearest spaCR copy within
  ops_report_external_max_px, trying nucleus and cell centroids and keeping
  the one with the smaller median distance (as the reference script does).
