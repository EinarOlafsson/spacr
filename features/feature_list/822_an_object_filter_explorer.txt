WHAT THE STATE IS
Notebook 06 chose the object filters by showing, for each candidate metric:
- the worst 2% tail as a 5 x 5 crop grid;
- the share flagged per condition;
- overlap between metrics;
- grids around a threshold (for example 1.4-1.8x background);
- the removed and kept grids for the combined rule.
