Related: 942 (decoys and the shared tuner), 920 (spacer-prefix decode), 939/946 (OPS decode auto mode).

WHY
With 12-nt prefixes and 80,862 guides the barcode-shuffle call rate stayed 2-4x
above 1.5 x the decoy FDR after 942 tuning (0.38 % against a limit of 0.134 %).
The far decoys sit two or more edits from every prefix, so they never catch the
read errors that land one edit from a guide.

SETTINGS (all alpha, ALPHA_FEATURES[949])
ops_prefix_nearmiss_decoy_k (0 = off): near-miss decoy prefixes per library
  prefix, one edit from that prefix and from no other key
  (spacr.ops_prefix.prefix_nearmiss_decoys). Calls on them are decoy calls,
  counted apart (cells_decoy_nearmiss, fdr_nearmiss_pct = near-miss calls per
  100 real calls / k, scale 1) and never genotyped. Needs ops_prefix_decoy_k.
ops_prefix_fdr_calibration ('none' | 'shuffle'): the spacer-prefix decode also
  matches every read against 3 shuffled whitelists (seeds ops_control_seed..+2)
  and raises the FDR scale per well (spacr.ops_decode.calibrate_fdr_scale):
  tune at the current scale; at the chosen setting the needed scale is the
  tuning sites' shuffle call rate / (1.5 x the scale-1 FDR), the upper end of
  its site-bootstrap interval with ops_decode_auto_ci; repeat while the scale
  rises. Without tuning the entered setting is calibrated on the tuning half.
  Decoy and shuffle calls only; the report half never sets the scale.
ops_decode_fdr_calibration ('none' | 'shuffle'): the same calibration in the OPS
  (barcode) decode's auto mode; each field is voted again against 3 shuffled
  libraries (spacr.ops_decode._field_shuffle_counts), about three more vote
  passes per field.

OUTPUTS (only when a setting is on)
site_summary.csv: cells_decoy_nearmiss, fdr_nearmiss_pct; shuffle_called,
shuffle_call_rate_pct, fdr_scale (fdr_pct then uses the calibrated scale).
fdr_<plate>_<well>.json: fdr_ci95; near-miss count, FDR and interval;
fdr_calibration, fdr_scale_entered, shuffle_call_rate_pct with shuffle_ci95,
shuffle_limit_pct, shuffle_le_limit, calibration (rounds, per-part rates).
cells_<plate>_<well>.csv: decoy_class ('far' | 'nearmiss').
OPS decode: decode_thresholds_<well>.json carries the calibration;
decode_thresholds_<plate>.csv gains fdr_scale and shuffle_call_rate_pct;
decode_fdr_<well>.json gains the shuffle keys.
