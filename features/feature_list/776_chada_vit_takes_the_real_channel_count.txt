CHADA-VIT TAKES THE REAL CHANNEL COUNT
======================================

Lifecycle: new
Status: open.
Requested: maintainer via the BMC software-paper session, 2026-11-05.
Source: BBBC021 computer-vision benchmark, /nas_mnt/data/sync/papers/BMCspacr/reanalysis/bbbc021/cv/README.md.

REQUIREMENTS
ChAda-ViT pads every crop to 10 channel slots. Pass max_channels=k for the
actual channel count; this makes embedding about 100x faster. Check the
embeddings are unchanged for the padded vs unpadded path where they should be.
