OPS CROSS-FIELD OWNERSHIP OF PHENOTYPE CELLS
============================================

Lifecycle: future
Status: DONE 2026-11-09 (alpha, under ALPHA_FEATURES[752]). — run_ops phase ph_ownership (after ph_masks): every pair of overlapping fields is compared label by label through the fields' transforms; mutual best partners scoring at least ops_cross_field_min_overlap (ops_cross_field_metric overlap_fraction or iou) are copies of one cell, connected copies are one physical cell, the largest copy owns it. Writes ops_cross_field_<well>.parquet and ops_cross_field_edges_<well>.parquet; never writes ops_phenotype. ops_cross_field_policy one_per_cell (default) / owner_only / flag_only reduces the aggregate's genotyped cells; merged/ owned flags and ph_crops skip non-owner copies. spacr/ops_engine.py, spacr/ops_settings.py, tests/test_ops_cross_field_ownership.py.
Requested: maintainer via paper session (BMC paper item spec ITEM_ops_cross_field_ownership.md), 2026-11-09.
Owner: paper session (lane A).
Source: Funk et al. 2022 plate A well A1; reference script explore_cross_field_ownership.py.
Related: 752, 813, 852, 871.

WHAT THE STATE WAS
Item 852 picks one copy per sequencing nucleus (the deepest field), but only
for linked cells and by the sequencing centroid; copies were never identified by
their masks, and unlinked or cut copies reached crop export.

RULE
ops_cross_field_ownership defaults to False; with it off nothing reads the new
tables and every output is identical to before.
