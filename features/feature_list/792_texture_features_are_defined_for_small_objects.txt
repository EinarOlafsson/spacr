--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE STATE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

nucleus_channel_*_homogeneity_distance_32 is NaN for nuclei smaller than
the offset, which left 6 BBBC021 wells with non-finite mean profiles.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Non-finite features break well means and every distance computed on
them; dropping the rows biases against small nuclei (mitotic, apoptotic).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Cap each GLCM distance by the object's size (or skip offsets that do
   not fit and record how many were used), so the feature is always finite.
2. Document the rule in the feature dictionary (spacr/feature_dict.py).
3. Register any new setting in ALPHA_FEATURES under 792.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

On BBBC021 no well mean is non-finite, and the homogeneity values of
nuclei large enough for every offset are unchanged.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Nothing yet.
