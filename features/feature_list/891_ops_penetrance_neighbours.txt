WHAT THE STATE IS
Penetrance, escapers, phase-coupled tests and neighbour effects were
custom scripts.

WHAT TO DO
- Port them into spaCR as one alpha item with the published controls.

RESULT
All on plate A in the reference environment, compared with the tables
the strategies scripts wrote:
- penetrance_tests on the 1,145 copairs-active genes: penetrance_genes
  (1,145 rows), penetrance_controls (62 NT pseudo-genes and 100 shuffled-
  label genes) and penetrance_guides (4,828 rows) bit for bit, every
  column (CIs, escaper fits, simulation calls, guide heterogeneity). 9 min.
- phase_tests with the targeting null: phase_tests_tnull (49,601 rows,
  fresh random-set controls included), phase_calls_tnull (5,111 genes)
  and phase_summary_tnull.json identical. 6 min.
- neighbour_effects, inactive receivers, 5 reps of each null:
  nbr_tests_B, nbr_null_B (51,120 rows), nbr_d_B and nbr_V_B identical.
  7 min.
- mask_neighbours on six fields (A1 and B3, sites 0-2): nbr_<well> pairs
  and recv_<well> receiver rows identical.
escaper_classes was tested on synthetic data only; its genotype-error
inputs come from item 877.

NOT DONE IN THIS ITEM
- The neighbour table and neighbour effects are library functions only:
  the aggregate phase does not read the phenotype masks, so
  ops_neighbour_radius_px, ops_neighbour_grid_px, ops_neighbour_effects,
  ops_neighbour_receivers and ops_neighbour_null_reps are not settings
  yet.
- s1c_read_support.py and the spill-over checks (spill_*.py) are not
  ported; escaper_classes takes the genotype-confidence columns (item 877)
  as inputs.

FINISHED 2026-11-12 (the neighbour settings)
The aggregate phase now runs the neighbour table and neighbour effects:
ops_neighbour_effects reads <run>/<well>/phenotype_masks/site-<n>.npz (cells)
and ops_maskpass_<well>.parquet (nucleus_cy, nucleus_cx), builds
nbr_<well> / recv_<well> with mask_neighbours (ops_neighbour_radius_px,
ops_neighbour_grid_px), and runs neighbour_effects (ops_neighbour_receivers
'inactive' = design B, 'nt' = design A; ops_neighbour_null_reps; receivers
nearer the edge than the radius left out; field centre = half the mask
height). Active genes come from ops_neighbour_active (tables with *_fdr or
below_corrected_p). Outputs in results/ops_neighbours with the reference
names: nbr_<well>, recv_<well>, nbr_tests_<design>, nbr_null_<design>,
nbr_d_<design>.npy, nbr_V_<design>.npy, nbr_names.npy and
nbr_summary_<design>.json. Tested on a synthetic masked plate. Not yet
rerun on plate A (design card needed). s1c_read_support.py and spill_*.py
remain unported.
