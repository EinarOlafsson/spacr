PH AGGREGATE: SWAPPABLE GENOTYPES AND BLOCK-WISE POOLING
========================================================

Lifecycle: future
Status: open, bug fix for the out-of-memory failure (not alpha).
Requested: maintainer via paper session, 2026-11-07.
Owner: paper session.
Source: plate A OPS pilot.
Related: 813.

WHAT THE STATE IS
_ph_aggregate runs out of memory at 46 GB on the plate A pilot because it
pools every cell at once, and its genotype table is fixed.

WHAT TO DO
- Let _ph_aggregate take a swappable genotype table.
- Pool a subset of wells, and pool block-wise so memory stays bounded.
- Tests: block-wise pooling gives the same aggregates as pooling all at
  once on a small plate; peak memory stays below a set bound.

RULE
The paper session builds this under the identical-output rule: with the new
option off, outputs stay identical to today's.
