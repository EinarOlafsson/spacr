================================================================================
HOVER TOOLTIPS ARE NEVER CUT OFF AND CAN BE TURNED OFF
================================================================================

Lifecycle: new
Status:    IN PROGRESS
Requested: 2026-10-31
Related:   371 (two tooltip surfaces the user chooses between), 290, 347, 113.

REQUEST (the maintainer, 2026-10-31, through the coordinator)
    Hover tooltips are wide boxes whose text is almost always cut in half,
    as if the box is not tall enough, at the maintainer's font scale. Add
    an option to turn ALL tooltips off. Text must NEVER be cut off, for
    tooltips and for all user-facing text. Also: in the Navigator composer
    at 200 % the search field's placeholder is elided ("a Pa...") beside
    Browse.

REQUIREMENTS
  1. A tooltip box is as tall as its wrapped text at every Zoom (10-200 %),
     in every interface language, light and dark. No elision.
  2. One switch turns every tooltip off: Qt tooltips, the settings box (371)
     and setting help written into the bottom strip on hover. Use the
     existing Preferences > Appearance "Show tooltips" master switch rather
     than a second toggle; while it is off, 371's two surface switches are
     greyed out and keep their stored values. Accessible names and
     descriptions stay on the widgets.
  3. The Navigator composer shows its whole placeholder at every Zoom when
     the window is wide enough to hold it.

ACCEPTANCE
  * A tooltip's needed height (label heightForWidth at its width) fits
    inside its box at 50, 90, 100, 150 and 200 % and, at 100 and 200 %, for
    a long tooltip in all nine translated languages and English.
  * With Show tooltips off, no QToolTip, no HoverTooltip box and no
    delayed hover help appears; the accessible description is unchanged;
    the two surface switches are disabled until it is turned on again.
  * The Navigator placeholder needs no more width than the field gives it,
    at 100 and 200 %, in English and all nine languages, at 1600 px.
  * Before/after screenshots in light and dark at 100 % and 200 %.

2026-10-31 — Cause: the stylesheet guard holds a sheet asked for inside a polish until the window's Show, so Qt measured every tooltip label in the unstyled application font without the sheet's padding, then drew it in the zoomed QToolTip font. Fixed in gui_scale._apply_now: a tooltip label is measured again whenever Qt is handed its sheet. The settings box widened with Zoom (380 px times the font ratio) and widens further when adjustSize's two-thirds-of-screen cap would cut it.
2026-10-31 — Off: the existing Show tooltips switch already stops QToolTip, the box and the setting help in the strip (all wait on HoverDelay); the box/bottom switches are now greyed while it is off. Accessible descriptions are untouched. The window strips (HintBar, ModuleHintBar, pinned category text) are panel text and stay.
2026-10-31 — Navigator composer: the field grows past 720 px to its placeholder's width at the current font, up to the window width less 48 px; below that width the placeholder is still elided (a QLineEdit cannot wrap it). tests/qt/test_746_text_is_never_cut_off.py (58 cases) fails 24 against the old code.
