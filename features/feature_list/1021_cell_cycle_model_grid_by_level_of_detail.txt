--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The paper uses cell-cycle-resolved OPS profiles. Without a comparison across
levels, nobody knows whether the 7 stages are resolved well enough to use,
or whether a coarser level (2 or 4 classes) gives more reliable gene calls.
