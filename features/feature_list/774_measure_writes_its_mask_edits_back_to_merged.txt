MEASURE WRITES ITS IN-MEMORY MASK EDITS BACK TO MERGED
======================================================

Lifecycle: new
Status: DONE 2026-11-06 — Measure records every label plane it changed (merged, size-filtered or excluded objects) in merged/.spacr_measured_masks/<field>.npz and MergedField serves those planes, so measured area equals the label area crops are cut from; merged/ keeps its bytes.
Requested: maintainer via the BMC software-paper session, 2026-11-05.
