OPS GENOTYPING THRESHOLDS, MANUAL OR AUTO: CHOSEN PER WELL FROM DECOYS AT A MAXIMUM FDR
=====================================================================================

Lifecycle: future
Status: IMPLEMENTED 2026-11-13 (alpha, under ALPHA_FEATURES[939]; default 'manual', so no decode changes unless a user picks 'auto'); proof on plate A well A1 and plate D well A1 below — ops_decode_threshold_mode 'manual' keeps every decode threshold as entered (ops_read_threshold and ops_spot_min_peak for spot detection, ops_single_read_quality and ops_pixel_quality for the calls); 'auto' decodes each field once, keeps its calls at every setting of a single-read floor x pixel-quality grid (spacr.ops_decode._field_votes / _field_candidates / _candidate_calls), and spacr.ops_decode.choose_well_thresholds picks the well's setting: fields with nuclei, in site order, split by numpy.random.default_rng(ops_decode_auto_seed).permutation into a tuning share (ops_decode_auto_tune_fraction) and a report share; on the tuning share the passing settings are those whose decoy FDR (upper end of the 95 % field-bootstrap interval, 1,000 resamples, seed 1, with ops_decode_auto_ci; else the estimate) is at most ops_decode_fdr_max (default 0.1 per 100); the passing setting with the most real calls wins, ties to the higher floor then the higher pixel quality; no passing setting -> the strictest setting and auto_met False. Results: decode report 'thresholds', results/ops_decode/decode_thresholds_<well>.json (with the whole tuning grid) and decode_thresholds_<plate>.csv (one row per well: chosen thresholds, auto_met, tuning/report/all-field genotyped % and FDR with CI). spacr/ops_decode.py, spacr/ops_engine.py, spacr/ops_settings.py, spacr/settings.py, spacr/settings_spec.py, tests/test_ops_decode_threshold_auto_939.py.

SETTINGS (all alpha, ALPHA_FEATURES[939])
ops_decode_threshold_mode ('manual' default; 'auto'), ops_decode_fdr_max
(0.1, false calls per 100 real calls), ops_decode_auto_grid (empty = the
S2-it1 grid: single-read quality 0.06, 0.08, 0.10, 0.12, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25,

DEVIATIONS FROM THE SPEC
- ops_decode_auto_scope ('well' | 'plate' | 'global') is not built: only
  per-well choice exists. Plate or global scope needs every well's
  candidates at once (wells decode one at a time), which is a second
  pass; spec open question 2 asks the maintainer which scope is wanted.
- A well where no setting passes gets the STRICTEST grid setting (spec);
  the study script took the setting with the lowest point FDR. On every
  well checked a setting passed, so the two never differed.
- Small-data guard (acceptance 6): a tuning share with zero decoy calls is
  judged as holding three (95 % bound of a zero count), and fewer than two
  tuning fields gives auto_met False.
- Seeds: each well draws its own split from default_rng(seed), so wells are
  order-independent. The study drew all wells of a plate from one rng
  (20261112 for D then C, 20261113 for A then B), so only the first well of
  each plate (A1) has the study's split at the study's seed.
