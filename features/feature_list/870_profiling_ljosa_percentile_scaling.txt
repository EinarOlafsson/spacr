LJOSA PERCENTILE SCALING FOR MEASURE PROFILES
=============================================

Lifecycle: future
Status: DONE 2026-11-09 (alpha, under ALPHA_FEATURES[547]) — profiling_normalization='ljosa_percentile': on each plate every feature is scaled as (x - p1) / (p99 - p1), with p1 and p99 the 1st and 99th percentiles of the plate's negative-control cells (sp_stats._control_percentiles, read from the object tables by sp_stats._reference_object_percentiles in a Measure run), or of the reference wells when no cell percentiles are given; features with a zero or non-finite range on any plate are dropped as zero_percentile_range. Same rule as run_and_score.py's normalise(..., 'ljosa'). BBBC021 CellProfiler: all 8 Ljosa-scaled 1-NN rows of cellprofiler_moa_accuracy.csv reproduced exactly with the scaling done in spaCR. spacr/sp_stats.py, spacr/settings_spec.py, spacr/settings.py, tests/test_profiling_ljosa_percentile_870.py.
Requested: maintainer, 2026-11-09 (follow-up to 795's acceptance).
Owner: 795 session.
Source: /nas_mnt/data/sync/papers/BMCspacr/reanalysis/bbbc021/cellprofiler_baseline/run_and_score.py (--norm ljosa), Ljosa et al. 2013.
Related: 795, 863, 547.

WHAT THE STATE IS
Measure's image-based profiling (547) offered mad_robustize, standardize,
robustize and tvn (863). The BBBC021 benchmark scales each plate to the 1st
and 99th percentile of its DMSO cells (Ljosa et al. 2013); 795's acceptance
had to take that scaling from the reanalysis script.

WHAT TO DO
- Add 'ljosa_percentile' to profiling_normalization, beside robustize and
  tvn, alpha with the profiling module (no new registry entry or deck row).
- Take the percentiles from the control cells, not the control wells, as
  run_and_score.py does; drop features with no range on some plate.
- Update the profiling_normalization tooltip (600 characters at most).

RESULT
Scratch script /mnt/firecuda2/codex/scratch-docs/795-accept/accept_870.py:
DMSO-cell percentiles from the 55 per-cell parquet files through
_control_percentiles agree with the reanalysis's dmso_percentiles.parquet to
1.1e-8 (same NaN pattern); wells scaled by _normalize_profiles
('ljosa_percentile') and scored by spacr.moa_benchmark give, exactly,
well mean / replicates median 84/103 NSC and 62/102 NSCB, and the other
three Ljosa rows (83/103 and 59/102; 83/103 and 61/102; 77/103 and 57/102),
with the same 2,473 features and the same 100-permutation shuffled control.
Outside spaCR were only the metadata join and run_and_score.py's NaN -> 0
fill of well values.

DELIBERATELY NOT DONE
- No new setting for the percentiles (fixed at 1st and 99th).
- Missing well values are not filled with 0 in the profiling run: the run's
  feature selection and its finite-wells rule handle them, as for the other
  normalisations.
