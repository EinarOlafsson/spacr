A BUDDING-YEAST CELL-CYCLE MODEL FOR BUDDING DIVISIONS
=====================================================
Lifecycle: future
Status: open
Requested: maintainer, 2026-11-08 (follow-on 4b of item 771).
Owner: unassigned (alpha when built).
Related: 771.

WHAT THE STATE IS
spacr/division_analysis.py (item 771) detects budding events (the mother
continues, each bud is one event) and aligns trajectories on them, but the
size-control fits (cell_cycles, size_control_fits: birth vs division size,
sizer/adder/timer slopes) assume fission. For budding yeast the division
time is cytokinesis, which the mask/lineage pair does not carry: a bud is
seen from emergence, and mother and bud stay one object or two touching
objects until they separate.

WHY IT MATTERS
Size control is one of the main questions asked of budding-yeast movies,
and today spaCR cannot answer it for them; fitting the fission model to
bud emergence gives slopes that mean something else.

WHAT TO DO
1. Model the budding cycle as bud emergence -> cytokinesis (separation):
   estimate cytokinesis per bud from the masks (mother-bud contact ending,
   or the bud's own growth curve flattening) and record how it was found.
2. Define birth size, division size and added size for mother and bud
   under that model, and fit sizer/adder/timer slopes per branch.
3. Use it from analyse_divisions when timelapse_division_mode is budding
   (or auto finds budding); keep the fission fits for fission.
4. Show the budding fits in the size-control figure of the Division
   analysis panel, labelled as the budding model.
5. Register any new settings under ALPHA_FEATURES[865].

HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
Synthetic budding lineages with planted sizer, adder and timer behaviour
give back the planted slope within its CI; a fission lineage is fitted as
before.

DELIBERATELY NOT DONE
No cytokinesis times are invented when the masks cannot show separation;
those buds are reported as not fitted, with the reason.
