A BUDDING-YEAST CELL-CYCLE MODEL FOR BUDDING DIVISIONS
=====================================================
Lifecycle: future
Status: DONE 2026-11-09 (alpha) — spacr.division_analysis gains bud_contacts (mother-bud contact per frame from the tracked label stack), budding_cytokinesis (cytokinesis per bud from contact ending, else from the bud's growth curve flattening, with the method and, when neither shows it, the reason it is not fitted), budding_cycles (cytokinesis-to-cytokinesis cycles for mothers and daughters: birth size at cytokinesis, division size mother + bud at the next one, added size, unbudded and budded frames) and add_budding_cycles (fits sizer/adder/timer slopes per branch for area and sphere-equivalent volume when the movie is analysed as budding); the Make Masks step uses it for budding (or auto finding budding) and the size-control figure draws mothers and daughters labelled as the budding model. Fission fits unchanged. Registered as ALPHA_FEATURES[865] (timelapse_division_analysis; no new settings).
Requested: maintainer, 2026-11-08 (follow-on 4b of item 771).
Owner: Home (claude-div2-1109 worktree).
Related: 771.

WHAT THE STATE IS
spacr/division_analysis.py (item 771) detects budding events (the mother
continues, each bud is one event) and aligns trajectories on them, but the
size-control fits (cell_cycles, size_control_fits: birth vs division size,
sizer/adder/timer slopes) assume fission. For budding yeast the division
time is cytokinesis, which the mask/lineage pair does not carry: a bud is
seen from emergence, and mother and bud stay one object or two touching
objects until they separate.

WHY IT MATTERS
Size control is one of the main questions asked of budding-yeast movies,
and today spaCR cannot answer it for them; fitting the fission model to
bud emergence gives slopes that mean something else.

WHAT TO DO
1. Model the budding cycle as bud emergence -> cytokinesis (separation):
   estimate cytokinesis per bud from the masks (mother-bud contact ending,
   or the bud's own growth curve flattening) and record how it was found.
2. Define birth size, division size and added size for mother and bud
   under that model, and fit sizer/adder/timer slopes per branch.
3. Use it from analyse_divisions when timelapse_division_mode is budding
   (or auto finds budding); keep the fission fits for fission.
4. Show the budding fits in the size-control figure of the Division
   analysis panel, labelled as the budding model.
5. Register any new settings under ALPHA_FEATURES[865].

HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
Synthetic budding lineages with planted sizer, adder and timer behaviour
give back the planted slope within its CI; a fission lineage is fitted as
before.

DELIBERATELY NOT DONE
No cytokinesis times are invented when the masks cannot show separation;
those buds are reported as not fitted, with the reason.

WHAT WAS BUILT (2026-11-09, alpha)
- The model and its assumptions (stated in the docstrings and in summary.json as budding_model): a
  bud emerges on the first frame its track is seen (bud emergence, the morphological mark of Start;
  Hartwell 1974); the cycle ends at cytokinesis, read as the last frame the bud touches its mother
  before they are seen apart on 2 consecutive frames (bud_contacts counts a one-pixel seam as contact).
  Without contact, the bud's growth rate over 3 frames must fall to 25% of its peak before the bud
  buds itself (method growth_plateau, a weaker reading: a daughter that keeps growing as fast after
  separation is not fitted). Birth size = size at cytokinesis; division size = mother + bud at the
  next cytokinesis, so the bud counts toward the cycle that made it (Soifer et al. 2016 Curr Biol;
  Chandler-Brown et al. 2017 Curr Biol). Mothers and daughters (a former bud's first cycle) are fitted
  separately (Hartwell & Unger 1977 JCB: daughters are born smaller and spend longer unbudded). volume
  = sphere of the same projected area, 4/3 * area^1.5 / sqrt(pi), summed over mother and bud.
- results gain buds (buds.csv: mother, bud, t_emergence, t_cytokinesis, budded_frames, method,
  status); cycles carry branch, bud, t_emergence, unbudded_frames, budded_frames and
  area/volume _birth/_emergence/_division/_added; size_control rows model=budding, branch,
  size_measure area|volume. calibrate_divisions adds _min and _um2/_um3 columns for them;
  pool_division_movies refits per branch and concatenates buds with their movie.
- analyse_divisions keeps its signature, docstring and fission behaviour (reviewed translations stay
  current); the step calls add_budding_cycles after it when the movie is budding.
- Tests: tests/test_division_budding_865.py -- synthetic budding lineages with planted sizer (0),
  adder (1) and timer (1.6) rules give back the planted slope within its CI for both branches; buds
  without visible separation are reported not fitted with the reason; the growth-plateau fallback,
  bud_contacts on real masks, writing, reading, pooling and the budding figure.

NOT DONE / NEEDS A DECISION
- No real budding-yeast movie was run (synthetic data only); the 2-frame separation rule, the
  one-pixel seam and the 25% plateau are defaults chosen here, not calibrated on real data.
- The timelapse_division_mode tooltip still says "Size control is fitted for fission only"; changing
  it means a new English string and nine translations, so it was left for the translation lane.
- Its English strings (docstrings, summary note, figure title and labels) have no catalogue records.
