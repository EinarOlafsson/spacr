EMBEDDINGS AGGREGATE BY WELL AND TREATMENT, WITH NSC/NSCB EVALUATION
====================================================================

Lifecycle: new
Status: DONE 2026-11-06 — public spacr.embeddings.aggregate_profiles(table, by=...) turns
per-cell or per-well embeddings (or any numeric profiles) into one profile per
treatment: cells to wells by mean or median, optional normalisation to control wells
(center, zscore, robust_zscore, or tvn through batch_correction.spherize_features),
per plate when plate_column is given, then wells to treatments by mean or median, with
each treatment's batches kept for NSCB. Public spacr.embeddings.evaluate_moa(profiles,
...) scores cosine 1-NN MoA accuracy under NSC and NSCB with Wilson 95% CIs, solvable
counts, per-treatment neighbours, optional reference feature sets (e.g. CellProfiler),
and control rows (shuffled MoA labels between compounds; majority class) plus a glossary
of the abbreviations. Library API only, no new GUI settings. Arrival contract
tests/data/release_contracts/moa_profiles_api_arrivals_2026-11-06.json; tests in
tests/test_embeddings_moa_profiles.py.
Requested: maintainer via the BMC software-paper session, 2026-11-05.
Source: BBBC021 computer-vision benchmark, /nas_mnt/data/sync/papers/BMCspacr/reanalysis/bbbc021/cv/README.md.

REQUIREMENTS
There is no well/treatment aggregation of embeddings and no evaluation by
not-same-compound (NSC) and not-same-compound-or-batch (NSCB) nearest-neighbour
mechanism-of-action accuracy. Add both, with a control row (e.g. random or
CellProfiler-feature baseline) in the reported table.
