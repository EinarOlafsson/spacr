BEHAVIOUR
- A run whose settings come from the panel defaults (GUI, spacr-run, a
  settings file stamped 940 or later) decodes with rule (a) + S2 and per-well
  thresholds chosen from decoys (single-read quality x pixel quality grid of
  S2-it1, FDR upper 95 % bound <= ops_decode_fdr_max 0.1 per 100 on a random
  half of each well's fields; results/ops_decode/decode_thresholds_<plate>.csv).
- 'auto' is the default, so a decode auto mode cannot tune (ops_decode_preset
  'none', ops_cell_rule other than 'mapped', no guide library, or no decoys)
  now keeps the thresholds as entered and logs why, instead of stopping with
  a ValueError (spacr.ops_engine._decode). Its output equals 'manual'.
- A mapping passed straight to spacr.ops_engine.run_ops without these keys
  still means preset 'none' and 'manual' (the engine fallbacks are unchanged);
  the panel defaults are spacr.ops_settings.OPS_DEFAULTS.
- ops_decode_auto_spot_grid unset now means no joint read-threshold axis
  (spacr.ops_decode.threshold_settings); spacr.ops_decode.AUTO_SPOT_GRID stays
  as the opt-in grid.
