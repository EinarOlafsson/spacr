OPS GENOTYPE CALLS AGAINST OTHER PIPELINES' CALLS (THREE-WAY COMPARISON)
=======================================================================

Lifecycle: future
Status: DONE 2026-11-09 (alpha, under ALPHA_FEATURES[878]).
Requested: maintainer via paper session (spec ITEM_ops_genotype_three_way_compare.md).
Owner: paper session.
Related: 873, 876, 877.

WHAT IT DOES
The compare_calls phase of spacr.ops_engine.run_ops (also run after the decode
when ops_compare_tables is set) reads other pipelines' per-cell call tables
(ops_compare_tables: a name -> path mapping, a JSON object or name=path
pairs; columns well, site, y, x in the tile frame and call), keeps the cells
of the well:site fields listed in ops_compare_fields (all when empty) that a
field owns away from its border (COMPARE_EDGE_PX, 10 px), and matches them
to spaCR's nuclei
by mutual nearest neighbours within ops_compare_match_px (4 px). The links
of every pair of tables are joined shortest first, and a link that would put
two cells of one table into a unit is skipped (spacr.ops_decode.match_units).
spaCR's side is the decode's own calls, decoys included, so no rule is
re-implemented. Writes results/ops_compare/: units.parquet (one row per
matched unit with every table's call), categories.csv (all agree, two agree,
one only, ... with a decoy FDR per category and table) and tables.csv
(assigned % and FDR per table over the cells every table holds).
Settings: ops_compare_tables, ops_compare_match_px (4.0), ops_compare_fields.
Code: spacr/ops_decode.py (match_units, compare_field, compare_summary,
compare_region, run_compare_calls), spacr/ops_engine.py (the compare_calls
phase). Test: tests/test_ops_compare_calls_878.py.

VALIDATION (2026-11-09, CPU, plate A of Funk et al. 2022)
- spaCR against Brieflow and Funk et al.'s published calls: 95,695 matched
  units, with categories identical to the reference three-way comparison of
  the decode-yield study.
- On the 91,591 cells all three pipelines saw: spaCR assigned 76.13 % at a
  decoy FDR of 0.055, Brieflow 63.73 % at 0.082 and Funk et al. 61.51 % at
  0.099.
