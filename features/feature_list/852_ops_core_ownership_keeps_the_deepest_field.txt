OPS CORE OWNERSHIP KEEPS THE DEEPEST FIELD
==========================================

Lifecycle: future
Status: open, alpha. Part of 813.
Requested: maintainer via paper session, 2026-11-07.
Owner: paper session.
Source: plate A OPS pilot.
Related: 813.

WHAT THE STATE IS
Overlapping fields give an SBS nucleus several copies. The pilot's A1b
ownership rule keeps, per SBS nucleus, the copy from the field where the
nucleus lies deepest (furthest from the field edge).

WHAT TO DO
- Add the deepest-field ownership rule to the OPS merge.
- Record which field owns each nucleus.
- Tests: a nucleus seen in two fields keeps the deeper copy only.

RULE
The paper session builds this under the identical-output rule: with the new
option off, outputs stay identical to today's.
