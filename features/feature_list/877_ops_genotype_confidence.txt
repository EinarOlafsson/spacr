CALIBRATED PER-CELL GENOTYPE CONFIDENCE
=======================================

Lifecycle: future
Status: DONE 2026-11-09 (alpha, under ALPHA_FEATURES[877]).
Requested: maintainer via paper session (spec ITEM_ops_genotype_confidence.md).
Owner: paper session.
Related: 873, 876.

WHAT IT DOES
Under the 873 decode rules every decode writes
results/ops_decode/calls_<well>.parquet: every call, decoys included, with the
read and pixel support behind it (the study's decode_diag.py columns).
ops_genotype_confidence (False) runs the decode_qc phase over the plate's
wells: P(decoy | evidence) by logistic regression, its isotonic recalibration,
histogram gradient boosting and matched strata, cross-fitted in five folds by
md5(well|site); a real call's error is c * P(decoy) / P(real), c =
ops_fdr_scale / ops_decoy_library_k, and genotype_confidence = 1 - error. A
shuffled-label control is fitted too. Writes results/ops_decode_qc/
confidence_reliability.csv (bin, predicted, observed, n, ...),
confidence_reliability_all_models.csv, confidence_metrics.json (AUC, ECE,
shuffled AUC, every model), confidence_strata.csv, confidence_calls.parquet,
and adds genotype_confidence to ops_barcodes; ops_confidence_min drops calls
below it. ECE = sum over bins of (real calls in bin / real calls) x
|predicted - observed| for the chosen model.
Settings: ops_confidence_model (hist_gbm | logistic | isotonic | strata |
auto), ops_confidence_calibration (target_decoy | none), ops_confidence_bins
(10), ops_confidence_seed (20261107, the reference seed), ops_confidence_min.
Code: spacr/ops_decode.py (genotype_confidence, run_decode_qc).
