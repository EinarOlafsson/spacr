PROBLEM
DAPI scaling differs between fields of Funk plate A (field G1-mode SD
0.04-0.13 log2), so one pooled histogram per plate broadens both peaks:
Measure put G2/G1 at its 2.4 bound and called 0 % G2.

BUILD
- cell_cycle_field_centre (False): x = log2(max(DNA content, 1)); every field
  (plate, time, row, column, field) with >= cell_cycle_field_min_cells (50)
  non-mitotic nuclei is shifted by g1_mode(field) - median g1_mode of its fit
  group; centred content 2 ** (x - offset). field_offset_log2 and
  dna_content_centred per nucleus (spacr.measure._dna_field_offsets,
  _field_centred_dna_phases).
- cell_cycle_fit_group ('plate' | 'well'): one _fit_dna_content per group on
  its non-mitotic nuclei (_cell_cycle_fit_groups).
- cell_cycle_mitotic_column (''): a boolean nucleus-table column; those
  nuclei are M, left out of the modes and the fit, and the condensation rule
  is not applied (_external_mitotic_call).
- Gates, subG1/>4N, confidence and posteriors unchanged (_gate_dna_content).
- measurements.db:cell_cycle_fit: per group g1, g2, ratio, CV, weights,
  gates, n, iterations, fields shifted (only on the centred path).
- OPS: ops_profile_cell_cycle_method 'measure_fc' calls the same function on
  the cell tables (_dna_integrated, well, site, mitotic) and folds
  S/G2/>4N -> SG2, subG1 -> G1 for the phase profiles; the report carries the
  fine call fractions and the per-well fits.
