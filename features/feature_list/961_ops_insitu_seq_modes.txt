BUILD
- Settings (alpha, all None by default, no schema bump): ops_insitu_preset
  (t7), ops_barcode_layout (single | combinatorial), ops_barcode_layout_file,
  ops_call_sort (count | quality | peak), ops_read_compartment (cell |
  nucleus | cytoplasm), ops_prefix_nuclei_masks, ops_recomb_min_quality.
- Combinatorial layout: a CSV/JSON of segments (segment, start, end, role
  map/recomb/umi, library column, max_distance, metric). Each segment is a
  spacer-prefix whitelist at its cycles (the decode's own matcher); map
  segments carry the decoys. A read's construct is the one construct its map
  and recomb segments agree on; a recomb segment of another construct flags
  the read recombined and drops it from the call; a recomb segment below
  ops_recomb_min_quality is not checked; UMI bases are recorded. Cells are
  called from their reads (count, quality or peak ranking; a tie is
  ambiguous). Needs ops_prefix_threshold_tuning none. Writes
  results/ops_insitu/: reads, cells, site_summary.csv and insitu_report.json
  (per segment mapping rate and map read decoy FDR; per well unique
  combination rate, cell decoy FDR, recombination rate, shuffle call rate
  over 3 shuffled constructs tables).
- Single layout: ops_read_compartment masks the cell labels by the nucleus
  labels (tuned or fixed decodes); ops_call_sort ranks barcodes by count,
  mean quality or peak strength (reads gain a peak column); the t7 preset
  sets nucleus, peak and ops_quality_min_reads 1 on keys left empty.
- With every new key empty the decode runs the same code and writes the same
  files.
