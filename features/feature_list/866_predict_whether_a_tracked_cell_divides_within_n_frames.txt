PREDICT WHETHER A TRACKED CELL DIVIDES WITHIN N FRAMES
======================================================
Lifecycle: future
Status: DONE 2026-11-09 (alpha) — new module spacr.division_prediction (kept out of division_analysis, as the item asks): division_prediction_features (per track and frame: shape, intensity and motion measures, their change over 3 frames, age, and the label 'divides within N frames'; frames whose horizon the movie does not show are left out), predict_divisions (GroupKFold by movie; model beside age-only, shuffled-within-movie labels and majority-class controls; pooled held-out AUROC/AUPRC with per-movie bootstrap 95% CIs from 5 movies on, row bootstrap with a too-narrow note below; paired CIs of the model's margin over each control; permutation importances on held-out folds), write/read_division_prediction and division_prediction_figure (bars + jittered folds). The Make Masks step writes prediction_features.csv per field and, from 3 fields on, tracks/division/prediction_<tracker>_tracks_<object>/. Four settings under ALPHA_FEATURES[866]; xgboost when installed, scikit-learn otherwise.
Requested: maintainer, 2026-11-08 (follow-on 4c of item 771).
Owner: Home (claude-div2-1109 worktree).
Related: 771.

WHAT THE STATE IS
The Fig 7 driver in
/nas_mnt/data/sync/papers/BMCspacr/reanalysis/division_biology/code/ trains
a "will divide within N frames" classifier (xgboost, grouped
cross-validation by movie, shuffled-label and age-only controls). It is not
part of spaCR: spacr/division_analysis.py (item 771) leaves prediction out
because it pulls xgboost/sklearn and needs its own settings.

WHY IT MATTERS
Whether shape, size and motion predict an upcoming division is the
forward-looking half of the item 771 question. Without grouped CV and the
controls, a classifier learns movie identity or cell age and looks better
than it is.

WHAT TO DO
1. Add a spaCR step that builds per-track, per-frame features from the
   measurements analyse_divisions already makes, with the label "divides
   within N frames".
2. Train with cross-validation grouped by movie; report AUC (with CI)
   beside the shuffled-label control and an age-only baseline.
3. Write per-fold metrics, feature importances and the held-out
   predictions as CSVs, plus one figure, next to the division results.
4. Settings (N, model, folds) under ALPHA_FEATURES[866]; xgboost stays an
   optional dependency with an sklearn fallback.

HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
On synthetic lineages with a planted pre-division change the model beats
both controls; with no planted change it does not beat the shuffled
control; folds never share a movie.

DELIBERATELY NOT DONE
Not added to spacr/division_analysis.py itself, to keep that module free
of model dependencies.

WHAT WAS BUILT (2026-11-09, alpha)
- Settings (Timelapse > Division Analysis α): timelapse_division_prediction (off),
  timelapse_division_prediction_frames (N, 5), timelapse_division_prediction_model
  (auto/xgboost/gradient_boosting/logistic) and timelapse_division_prediction_folds (5), registered as
  ALPHA_FEATURES[866]; resume treats them as cosmetic; deck row added (deck not re-rendered).
- Label: frame t is positive when the cell's next division (alignment frame of division_events) is
  in t .. t+N-1; negative only when the cell is seen past t+N without dividing or divides later;
  otherwise censored (left out). Budding mothers' age restarts at each bud emergence.
- Evaluation: folds never share a movie (checked per fold, n_shared_movies in folds.csv). The shuffled
  control permutes labels within each training movie. The majority control is one constant score
  (AUROC 0.5, AUPRC = prevalence). summary.json gives beats_shuffled / beats_age_only /
  beats_majority (paired CI of the AUROC margin above 0) and a verdict that names the cell-cycle
  clock when the model beats shuffled labels but not age alone.
- Outputs: metrics.csv, folds.csv, importances.csv, predictions.csv, summary.json,
  division_prediction figure.
- Tests: tests/test_division_prediction_866.py -- a planted pre-division elongation beats all three
  controls on held-out movies with the movie bootstrap; memoryless divisions with no planted change
  do not beat the shuffled control; a minimum cycle length (a pure age clock) beats shuffled labels
  but not the age-only control; folds never share a movie; censoring; metrics match scikit-learn;
  xgboost fallback; the step waits for 3 fields.

NOT DONE / NEEDS A DECISION
- Not compared with the Fig 7 driver in the BMC reanalysis folder (that driver and its data were not
  read here); the paper numbers should be rerun through spacr.division_prediction before they cite it.
- Every frame of a track is one row, so long-lived tracks weigh more; whether to subsample one frame
  per track per horizon is a modelling choice for the maintainer.
- With fewer than 5 fields the intervals resample rows and are too narrow (said in ci_note); the
  5-movie threshold follows item 867.
- The prediction reruns after every field once 3 have features (like the pooling); a run with many
  fields could instead predict once at the end.
- Its English strings (settings tooltips, docstrings, verdicts, figure labels) have no catalogue
  records.
