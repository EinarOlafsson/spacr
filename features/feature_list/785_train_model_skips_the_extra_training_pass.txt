TRAIN_MODEL SKIPS THE EXTRA PASS OVER THE TRAINING SET
======================================================

Lifecycle: new
Status: open.
Requested: maintainer via the BMC software-paper session, 2026-11-05.
Source: BBBC021 computer-vision benchmark, /nas_mnt/data/sync/papers/BMCspacr/reanalysis/bbbc021/cv/README.md.

REQUIREMENTS
train_model re-evaluates the whole training loader every epoch in fp32 (about
40% of run time) and counts classes by iterating the whole loader. Track
training metrics during the training pass (mixed precision where training uses
it) and count classes from the dataset labels.
