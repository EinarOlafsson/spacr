WHAT THE STATE IS
The BBBC021 MoA benchmark with the CellProfiler Analyst classifiers, per-MoA
accuracy and majority / shuffled controls was computed by scorers that do
not import spaCR. 795 gave spaCR the 1-NN, logistic and XGBoost arms.

WHAT TO DO
- One scorer, run from a settings file, with five seeds.
- The CPA 3.0.5 classifiers, LDA, shrinkage LDA and KNN as arms.
- Per-MoA table, confusion tables, majority and shuffled controls.
