RESULT
From the plate A cell tables (improved/cells/tvn_<well>.parquet, interphase
cells, pseudo-gene NT rows), responder_profiles reproduces
profiles/responder_summary.parquet (5,111 genes, energy distance included),
responder_genes.parquet, responder_guides.parquet (20,437 guides) and
sw_quantiles.npy bit for bit in the reference environment, on the same
default_rng(0) stream as build_profiles.py. 45 min on one core.

A gene whose control reference would be empty (all control guides are its
own) is skipped instead of producing NaN; on the reference data no gene is.
