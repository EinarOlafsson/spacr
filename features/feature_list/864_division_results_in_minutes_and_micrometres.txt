DIVISION RESULTS IN MINUTES AND MICROMETRES: FRAME INTERVAL AND PIXEL SIZE PER MOVIE
====================================================================================
Lifecycle: future
Status: DONE 2026-11-08 (alpha) — the new spacr.division_analysis.calibrate_divisions takes a movie's frame_interval_min and pixel_size_um and adds minute, um and um^2 columns beside the frame and pixel ones in the events, effects, trajectories and cycles tables; the Make Masks step reads the interval from the tracks table's time_s column or the existing frame_interval_s setting and the pixel size from the existing pixel_size_um setting (item 831); a movie without them stays in frames and pixels and summary.json and the figure axes say so. No new settings: ALPHA_FEATURES[864] registers timelapse_division_analysis, the alpha switch it runs under.
Requested: maintainer, 2026-11-08 (follow-on 4a of item 771).
Owner: Home (claude-div-1108 worktree).
Related: 771.

WHAT THE STATE IS
spacr/division_analysis.py (item 771) reports every result in frames and
pixels: cycle length, the alignment window, rel_t on the trajectory plots,
speed and displacement, area and axis lengths, birth and division size. The
module deliberately does not guess a frame interval. The Fig 7 corpus
manifest has the frame interval and pixel size for 6 of its 13 datasets;
the rest are analysed in frames and pixels only.

WHY IT MATTERS
A cycle of "42 frames" or a speed of "1.3 px/frame" cannot be compared
across movies taken at different intervals or magnifications, and a user
cannot read it against the literature. Pooling movies with different
intervals in the meta-analysis mixes units silently.

WHAT TO DO
1. Read the frame interval (minutes) and pixel size (um) per movie: from
   the image metadata spaCR already reads when it is there (OME/ND2/CZI
   physical size and time increment), else from new Make Masks timelapse
   settings, else leave them unset.
2. Carry both values through lineage_from_tracks and analyse_divisions as
   per-movie metadata, and write them to summary.json.
3. When both are known, add minute and um columns next to the frame and
   pixel ones in the events, trajectories, cycles and size_control CSVs,
   and label the figure axes in minutes and um (um^2 for area).
4. When a value is missing, keep frames/pixels and say so in summary.json
   and on the figure axes; never guess.
5. Register the new settings under ALPHA_FEATURES[864].

HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
A synthetic movie with a known interval and pixel size gives the same
cycle length in minutes and size in um^2 as the hand calculation; a movie
without metadata still runs and reports frames/pixels with the unit stated.

DELIBERATELY NOT DONE
No default interval or pixel size: a wrong unit is worse than no unit.

WHAT WAS BUILT (2026-11-08, alpha)
- ALPHA_FEATURES[864]: timelapse_division_analysis (shared with 771; it is the only way to reach this);
  alpha deck row added in tools/refresh_alpha_deck.py (deck not re-rendered).
- calibrate_divisions(results, frame_interval_min=None, pixel_size_um=None): analyse_divisions still
  runs in frames and pixels (its signature and docstring are unchanged, so their reviewed translations
  stay current); calibrate_divisions then adds the units, and calling it again replaces them. Events gain t_div_min and base_size_um2, cycles t_birth_min,
  t_division_min, duration_min and <size>_um2 / major_axis_*_um, trajectories rel_t_min and, for
  speed, mean/sd/sem/ci_low/ci_high_um_per_min, effects a change_unit column (log ratio, um/min or
  px/frame, rad, unitless) and speed mean changes in um/min. Each column appears only when its value
  is known (time columns need the interval, area/length columns the pixel size, speed both).
- summary.json: frame_interval_min, pixel_size_um, time_unit, length_unit, area_unit, speed_unit,
  units_note ("frame interval unknown: times are in frames", "pixel size unknown: lengths are in
  pixels", or "times in minutes, lengths in um"), window_min and median_cycle_min; the step adds
  frame_interval_source, pixel_size_source and frame_interval_note when time_s and frame_interval_s
  disagree (time_s wins).
- Figures: "minutes from division" or "frames from division (frame interval unknown)"; speed in
  um/min or px/frame; size control in um^2 or "px^2, pixel size unknown".
- Sources reused, nothing new added: the tracks CSV time_s column (as the lineage step reads it), the
  frame_interval_s and pixel_size_um Make Masks settings. A non-positive value is refused.
- Arrival contract: tests/data/release_contracts/864_867_division_units_bootstrap_api_arrivals_2026-11-08.json
  (calibrate_divisions). 9-locale catalogue records are owed.
- tests/test_division_units_and_movie_bootstrap.py: a 12-frame cycle at 5 min/frame reads 60 min and
  the birth area times 0.5^2 um^2/px^2 matches the hand calculation; an uncalibrated movie keeps
  frames/pixels and says so.

NOT DONE
- Image headers (OME/ND2/CZI physical size, time increment) are not read in the step: Make Masks hands
  it stacked arrays and file names, not the source files. spacr.point_spread.image_optics_metadata
  can read a TIFF's pixel size and could feed pixel_size_um later; spaCR has no time-increment reader.
