DIVISION ANALYSIS: ONSET OF PRE-DIVISION CHANGES
================================================

Lifecycle: future
Status: DONE 2026-11-09 (alpha) — spacr.division_analysis gains frame_effects (Hedges' g of dividing vs control units at every frame of the window, baseline-normalised own values, bootstrap CI) and onset_times (per measure: direction = sign of g at the last frame before division; a frame is significant when its interval excludes 0 in that direction; no onset when the last frame is not; otherwise the earliest rel_t of the unbroken significant run that ends at the division). analyse_divisions(onsets=True) adds frame_effects and onsets (onset_rel_t, direction, onset_cycle_fraction, onset_minutes once calibrated) after every other step, so the other results do not change; the Make Masks division step writes onsets.csv and frame_effects.csv. Settings timelapse_division_onset and timelapse_division_onset_ci under ALPHA_FEATURES[901]. On the 13 datasets of the BMC reanalysis, onset and direction per dataset x measure are identical to stage2_analyse.onset (130 rows, 100 onsets).
Requested: maintainer, 2026-11-09 (BMC paper item spec ITEM_division_onsets.md, port P21).
Owner: Home (claude-lane-e-1109 worktree).

WHAT THE STATE IS
The onset of each measure's pre-division change came from onset() in a
custom stage-2 script. spacr.division_analysis had no per-frame comparison
and no onset.

WHAT TO DO
1. A public onset_times(effects, settings) that returns one row per measure
   (onset, sign), with the interval columns as a setting.
2. Run it inside the division analysis and write onsets.csv.
3. Report onsets in minutes once the frame interval is known (864).

HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
The onset and sign per dataset x measure equal stage2_analyse.onset on the
reference per-frame effect tables of the 13 datasets.

RESULT
tests/data/division_onsets_901 holds the reference per-frame effects
(panelA_per_frame_effects_dividing_vs_control.csv, columns trimmed) and the
reference onsets (panelF_onset_before_division.csv). onset_times reproduces
onset_rel_t and direction for all 130 dataset x measure rows (100 onsets, 30
without), checked in tests/test_division_onsets_901.py.

DELIBERATELY NOT DONE
- Settings are named timelapse_division_onset / timelapse_division_onset_ci
  (the spec wrote division_onset / division_onset_ci) to match the other
  timelapse_division_* settings in the same GUI group.
- Pooled results (pool_division_movies) carry no onsets.
- The spaCR per-frame effects are computed by spaCR's own division pipeline
  (its controls and bootstrap stream); an end-to-end rerun of the 13 datasets
  through spaCR needs a spaCR movie reader for them (the spec leaves that
  out), so the frame effects themselves were not compared with the script's.
- Catalogue (9-locale) records and the arrival contract are left to the
  master session.
