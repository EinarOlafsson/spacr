Lifecycle: new

Requested: maintainer, 2026-11-06 (BMC paper decode-yield study).
Owner: home spacr software paper.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT AND WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every decode strategy from the decode-yield study becomes a spaCR setting.
The defaults reproduce the final rule, "rule (a) + S2": 70.98% of cells
assigned at FDR 0.099 / 0.122 on 176 fields. Brieflow's rule assigns 58.19%
at FDR 0.130.

Evidence:
/nas_mnt/data/sync/papers/BMCspacr/reanalysis/decode_yield_2026-11-04/results_v3/final/master_table_decode_strategies.csv

Defaults are the final rule unless stated.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BASE CALLING (hook ops_engine._decode_field -> ops_sbs.called_bases)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- ops_crosstalk: none | argmax_median | argmax_median_per_cycle | median |
  percentile (default argmax_median).
- ops_decode_library_calibration (True). Companions:
  - ops_calibration_passes (2);
  - ops_calibration_min_labels (30).
- ops_base_caller: argmax | gbm | cnn_per_cycle | cnn_end_to_end (default
  argmax). Companion: ops_base_caller_model (path).
- ops_decode_preprocess (False). Companions:
  - ops_flatfield_sigma (64);
  - ops_tophat_size (7).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REGISTRATION (hook ops_cycles.align_field)

SPOTS
