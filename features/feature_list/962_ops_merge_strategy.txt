PROBLEM
spaCR's merge phase aligns each phenotype field from the placement the
phenotype phase made (anchors plus a raster fit), re-solved on the field's
own nuclei. Brieflow offers two routes instead: a cell-pattern hash per
phenotype/sequencing tile pair, and, for sparse screens whose tiles hold too
few cells for a pattern, a well-level stitch placed from the microscope's
stage coordinates. spaCR had no strategy switch, no automatic fallback and
no placement from stage coordinates alone.

BUILD
- ops_merge_strategy (str, default 'current', alpha), category OPS phenotype:
  - 'current': today's path; the merge tables are byte-identical (no code
    on this path changed; checked on Funk plate A well A1, all 1,281 fields).
  - 'tile': each phenotype field is hashed against the sequencing nuclei
    under its stored footprint plus 200 px (spacr.ops_merge._triangle_votes:
    the existing triangle_descriptors, every descriptor match solved at once,
    votes = triangle pairs whose three vertices land within 3 px of the
    consensus), then re-solved (refine_field_transform) and paired
    (merge_field). A field whose hash finds nothing is left unmerged.
  - 'stitch': both well mosaics, one similarity between them
    (_well_similarity: up to 9 regions hashed, the agreeing regions' cell
    pairs pooled into one least-squares similarity, re-solved once), then
    each field re-solved and paired. ops_merge_stitch_source: 'metadata'
    (stage positions from the images' OME tags through the existing
    spacr.image_stitch.read_stage_positions, or from a table given in
    ops_merge_stage_positions; a clear error when missing) or 'registered'
    (the stitch and phenotype phases' placements).
  - 'auto': 'tile' first; a tile pair is weak when its votes are below
    ops_merge_min_votes or its matched fraction below
    ops_merge_min_match_fraction; the well falls back to 'stitch' when the
    share of weak pairs exceeds ops_merge_fallback_share.
- merge_strategy_report.csv (run folder), one row per well: requested and
  used route, reason, tile pairs and weak pairs, vote and matched-fraction
  percentiles (10/50/90), the well similarity of the stitch route, matched
  phenotype and SBS %, median residual (px), time. The same row is in the
  well's merge report (ops_report.json, report["merge"]["strategy"]).
- Thresholds: ops_merge_min_votes 4 and ops_merge_min_match_fraction 0.51,
  tuned on A1 only by the pre-set rule and validated on A2/A3.
  ops_merge_fallback_share 0.05: the rule gave 0 (no weak field in any dense
  A1 well); the maintainer set a 5 % margin on 2026-11-16, so a well falls
  back only when more than 5 % of its tile pairs are weak.

PROOF (Funk plate A, A1-A3; reanalysis/item962_proof/REPORT.md)
- auto within 0.5 points of current on dense wells: PASS.
- auto beats tile at <= 10 % density: PARTIAL (maintainer decision
  2026-11-16, landed as alpha). auto beats tile at 2 % in every well and at
  5 % in A2, ties elsewhere and is never worse; spaCR's tile route still
  succeeds at 5-10 %, so there is nothing to beat there.
- spaCR matches or beats Brieflow's best route at every density: PASS
  (+2.4 points at 20 % to +25.5 at 2 %).

  _choose_route, _well_similarity;
  ops_engine._merge_*), so no API catalogue records. Labels and tooltips
