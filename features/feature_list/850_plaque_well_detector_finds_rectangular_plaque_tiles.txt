PLAQUE WELL DETECTOR FINDS RECTANGULAR PLAQUE TILES
===================================================

Lifecycle: new
Status: open, alpha; needs one GPU training run, on the workstation after plaque v8.
Requested: maintainer, 2026-11-07.
Owner: home spacr software paper.
Related: 845, 848.

WHAT THE STATE IS
The zoo's toxoplasma_well_detector_v3 (YOLO11) finds round wells only. In
407 held-out literature figures it boxed about 443 plaque images and missed
about 1,182, almost all of them rectangular plaque-monolayer tiles or
uncropped panels. Multi-scale and tiled re-detection recovered only about
0.5%, with 92-95% false boxes.

Evidence: /nas_mnt/data/sync/papers/BMCspacr/reanalysis/plaque_meta/:
heldout/redetect_recovery_by_figure.csv and review/redetect/.

WHAT TO DO
- Add a "plaque tile" class to the detector.
- Train on the meta-analysis tile boxes (agent rough boxes, edge-snapped,
  then reviewed) plus the existing well training set.
- Figure mode measures tiles the same way as wells and adds a box_kind
  column (well/tile).
