OPS FALSE-CALL MODEL: WHAT MAKES A GENOTYPE CALL A DECOY
========================================================

Lifecycle: future

Owner: paper session.
Related: 873, 877.

WHAT IT DOES
ops_decode_qc (False) adds the decode_qc phase after the decode (or run alone
with phases=("decode_qc",)). The decode then stores each field's evidence
with the spot features (decode_cache/siteNNNN.npz). The phase decodes those
fields permissively (threshold 150, up to two mismatches, territory, plain
majority), labels each nucleus FP (decoy), TP (guide) or TN, holds out half
the fields of each plate (seed 7) and splits the decoys in halves by md5, and
trains gradient boosting on decoy-versus-library calls with a sequence-only
control and chance. results/ops_decode_qc/: model_metrics.csv (main,
control_sequence_only, chance; AUROC and AP with field-bootstrap CIs),
shap_mean_abs.csv, permutation_importance.csv, model.json and
false_call_cells.parquet.
Settings: ops_decode_qc_model (xgboost, optional dependency imported lazily
with an install message | hist_gbm), ops_decode_qc_features, ops_decode_qc_split
(field), ops_decode_qc_seed (0), ops_decode_qc_max_calls (20000, the SHAP
sample). SHAP: the shap package when installed; for XGBoost without it the
booster's exact TreeSHAP (pred_contribs).
Code: spacr/ops_decode.py (false_call_tables, false_call_model,
run_decode_qc), spacr/ops_engine.py (decode_qc phase).

VALIDATION (2026-11-09, CPU)
Over the study's subset_v1.1 field cache (176 fields, samples_v11):
- the cell table (705,027 rows, 78 columns) is identical to
  fp_characterisation/cells_permissive.parquet, row for row;
- XGBoost, seed 0, n_jobs 2 as in the reference: main AUROC 0.9813 (CI
  0.97935-0.98284) and sequence-only 0.8262 (CI 0.81734-0.83398), equal to
  model_metrics.csv; mean |SHAP| over the 20,000-call sample within 2.1e-8 of
  shap_mean_abs.csv; permutation importance within 6.2e-17 of
  permutation_importance_repeats.csv.
Remaining: none for the model; the engine path from raw tiles shares 873's
field evidence code, validated bit-identical on A1/74 and A1/190.
