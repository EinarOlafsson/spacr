--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE STATE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

nucleus_zernike_1 and cell_zernike_1 are numerically zero (~1e-15) apart
from rare ~3e-3 spikes; *_zernike_0 is the constant 1/pi. All are exported as
features. After per-plate scaling the spikes dominate cosine similarity and
1-NN mechanism-of-action accuracy on BBBC021 falls to chance (6.8%; 89.3%
without the two columns). Nothing in spaCR checked feature columns.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A single degenerate column can silently destroy a profiling result, and
the user sees no warning.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. At the end of every complete Measure run, flag per-object feature
   columns that are all or mostly missing, numerically zero, constant, or
   spike-dominated (MAD ~0 while the largest deviation is far larger, carried
   by a small share of rows).
2. Report them in the log and in feature_qc.csv / feature_qc.json beside
   measurements.db; leave measurements.db byte-identical by default.
3. A setting feature_qc_drop (alpha) removes the flagged columns from the
   object tables before profiling reads them.
4. Register feature_qc_drop in ALPHA_FEATURES under 756.
5. Later: fix the source (drop Zernike moments that are identically zero for
   a centred disk, or clamp below a tolerance to exactly zero).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The BBBC021 zernike_1 pattern is flagged as spike_dominated, well-behaved
columns (areas, intensities, sparse counts, partly missing texture) are not,
and a run with feature_qc_drop on scores 1-NN MoA like the run without the
two columns.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DELIBERATELY NOT DONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Thresholds are fixed function defaults, not settings, until real plates
show they need tuning. Step 5 (fixing the Zernike source) is not done.

spacr.sp_stats.flag_degenerate_features judges each numeric per-object
column (identity, position and Metadata_ columns are skipped) and reports
the first of all_missing, mostly_missing (>50% NaN/inf), numerical_zero
(|x| <= 1e-12), constant, spike_dominated (MAD < 1e-6 x largest deviation
and at most 5% of values deviating; needs 10 values). measure_crop calls
_emit_feature_qc after every complete run: measurements.db is opened
read-only and stays byte-identical; feature_qc.csv and feature_qc.json are
written beside it and the first twenty flagged columns are logged.
feature_qc_drop (alpha, ALPHA_FEATURES[756], under "Profiling α") drops the
flagged columns with ALTER TABLE ... DROP COLUMN before profiling.
