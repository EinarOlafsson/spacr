BUILD
- ops_voyager3_method 'ai' (default) | 'enrichment'; ops_voyager3_reactome (GMT),
  ops_voyager3_corum (.txt/.zip, human complexes only), ops_voyager3_enrichment_fdr
  (0.05).
- Terms: complete UniProt GO (process, component, function) and keywords of
  reviewed entries (own field set and cache key, so AI-mode fetches and outputs
  are unchanged), CORUM complexes, Reactome R-HSA pathways; references copied to
  results/ops_voyager3/reference_cache/ under their SHA-256 with file date and
  PROVENANCE note; a missing source falls back on the last copy (offline).
- Per cluster: one-sided hypergeometric test of every term with >= 2 cluster
  genes, background = all the screen's genes (controls and unassigned rows
  dropped); BH FDR across the cluster's terms; significant = FDR <= limit,
  >= 3 genes, fold >= 2; best = lowest FDR, then higher fold, smaller K, id.
  Confidence = Voyager3's coverage rule on the best term's share (>70 % high,
  50-70 medium, 30-50 low, else none). Candidates = genes without the best
  term in high and medium clusters.
- Outputs: the AI mode's tables, report and provenance (method, references,
  background size, thresholds, runtime) plus voyager3_enrichment_terms.csv;
  voyager3_replay rebuilds them byte for byte. No AI call is made.
