DEFINITIONS
Funk's mass displacement and spaCR's intensity_moments mass displacement are the
same definition (centroid to intensity-weighted centroid, pixels), so no
separate column. The OPS Funk columns now come from the spaCR code: they move
by at most 1 ulp (np.hypot in place of sqrt of squares). Pearson is
np.corrcoef's, clipped to [-1, 1] as np.corrcoef clips; NaN for a constant
channel. Form factor is NaN, not inf, for a zero perimeter.
