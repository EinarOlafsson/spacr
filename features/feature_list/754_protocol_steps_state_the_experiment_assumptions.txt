PROTOCOL STEPS STATE THE EXPERIMENT ASSUMPTIONS
===============================================
Lifecycle: new
Status: DONE 2026-11-01 — assumption sentences shipped with their nine-language reviewed translations.
Requested: maintainer, 2026-11-01.
Owner: Home (coordinator assigns an agent).
Related: 739 Navigator Protocols, 733 Navigator, 746 (no text is cut off), N678 factual language.

WHAT THE USER REQUESTED
"for the text in the steps in the protocol, or getting to the protocol in the text box at the bottom of
the screen. it should just state the assumptions about the experiment as the user clicks their way to a
protocol. like: 'Images use the Yokogawa CellVoyager metadata structure' or 'Images are from a mid or high
content microscope.' etc"

WHAT TO DO
- While the user picks choices toward a Navigator protocol (input family, data format and every dependent
  choice: image format, filename metadata, sequencer, read pairing, read orientation, experiment, barcode
  mapping, organism, analysis route), the description box at the bottom states what each choice assumes
  about the experiment: one short declarative sentence per choice, for example "Images use the Yokogawa
  CellVoyager metadata structure." or "Images are from a mid- or high-content microscope."
- No instructions, no "you", no questions, no marketing. The existing labels stay.
- The sentences live beside the choices in spacr/resources/module_workflows.json (protocol_wizard) and are
  derived from what the choice means in code; no sentence claims something the protocol does not assume.
- Each step shows the assumptions of the choices made on that step. When the protocol is reached, the box
  lists all accumulated assumptions, one per line, in choice order, followed by any requirement that keeps
  a route from starting. Back navigation and changed choices update the list.
- Navigator search keeps working while a protocol is active (739 requirement).

ACCEPTANCE
- Every protocol choice has an assumption sentence; tests check that the sentences are declarative, that the
  box shows the current step's assumptions and the full ordered list at the end, and that the list follows
  Back navigation.
- No text is cut off (tests.qt.text_fit 2.0 en and de; known-offender list stays empty). Screenshots in both
  themes at 100 % and 200 %, offscreen and native Xvfb/Openbox.
- New strings closed through the normal translation generators in nine languages.
