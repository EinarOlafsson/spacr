EVIDENCE
    https://forum.image.sc/t/normalization-of-fluorescence-intensity-to-compare-different-images/82444

WHAT TO BUILD
    Calibrate image intensities across imaging sessions/days using
    fluorescent beads or reference wells imaged each session, applied at the
    image level before measurement (today's batch correction works on tables
    only), recorded in provenance.

DONE WHEN
    On two sessions of the same reference sample with different exposure,
    calibrated intensities agree within a stated tolerance.

ALPHA: 2026-09-27
    Measure setting intensity_calibration (heading "Intensity Calibration
    (Alpha)", ALPHA_FEATURES[580]) with intensity_calibration_wells,
    intensity_calibration_statistic (foreground = median above Otsu, for
    beads; median = whole field, for a uniform reference well) and
    intensity_calibration_offset (camera dark offset). Each plate is one
    session. Before workers start, Measure takes the statistic of each
    intensity plane in every reference field (in its uint16 working units),
    the median over a plate's fields, and gives each plate the gain that
    matches the first plate by name. Every field's intensity planes become
    offset + (value - offset) x gain (label planes untouched; uint8 widened
    to uint16; clipping at the ceiling warned and recorded). The per-field
    record is JSON in measurements.db:intensity_rescale.intensity_calibration.
    A plate without a reference field stops the run.
    Code: spacr/intensity_rescale.py (_build_calibration_plan,
    _apply_calibration), wired in spacr/measure.py.

    Done-when check (CPU, tests/test_intensity_calibration.py): two plates
    of the same bead well and cell sample, the second at 2.5x exposure, both
    over offset 100 with Poisson noise, measured end to end by measure_crop.
    Uncalibrated, cell mean intensities (above offset) differ 2.5-fold;
    calibrated against the beads they agree within the stated 2% tolerance
    (gains 1/2.5 within 2%). The median statistic on a uniform well gives the
    same gain.

    LEFT (updated 2026-09-28): sessions are plates only (no separate session
    column or bead folder outside the plate); no GUI preview of the gains;
    the reference plate is always the first by name. Incompatible calibration
    and changed reference content now refuse reuse of retained measurements.

REAL-DATA VALIDATION 2026-09-30 (CPU; no code change)
    Data: cpg0002-jump-scope (Cell Painting Gallery; Tromans-Coia & Jamali
    et al. 2023). Plate P1 (JUMP-MOA, U2OS, 5-channel Cell Painting) on a
    PerkinElmer Phenix, confocal, one plane, imaged in two sessions:
    2020-11-09 with 2x2 binning and 2020-11-11 unbinned. Field f01 of 60
    wells in each, the same field in both. Session gains differ by channel
    (0.4-1.0x). Reference = 12 of the 24 DMSO wells. Evaluated = the other
    12 DMSO wells + 36 random compound wells: whole-field mean above the
    camera offset, session B / session A per field and channel. Run
    through _build_calibration_plan and _apply_calibration, foreground
    statistic. Script: /mnt/wd4tb/scratch/538/580/val580.py. Receipt:
    features/data/538_541_580_real_data_validation_2026-09-30.json.
    - Offset 100 (Andor Zyla): compound wells, median |B/A - 1| per channel
      41, 35, 3.3, 60, 7.6 % uncalibrated -> 7.2, 6.6, 7.6, 7.0, 5.9 %
      calibrated. Held-out DMSO: 2.8-7.8 %. Fields within 10 % on every
      channel: 31 % -> 70 % (DMSO 35 % -> 83 %). p90 13-22 %.
    - Offset 0: the Hoechst channel is left 11 % off (vs 7 %). Set
      intensity_calibration_offset to the camera offset.
    - Residual bias: channels that already matched (ratio 0.98) are pulled
      to 0.92-0.93. The median-above-Otsu reference statistic is not
      invariant to the binning (resolution) change. A whole-field mean
      statistic fixed two channels and worsened two. Not adopted, because
      the gain from 12 reference fields is limited by field-to-field
      scatter (focus, bleaching between sessions).
    Verdict: on real sessions the calibrated intensities agree to about
    6-8 % median per field (not the 2 % of the synthetic check). A
    resolution change between sessions is the main limit. Still left: a
    bead-plate check (no public bead plate imaged twice was found).
