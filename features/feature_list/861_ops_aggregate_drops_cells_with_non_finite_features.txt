OPS AGGREGATE DROPS CELLS WITH NON-FINITE FEATURES
==================================================

Lifecycle: future
Status: DONE 2026-11-08 (alpha, under ALPHA_FEATURES[752]). — ops_drop_nonfinite_rows (default False): the aggregate phase leaves out cells with more than 1% of their measured features not finite (Brieflow's drop_rows_threshold) and reports dropped_nonfinite_rows. spacr/ops_engine.py, tests/test_ops_field_drop_zernike.py.

WHAT THE STATE IS
Cell rows with many NaN or infinite feature values reach the OPS
aggregate phase and the per-guide statistics.

WHAT TO DO
- In the OPS aggregate phase, drop cell rows in which more than 1% of the
  features are not finite.
- Report how many cells were dropped.
- Tests: a row with >1% non-finite features is dropped; a row at or below
  1% is kept.

RULE
The paper session builds this under the identical-output rule: with the new
option off, outputs stay identical to today's.
