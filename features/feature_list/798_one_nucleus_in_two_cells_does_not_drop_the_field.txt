--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE STATE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Measure drops a whole field (MergeError) when one nucleus overlaps two
cells; taxol and other mitotic fields are hit most. The lost fields appear
only as failed: lines in the logs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Dropping fields biases exactly the treatments with the strongest
phenotypes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Resolve the ambiguous nucleus (assign it to the cell with the larger
   overlap, or drop that one object) and keep the field.
2. Count resolved and dropped objects per field in the run log and a table.
3. Register any new setting in ALPHA_FEATURES under 798.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

No BBBC021 field is refused for this reason, and the per-field count of
resolved objects is reported.
