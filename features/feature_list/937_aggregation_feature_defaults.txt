Lifecycle: future
Status: DONE 2026-11-13 (alpha, under ALPHA_FEATURES[937]; aggregation_defaults 'per_feature' is the default for new screen settings files, older files keep 'legacy' through DEFAULT_CHANGES[934]) — spacr.merge_tables.SUMMARY_AGGREGATION_RULES gives every column of a per-cell table its own method, with the reason, when cells are summarised per well, guide or gene: identifiers, positions and link bookkeeping dropped; provenance stamps kept once (a group that mixes two values stops the summary); intensities, intensity spreads, sizes, shapes, textures, correlation coefficients, distances and embeddings by the median; shares bounded by 0 and 1 by the mean; yes/no flags as <column>_fraction; counts as the per-cell mean plus <column>_sum; neighbour counts by the mean only; orientations by the axial circular mean plus <column>_coherence; a column no rule names takes the median and is listed as 'unruled'. spacr.merge_tables.aggregate_by_feature applies it (overrides by name or regex; fisher_z_mean and trimmed_mean as override methods); summary_aggregation_plan returns column, method, rule, reason and family; circmean_axial is the axial mean. MAINTAINER DECISIONS (2026-11-13)
Counts: per-cell mean as the main column plus a _sum total. Correlations:
median. area_bbox: max at roll-up. OPS gene profiles stay the median for
every feature (Brieflow); no ops_aggregate_method setting was added, the
library keeps method='median' as the default. per_feature becomes the
default for new settings files; existing files stay legacy through the
settings-schema version.

DEVIATIONS FROM THE SPEC TABLE (with reasons)
- compactness is in the size/shape row (median), not the shares row: it is
  2*pi*sum(r^2)/area^2, at least 1 and unbounded above.
- k1/k2 (Manders' split coefficients) are with the correlations (median):
  they are not bounded by 1.
- M1_correlation_<t> / M2_correlation_<t> (the thresholded Manders M1/M2)
  are with the shares (mean), like manders_m1/m2: they are fractions of
  intensity, not correlation coefficients.
- The GUI read-only plan table, the ops_aggregate_method setting and the
  assay_report methods are not part of this item (the maintainer kept OPS
  on the median; assay_report's listed columns are unchanged).
