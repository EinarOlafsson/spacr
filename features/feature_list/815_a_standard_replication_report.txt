--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE REPORT CONTAINS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The standard output of analyze_replication, the counterpart of the
recruitment report:
- representative images for each bin;
- the graphs;
- control graphs with statistics;
- one folder per panel with the data CSV, stats CSV, PNG and PDF;
- a main figure on A4 with automatic pagination;
- a generated figure legend as a .txt file, also appended to the PDF.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SPEC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Representative tiles per bin
   - One representative vacuole per bin: within +/-10% of the bin's median
     area, solidity in the bin's top quarter, parasite-channel mean between
     the 25th and 90th percentile, no other object within 12 px; of those,
     the one closest to the median area.
   - A 5 x 5 random grid per bin (seeded), drawn from the filtered objects.
   - One fixed crop size and one display scaling for all crops.
   - 16-bit throughout (item 772, points 7-10): a raw uint16 multichannel
     TIFF per crop including the mask, plus a 16-bit RGB display PNG/TIFF at
     native resolution. Only the labelled PDF/PNG are 8-bit (interpolation
     'none').
2. Graphs, each unfiltered and filtered, at well and replicate level
   - Stacked bins, with chi-square and with Fisher-Freeman-Halton, showing
     mean +/- SD of the replicate fractions.
   - A replicate-mean bar (area, or parasites per vacuole) with one test per
     graph and an assumptions table.
3. Well-level control graphs, before and after filtering
   - Per image channel, the mean intensity in every object type.
   - Objects per well, per object type.
   - filter_summary.
4. Works for any number of object types and channels.

Tests: a synthetic plate gives one folder per panel with all four files; the
legend .txt matches the text appended to the PDF; the tiles follow the
selection rules; pagination keeps every page A4.
