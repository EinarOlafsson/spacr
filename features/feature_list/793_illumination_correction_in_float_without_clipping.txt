ILLUMINATION CORRECTION IN FLOAT WITHOUT CLIPPING

Lifecycle: future
Status: DONE 2026-11-07 — measure_crop promotes the intensity channels to float32 before any preprocessing hook that sets measures_in_float (IlluminationCorrector does), so the flat field is divided out in float and every feature is measured on the float image: no rounding and no clipping at 65535. The image table's channel_<c>_saturated_fraction is read from the acquired integer pixels. The integer path that remains (direct callers, segmentation inputs) rescales the field by dtype max / field max instead of clipping and keeps the factor per field in IlluminationCorrector.stats['scales'] and in the segmentation run-journal stage (integer_rescale_by_field); only negative values after a dark offset are still clipped, counted and reported. No setting, so nothing in ALPHA_FEATURES (exempted in tests/qt/test_alpha_features_gate.py). Tests tests/test_illumination_float_measure_793.py and tests/test_illumination.py. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE STATE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Measure applies the retrospective flat field in the input dtype (uint16),
so bright corrected pixels saturate: "illumination correction clipped 222
pixel(s) ... at the top of its dtype".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Clipping removes real signal from exactly the brightest objects, and the
loss differs between plates with different flat fields.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Divide by the flat field in float32 and measure the float image.
2. If a stored image must be integer, rescale rather than clip, and record
   the scale in provenance.
3. Register any new setting in ALPHA_FEATURES under 793.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A BBBC021 plate reports zero clipped pixels, and intensity features of
unclipped pixels are unchanged within float precision.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DELIBERATELY NOT DONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Segmentation inputs keep their dtype (rescaled, not float): they are
  normalised before the model sees them, so the scale does not reach a
  measured number.
- Crops still come from the uncorrected source image, as before.
