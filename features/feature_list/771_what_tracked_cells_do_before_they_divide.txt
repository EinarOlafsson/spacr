WHAT TRACKED CELLS DO BEFORE THEY DIVIDE: DIVISION-ALIGNED TRAJECTORIES WITH CONTROLS
===================================================================================
Lifecycle: future
Status: DONE 2026-11-07 (alpha) — Make Masks timelapse runs the analysis on spaCR's own tracks CSV and tracked label stack when timelapse_division_analysis is on, writes tidy CSVs, a JSON summary and three figures to tracks/division/<tracks file>/, and the Track preview card shows them in a Division analysis panel; the four settings and the panel are registered as ALPHA_FEATURES[771]. Follow-ons 4a-4d and the 9-locale catalogue records are not done.
Requested: maintainer, 2026-11-04 (BMC Bioinformatics paper, Fig 7 "Timepose applied: what happens before cells divide"; analysis approved on gold tracks, no model).
Owner: Home (division-analysis worktree).
Related: 426 timeflows (the tracker whose output this reads), 535 cell-cycle phase, 539 bleach correction, lineage trees.

WHAT THE STATE IS
spacr/division_analysis.py turns a tracked label stack (label value = track id) plus a lineage table
(label, t_start, t_end, parent; CTC man_track semantics) into a controlled answer to "does this
measurement change before division more than in cells that are not about to divide?":
- measure_label_stack, add_motion_features: area, major/minor axis, aspect ratio, eccentricity,
  circularity, solidity, mean intensity normalised to the frame median; speed, displacement, turning.
- division_events, median_cycle_frames: fission (parent ends, two daughters next frame) and budding
  (mother continues, each bud one event); t_div = last frame before a daughter is seen.
- control_slots, match_controls: same-movie, time-overlapping, size-matched (+/-25%) tracks that do
  not divide in the window or the horizon after their pseudo-division time.
- shuffled_alignment: the dividing tracks aligned on a random time away from division (null control).
- align_units, unit_changes: rel_t trajectories (daughters after 0) and late-vs-baseline change
  (log ratio for positive measures, difference otherwise).
- effect_size (Hedges g, bootstrap 95% CI, large-sample variance), random_effects_meta
  (DerSimonian-Laird, tau2, Q, I2).
- cell_cycles, size_control_fits: birth vs division size, added size; sizer/adder/timer slopes.
tests/test_division_analysis.py builds synthetic lineages with a planted pre-division change and
checks that the pipeline finds it while the shuffled control does not, plus the meta-analysis and
size-control slopes against hand-computed cases.

The first use is the Fig 7 reanalysis in
/nas_mnt/data/sync/papers/BMCspacr/reanalysis/division_biology/ (REPORT.md there, driver code in code/).

WHY IT MATTERS
Tracking is only worth its cost when the tracks answer a biological question. This is the question
almost every time-lapse user asks first (what changes before a cell divides), and without matched and
shuffled controls the usual answer is a growth curve mistaken for a pre-division signal.

WHAT WAS BUILT (2026-11-07, alpha)
- spacr.division_analysis.lineage_from_tracks: a spaCR tracks table (any tracker) to the lineage table,
  using the tracker's parent_track_id links, else the lineage-tree inference within
  timelapse_lineage_max_distance.
- spacr.division_analysis.analyse_divisions: the Fig 7 pipeline in one call (QC of each division with a
  status, window from the median cycle, matched controls, shuffled alignment with its own matched
  controls, Hedges g per measurement, baseline-normalised trajectories, size-control fits).
- write_division_results / read_division_results: events, effects, trajectories, cycles and size_control
  CSVs through spacr.tabular, summary.json, and the figures through spacr.plot.save_figure.
- division_figures: aligned trajectories with 95% bands (dividing, matched control, shuffled), forest
  plot of Hedges g (dividing vs control, shuffled vs its control), size at division vs birth with
  sizer/adder/timer lines.
- Make Masks (timelapse): settings timelapse_division_analysis (off), timelapse_division_mode
  (auto/fission/budding), timelapse_division_window (blank = 0.3 x median cycle, 4-30 frames) and
  timelapse_division_size_tolerance (0.25), under "Division Analysis α"; the step runs after the lineage
  step for each tracked field and a failure is printed, not raised.
- spacr.qt.widgets.division_panel.DivisionAnalysisPanel in the Track preview card: open a results folder
  (or tracks/division to list every field) and see the three figures in tabs.
- ALPHA_FEATURES[771]: the four settings and the DivisionAnalysisPanel widget; alpha deck row added
  (deck not re-rendered).
- Arrival contract tests/data/release_contracts/division_analysis_api_arrivals_2026-11-07.json for the
  new public callables. The fourteen older functions were counted into the API ratchets on 2026-11-05
  without a contract; their 9-locale catalogue records are still owed (step 1).
