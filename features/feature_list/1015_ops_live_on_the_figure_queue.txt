--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE STATE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

spacr/qt/widgets/ops_live_preview.py shows every figure of a tab at once,
each small, under the controls. The align and stitch mosaic blurs when zoomed.
Align and stitch and genotyping have no Settings button; objects and
phenotype have a short one in another place. There is a font scale slider.
Work started 2026-11-24 on branch ws-offload-ws-ops-live-queue.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The live view is where the OPS settings are tuned before a long run. When
each figure takes a small part of the screen, the alignment, stitching, spot
and genotype calls cannot be judged, and settings cannot be tried out and
carried over to the run.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Every tab shows its figures, graphs and tables in the Mask generation
   figure queue (spacr/qt/widgets/figure_queue.py): one at a time, the deck
   on the left, an opaque container. One implementation, reused.
2. Graphs in pyqtgraph, with square axes.
3. The stitched mosaic is drawn again at higher resolution when zoomed.
4. Controls per tab, left to right: Sample (how many images the preview
   uses), Settings (all settings of that tab and only those, applied live;
   an action carries them over to the OPS module run), Run preview.
   Genotyping gets Settings; objects and phenotype get the full set.
5. Align and stitch offers the available regions (at least 3 in the test
   data). Spots and genotyping use that region; objects and phenotype use
   the whole genotyped region.
6. Clustering: clicking a point shows an information tile about it, as in
   the regression results.
7. Merge and aggregation: more useful graphs; candidate forms are shown to
   the maintainer before redrawing.
8. Remove the font scale slider.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Screenshots of the real main window, one per tab, show one figure filling
the view with the deck on the left and Sample / Settings / Run preview
beside each other. A changed align setting, applied to the OPS module, is
the value the OPS run uses. Tests cover the per-tab settings filter, the
carry-over, the region choice, the click tile, the zoom redraw and that the
slider is gone.
