Lifecycle: future
Status: DONE 2026-11-09 (alpha, under ALPHA_FEATURES[887]) — spacr.gene_measurement_compare.guide_efficacy_profiles fits each gene's guide rows in the S02-weighted space as x_g = e_g m (alternating m = sum w e x / sum w e^2 and e = clip(<x, m> / <m, m>, 0, emax), the largest e rescaled to 1, for at most ops_guide_efficacy_iters rounds or until e moves by less than 1e-6) and returns Z/|Z| + M/|M| and every guide's efficacy; a gene row without guide rows keeps Z. ops_guide_efficacy='jacks_blend' (with ops_profile_weighting) writes ops_genes_<plate>_efficacy and ops_guides_<plate>_efficacy. spacr/gene_measurement_compare.py, spacr/ops_engine.py, spacr/ops_settings.py, spacr/settings.py, tests/test_ops_guide_efficacy_blend_887.py.

WHAT THE STATE IS

WHAT TO DO
- Port the efficacy fit and the blend, alpha, after contrastive_weighting.

RESULT
On the xfield BF/full inputs (X_bf_spacrcells, genes with more than 10
cells in both bases, one row per control barcode),
contrastive_weighting(drop_zero=True) and guide_efficacy_profiles
reproduce X04_jacks_blend.npz bit for bit (5,299 x 69, float32) in the
reference environment, and the share of guides with e < 0.5 is
0.41455061402221244, as recorded. The cosines are therefore those of the
scored arm; the SELECT and REPORT retrieval scores were not recomputed
here (that needs the retrieval scorer of item 880).

The stopping rule is the reference's np.allclose(e_new, e, atol=1e-6),
which also has a relative tolerance of 1e-5, and the clip at emax slows
convergence: a planted efficacy is recovered to about 1e-4, not 1e-6.
