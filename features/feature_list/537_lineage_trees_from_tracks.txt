WHAT TO BUILD
    Draw publication-style lineage trees from the tracker's division links
    (host cells and parasite endodyogeny), coloured by any measurement, with
    export to Newick/CSV and per-lineage statistics (generation time,
    sibling correlation).

DONE WHEN
    Trees render for a timelapse with divisions; generation times match the
    tracks.

ALPHA: 2026-09-27. Built behind Preferences -> Show alpha features, registered
    as ALPHA_FEATURES[537]: settings timelapse_lineage (default False),
    timelapse_lineage_color_by (default generation_time) and
    timelapse_lineage_max_distance (default 30.0), shown on the Timelapse app
    under "Lineage Trees (Alpha)" and kept off Make Masks with the rest of the
    Timelapse category.

WHAT WAS BUILT (2026-09-27)
    spacr/timelapse.py, private helpers only. After each field is tracked,
    _run_lineage_step reads that field's tracks CSV (any backend) and writes
    tracks/lineage/<stem>_segments.csv, <stem>_lineage_stats.csv, <stem>.nwk
    and the <stem>_lineage figure (via spacr.plot.save_figure).
    - Division links: Trackastra's own links are now kept in a
      parent_track_id column of its tracks CSV and used as they are. For
      every other backend a division is inferred where new tracks start
      within timelapse_lineage_max_distance of a mother, either beside a
      mother that goes on (one daughter kept her id; that track is cut at
      the division) or as two or more tracks beside a mother that ends.
    - Generation time = first frame of the daughters minus the cell's birth
      frame, in frames, for cells born by a division that divide again.
      Per lineage: cells, divisions, generations, complete cycles, mean,
      median and SD generation time, sister pairs and sister correlation
      (Pearson, symmetric, three or more pairs); plus an "all" row.
    - Colour: a segment statistic or any numeric tracks column averaged over
      the cell's frames; _lineage_trees_from_tracks also takes a measurement
      table keyed by frame and track_id.
    Tests: tests/test_lineage_trees_from_tracks.py (known divisions,
    generation times match the tracks, Newick, statistics, run step),
    tests/qt/test_lineage_trees_alpha_gate.py.

WHAT IS LEFT
    - Colouring by a Measure-database column needs the step to run after
      Measure (the per-field hook runs at mask time, before measurement);
      today only the private helper accepts a measurement table.
    - Generation time is in frames; no hours-per-frame conversion.
    - Parasite endodyogeny needs the parasite objects tracked
      (timelapse_objects including pathogen); not tested on real data.

2026-10-01 — POST-MEASURE FEATURE COLOURING (implementation complete; alpha)
Measure now exposes the same three lineage controls under Lineage Trees α,
subject to the existing Time and Show alpha features switches. Saved values
are retained while hidden. The post-measure step runs after measurements and
other derived measurement steps, reading only the selected tracked object's
numeric feature. Available observed frames are averaged for each segment;
segments without feature values stay uncoloured. Counts of matched objects,
unmatched objects and missing feature values are reported and saved.

Both existing tracking hooks capture their actual batch_filenames and final
mask labels in <tracks CSV>.lineage_sources.json. The versioned manifest is
bound to the tracks CSV's SHA-256 and records each frame's filename, canonical
field/time identity and final track-to-object label identity. It also retains
an explicitly supplied frame_interval_s; Measure reuses it when unset and
rejects a conflicting explicit override. This does not use the motility
interval default or infer times from sorted measurements. Missing, stale,
malformed or ambiguous mappings require tracking to be repeated with lineage
enabled. Calibration and mapping validation precede publication of new field
outputs. Valid manifests are written by atomic replacement.

The private reader uses a read-only SQLite connection, exact prcf/object_label
keys and one-to-one joins. Repeated labels in another field cannot match.
Duplicate measurement keys, unknown feature columns, nonnumeric/nonfinite
values and entirely unmatched objects stop that field's measured-colour
export. Missing measured objects remain missing rather than shifting frame
indices. The measured outputs go to tracks/lineage_measured, with a provenance
JSON containing the selected column, source identities, the exact mapping-file
SHA-256 and an exact digest of the matched numeric values. Source CSVs, measurement tables and earlier
tracks/lineage outputs remain unchanged.

2026-10-11 — DIVISION PRECISION ON THE CIL MOVIE: EDGE ENTRY, VACUOLE COUNT,
BUD GUARD (CPU; maintainer asleep, design decided by the session lead)
    Three rules for INFERRED divisions (tracker-reported links untouched),
    alpha settings under "Lineage Trees α", in ALPHA_FEATURES[537]:
    - timelapse_lineage_edge_entry (default True): a track whose first
      position lies within one object radius of the field edge (half the
      median major axis of the tracked masks; half of max_distance
      without masks) is never a daughter, and a mother that is that close
      to the edge in any of the 3 frames before is still crossing into
      the field and does not divide. An edge_distance column (pixels to
      the imaged field's edge, for registered/stitched movies) overrides
      the image shape.
    - timelapse_lineage_vacuole_count (default True): objects of a frame
      closer than half of max_distance are chained into vacuoles. The
      median size of the mother's vacuole over the 6 frames before must
      be exceeded, by the objects the division adds, by the median number
      of objects in the vacuoles holding the daughters over the 6 frames
      from it (2 -> 4 -> 8 doubling; a mature rosette re-segmented keeps
      its count). The mother must be seen in 3 of the 6 frames before.
      Implemented as a lineage-level constraint from positions only, so it
      also works at the Measure stage. A mask variant (daughter overlap /
      mother shrink / minimum daughter size) was tried and dropped: it
      added nothing over the count rule here (P 0.92 but recall 0.50).
    - timelapse_lineage_bud_guard (default False) with
      timelapse_lineage_bud_threshold (2.6): the mother's highest IMC1
      p95/median in the 8 frames before must reach the threshold. Read at
      mask time from the first channel of the tracked object's
      segmentation input (the channel it was segmented on); a bud_signal
      column in a tracks table is used too. Measure has no images, so the
      guard is skipped there unless that column exists.
    Code: spacr/_lineage_trees.py (_lineage_edge_distance,
    _lineage_vacuoles, _lineage_vacuole_grew, _lineage_bud_signal,
    _lineage_object_radius, _lineage_guard_options); _run_lineage_step
