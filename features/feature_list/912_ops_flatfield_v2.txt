OPS DNA FLAT-FIELD MAP FROM PER-NUCLEUS INTENSITIES
====================================================

Lifecycle: future
Status: DONE 2026-11-10 (alpha, under ALPHA_FEATURES[912]) — spacr.ops_flatfield.build_nuclear_flat builds a plate's DNA map from its per-nucleus table: the median nuclear DNA mean per 16 px map cell (nuclear_median_grid), a count-weighted local quadratic fit of log(median - b) with Gaussian sigma 32 px and ridge 1e-6 (fit_nuclear_flat; 'gaussian' and 'local_linear' kept as the losing arms), normalised to the central 18 x 18 cells and floored at 0.10. The camera offset b is required (ops_flatfield_dark, else the plate's flat_info.json), never 0. flat_to_field reads a map at block centres (16 i + 8) or, for every earlier map, by zoom; the lookup is recorded with each map (flat_lookups) and ops_engine._ph_flat_planes honours it, so earlier maps read exactly as before. write_nuclear_flat writes one new folder per plate with the map, a JSON sidecar (offset, floor, held-out fields, nucleus counts, centre value, corner mean, % at floor, fit, sigma, grid, lookup, versions, md5) and the plate's other channel maps copied byte for byte. record_flat_md5 keeps the md5 in <dst_root>/illumination_md5.json the first time ph_masks, ph_measure (exact) or aggregate use such maps and refuses a changed map with both md5s. validate_nuclear_flat re-measures the held-out nuclei exactly ((I - b)/F + b) and analytically ((v - b) g + b) and writes the radial table with 2,000 field bootstraps. Run as OPS phases flat_nuclear and flat_validate (run_flat_phases). spacr/ops_flatfield.py, spacr/ops_engine.py, spacr/ops_settings.py, spacr/settings.py, tests/test_ops_flatfield_v2_912.py.

WHAT THE STATE IS
The DNA map was a pixel median with a wide Gaussian, a 4th-order polynomial and a 0.25 floor, read by zoom; the field corners stayed at about half the centre after correction.
