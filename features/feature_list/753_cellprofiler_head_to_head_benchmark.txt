--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE STATE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The BMC spaCR platform paper (/nas_mnt/data/sync/papers/BMCspacr) compares
spaCR with CellProfiler outside spaCR. Nothing in spaCR runs a published
.cppipe or scores the two side by side.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The comparison is the paper's central benchmark; done by hand it cannot be
re-run when either tool changes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Run a published pipeline (BBBC021 analysis.cppipe) headless through
   CellProfiler in its own environment, and spaCR's measure on the same
   images.
2. Profile both into per-treatment medians and score mechanism-of-action
   accuracy by leave-one-compound-out nearest neighbour (NSC) and
   not-same-compound-or-batch (NSCB).
3. Record wall time and peak RAM for each tool on the same hardware.
4. One table (spacr.tabular) with a control row (shuffled MoA labels) and a
   glossary; register in ALPHA_FEATURES under 753.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The CellProfiler arm reproduces the published BBBC021 NSC accuracy within a
few points; both arms report runtime on the same machine.
