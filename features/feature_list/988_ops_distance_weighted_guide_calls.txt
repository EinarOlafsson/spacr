PROBLEM
In PERISCOPE CP186A Well1, 35.6 % of cells with an exact read hold two or
more barcodes, and in 60 % of the clear-majority two-guide cells the
runner-up is the top guide of a touching cell (null 0.32 %). In Funk A1 the
same holds for 48.8 % (null 0.07 %). Such cells are blocked or weakened
although the second guide is bleed from the neighbour.

BUILD
- Spacer-prefix decode: ops_prefix_quality_method 'weighted_majority'.
  A read weighs q x d: q = min(1, quality) + 0.05 (ops_prefix_weighted_quality,
  else 1), d = max(exp(-(g - g0) / L), d_min) (ops_prefix_distance_weight
  'decay' / 'distance_only', ops_prefix_distance_decay_px = L,
  _interior_px = g0, _min_weight = d_min). g = distance to the nucleus of the
  read's own cell (0 inside); when the nearest nucleus is another cell's, the
  distance to the cell centroid minus the site's median nucleus radius,
  flagged in nuc_gap_fallback (spacr.ops_prefix._read_nucleus_gaps; nuclei from
  ops_prefix_nuclei_masks or the merged/ file). N1: a barcode with strictly
  fewer reads than the cell's top barcode that is a top barcode (by reads, ties
  kept) of a 4-connected touching cell has its weight multiplied by
  ops_read_neighbour_discount (b). The top barcode is called when it holds
  >= ops_prefix_weighted_min_share (0.6) of the weight alone. Decoy-top cells
  stay decoy calls. Not combinable with 967 (alternative rule family).
- Tuner: under 'weighted_majority' the per-well decoy tuner gains the axes
  ops_prefix_distance_decay_px (0 = off), ops_read_neighbour_discount
  (1 = off) and ops_prefix_weighted_quality; off is always in the grid and
  ends each axis, so ties keep the weight off. The empty-grid default
  (PREFIX_TUNING_GRID_WEIGHTED) adds read thresholds 20 and 30 and
  L in {4, 8, 16, 32, off}, b in {0, 0.25, 0.5, 1}, quality {off, on}.
  The default grid of the other methods is unchanged.
- Outputs (only when on): read table nuc_gap_px and nuc_gap_fallback; cell
  table weight_top, weight_total, d_weight_top, g_median_top,
  bleed_discounted; ops_keep_multi_guide_cells writes
  guide_multiplicity_<plate>_<well>.csv (0/1/2/3/>=4 guides under two
  counting rules, cells with a decoy barcode, the share of two-guide cells
  explained by neighbour bleed) and multi_guide_cells_<plate>_<well>.csv
  (>= 2 guides of ops_multi_guide_min_reads reads each, none flagged as
  bleed).
- OPS barcode decode (Funk): spacr.ops_sbs.vote_mapped_reads takes gaps,
  distance_weight, decay_px, interior_px, min_weight, neighbours and
  neighbour_discount; decode_rule carries read_weight only when
  ops_read_distance_weight is on or ops_read_neighbour_discount < 1 (needs the
  decode rules, e.g. ops_decode_preset 'rule_a_s2'). g = max(0, disc gap);
  neighbours = nuclei whose equal-area discs come within ops_footprint
  (spacr.ops_sbs._disc_neighbours, from the field's objects). With the
  distance term the single-read floor is applied to quality x d.
