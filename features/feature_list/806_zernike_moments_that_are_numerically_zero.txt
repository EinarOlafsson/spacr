Lifecycle: new

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE STATE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

nucleus_zernike_1 and cell_zernike_1 are about 1e-15 in almost every cell,
with rare spikes of about 3e-3. After DMSO-based scaling, single wells reach
about 1e10, and these two columns carry about 96% of each well's squared
norm. 1-NN cosine and unscaled logistic regression fall to chance: BBBC021
NSCB (not-same-compound-or-batch) accuracy went from 64.7% to 6.9%.

The moments come from spacr.measure._calculate_zernike (mahotas on CPU,
_torch_zernike on GPU).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Set moments whose magnitude is below a tolerance to exactly 0, or drop the
  always-zero *_zernike_1 and the constant *_zernike_0 features from the
  measure output. The CPU and GPU paths must give the same columns.
- Test: a disk-shaped and an ellipse mask give zernike_1 == 0 exactly (or no
  such column), on both paths.
