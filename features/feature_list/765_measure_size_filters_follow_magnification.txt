--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE STATE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

cell_min_size (8000 px) and nucleus_min_size (2000 px) are fixed pixel
counts for 40-60x (spacr/settings.py get_measure_crop_settings); Mask's
diameters follow magnification but these do not. At 20x they remove nearly
every BBBC021 cell, so the driver set 177 px by hand.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A default that silently removes every object is worse than no filter.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Derive the defaults from magnification (or pixel size), as Mask's
   diameters are, or express object_filters area rows in um^2.
2. Warn when a filter removes most objects of a field.
3. Register any new setting in ALPHA_FEATURES under 765.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

With magnification=20 and no size settings, BBBC021 keeps the cells
CellProfiler keeps (median area ~2,800 px).
