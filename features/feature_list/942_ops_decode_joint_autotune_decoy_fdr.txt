OPS DECODE AUTO-TUNING OF SPOT DETECTION AND CALLING TOGETHER, AND A DECOY FDR FOR THE SPACER-PREFIX DECODE
==========================================================================================================

Lifecycle: future
Status: IMPLEMENTED 2026-11-14 (alpha, under ALPHA_FEATURES[942]; ops_decode_threshold_mode stays 'manual' and ops_prefix_threshold_tuning stays 'none' by default, and ops_prefix_decoy_k 0 writes byte-identical prefix_decode outputs). One shared tuner, spacr.ops_decode.tune_thresholds, is used by the OPS decode's auto mode (item 939's ops_decode_threshold_mode 'auto', whose follow-up folds in here) and by the spacer-prefix decode (ops_prefix_threshold_tuning 'per_well_decoy'). It searches spot detection (the read threshold, ops_read_threshold) and calling thresholds jointly over a grid, on a seeded random half of each well's fields or sites, from decoy calls only (no reference call set is read), and reports on the other half. Objectives: 'recall_at_fdr' (default: most cells genotyped with the decoy FDR bound at or below the target; if unreachable, ops_decode_auto_unreachable 'lowest_fdr' uses the setting with the lowest FDR reached and reports it, auto_met False) and 'f_score' (F-beta of precision = 1 - FDR and recall = genotyped share). Each well gets its genotyped-vs-FDR curve (every grid setting, the front and the chosen point) as CSV and PNG (spacr.ops_decode.write_tuning_curve).

PREFIX DECODE (spacr.ops_prefix)
prefix_decoy_library adds spacr.ops_sbs.decoy_library(keys, k, seed) decoys
(collision prefixes in the exclusion set) to the matcher as callable keys of
gene DECOY; a read as close to a decoy as to a guide is ambiguous. A cell whose
top barcode is a decoy gets decoy True and no guide, gene or level, and is never
in cells_per_guide/gene. site_summary.csv gains cells_in_labels, cells_real,
cells_decoy and fdr_pct; fdr_<plate>_<well_id>.json has the fdr_report layout.
Tuning finds each site's spots once down to the lowest read threshold
(_spot_candidates; identical to detect_spots at any higher threshold), then
per read threshold compensates, calls and matches the reads (each distinct read
matched once per run), and counts real and decoy cells at every read-quality
floor and mismatch limit exactly as prefix_cell_calls calls them. Outputs per
well: threshold_tuning_<plate>_<well_id>.csv / .png / .json; reads and cells
are written at the chosen setting.

OPS DECODE (spacr.ops_decode, spacr.ops_engine)
With a spot grid each field's evidence is kept down to the lowest read
threshold and _joint_field_candidates votes the reads again at every read
threshold (library calibration refits; pixel votes made once); the well's
setting is chosen by tune_thresholds over (read threshold, single-read
quality, pixel quality) and the fields' calls are rebuilt at it. Outputs:
decode_thresholds_<well>.json and the plate table (now with read_threshold
and objective), decode_tuning_curve_<well>.csv / .png.
