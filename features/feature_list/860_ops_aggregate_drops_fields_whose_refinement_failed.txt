OPS AGGREGATE DROPS FIELDS WHOSE REFINEMENT FAILED
==================================================

Lifecycle: future
Status: DONE 2026-11-08 (alpha, under ALPHA_FEATURES[752]). — ops_drop_failed_fields (default False): the merge phase writes ops_fieldcheck_<well>.parquet with each field's link rate against chance (sequencing-nucleus density under the field's footprint times pi * threshold^2); the aggregate phase leaves out a field whose transform re-solve failed and which links under 3x chance, or any field under 2x chance, and writes dropped_fields_<plate>.csv with each field's reason. WHAT THE STATE IS
On plate A (B1, field 589) a field whose transform refinement failed
(refined == 0) still reaches the aggregate phase, and its cells link at
the rate expected by chance. Their genotype calls are noise.

WHAT TO DO
- In the OPS aggregate phase, drop fields whose refinement failed and
  whose link rate is not above the local chance rate.
- Report how many fields and cells were dropped and why.
- Tests: a field with refined == 0 and a chance-level link rate is
  dropped; a refined field is kept.

RULE
The paper session builds this under the identical-output rule: with the new
option off, outputs stay identical to today's.
