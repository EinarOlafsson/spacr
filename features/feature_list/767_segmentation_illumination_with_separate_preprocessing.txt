preprocessing and masks run in one call (item 339 documents this), so with
the prep/segment split of item 766 segmentation is uncorrected.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Splitting the stages for throughput should not change the masks.

gives masks identical to one combined call with illumination on.
