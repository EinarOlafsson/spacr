BUILD
- spacr.ops_feature_info.screen(old, new, cells, ...): Gaussian conditional
  mutual information I(gene; new | old) per new column and per declared
  family, in bits, from one streamed covariance of [old, new] (float64) and
  gene group sums (scatter-add); conditional covariances by Schur
  complements; ridge (relative, recorded); unique fraction = 1 - R2.
- Null: gene labels permuted within well (default gene-by-field units, or
  single cells), only the group sums recomputed; observed minus null mean,
  null 2.5-97.5 % range, interval +- 1.96 null SD.
- Held-out estimate: gene means from the other wells (fields in a one-well
  table). Guide-replicated estimate: each cell predicted by the other guides
  of its gene, against the same predictors given to guides of other genes
  with as many cells behind them (size-matched bins of 20).
- Greedy forward selection over families (or columns), stopping at a user
  threshold or the null level; optional MLP cross-entropy check (nats).
  KSG kNN estimator not built.
- Phase ops_feature_screen (run_ops, on its own): feature_information.csv,
  feature_information.png (bars + jittered permutation points),
  feature_information.json. Same maths on numpy (CPU) and torch (GPU).
- 15 alpha settings ops_feature_screen_* in OPS phenotype (proposed 969
  subcategory "Feature information screen"); no schema bump.
