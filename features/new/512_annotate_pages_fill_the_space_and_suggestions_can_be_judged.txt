WHAT TO BUILD
  1. THE PAGE IS WHAT FITS. The grid holds exactly as many crops as the space
     allows and never scrolls; opening the console, collapsing the settings or
     resizing the window recomputes the page and pushes the rest to the next
     one. The page counter follows.
  2. JUDGING A SUGGESTION IS ITS OWN ACTION. When Suggest has labelled a page,
     each suggested crop is marked as suggested, and the user confirms or
     rejects it with one key or click, with the instruction on screen and
     visible feedback for what was confirmed, rejected and left. What the user
     confirmed and what they rejected both reach the next round of training --
     a rejection is information, not silence -- and the round's counts are
     shown.

DONE WHEN
No page of Annotate scrolls at any window size or console state, and a user
can accept or reject every suggestion on a page without editing labels by
hand, with the counts visible.
