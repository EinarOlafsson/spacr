601. Preferences: Theme and Animation are categories of Appearance

Requested by the maintainer (2026-09-30), verbatim:
"in preferences theme and animation can be categories in appearence instead of their own tabs.

the rim arround the window perifery looks like it is made up of many small boxes. please make it a continuous gradient with no boxes (e.g. by removing the lines between the boxes.)"

(The second paragraph is item 602.)

Brief:
In the Preferences dialog, remove the separate Theme and Animation tabs.
Their settings become categories (collapsible sections, styled like the
other settings categories) inside the Appearance tab. Keep every setting
and its behaviour; only the layout changes. Update the Qt tests that
assert the tab list.

Done when: the tabs are General, Appearance, Performance, Modules,
Figures, Logging, AI (plus Fractal and Sound in spaceout mode); Appearance
holds folded THEME and ANIMATION categories with every control they had;
the Help search still lands on a Theme or Animation row (Qt tests).

Status: IMPLEMENTED 2026-09-30

What was built: Theme and Animation are built as settings categories (the
same Section widget the module screens use, folded by default) at the
bottom of the Appearance tab, under its own rows. Every row and its wiring
is unchanged. Each category's rows sit in a holder that keeps the old page
name (PreferencesTabTheme, PreferencesTabAnimation), so the Help search
index and its hand-off keep their names; opening Preferences on one of
them now selects Appearance and unfolds the category before marking the
row. tools/build_help_search_index.py lists a row under its innermost
named page only, and spacr/qt/help_api_index.py's preference rows were
regenerated (tab title now Appearance). Tests: tests/qt/test_preferences_tabs.py.

Left: tutorials that show the old Theme and Animation tabs need
re-recording (docs lane).
