WHAT WAS DONE — round 7
- Original is the top of the image-quality slider. The Original buttons (enlarged image, every
  well view, every stacked image, All images) are gone; the slider (and the Preview resolution box)
  runs 128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048, 4096, Original, and at Original the plate thumbnails, every well
  view's tiles, every stacked image and the enlarged image read the source pixels (PSF and
  restoration still apply to the enlarged and stacked images only, as before). The PDF export keeps
  its own quality setting. overview_preview_resolution takes 0 for Original; the
  overview_original_on_open setting is retired, and an old file holding it true is folded into
  overview_preview_resolution 0 (spacr.settings._fold_overview_original_on_open, run by the Qt
  loader, the CLI loader and get_overview_default_settings; spacr-doctor names the fold).
- The slider is on the Plate header as well as the Image header; both move the same setting.
  Moving it restarts a 200 ms timer, and only the resting position is applied: the plate
  thumbnails, the well tiles, every stacked image and the enlarged image decode again on worker
  threads at once (no new image or event needed), each keeping the zoom and centre the user chose.
  Before, the stacked images kept their old pixels and the enlarged image stayed at its old edge.
- The All images bar (Original and metadata for every stacked image) is removed; each stacked
  image keeps its own metadata toggle, the enlarged image keeps its own.
- Zoom: wheel and touchpad pinch zoom only the image under the pointer. The stacked images used to
  copy each other's zoom and scroll on every move (_sync_image_views, a deliberate comparison
  feature); that is now the optional "Link zoom" tick in a stacked image's right-click menu, off
  by default.
- Tooltips: Qt draws a plain-text tooltip on one line however long it is. The application tooltip
  policy now shows any plain tooltip longer than 60 characters as wrapped rich text
  (tooltip_policy._wrapped_tooltip), so it wraps at a quarter of the screen in the rounded spaCR
  QToolTip style like every API tooltip. This covers widget tooltips, menu-action tooltips and
  table/list/header cell tooltips (the replayed path) across spacr/qt, and the one direct
  QToolTip.showText call (Organize for Measure's remove mark). Navigation buttons still carry no
  tooltip (tooltip_policy rule). No Overview tooltip only repeated its label, so none was removed.
- Tests: tests/qt/test_725_overview_round7_quality_zoom.py (8: buttons gone and slider top is
  Original; quality change redraws open plate, well, stacked and enlarged views; slider drag
  decodes once; zooming A leaves B; pinch zooms one; Link zoom tick off by default and linking;
  no Link zoom on the enlarged image; the setting fold); three tooltip checks in
  tests/qt/test_tooltips_wait_two_seconds.py (a widget, a menu action and a table cell must show
  wrapped rich text in a word-wrapped label at most a quarter screen wide, multi-line, with the

WHAT WAS DONE — round 6, display, normalization and settings
- 1: overview_measure_saturation is gone. The Intensity switch (overview_measure_intensity)
  now measures the saturation group too (spacr.measure._IMAGE_FEATURE_SETTING maps
  'saturation' to it); its tooltip says so. Old settings files that still name the key
  are ignored for it.
- 2, 3: overview_color_mode offers RGB on black ('additive', the unchanged default),
  RGB on white ('rgb_white'), CMY on black ('cmy_black'), CMY on white ('cmy'), CMYK on
  black ('cmyk_black'), CMYK on white ('cmyk'), Green and magenta on black
  ('green_magenta', red-green colour-blind safe), Each channel in grey, side by side
  ('greys') and Custom colours ('custom'). _overview_color_plan gives each channel a
  colour and a background; _overview_composite adds light on black
  (out = sum(plane * strength * colour)) and absorbs the complement on white
  (out = 255 - sum(plane * strength * (1 - colour))), clipped. On black the CMYK key
  channel is drawn white. One channel in the RGB modes stays grey (inverted on white).
  The ink intensity applies to the CMY and CMYK modes on either background. Custom
  colours: new setting overview_custom_colors ('1=#ff0000, 2=#00ff00'), edited with one
  colour button per channel under Channels (spaCR's pick_colour), greyed out unless the
  mode is Custom colours; unset channels take a default colour by position; any number
  of channels is blended. Mosaic layout (more than three channels in the RGB modes, or
  Greys) is decided by the colour plan everywhere, the pixel readout included.
- 4: the Display category is gone. overview_tile_size and overview_tile_spacing stay
  settings (spacr.settings.OVERVIEW_WELL_VIEW_KEYS, filed under Image but kept off the
  form); every well view's header has an "Overview tile spacing" field (0-64 px) just
  left of the size slider, written back to the setting.
- 5: Normalized channels (overview_normalize_channels) is a text field again: 'all',
  'none' or channel numbers such as 1,2,3,4. _overview_channel_list_error checks it as it
  is typed against the source's channels; a wrong list is framed in the error colour, the
  reason shows on the Apply bar and the status line, Apply is disabled, and the stretch
  keeps its previous channels.
- 6: rules in spacr.settings.get_setting_dependencies (_OVERVIEW_NORMALIZATION_RULES)
  grey out, live, with the reason in the help: Low and High when Overview normalize is
  off; Normalized channels when normalize is off or the advanced normalization is on;
  method, background and gamma while the advanced normalization is off; advanced low and
  high except for percentile, log, asinh, ratio and difference; fixed low and high except
  for manual; the reference channel except for ratio and difference; epsilon except for
  ratio.
- 7: Normalization holds Overview normalize, Overview percentile low and high only. The
  new Advanced normalization category (under Image) holds Normalized channels, the
  per-channel Normalization scope, the advanced normalization switch, method, bounds,
  background, gamma, reference channel and epsilon, plus the Reference group and Channel
  to edit rows.
- 8: an Apply bar is pinned to the bottom of the Overview settings column (over the
  scroll area's bottom margin). Display and normalization settings wait for it
  (_OVERVIEW_STAGED_SETTINGS plus every enhance_* setting); while some wait the bar says
  "Unapplied changes" and the button reads "Apply changes" in the accent colour. Apply
  pushes them to the browser and _FieldOverview._apply_display clears the preview and
  thumbnail caches and redraws the plate thumbnails, every well view, every open image
