TOOLTIPS WAIT TWO SECONDS, LINGER ONE, AND CAN BE SWITCHED OFF

Status: OPEN, filed 2026-09-24. Not started.
Owner: unassigned.

REQUEST (the maintainer, 2026-09-24, verbatim)
    "the tooltipps pop up to fast have them uniformelly pop up after 2 seconds
    and disappear if the mouse leavs them after 1 second. ig the mouse stays on
    them they do not dissapear. and there should be a setting in preferences
    that toggles all tooltips off, if there is not already. tooltips should be
    on bu default."

WHAT TO BUILD
  * One rule for every tooltip in spaCR: it appears after 2 s of hovering,
    stays while the pointer is on it or on its widget, and fades 1 s after the
    pointer leaves. Set centrally (Qt's tooltip timings, or one event filter
    installed once) rather than per widget, so a tooltip added later obeys it
    without anyone remembering.
  * A Preferences switch that turns every tooltip off, on by default.

DONE WHEN
A tooltip anywhere in spaCR appears at 2 s and not before, lingers as
described, and the preference silences all of them.
