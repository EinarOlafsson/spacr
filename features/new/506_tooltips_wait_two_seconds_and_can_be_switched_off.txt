TOOLTIPS WAIT TWO SECONDS, LINGER ONE, AND CAN BE SWITCHED OFF

Status: DONE 2026-09-25 — Filed 2026-09-24.
Owner: Claude.

REQUEST (the maintainer, 2026-09-24, verbatim)
    "the tooltipps pop up to fast have them uniformelly pop up after 2 seconds
    and disappear if the mouse leavs them after 1 second. ig the mouse stays on
    them they do not dissapear. and there should be a setting in preferences
    that toggles all tooltips off, if there is not already. tooltips should be
    on bu default."

WHAT TO BUILD
  * One rule for every tooltip in spaCR: it appears after 2 s of hovering,
    stays while the pointer is on it or on its widget, and fades 1 s after the
    pointer leaves. Set centrally (Qt's tooltip timings, or one event filter
    installed once) rather than per widget, so a tooltip added later obeys it
    without anyone remembering.
  * A Preferences switch that turns every tooltip off, on by default.

DONE WHEN
A tooltip anywhere in spaCR appears at 2 s and not before, lingers as
described, and the preference silences all of them.

DONE, 2026-09-25
`spacr/qt/tooltip_policy.py` is the one rule, an event filter installed on
`QApplication` by `apply_preferences_to_app`, so a widget written tomorrow
obeys it without anyone remembering. A tooltip appears two seconds after the
pointer rests -- the style's own wake-up delay is SUBTRACTED, so the total is
two seconds and not 2.7 -- stays while the pointer is on the widget or on the
tooltip itself, and goes one second after it leaves both.

Two things the filter had to keep working, and does. Qt propagates a tooltip
event to the parent when the widget under the pointer has none, which is how
a card explains itself while the pointer is on the label written on it: the
text is resolved up the parent chain by `tooltip_text_for`. And a table, a
header or a list answers from `Qt::ToolTipRole` inside its own `event`, which
no filter can read: when nothing in the chain has a tooltip, the event is
SENT AGAIN after the wait, so those appear too, on the same rule.

The switch is `Preferences > Appearance > Tooltips`, `TooltipsEnabled`, ON by
default (`get_tooltips_enabled` / `set_tooltips_enabled`). Cleared, the event
is swallowed and nothing appears anywhere; setting it takes down whatever is
on screen at that moment rather than letting one last popup through. The two
older switches -- the settings tooltip BOX and the bottom STRIP -- are
untouched and independent: they answer "what is this setting", this one
answers "do small labels pop up at all".

A widget with a reason can keep Qt's own instant tooltip by setting the
`spacrNoTooltipPolicy` property; nothing in spaCR does.

Tests: `tests/qt/test_tooltips_wait_two_seconds.py`, 17 cases, including the
default, both numbers, the parent chain, the replay, the linger, the switch
and the Preferences row. `tests/qt/test_tooltips.py`,
`test_category_tooltips.py`, `test_setting_tooltip_footer.py`,
`test_the_bottom_tooltip_survives_the_pointer_leaving.py`,
`test_tooltip_no_duplicate.py`, `test_the_tooltip_is_on_the_title_only.py`
-- 357 cases -- still pass, as do the Preferences dialog's own.

NOT CHECKED IN A SCREENSHOT: the timings are read from the code and the
filter's decisions, because a two-second wait per case would cost the suite
a minute to measure a number this file names in one place.
