WHAT TO BUILD
1. Measure's "QC from masks" panel (the segmentation-QC banner) leaves the
   actions container. It is opt-in behind a "QC" button left of the 3D and
   Time buttons, opening a popup in the app's glass/rounded card style.
   Run/Stop and the other actions sit where they did before.
2. Cloud settings get their own alpha sub-category "Cloud α" under Inputs,
   in both Measure and Mask generation.
3. Preview and diagnostics settings move into the Input/Experiment category
   directly, not as a sub-category.
4. Measure gets an "Image Preprocessing" category with alpha sub-categories:
   Bleach Correction α (also in Mask generation if it applies there),
   Spectral Unmixing α, Image Deconvolution α (PSF), Illumination
   Correction α, Image Enhancement α and Plate Barcode Linkage α, named as in
   item 591.
5. "Measurement Features" becomes "Features", with confluency, cell cycle,
   wound closure, viability, CellProfiler and GPU measurement as
   sub-categories (alpha ones marked α).
6. A new "Postprocessing" category holds Runtime & Reliability and
   Profiling.
7. Time-to-event settings are shown or enabled only when timelapse is on,
   through the existing greying/dependency rule mechanism.

2026-09-28: what was built.
  1. The segmentation-QC banner (spacr.qt.prerun.SegQCBanner) no longer
     sits above the Run row. install_qc_banner puts it in a SegQCDialog (a
     QDialog, so it wears the glass card: translucent, rounded, rimmed) and
     adds a "QC" button to the action row in front of the 3D and Time
     switches (or Live / AI when those are absent). The verdict is still
     read in the background as before (cheap, threaded); it is shown only
     when QC is pressed. Run/Stop keep their position:
     tests/qt/test_593_measure_regrouped_and_qc_popup.py compares the Run
     button's and the action row's geometry with and without the QC.
  2. "Cloud α" is an alpha sub-category of Measure's "Input & Experiment"
     and of Mask generation's "Input & Metadata"
     (settings_model._APP_CATEGORY_PARENTS).
  3. plot, test_mode and test_nr are rows of "Input & Experiment"; the
     "Preview & Diagnostics" heading is gone.
  4. Measure's "Image Preprocessing" is an umbrella of alpha sub-categories:
     Bleach Correction α, Spectral Unmixing α, Image Deconvolution α (PSF),
     Illumination Correction α, Image Enhancement α (empty on Measure: it has
     no enhancement settings, so it is not drawn) and Plate Barcode Linkage
     α. The illumination and PSF settings are alpha on Measure only
     (ALPHA_FEATURES[593] 'module_settings'); the Illumination Correction
     module keeps them. An umbrella whose every sub-heading the alpha gate
     hides hides with them (AppScreen._every_heading_below_is_hidden).
     Bleach correction is not added to Mask generation: it rescales
     measured intensities over time and Mask generation has no such
     setting, so it does not apply there.
  5. "Measurement Features" is "Features", with Confluency α, Cell Cycle α,
     Wound Closure α, Viability α, CellProfiler α and GPU Measurement α
     nested in it.
  6. "Postprocessing" is an umbrella over Runtime & Reliability and
     Profiling α.
  7. Every time_to_event setting is greyed, with a reason, unless timelapse
     is on (spacr.settings.get_setting_dependencies, the rule bleach
     correction already uses).
  Intensity Calibration α and Time To Event α stay top-level (not named in
  the request).
