RANKED SUMMARY

  * BEST IN-DISTRIBUTION: the in-house PV models. All three are 0.07 to
    0.20 F1 above every general model on both datasets and at both IoUs.
    r2 leads on the ten test fields (0.817). r5, the promoted model, leads
    once both datasets are pooled (0.800). r6 has the highest precision
    (0.85) and the lowest recall (0.72 to 0.74), so it is the one to use
    when false vacuoles cost more than missed ones. On the five fields that
    round 6 never trained on (its validation fields), the order is the
    same: r2 0.853, r5 0.836, r6 0.826 at IoU 0.5, against 0.800 for the
    best general model there (cyto2) and 0.718 for ViT-B.
  * BEST GENERAL: Cellpose-DINO ViT-B, but only just. It is first on the
    ten test fields (0.749) and on both datasets pooled (0.675). cpsam,
    spaCR's default, is within 0.01 on the test fields. ViT-B is not first
    everywhere: on the training sample, cyto3 (0.626) and cyto2 (0.598) beat
    it (0.593). It finds 17 to 26 false vacuoles on each curated negative
    field (cpsam finds 17 to 23). Its precision is 0.58 and its recall 0.81,
    so it over-calls. No general model is good enough for PV counting
    without curation.
  * Cellpose-DINO ViT-L is NOT better than ViT-B. On the ten test fields it
    edges ViT-B at IoU 0.5 (GPU 0.754 against 0.749; CPU 0.755 against
    0.747), but its outlines are much looser: F1@.75 0.415 against 0.581,
    and mean matched IoU 0.760 against 0.826. On the training sample it
    falls to 0.497 / 0.222, below every general model except OC1-66 and
    DINOCell. Two things drive that: 47 objects on A03_6_1's 11 vacuoles
    and 41 on the two negative fields, and ME49 M02_009 at 0.220 against
    ViT-B's 0.486. Pooled over both datasets it is 0.633 against ViT-B's
    0.675. It costs about the same on a GPU (2.6 s against 2.5 s per field)
    and 3.4 times as much on a CPU (178 s per field at 8 threads). ViT-B
    stays the best general model, and ViT-L did not earn a rerun of the
    variants.
  * BEST SPEED/ACCURACY: on a GPU, every Cellpose-family model takes 2 to 4
    s per field, so the choice is accuracy only: use r5 for Toxo PVs and
    ViT-B or cpsam for anything else. On a CPU, ViT-B is the clear pick. It
    has the best general F1 at about 3.5 times cpsam's speed (52 s against
    183 s median on the loaded machine), and its F1@.75 is 0.58 against
    cpsam's 0.55. cyto2 is cheaper again (36 s) with a similar F1@.5, but
    its outlines are loose (F1@.75 0.48). OC1-66 is the fastest (8 s) and
    too inaccurate to use for PVs. DINOCell is both the slowest and the
    worst.

What each one does:
  * Background + CLAHE HURTS. It removes a sixth of the true vacuoles (recall
    -0.14) and no field improved. The same direction as item 508's cpsam
    measurement (-0.036).
  * Percentile clip + Gaussian is NEUTRAL at IoU 0.5 and LOOSENS OUTLINES:
    F1@.75 falls by 0.05 and mean matched IoU by 0.02. Item 508's +0.012 on
    cpsam is the same "no real effect".
  * The logarithm is NEUTRAL at IoU 0.5 and slightly worse at IoU 0.75.
  * TV denoise TRADES recall for precision (258 fewer objects) and SHRINKS
    what it keeps: mean IoU -0.05 and F1@.75 -0.09. The F1@.5 tie hides a
    worse segmentation. It is the slowest step, adding about 6 s per field.
  * PSF deconvolution does NOTHING measurable. Item 509's inference gives
    these untagged 20x fields a 0.325 um pixel and a 0.354 um FWHM, which is
    a 5 x 5 kernel about one pixel wide. Twenty Richardson-Lucy iterations
    at that width hardly change the image. It costs about 5 s per field.
  * Flow threshold: 0.4 (the default) is right. 1.0 and "off" add 134 and
    240 false objects and lose F1 on every field.
  * Diameter: setting it matters a little. Blank (Cellpose's native scale)
    costs 0.014 and loses on 9 of 10 fields. A single dataset-wide median
    does as well as a perfect per-field value (+0.009, 5 fields better and
    5 worse), so the user's usual "one diameter per plate" loses nothing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WAS RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Data (spaCR's example-data cache; nothing modified):
  * toxo_pv: the ten Make Masks test fields of item 412 with
    ground_truth_masks/. 910 vacuoles. The per-field median diameter runs
    from 22.8 to 45.9 px, and the dataset median is 33.7 px.
  * training_sample: item 450's PV training-dataset samples
    (mask_datasets/toxoplasma_pv_random2 and /toxoplasma_pv). These are the
    fields with objects (seven from random2 and A03_6_1 from the first draw),
    plus the two curated negatives of random2. plate5_G12_7 is left out
    because it is also in toxo_pv. The set has 701 vacuoles and includes
    three literature figures (692 to 1,200 px), two ME49 fields, two in-house
    plates and the 4h MEF field. The first draw's other nine fields are empty
    masks and were not used.
  * Not used: toxoplasma_from_cellmask. Its task is to find PVs from a
    CellMask stain, which is a different problem from segmenting the PV
    channel. The plaque and figure samples are also a different object.

Path. Each field is staged as its own one-field cellvoyager plate (plate1,
unique well, C0). spacr.core.preprocess_generate_masks runs on it with
masks=False, which does the metadata, merge, enhancement chain / PSF and
normalization exactly as a Mask run does, and leaves masks/*.npz.
spacr.object.generate_cellpose_masks_sam(masks_dir, settings, 'cell',
batch_paths=[that npz], run_qc=False) then segments it. That is the function
Mask calls, with the model setting written the way "Use this model" writes it:
'cpsam', 'cpsam_v2', 'cellpose3:cyto|cyto2|cyto3|nuclei',
'cellpose3:<oc1_project_66_cellpose.pth>', 'cellpose_dino:<checkpoint>', the
PV checkpoint path, or segmentation_backend='dinocell'. The saved
cell_mask_stack/*.npy is what gets scored. Mask defaults are used
throughout, with these settings: cell_channel 0 (the vacuole as "cell"),
channels [0], magnification 20, cellpose3_add_nucleus_channel False (there
is no nucleus channel), seg_qc off, batch_size 1, and cell_diameter = the
field's ground-truth median (or blank / the dataset median in the diameter
variants). Each field is its own plate so that the batch normalization cannot
mix fields of different sizes. Plate-wide normalization over mixed fields was
therefore NOT measured.

Scoring. spacr.scorecard.match_objects (optimal one-to-one assignment) at
IoU 0.5 and 0.75. Pooled rates come from summed TP/FP/FN, so each vacuole
counts once. "Mean IoU" averages all matched pairs at 0.5. Detections on a
negative field count as false positives.

Timing. eval = the outermost CellposeModel.eval / _RemoteBackend.eval call
for the field, measured by wrapping both classes in the driver; spaCR is not
patched. run = the whole generate call for the field (model load or worker
start, filtering, saving). prep = preprocess_generate_masks for the field.
The GPU pass ran through tools/gpu_turn.sh (label bench-segmentation-532,
04:23 to 05:18), the only GPU user at the time, with the CPU pass running
beside it.

Models. The in-house checkpoints come from
/mnt/wd4tb/af3/projects/toxoplasma_pv_model, and each is accepted only when
its sha256 matches the zoo row (toxoplasma_pv_v1/v2/v3: 182d8cf6..., 17c689e3...,
146ef269...). The bioimage.io checkpoints are verified against their zoo
rows: ViT-B 3ed4c06a... (cached by item 525), OC1-66 4deb1ac3... (fetched by
model_zoo.fetch) and ViT-L fad0dda2... (1.21 GB, fetched by model_zoo.fetch in
27 min). Backends are the installed ones: cellpose3
(3.1.1.3), cellpose_dino (4.2.1.1 + dinov3) and dinocell (0.74).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CAVEATS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * The in-house models were TRAINED ON THIS DATA. Round 6's split.csv lists
    15 of the 20 fields as round-6 training fields and 5 as validation
    fields. r5 trained on round 5's 556 curated fields, which include these,
    and item 412's README calls the ten test fields the vacuole model's
    training data. r2's 229-image set is not itemised here. Their numbers
    are in-distribution and are not a claim about new data.
    The five r6-validation fields are the fairest in-house comparison here,
    and they keep the same order. The eleven anchor wells, held out since
    round 1, are the right test and are not in either sample.
  * Twenty fields is a small sample. Differences under about 0.02 F1 (ViT-B
    against cpsam, most of the chains) are within field-to-field noise:
    per-field wins and losses split evenly. The bigger gaps are not noise:
    in-house against general, the Cellpose-3 cyto models at IoU 0.75,
    DINOCell, and bg+CLAHE / flow / blank diameter.
  * The general models miss whole fields. cyto and cyto2 find 0 to 2 of
    plate3_I10_4's 18 vacuoles and 1 to 3 of plate2_E08_13's 23, where
    ViT-B, cpsam and the PV models find most of them. The CPU times are
    from a shared machine and are not a spec.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RECOMMENDATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. For Toxoplasma PVs, recommend the in-house PV model and not a general
     one. Keep v2 (r5) as the promoted choice: it is best on both datasets
     pooled, and r2's lead on the ten test fields (+0.010) is within noise.
     Offer v3 (r6) where precision matters. No change to the zoo is needed.
  2. Leave cpsam as the default general model. ViT-B's lead is +0.009 and
     it is not first on the training sample. Name ViT-B in the Mask and
     Model-zoo help as the fast general choice ON A CPU: about 3.5 times
     faster than cpsam, with the same or better accuracy on these fields.
     Needs the cellpose_dino backend.
  3. Keep the enhancement chain OFF by default and say in its help that it
     did not improve segmentation here. bg+CLAHE lost 0.05 F1 on both cpsam
     (508) and ViT-B. TV denoise shrinks objects. Clip+Gaussian and log do
     nothing. Candidate tooltip line for enhance_background / enhance_clahe:
     "On Toxo PV fields this lowered F1 by about 0.05 (item 532)".
  4. PSF deconvolution with the inferred optics is a no-op on 20x fields.
     Keep it off by default. Worth using only with a measured PSF or with
     calibration from a high-NA objective.
  5. Keep flow_threshold 0.4, and say that 1.0 / 0 add false objects.
  6. Tell users to set a diameter. One plate-wide median is enough, and blank
     costs 0.01 to 0.02. The "Estimate diameters" helper (spacr.diameter)
     is the natural way to get one. Its accuracy against the ground-truth
     median was NOT measured here.
  7. Do not recommend DINOCell, OC1 Project 66 or Cellpose 3 nuclei for PVs.
     DINOCell over-splits (twice as many objects, F1@.75 0.13), ignores
     diameter and flow, and takes 43 s per field even on the GPU. The other
     two are nucleus models.
