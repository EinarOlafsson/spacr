598. The contribute dialog shows a working dataset link

Requested by the maintainer (2026-09-30), verbatim:
"it tells me to got tp huggingface.co/datasets/einarolafssoncommunity_toxoplasma_datasets but i cant find that link? also that text should be a link or at least copyable."

Brief:
Make Masks' "Contribute images and masks" dialog names its destination as
"Goes to huggingface.co/datasets/{repo} (made if it is new).", read as the
repo without the "/" between owner and name. Find why and fix it at the
cause. Make the destination a clickable https link that can also be
selected and copied, and do the same for the dataset and pull-request links
in Plaque Assay's contribute dialog and in the thank-you after sending.

Done when: the label text contains "einarolafsson/community_<name>", the
label is a link, and the same holds in Plaque Assay and after sending (Qt
tests).

Status: DONE 2026-09-30 (evidence b40aa4349)

Cause (2026-09-30): the text always held the slash. tr() formatting, every
language's catalog and community_repo() all give
"einarolafsson/community_toxoplasma_datasets". The word-wrapped label broke
the address right after "einarolafsson/" (measured with QTextLayout at the
dialog's 447 px width), leaving the slash at a line end, and the label could
not be clicked or selected, so the address had to be retyped. Also, a
community dataset is made by its first upload, so before that the address
finds nothing (einarolafsson/community_toxoplasma_datasets does not exist
yet on Hugging Face).

Fix: the destination is now a caption, "Goes to this dataset on Hugging
Face (a new one appears with its first upload):", followed by the https
address as a link on a line of its own that does not wrap. The label opens
the link in the browser and can be selected and copied. Plaque Assay's
contribute dialog gained the same destination link (objectName
ContributeTarget), and the thank-you in both dialogs puts the pull request's
link on its own line. Plain status messages are HTML-escaped. Tests:
tests/qt/test_598_contribute_dialog_dataset_link.py.

Left: the two new captions wait in _AWAITING_CATALOG_REBUILD for the next
catalog pass. Until then the report-only external-caption count check
xfails because the two old captions were removed.
