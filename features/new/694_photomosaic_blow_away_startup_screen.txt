================================================================================
PHOTOMOSAIC BLOW-AWAY STARTUP SCREEN
================================================================================

Status:    built 2026-10-12 (not alpha: it is THE startup screen); ships in
           1.5.1.5; translations current in all nine languages
Requested: maintainer, 2026-10-12: "When you start spaCR, the photomosaic
           picture appears, and then each picture in the tiled picture
           unravels and flips away, like the individual pictures are being
           blown away. Have it as the starting screen for spaCR."
Owner:     Home (build); WS (translations, tutorial captures)
Related:   the loading cover (spacr/qt/widgets/loading_screen.py), N655
           Accessibility > Reduce motion, the genAI talk's color_final mosaic.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE STATE WAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The window opened under a black loading cover with the white mark and the strap
line. Under the default (lazy) preload policy the cover was closed as soon as
the window was built; under the eager policy it stayed until the pipeline
modules were imported and was then hidden in one step.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WAS BUILT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Resource: spacr/resources/splash/photomosaic.jpg (1600 px, 80 x 80 tiles of
   20 px, ~0.5 MB) and photomosaic.json (grid, tile size, and which 4,004 of
   the 6,400 tiles are occupied), written by tools/build_splash_mosaic.py from
   the maintainer's full-colour mosaic color_final_v1.png (made by the genAI
   talk's mosaic.py color: yellow-green cell outline, blue nucleus, red
   parasites, single-cell microscopy tiles on black). The tool uses the
   generator's exact grid (G = 80, T = 41; 3280 px), resamples each tile on
   its own so tile edges stay on pixel rows, and marks a tile occupied when
   its mean brightness exceeds 2.5 x the background level (the median of the
   darkest 30 % of tiles): background 2.0, cell interior 6-8, logo 15-95, so
   the cut is flat (4,003-4,020 tiles for any threshold from 4 to 5). The
   faint background tiles do not fly; they fade with the front. Packaged
   through setup.py package_data and MANIFEST.in.
2. The loading cover shows the mosaic centred, with the strap line and its
   progress rule under it. The picture is decoded by the JPEG reader straight
   to a whole number of device pixels per tile (DPI-aware, up to 1600 device
   px), and its black is made transparent (brightest channel as alpha), so
   over black it is the same picture and over the window the tiles glow.
3. When the window is ready (the preload ends, or at once under the lazy
   policy) and at least 0.6 s after the mosaic first painted, the blow-away
   plays (spacr.qt.widgets.loading_screen.BlowAway): a wind front sweeps
   left to right with a slight downward slant over 1.1 s, ragged by row and
   jittered per tile (seed 694). As it reaches a tile, the tile lifts (scale
   1 -> 1.25), turns over like a card (one axis through cos of the flip
   angle, a skew of 0.22 sin, 14 % foreshortening), spins, flies downwind
   with an accelerating gust, turbulence across the wind and slight gravity,
   and fades over 0.5-0.85 s. Background tiles leave with the front. The
   cover's ground clears behind the front and fades ahead of it, so the live
   window and its field backdrop appear underneath. About 1.85 s in all.
   Motion is a pure function of elapsed time; frames are drawn by QPainter on
   the CPU from tiles cut once, with a single reused QTransform per tile, a
   16 ms precise timer parented to the cover, and an adaptive floor that drops
   the faintest tiles while frames are slow.
4. Any click or key skips to the end (the cover holds the keyboard focus
   while it plays and gives it back). A click during loading skips the
   animation when loading ends. Screen prewarm and Preferences warming wait
   while it plays (blow_away_running), so their slices do not take frames.
5. Off: Accessibility > Reduce motion; Preferences > Animation > Startup
   animation (new switch, on by default; Reduce motion overrides it); the
   offscreen platform; SPACR_NO_SPLASH=1, which also drops the mosaic and
   keeps the previous plain cover. tools/tutorials/run_native_capture.py sets
   SPACR_NO_SPLASH=1 so tutorial captures neither wait for nor show it;
   unset it there to record the splash.

Tests: tests/qt/test_694_photomosaic_blow_away_startup_screen.py (resource and
tile map against the picture; switches; still layout; motion is time-based,
ordered by the wind, lift/flip/spin/fade; hold, play, finish, skip by click and
key, focus returned, no leftover timers or widgets; prewarm and Preferences
warming wait; window integration under eager and lazy policies; the
Preferences switch saves and resets).

Measured (Home, 2026-10-12, colour mosaic): paint of one frame 5-7 ms mean
offscreen at 1600 x 1000 with up to ~3,200 tiles in flight. Real launch on a
private Xvfb (1920 x 1080, CPU only), two runs each: main window shown at
4.00 / 3.90 s with the splash and 3.85 / 3.81 s without (within run-to-run
noise; the mosaic overlaps loading and its JPEG is decoded at the cover's
first paint), blow-away at 54-56 fps mean, median frame interval 14 ms, cover
gone 2.75-2.85 s after the window was shown. Before prewarm and Preferences
warming were made to wait, the same run gave 10-19 fps.

New English strings for WS: "Startup animation" and its tooltip "When spaCR
starts, the logo photomosaic blows away tile by tile to reveal the window. Off
shows the mosaic without the animation. Reduce motion also turns it off. A
click or a key skips it."
