--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Left as is, the zoo shows the OLD detector at 0.993 and the new one at 0.946,
so a user comparing rows picks the worse model. The two numbers come from
different test sets; only one of them was ever measured against hard negatives.
The detector is also the front half of the plaque pipeline -- it supplies the
well crops and the well diameter that makes areas comparable across
microscopes -- so its false positives propagate into every downstream count.
