605. Spaceout: a slider for the magnifying glass's size

Requested by the maintainer (2026-09-30), verbatim:
"in spaceout mode the mandelbrot camera should smoothely center on regions with high complexity, i.e. not single color.
now the camera jumps which looks wierd. please make the camera movement smooth.
for the other spaceout modes theis a giant magnigying glass where the mouse is. there should be a slider deffining its size.
for the space mode there are small stars that look great passing like little dots. but there should also be galaxies passing i want the user to feel like they are really passing through space."

Brief (this item is the third sentence; 604 and 606 are the others):
Add a Preferences slider for the magnifier ("magnifying glass") size, with
the spaceout settings, with a sensible range. The default is the current
size. Every spaceout mode that has the magnifier honours it live. Persist
it and give it a tooltip.

Done when: the slider is in the Fractal page, saved and read back, reaches
a running backdrop without a rebuild, and the rendered lens visibly scales
(Qt tests and PNGs in /mnt/wd4tb/scratch/604).

Status: DONE 2026-09-30 (evidence ffd050854)

The "magnifying glass" is the 1/r^2 pointer warp in toward_pointer; its
size was fixed by the shader constants ("Pointer reach" only fades the pull
with the pointer's distance from the window centre, which is why the
earlier "size of the gravity ball" request did not change the ball).

Built: setting magnifier_size (key spaceout/fractal_magnifier_size,
default 1.0 = the old lens, range 0.25-3.0, clamped on read and write);
Preferences > Fractal "Magnifier size" QSlider (FractalMagnifierSize,
25-300 %, value label, tooltip ending "Default 100%.", greyed with the
Pointer toggle). It reaches running backdrops through apply_saved_controls
(RuntimeControls.magnifier_size) and is read every frame: uniform u_lens
in the orbit, orbit GPU, cascade and space shaders, and the CPU orbit
kernel's lens argument. Distances are measured in lens radii and the
displacement scaled back, so the whole lens scales without changing shape.

Not applicable: the CPU cascade and space renderers have no lens (the
pointer steers them), and the Mandelbrot is dragged.

Frames: /mnt/wd4tb/scratch/604/magnifier_sizes_gpu.png (rows orbit GPU,
cascade, space, orbit; columns no lens, 50 %, 100 %, 200 %, 300 %).
Tests: tests/qt/test_the_magnifier_has_a_size.py and the live-apply case
in test_fractal_changes_apply_without_a_restart.
