Status: IMPLEMENTED 2026-09-30

Cause: the lit run round every glassed window (SetupCard, in
spacr/qt/widgets/setup_card.py) was stroked as up to 320 short lines with
round caps, because a pen carries one colour and the run fades. Every cap
overlapped its neighbour, the overlap was painted twice, and the rim's
brightness rose and fell every 2-4 px: 85-218 reversals along the run.

Fix: the run is one filled polygon (its outline sampled along the rim,
tapering in width as before) shaded by one QConicalGradient centred on the
card, with a stop per sample. Nothing is painted twice, so there are no
seams; glow, rainbow, beat and spaceout keep their colours, position and
length. Renders: /mnt/wd4tb/scratch/602/before and /after (3 sizes, light
and dark, glow and rainbow; zoomed_* crops). Test:
tests/qt/test_602_the_rim_is_one_continuous_gradient.py (1 reversal per
run now).

Left: nothing.

Status:    not started (filed 2026-10-01; scoped, see below)

           better. Preserve that improvement. However, rounded edges
           sometimes look slightly jagged and show stray white pixels at the

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE STATE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Shared path: spacr/qt/widgets/glass.py glass() puts a SetupCard
(widgets/setup_card.py, radius 18) behind every QDialog, makes it frameless
and translucent, and round_the_corners() (~653-691) sets a QRegion MASK
built from an integer polygon. A window mask is all-or-nothing per pixel,
so the corner outline is aliased (the jaggedness), and pixels the mask
keeps but the antialiased card only partly covers show whatever sits
behind -- the stray contrasting pixels. No devicePixelRatio handling. The
card's 1 px border is stroked on rect.adjusted(1,1,-1,-1), i.e. on pixel
boundaries, so it straddles two pixels. setup_slides.py repeats the mask.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Where the window is translucent and a compositor is present, do not mask:
let the antialiased card define the shape (pixels outside it are fully
transparent). Keep the mask only as the fallback when translucency is not
available, and then build it from the physical-pixel geometry. Stroke the
border on half-pixel coordinates inside the body and clip the glow to the
same rounded path so nothing paints outside the card's alpha edge. Theme
colours stay theme tokens.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Offscreen renders of a glassed dialog in dark and light themes, at two
sizes and device-pixel ratios 1 and 2: corner pixels outside the rounded
path are fully transparent, alpha ramps monotonically across the edge (no
isolated opaque high-contrast pixel), and the 602 rim-continuity test still
passes. Visual checks record exactly which platform/scaling was looked at.
