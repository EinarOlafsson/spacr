     Cellpose output the mask came from.
  2. RIGHT-CLICK SAVE on any of those pictures: PNG or PDF, at the resolution
     and figure format Preferences already carries.
  3. A COMPARISON PANEL once more than one mask exists: a new button opens a
     popup listing the masks and the images, each with a tick, an opacity and
     a position in the stacking order; accepting it draws that composite in a
     third panel, to the right of the existing views.
