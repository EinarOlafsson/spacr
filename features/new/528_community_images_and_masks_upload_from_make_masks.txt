COMMUNITY IMAGES AND MASKS UPLOAD FROM MAKE MASKS

Status: OPEN, filed 2026-09-25. Not started. Builds on 523 (community plaque
training data uploads to Hugging Face): reuse its upload plumbing, consent
step and conscience text rather than a second copy.
Owner: unassigned.

REQUEST (the maintainer, 2026-09-25, verbatim)
    "the community data should be possible to uploade to through make masks.
    the tooltip shoudl be something like. "If you do not have the ability to
    train your own model, uploade your images and masks here and we will
    train it for you." and so on. the user should be able to easily uploade
    their image+ mask during curation or navigate to a folder with images
    then a folder with masks (this window should have 2 paths to fill) when
    both are filled and the same number of files with the same names are
    present in both then the image and mask files are uploaded as whatwever
    the user specifies to a new community foldr."

WHAT TO BUILD
  1. A "Contribute images and masks…" button in Make Masks, with the tooltip
     in the maintainer's spirit: "If you can't train a model yourself, upload
     your images and masks here and we'll train one for you. ..." (plus what
     happens next, the licence and the care note from 523).
  2. TWO WAYS IN:
       * During curation: the field on screen and its current mask, one click
         (and the whole curated queue, if the user wants).
       * A dialog with TWO path fields: an images folder and a masks folder.
         Upload is enabled only when both are filled and every image has a
         mask with the same name (and the counts match); mismatches are
         listed by name.
  3. THE USER SAYS WHAT IT IS: a name for the dataset (e.g. organism, object
     type, stain/modality) that becomes the community folder the files go to
     (community_<name>, created if new), plus short free-text notes.
  4. No mask, no upload; the 523 consent and conscience text apply.

DONE WHEN
An offscreen test drives both routes (upload faked): matched folders upload
to community_<name>; a name mismatch or a missing mask blocks the upload
and names the files; curation's one-click route sends the on-screen image
and its mask.
