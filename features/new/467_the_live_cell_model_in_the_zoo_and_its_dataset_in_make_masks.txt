--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE MODEL IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A Cellpose-SAM fine-tune of cpsam_v2 for cells in TRANSMITTED-LIGHT microscopy --
phase contrast, brightfield and DIC -- which is the half of microscopy the zoo
has nothing for. Every spaCR model to date is fluorescence (PV, cross-channel)
or a plaque assay.

  data      11,007 fields from 14 public datasets, group-split so no
            acquisition, well or time-lapse spans two sets:
            6,778 train, 2,030 valid, 2,199 test
  recipe    100 epochs asked for; PUBLISHED AT EPOCH 37 at the maintainer's word
            ("ok lets just publish that model at 37 epchs"). 
            lr 1e-5, batch 4, bsize 256, min_train_masks 1, file mode
  project   /mnt/wd4tb/af3/projects/live_cell (START_HERE.md, INGEST.json)
  repos     einarolafsson/live-cell-segmentation-cpsam            (model)
            einarolafsson/live-cell-segmentation-dataset          (dataset)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART A -- THE ZOO ENTRY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

One dict in spacr/model_zoo.py, shaped like `toxoplasma_pv_v3`: key, name,
kind "cellpose", repo_id, repo_type "model", uri to weights/live_cell_v1,
sha256 OF THE PUBLISHED FILE, metrics, display_name, architecture, dataset,
versus_stock, trained_on, trained_by, notes.

UNDER spaCR, and no code is needed to put it there: `model_zoo.py:2430`
returns "spaCR" as the source for any entry that does not name another, and
the picker's "spaCR" heading is "Models trained by the spaCR maintainer and by
spaCR users, each with the scorecard it was measured against." The scorecard is
the part that has to be right.

THE SCORECARD MUST CARRY TWO STOCK COMPARISONS, NOT ONE. Stock cpsam_v2 was
trained on LIVECell (5% of its samples) and on YeaZ -- verified from the
Cellpose-SAM paper's Methods, see WHAT_STOCK_CPSAM_HAS_SEEN.md in the model
repo. On those datasets stock's score is partly memorisation. `versus_stock`
and `notes` must give the overall figure AND the figure over the datasets stock
never saw -- DeepSea, the five CTC sets, BBBC009, BBBC030, QPI, Revvity -- and
say which is which. A single stock number here would be the plaque r3 mistake
the r5 card already warned about.

THE sha256 IS OF THE UPLOADED FILE. Computed from what Hugging Face serves, not
from the local checkpoint, because the zoo verifies downloads against it and a
mismatch refuses the model.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART B -- THE DATASET IN MAKE MASKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

One MaskDataset in spacr/qt/make_masks_datasets.py (item 450's registry):
repo einarolafsson/live-cell-segmentation-dataset, images "images", masks
"masks", apps ("mask",). The repo is published in exactly that layout so there
is no special case.

TEN FIELDS BY SORTED NAME WILL ALL BE ONE DATASET. The curated names are
`<source>__<stem>`, and sorting puts every `bbbc009_dic__` first. Item 450's
first-ten rule is right for its five sets, which are one domain each, and
wrong for this one, which is fourteen. This entry needs a sample that takes
fields ACROSS sources -- still deterministic, so two machines agree -- or a user
opens "live cell" and sees five red blood cells and five CHO cells and nothing
else. That is the one real design question here and it is to be settled in
the build, not assumed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * The model appears under the spaCR heading in the picker, installs, and its
    download verifies against the sha256.
  * Its scorecard shows both stock comparisons and names which datasets stock
    had already seen.
  * "Training datasets…" in Make Masks lists live cell, and the ten fields it
    opens come from more than one source.
  * choose_sample on the live repo listing is run, not only against a fixture.

Test split, F1 at IoU 0.5, 2,199 fields:

It MISSED its own pre-registered promotion bar (overall +0.02 over stock) because
of LIVECell phase, 0.724 to 0.671. The zoo entry says so in versus_stock and notes,
with both stock comparisons, as Part A asks.

THE DESIGN QUESTION, SETTLED. Between filing and building, item 450's first-ten
rule was replaced by a seeded random draw. That fixes the name-order problem but
not the size one: a uniform draw of 10 from these 11,007 fields is expected to
be about 5 LIVECell and 3 DeepSea, all phase. So the entry carries a quota:
LIVECell 2, DeepSea 2, QPI 1, YeaZ brightfield 1, yeast microstructures 1, CTC
MuSC brightfield 1, BBBC030 DIC 1, CTC HeLa DIC 1 -- 8 sources, all three
modalities. Foreground counts come from the repo's splits.csv (name, n_objects).

choose_sample was run on the real 22,014-file images/ + masks/ listing with the
real splits.csv: 10 pairs, 8 sources, 3 modalities. Test:
test_the_live_cell_sample_spans_its_sources_and_modalities.
