WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

spaCR's plaque users get a model that has never seen a phone-camera well, and a
row whose numbers come from a set that no longer represents the data. Per-domain
scores also let a user predict whether the model suits THEIR images, which a
single blended F1 cannot.

WHAT HAPPENED (2026-09-20)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Round 5 trained 21:39-23:25 (101 min, 100 epochs) on cpsam_v2 -- not cpsam v1,
per the user's instruction that day -- with per-epoch loss, pixel accuracy,
precision, recall, Dice, IoU and MCC recorded, the same instrumentation the PV
models publish. Published automatically on R5_OK:

  model    einarolafsson/toxoplasma-plaque-segmentation-cpsam-r5   14 files, 1.22 GB
  dataset  einarolafsson/toxoplasma-plaque-dataset                981 files, 1.28 GB

F1 at IoU 0.5 on the 81 held-out fields, all three models scored in one process:

  domain       fields   r3      r4      r5
  literature     39     0.820   0.819   0.819
  bigbean         6     0.872   0.881   0.876
  patrick        10     0.836   0.822   0.808
  malnio         20     0.249   0.217   0.415

NOT PROMOTED: -0.001 literature F1 against round 3, where the bar fixed before
the run was 0.02. Round 3 remains production.

**The flat literature score is not saturation, and an earlier reading of it as
saturation was wrong.** Round 5's literature TRAINING set is 208 fields against
round 4's 211 -- it shrank, because round 5 holds out 45 for validation and 45
for test where round 4 held out 52 and had no validation set. The extra images
are malnio (62) and that is exactly where it improved, by 91%. Performance
tracks per-domain data; literature will only move when literature wells are
added, and 513 remain unreviewed in curation/pmc_staged.

Zoo rows added in spacr/model_zoo.py (user: "Promote r5 as a second zoo row"):
toxoplasma_plaque_v2 alongside toxoplasma_plaque_v1, each pointing at the other
with the trade-off stated -- v1 for microscope-only work (0.836 vs 0.808 on PFA
wells, precision 0.93 vs 0.81), v2 for mixed or phone-camera sources. v2 needs
an explicit "uri": its weight sits under weights/ while the older repos keep
theirs at the repo root, so the URL hf_uri() would build 404s. Verified: HEAD
200, 1.22 GB, and the published sha256 matches the local file byte for byte.
