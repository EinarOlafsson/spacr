604. Spaceout: the Mandelbrot camera glides to complex regions

Requested by the maintainer (2026-09-30), verbatim:
"in spaceout mode the mandelbrot camera should smoothely center on regions with high complexity, i.e. not single color.
now the camera jumps which looks wierd. please make the camera movement smooth.
for the other spaceout modes theis a giant magnigying glass where the mouse is. there should be a slider deffining its size.
for the space mode there are small stars that look great passing like little dots. but there should also be galaxies passing i want the user to feel like they are really passing through space."

Brief (this item is the first paragraph; 605 and 606 are the others):
The Mandelbrot camera must never jump.
- Pick targets by a complexity score (local colour/escape-time variance or
  entropy on a coarse grid of the current frame); never a single-colour
  region.
- Glide to the target smoothly: ease-in-out or critically damped
  interpolation of centre and zoom, continuous in velocity, zoom in
  log-space.
- Retarget smoothly before reaching a flat region.

Done when: a test shows frame-to-frame centre/zoom deltas are bounded (no
jumps) and that chosen targets score above a complexity threshold; rendered
frame strips in /mnt/wd4tb/scratch/604 show a smooth glide.

Status: COMPLETE — hardware GPU acceptance verified 2026-10-01

Why it jumped (2026-09-30): three causes. (1) The "tour" path aimed the
camera at the twenty committed region coordinates, which are ABSOLUTE
points of the plane, as if they were offsets from the reference orbit, so
it glided off the set into flat colour. (2) Every decade the reference
orbit is moved onto a boundary point somewhere in the view, and the camera
offset was then reset to (0, 0): the picture jumped by up to a whole
view each time, on every path. (3) The depth wrapped from 21 decades to 0
in one frame.

Fix: the tour path (still the default, still labelled "Tour the
interesting places") is now _GlideCamera in fractal_mandelbrot.py. Every
half second a worker thread surveys the view (perturbation escape map)
and _complexity_map scores each 9x9 window: palette-band entropy weighted
by how often neighbours differ, so one colour scores 0 and smooth
gradients score low. The camera heads for the best nearby window above
_COMPLEXITY_FLOOR, keeps it while it scores well, and drops it early when
it fades toward the floor. The centre follows as an exactly solved
critically damped spring, speed-capped in screen units; the depth (log
zoom) eases its velocity toward the dive rate, slowed while the centre
travels, reversed gently when nothing in view has structure, and run
backwards to the surface at the end of a dive (and on Ctrl+R) instead of
cutting. A rebased reference now shifts the camera's centre and target by
the opposite offset on every path, so the picture stays still.

Measured (scratch /mnt/wd4tb/scratch/604): over a real orbit the largest
frame-to-frame move is 0.006 of the half-height, the depth's frame-to-frame
acceleration below 1e-4 decades. Frames: strip_glide_8_every_third_second.png
(consecutive, 1/3 s apart), sheet_glide_8_every4s.png,
mandel_gpu_glide_overview.png (the real shader on Mesa llvmpipe, 2.5 s apart),
mandel_gpu_glide_rewind.png (the glide back up).

Tests: tests/qt/test_the_mandelbrot_camera_glides_to_detail.py; updated
the tour and rebase pins in test_the_fractal_tour_and_shaders_at_their_edges
and test_cov_r5_fractal_travel.

Left: RegionTour/_TourPilot and fractal_regions.py are no longer used by
the canvas (kept; their own tests still exercise them). Not run on a real
GPU here; the shader was rendered on llvmpipe only.
