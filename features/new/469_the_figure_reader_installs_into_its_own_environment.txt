================================================================================
THE FIGURE READER (YOLO + OCR) INSTALLS INTO AN ENVIRONMENT OF ITS OWN
================================================================================

Status:    BUILDING 2026-09-21.

Requested: 2026-09-21, verbatim -- "if the downlode changes the dependencies
           and so on it would be great if we could contain it in a different
           environment"

Owner:     Claude (spacr/_segmentation_backends.py, spacr/plaque_papers.py,
           spacr/qt/widgets/plaque_preview.py, spacr/model_zoo.py).

Plaque Assay's Figure mode needs ultralytics (the YOLO detector) and
RapidOCR -- the spacr[papers] extra. Installing that extra into spaCR's own
environment lets ultralytics pull its own torch, opencv and friends and move
what spaCR runs on. Cellpose 3, DINOCell and SAMCell already install into
environments of their own under ~/.spacr/backends/<name> and are called out of
process (item 423); the figure reader becomes one more such backend, named
'papers', which the Model Zoo lists and installs but which is not offered as a
segmentation backend. Its worker answers two requests besides hello: detect
(plaque images in a figure, at the chosen sizes) and read_text (the words and
their boxes). Figure mode -- preview and run -- uses it whenever ultralytics
is not importable in spaCR itself.
