================================================================================
THE WINDOW MARKS HAVE NO BLACK PLATE
================================================================================

Status:    DONE 2026-08-27. Reproduced in the dark theme, fixed and rendered
           in a regression test.
Owner:     spacr/qt/app.py window chrome.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WAS REPORTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A black box appeared behind the top-right minus, square and x. It must be
transparent or removed so the three marks sit directly on the menu bar.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CAUSE AND FIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The three ``QToolButton`` backgrounds were already transparent. Their shared
corner ``QWidget`` was not styled, so it filled itself from MainWindow's black
first-frame palette and produced one 67-by-19-pixel black plate in the dark
theme. The corner now has an explicit identity and transparent styled surface;
all button states remain transparent while only their painted glyph changes on
hover and press.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The dark-themed menu bar is rendered offscreen. A background pixel inside the
window-chrome corner must equal the adjacent menu-bar pixel. The test fails on
the reported revision, where those pixels are ``#000000`` and ``#0d0e10``, and
passes only when the shared plate is gone.
