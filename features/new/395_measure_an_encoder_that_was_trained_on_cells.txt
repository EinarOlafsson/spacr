--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

386's retrieval measurement ran on 2026-09-12 and the default encoder LOST to
the hand-designed panel by about twenty points of balanced accuracy:

    gene retrieval, embeddings          0.695
    gene retrieval, measured panel      0.894
    position control, embeddings        0.494
    position control, panel             0.514

The measurement is sound -- the position control reads chance across all
twenty adjacent library-column pairs, the shuffled nulls read chance, the
split is grouped by well, and the obvious excuse was falsified (restricting
the panel to the encoder's three channels WIDENS the gap rather than closing
it). So the number is real and it is not an artefact.

WHAT IT IS A NUMBER ABOUT IS THE POINT. It was measured with
`EmbeddingSpec(backbone='resnet18')` -- ImageNet-supervised weights, a
network trained to tell a dog from a bicycle, applied to fluorescence crops.
That is the weakest thing the module can be pointed at and the least
interesting encoder to have measured. 386 could not say this because it had
not run anything; now it has, and the honest reading is "this encoder loses",
not "embeddings lose".

HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

REWRITTEN 2026-09-14. The first bullet used to read "reported beside 0.695
and 0.894, on the same plate, the same objects and the same folds". That is
unsatisfiable -- see the Status block -- and an unsatisfiable criterion is how
an item stays open forever or gets closed dishonestly.

  * BOTH ARMS COME OUT OF ONE RUN. The cell-trained encoder AND the measured
    panel, same plate, same objects, same folds, same harness, reported
    together. The comparison is internal to the new run.
  * THE PLATE IS NAMED, in the harness and in the result. This item exists in
    its present form because the last one was not.
  * The position control is run for every arm and still reads chance. Without
    that the new number means nothing, for the same reason the old one would
    not have.
  * resnet18 IS ONE OF THE ARMS. It is the cheap way to find out whether the
    new harness reproduces the old finding at all: if resnet18 scores far from
    0.695 on the new plate, that is information about the harness and the
    data, not about encoders, and it must be reported rather than quietly
    absorbed.
  * Whatever the result, it is written into 386 as a NEW TABLE beside the
    2026-09-12 figures rather than replacing them or merging with them. Two
    encoders measured the same way is the useful artefact; two numbers from
    two harnesses in one column is the thing this file spent two days
    preventing.

A RESULT THAT IS STILL BELOW THE PANEL IS A SUCCESS FOR THIS ITEM. The
question is what a cell-trained encoder scores, not whether it wins, and
"the panel is better on this task too" is a finding worth having written
down once rather than assumed either way.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS DOES NOT COVER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Not "the interesting result" -- whether an embedding separates something the
panel does NOT. 386 says plainly that retrieval of a KNOWN phenotype is the
wrong instrument for that, because the positive control is the screen's top
hit precisely because the panel already detects it. That needs a different
design and a human looking at crops, and it is not this item.

TWO DELIBERATE DEPARTURES FROM `embed_array`'s DEFAULTS, AND WHY.

  1. normalize=False, with ONE plate-wide per-channel scale instead.
     EmbeddingSpec(normalize=True) divides each channel by the 99th percentile
     of THE WHOLE STACK IT IS HANDED. An object's embedding therefore depends
     on which other crops share its batch. Checked tonight with a stand-in
     encoder on one c1 crop, channel-1 input mean:
         alone 0.311    in a batch of c1 crops 0.076    in a batch of c2 0.094
     and identical alone vs batched once normalize=False. A harness that
     batches a column-sorted index (the 09-12 one did) partly normalises PER
     COLUMN -- on exactly the axis the contrast is. Tonight every crop is
     divided by the p99 of 2,048 random crops (seed 0): [85, 79, 198], then
     clipped to [0, 1], for every encoder.
     THIS IS A DEFECT IN spacr/embeddings.py and is NOT fixed here (scope):
     the percentile should be per crop, or fixed, never per batch.

  2. The panel is also scored on channels 0-2 only (panel3ch, 116 of 156
     columns) because the crops carry only those three.

ENCODERS.
  resnet18          spacr.embeddings._timm_encoder, timm resnet18.a1_in1k,
                    per-channel, [0,1] input as the module feeds it
  resnet18_imnorm   same weights with ImageNet mean/std, to separate the
                    encoder from the module's preprocessing
  biomedclip        microsoft/BiomedCLIP-PubMedBERT_256-vit_base_patch16_224,

WHAT IT SAYS.
  1. THE CELL-TRAINED ENCODER BEATS IMAGENET, BY A LITTLE. OpenPhenom scores
     0.673 per-channel and 0.685 in its native joint mode, against resnet18's
     0.634, and wins on every fold. Roughly half of that gap is preprocessing
     rather than training data: resnet18 given ImageNet mean/std gets 0.663.
  2. IT IS NOT THE BEST ENCODER. BiomedCLIP, trained on PubMed figures and not
     on cells, scores 0.752 and beats OpenPhenom by 6.8 points on every fold.
     "Trained on cells" is not what predicts the score on this task.
  3. EVERY ENCODER LOSES TO THE PANEL, AND BY A LOT. The best one is 13.6
     points behind (0.752 vs 0.888), on every fold. By 395's criterion this is
     a success: the question was what a cell-trained encoder scores.
  4. THE CONTROLS HOLD FOR EVERY ENCODER. c5-c6 reads 0.506-0.535, the 20-pair
     means are 0.505-0.518, and every shuffled null is 0.463-0.508. The
     highest single pairs (openphenom_joint c11-c12 0.620, panel c6-c7 0.597)
     have fold sd 0.15-0.18, sit on columns with 11-16 wells, and are not the
     same pair across encoders -- noise, not position.
  5. CHANNEL 3 DOES NOT HELP. The panel scores HIGHER without its 40
     channel_3 columns (0.902 vs 0.888, 5/5 folds), so the encoders are not
     losing for want of the fourth stain.

CAVEATS, NOT TESTED TONIGHT.
  * OpenPhenom's model card asks for PCA-CenterScale or TVN batch correction
     on control wells before use. Nothing here does that beyond a
     StandardScaler, for any encoder; it may lift OpenPhenom more than the
     others.
  * OpenPhenom was trained on 6-channel Cell Painting fields; these are
     3-channel single-cell crops upsampled 224->256. That is a domain shift
     for it, as it is for BiomedCLIP.
  * The fresh resnet18 figure (0.634) is below the 09-12 one (0.695). The
     per-column normalisation in the 09-12 harness is one candidate
     explanation; it has not been tested and the old number is retired anyway.
