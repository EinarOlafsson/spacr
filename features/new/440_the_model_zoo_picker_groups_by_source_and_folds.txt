--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE STATE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ONE OF THE FOUR COMPLAINTS IS ALREADY FIXED. cell_from_hoechst was missing
because it was never in the catalogue: `cross-channel-cell-from-hoechst-cpsam`
has been published on Hugging Face with full QC (F1 0.8697 against stock
cpsam_v2's 0.3012 on 451 held-out fields) while its twin,
`cross-channel-nuclei-from-cellmask-cpsam`, was listed. It was added in
32d7b5250 along with PV round 6 and well detector v4, so the picker has ten
rows now rather than seven -- which makes the rest of this item sharper, not
softer.

THE PICKER TODAY shows every catalogue row in one list, with a single boolean,
"show unvetted community uploads", deciding whether the shared catalogue's rows
appear beside the bundled ones. Ten bundled rows plus a community catalogue
plus, since 423, the Cellpose 3 backend's own models is more than a list can
carry without grouping.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. FIVE SOURCE HEADINGS, each clickable, each folding its rows away:

       cellposeSAM      the stock Cellpose-SAM weights
       spaCR            models trained by the maintainer, and by spaCR users
       spaCR community  the shared catalogue's unvetted uploads
       bioimage.io      models read through bioimage.io's artifact API
       cellpose3        the Cellpose 3 backend's own models (cyto, nuclei,
                        cyto2, cyto3), which 423 made installable

   A heading that is ON is blue and its models are visible; a heading that is
   OFF is muted and its models are not. ON by default: cellposeSAM and spaCR,
   and nothing else. The state persists per user, like the other picker
   preferences.

2. REMOVE the "show unvetted community uploads" boolean. "spaCR community"
   replaces it, and turning that heading on is what now asks the shared
   catalogue. KEEP THE WARNING: the first time it is turned on, say what an
   unvetted upload is, exactly as the boolean did.

3. EVERY ROW MUST LAND IN EXACTLY ONE SOURCE, decided from the row itself
   rather than from a hand-written list: source="shared" is community; a
   bioimage.io URI is bioimage.io; the Cellpose 3 backend's rows are
   cellpose3; stock Cellpose-SAM is cellposeSAM; everything else the
   catalogue bundles is spaCR. A row that matches nothing goes under spaCR and
   says so in the log, rather than vanishing from the picker -- a model a user
   cannot see is worse than one under the wrong heading.

4. The same grouping wherever the catalogue is offered: the Model Zoo tab, the
   Mask module's model box, and Make Masks' Mode box.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * Opening the picker shows two blue headings, cellposeSAM and spaCR, and the
    rows under them; the other three are muted and their rows are not on
    screen.
  * Clicking "spaCR community" turns it blue, warns once, and the shared rows
    appear; clicking it again folds them away.
  * cell_from_hoechst is visible under spaCR without turning anything on.
  * Every catalogue row appears under exactly one heading -- a test walks the
    catalogue and asserts the partition is total and disjoint.
  * A test drives the headings: default state, toggle, persistence across a
    reopen, and the warning appearing once.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2026-09-19 -- BUILT. WHAT WAS THERE, WHAT WAS NOT, AND WHAT IT MEASURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WRITTEN AS A TEST FIRST, AND IT FAILED, which is why the rest of this section
describes building rather than finding. `tests/qt/test_the_model_zoo_picker_-
groups_by_source.py` was written against the behaviour this file asks for and
run on unmodified nightly: 13 of 13 red, every one of them an AttributeError
for something that did not exist. Three sessions this week built something
that already worked; this was not one of them.

WHAT WAS ALREADY THERE, and was reworked rather than replaced:

  * `ModelZooPicker._groups`, the per-model-family grouping with a version
    combo box in column 5. Untouched. The source headings FOLD rows, they do
    not regroup them -- the row a user clicks still means a model family.
  * The Cellpose 3 rows, from item 423: the four backend models and the
    backend package row itself. They were already listed; what was missing
    was a heading to fold them away.
  * `cell_from_hoechst_v1`, added in 32d7b5250. It was in the catalogue and
    is now visibly under spaCR with nothing turned on, which is the
    acceptance this file asked for.
  * The community warning's words. They are unchanged, moved from
    `_community_toggled` to `confirm_community_uploads`.

WHAT WAS BUILT:

  * `spacr/model_zoo.py`: `ZOO_SOURCES`, `DEFAULT_ZOO_SOURCES`, `source_of`,
    `group_by_source`, `entries_from_sources`. Qt-free on purpose -- the
    partition is a property of the catalogue, not of a dialog, and all three
    consumers ask the same function.
  * `spacr/qt/widgets/model_zoo_picker.py`: `_SourceHeading` (one clickable
    label, accent when on and fg_muted when off, both resolved from the
    palette in force rather than the frozen module-level one), `SourceStrip`
    (the five of them, the QSettings key, the guard seam),
    `confirm_community_uploads` and `community_guard`. `community_toggle` and
    its QCheckBox import are gone.
  * `spacr/qt/screens/model_zoo.py`: the same strip above the page's table,
    `_apply_source_filter`, and the scan asking for community rows exactly
    when that heading is on.
  * `spacr/qt/screens/make_masks.py`: `_zoo_cellpose_models` filters by the
    persisted headings, so the Mode box is not the one place that ignores a
    user who folded bioimage.io away.

THE MASK MODULE'S MODEL BOX NEEDED NOTHING. Its zoo button is
`ObjectSettingsGrid.choose_model_for`, which opens this very picker
(`choose_model`), so it inherited the headings the moment the picker had
them. Checked rather than assumed -- `tests/qt/test_a_model_row_has_a_zoo_-
button.py` passes unchanged.

THE PARTITION, MEASURED on this machine against the live catalogue (21 rows,
no shared catalogue cached, bioimage.io cache cold):

    cellposeSAM       4   cpsam, cpsam_v2, cpdino, cpdino-vitb
    spaCR            12   the ten bundled rows, plus the DINOCell and SAMCell
                          backend packages
    spaCR community   0   nothing cached
    bioimage.io       0   cache cold; with it warm, 4 more
    cellpose3         5   cyto, cyto2, cyto3, nuclei, and the backend package

    total 21 of 21, and no row under two headings.

TWO DECISIONS THIS FILE DID NOT MAKE, made here:

  * A BACKEND PACKAGE ROW GOES WHERE ITS BACKEND GOES. The Cellpose 3 backend
    is under cellpose3, beside the four models it runs; DINOCell and SAMCell
    are under spaCR, because they are packages this project offers and there
    is no heading of their own. They are not logged as unmatched -- the log
    line is for a row nobody meant, and these are meant.
  * A BIOIMAGE.IO CELLPOSE 3 CHECKPOINT IS A BIOIMAGE.IO ROW. The order of
    the tests decides it, and this file's own ordering -- community, then
    bioimage.io, then cellpose3 -- is what was followed. "cellpose3" means
    the backend's own models.

ONE THING THE FOLDING BROKE, AND IT IS FIXED. Qt numbers a table's vertical
header by model row, so a folded picker counted "1 2 3 5 6 11" down its left
edge; the gaps ARE the hidden rows and they read as a table that has lost
some of itself. The row numbers are off now, as they already were on the
Model Zoo page. The name was always the identity here.

AND ONE THAT BROKE SIX TESTS. `tests/qt/test_model_zoo_picker.py` picked "the
first row needing a download" without asking whether it was on screen, and
the first such row is now a folded-away bioimage.io one. `selectRow` on a
hidden row selects nothing, so six tests failed reading "the picker lost its
selection". Both helpers there now skip hidden rows, which is what they were
always asking for.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS STILL OPEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE NINE LANGUAGES. Eleven new user-facing strings were added and the
catalogs were deliberately NOT regenerated, for the consolidated pass that
item 316 owns: the five headings (`cellposeSAM`, `spaCR`, `spaCR community`,
`bioimage.io`, `cellpose3` -- four of the five are proper names and may well
stay as they are), their five tooltips, and the community warning's title and
body, which are the retired checkbox's own text and so are already in
whatever state that string was in.

NOT DONE ON PURPOSE: the picker's own strings do not go through `tr()` today
-- none of them, not before this change either -- so the headings do not
either. Wrapping this file's strings alone would leave a dialog half
translated and half not, which is worse than a dialog that is consistently
English until 316 reaches it.
