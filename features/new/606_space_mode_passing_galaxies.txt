606. Space mode: galaxies pass by along with the stars

Requested by the maintainer (2026-09-30), verbatim:
"in spaceout mode the mandelbrot camera should smoothely center on regions with high complexity, i.e. not single color.
now the camera jumps which looks wierd. please make the camera movement smooth.
for the other spaceout modes theis a giant magnigying glass where the mouse is. there should be a slider deffining its size.
for the space mode there are small stars that look great passing like little dots. but there should also be galaxies passing i want the user to feel like they are really passing through space."

Brief (this item is the last sentence; 604 and 605 are the others):
In space mode, add galaxies that pass by along with the star dots.
- Procedural spiral/elliptical galaxies with varied size, tilt, colour and
  brightness, drawn as soft glowing sprites, sparser than the stars.
- They approach with perspective (grow and speed up as they near the
  camera), with parallax, then pass and fade.
- Keep the frame rate: precompute sprites and cap the count. It must work
  in the CPU fallback path.

Done when: both renderers draw them (tests), and a frame sequence in
/mnt/wd4tb/scratch/604 shows galaxies approaching, growing and passing.

Status: IMPLEMENTED 2026-09-30

Built in fractal_space.py: eight galaxy pictures (two-armed, three-armed,
barred and flocculent spirals with bulges, dust lanes and blue knots, and
de Vaucouleurs ellipticals) drawn once into an atlas (_galaxy_atlas).
_galaxies_at(t, speed) places up to GALAXY_SLOTS = 4: each slot carries
one galaxy from depth 7 to 0.35 over 70 s at speed 1, so its position
(offset / depth) and size grow ever faster -- perspective -- and slots at
different offsets and depths cross at different rates (parallax). Each has
its own size, tilt (squash), turn, tint and brightness, fades in from the
distance and out as it passes; about one slot in five stays empty.

CPU fallback: _add_galaxies (numba) lays them over the star field visiting
only each galaxy's own pixels (0.4 ms at 480x270 against 55 ms for the
star field). GPU: the atlas is uploaded once as RGBA8 holding sqrt(light),
and the canvas sets u_galaxyN_place/_shape/_tint every frame.

Frames: /mnt/wd4tb/scratch/604/galaxies_pass_cpu_every3s.png,
galaxies_close_cpu.png, galaxies_pass_gpu_every3s.png (llvmpipe),
galaxy_atlas.png.
Tests: tests/qt/test_space_galaxies_pass_by.py.
