--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE PARTS, AS ASKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. BOX SIZE: the maximum is no longer 512 (spacr/qt/screens/make_masks.py
     _MAGNIFIER_SIZE_RANGE = (32, 512)); it can go as high as the open image
     is high or wide.
  2. COLLAPSIBLE SETTINGS CATEGORIES in Make Masks, the way the core
     applications fold theirs.
  3. CELLPOSE MODELS: with Cellpose chosen, every Cellpose model in the model
     zoo can be picked, and there is a Model zoo button for Cellpose.
  4. THRESHOLDS: the flow threshold and the cell-probability threshold the
     detection uses can be chosen.
  5. CLICK-AND-DRAG: pressing and pulling keeps generating and applying new
     objects, merging the objects the cursor passes over.
  6. WHAT IS SAVED: an option between saving every object in the zoom area and
     saving only the objects touching the mouse -- so non-centred objects can
     be turned off, and dragging over an object selects and merges all of its
     frames into one object over the centre of the ROI.
  7. OTSU: controls over the Otsu threshold (threshold correction).
  8. SHIFT + SCROLL increases or decreases the box size.
  9. THE EXISTING CELLPOSE-SAM SETTINGS CATEGORY applies to magnifier detection
     when Cellpose is on, and gains the Otsu settings.
 10. OTHER MODELS "if you can": a live YOLO, or DINOCell (spaCR already has
     DINOCell and SAMCell behind `segmentation_backend`, items 404/405).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * The box can be set to the image's full height/width, by field and by
    Shift+scroll, and stops there.
  * Every Make Masks settings category folds and unfolds, and remembers it, as
    the core applications' do.
  * With Cellpose on, the model list is the model zoo's Cellpose models, the
    Model zoo button opens the zoo, and the flow / cell-probability / Otsu
    values set in the Cellpose-SAM category are the ones the magnifier's
    detection actually uses (a test checks the values reach the call).
  * A press-and-drag across cells produces merged objects along the path, as one
    undo step; with "only objects touching the mouse", objects away from the
    cursor are not saved, and dragging over one object merges its pieces into a
    single object centred on the ROI.
  * DINOCell (and SAMCell) are selectable detectors where installed, with a
    clear message where not; YOLO is either added or recorded here with why not.
  * New labels and tooltips are translated in all nine languages.

WHAT IT DOES

  * PRESS AND DRAG (part 5). With the magnifier on, a left press starts a
    stroke and adds nothing yet. Once the mouse has travelled the platform's
    drag distance (10 px) it is a drag: every quarter of a box of travel
    (Size 128 -> every 32 image px) the box there is segmented, on the
    existing region worker and pinned so later moves cannot supersede it. A
    long jump between two mouse events asks for a box at every quarter-box
    along the line, so no stretch of the path goes unseen. What the stroke
    adds is pasted onto the mask LIVE, at most every 40 ms, and the model
    never runs on the GUI thread.
  * THE OBJECTS THE CURSOR PASSES OVER BECOME ONE OBJECT, from every box along
    the drag. On release the stroke waits for any box still on the worker
    ("Magnifier: segmenting the last regions ..."), then goes in as ONE edit:
    one undo step and one "magnifier" ledger entry with drag=True, save,
    frames and merged (pieces joined into the object under the path). The
    Overlap rule and Min area apply exactly as for a click.
  * WHAT IS SAVED (part 6): "Objects added" on the Live magnifier card,
    "All objects in the zoom area" (the default, 407's behaviour) or "Only
    objects touching the mouse". With the second, a click adds just the
    object under the cursor and a drag adds just the object(s) under the
    path; objects elsewhere in the box are not saved.
  * A PRESS THAT DOES NOT MOVE IS STILL 407's CLICK, now on release rather
    than on press, under "All objects in the zoom area" and under Whole
    image. Under Whole image a drag merges the already-found objects the path
    passes over and asks no model.
  * RIGHT BUTTON: unchanged. Under Region under the mouse a right drag still
    sweep-deletes every object it passes over as one undo step; under Whole
    image a right press still removes the one object under it and does not
    sweep.
  * Moving to another field mid-drag drops the stroke without adding to
    either field; a box that cannot be segmented is dropped from the stroke
    rather than holding it up; a stroke that adds nothing says so and leaves
    no undo step.

HOW "MERGE ALL OBJECT FRAMES INTO ONE OBJECT OVER THE CENTER OF THE ROI" WAS READ

  The magnifier's ROI is always centred on the cursor, so "the object over
  the centre of the ROI" is the object under the cursor, and its "frames" are
  the pieces of it that the successive boxes along the drag each found. They
  are merged by UNION AT THEIR TRUE IMAGE POSITIONS: nothing is moved or
  re-centred. The rules, in spacr/qt/_magnifier_drag.py:
    1. every piece the path passes over, in any box, goes into one object;
    2. a piece sharing at least half of the smaller of the two with an object
       already gathered (counted inside the new box) is that object seen
       again and joins it -- so a piece the path did not cross still joins
       the object it belongs to, while neighbours whose outlines disagree by
       a few pixels stay apart;
    3. other objects are added separately under "All objects in the zoom
       area" and left out under "Only objects touching the mouse";
    4. a pixel two objects both claim stays with the first.

  EXAMPLE. Size 128, Only objects touching the mouse, border option unticked.
  A cell 300 px long lies along a row; press on its left end and pull to its
  right end. A box is taken every 32 px; each sees the part of the cell inside
  it, cut by the box's edges. The pieces overlap, so they become ONE object
  covering the whole cell, exactly where the cell is, and a neighbouring cell
  visible at the side of every box is not saved.

  THE BORDER OPTION KEEPS ITS 407 MEANING IN EVERY BOX. With "Exclude objects
  touching the box border" ticked (the default) a piece the box cuts is not
  offered, so an object LARGER than the box contributes nothing and is not
  added; untick it to assemble such objects from their pieces. An object
  smaller than half the box is never cut under the cursor, so the default
  works for it, and the successive boxes' outlines of it are merged into one.
