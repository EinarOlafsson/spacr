Image/table vacuole selection, per-channel ratio/state columns, unknown counts,
host infection summary and API link. Source images are matched by recorded
field identity; merged plane manifests choose overlays, with explicit plane
controls for legacy projects. Image absence/invalid planes leave results usable.
