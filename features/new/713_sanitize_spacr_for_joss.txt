--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

JOSS reviewers work through a fixed checklist (https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/review_checklist.html). Each unchecked box is a round trip with the reviewer, and a cluttered or very large repository, unclear authorship, or a paper that reads like the research article are common reasons for long reviews or rejection.

Submitting in parallel with iScience also needs the two papers to be clearly different: the JOSS paper describes the software, and the iScience paper the science. JOSS's policies on prior publication and co-submission must be checked before submitting.

   - Statement of need: who the software is for, and the gap compared with CellProfiler, napari, Cellpose, scikit-image and other tools, with citations;
   - State of the field;

   - Software design;
   - Research impact or ongoing use;
   - An AI-usage disclosure, if JOSS requires one;
   - Acknowledgements and references.
   Keep it within the JOSS word limit. Add .github/workflows/draft-pdf.yml (the openjournals draft action) so every push renders the paper.
3. Review-checklist pass. Record one line per checklist entry here: repository, licence, contribution and authorship, substantial scholarly effort, data sharing, reproducibility, human/animal research, installation, functionality, performance, documentation (statement of need, installation, example usage, functionality, automated tests, community guidelines) and the software paper. Fix each gap. Likely gaps:
   - README installation and example sections a reviewer can follow in ten minutes on a clean machine, with a small public example dataset;
   - CONTRIBUTING that says how to report issues, get support and contribute;
   - an obvious test command plus the CI badge;
   - API documentation linked from the README.
4. Repository sanitising, without losing anything the maintainer needs:
   - move or remove root clutter (.stfolder, .virtual_documents, rtd_trigger.txt, the stray settings and measurement folders, internal proposals and services that are not part of the package) and add them to .gitignore;

5. Authorship: one consistent contributors list across CITATION.cff, .zenodo.json and paper.md. The maintainer decides the authors and their order.
