MEASURED DIAMETERS COME OUT IN THE WRONG ORDER: CELL 7.2, NUCLEUS 91.3, PATHOGEN 10.9

Status: OPEN, filed 2026-09-25. Not started.

REQUEST (the maintainer, 2026-09-25, verbatim)
    "i tried measure diameter from the test images in mask generation and got
    cell 7.2, nucleus 91.3 and pathogen 10.9. thi smakes no sense, cell
    should be the biggest then nucleus then pathogen. look into this and try
    to fix it."

WHAT IS WRONG
Measuring diameters from Mask generation's test images gives cell 7.2,
nucleus 91.3, pathogen 10.9 (pixels). Biology says cell > nucleus >
pathogen. Candidates to check, by measurement not by reading: the channels
are mapped to the wrong object (cell measured on the pathogen channel and so
on), the estimate runs on a downscaled image and is not scaled back, the
size model is the wrong one for the object, or the nucleus estimate merges
touching nuclei into one object.

DONE WHEN
On the test images the measured diameters come out cell > nucleus >
pathogen and within 20 % of the diameters measured from ground-truth or
hand-drawn masks on the same fields (record both), with a test that pins
the order on the test data.
