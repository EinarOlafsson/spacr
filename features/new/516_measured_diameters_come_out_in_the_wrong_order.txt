Status: FIXED 2026-09-25, on nightly (23ab038e7, abf8ffbb9; checked
2026-09-25); awaiting the maintainer's check in the GUI.
Owner: unassigned. Related: 503/507 (Diameter 0 guesses 16 px for 44 px
cells), the "measure diameter" action in Mask generation.

REQUEST (the maintainer, 2026-09-25, verbatim)
    "i tried measure diameter from the test images in mask generation and got
    cell 7.2, nucleus 91.3 and pathogen 10.9. thi smakes no sense, cell
    should be the biggest then nucleus then pathogen. look into this and try
    to fix it."

WHAT IS WRONG
Measuring diameters from Mask generation's test images gives cell 7.2,
nucleus 91.3, pathogen 10.9 (pixels). Biology says cell > nucleus >
pathogen. Candidates to check, by measurement not by reading: the channels
are mapped to the wrong object (cell measured on the pathogen channel and so
on), the estimate runs on a downscaled image and is not scaled back, the
size model is the wrong one for the object, or the nucleus estimate merges
touching nuclei into one object.

DONE WHEN
On the test images the measured diameters come out cell > nucleus >
pathogen and within 20 % of the diameters measured from ground-truth or
hand-drawn masks on the same fields (record both), with a test that pins
the order on the test data.

2026-09-25 -- FIXED (spacr/diameter.py, estimator only)
Reproduced on the Mask generation test plate (~/.cache/spacr/example_data/
plate1, which the estimator reads through merged/; channels from its
gen_mask_settings: nucleus 0, cell 1, pathogen 2), 5 fields:
    before  cell 8.4   nucleus 91.3   pathogen 10.9   (maintainer: 7.2/91.3/10.9)
    after   cell 159.5 nucleus 97.3   pathogen 32.3
    masks   cell 175.1 nucleus 94.6   pathogen 32.0   (median equivalent
            diameter of the curated label masks, planes 4/5/6 of all 16
            merged arrays, border objects dropped; 529/699/589 objects)
After is within 9 %, 3 % and 1 % of the masks, and in biological order.

Ruled out by measurement: the channel mapping is right (merged plane i is the
i-th sorted chanID, and planes 4/5/6 are the cell/nucleus/pathogen masks of
those same channels); nothing is downscaled (fields are centre-cropped to
1500 px, not resized); there is no size model, it is one Otsu/label path for
every object; and nuclei do not merge (the nucleus was the one right number).

Root cause: the characteristic size was a plain median over every
thresholded component. The cell stain (mitochondrial) and the parasite
marker threshold into a few whole objects plus hundreds of 4-10 px specks:
235 of 321 cell components and 87 of 129 pathogen components, holding 0.8 %
and 6.7 % of the stained area. The specks outnumber the objects, so the
median landed on them. The solid nuclear stain has few specks, which is why
only the nucleus came out right.

Fix: spacr/diameter.py:701 _without_specks, applied at :759 in _aggregate.
The typical object is found by the area-weighted median (which specks
cannot move), components narrower than SPECK_FRACTION = 0.25 of it (:158)
are set aside, and the median is taken over the rest. Small components that
together hold SPECK_MAX_AREA_SHARE = 0.15 (:163) of the stained area or more
are a real second population and are kept, so the existing two-population
warning still fires. The note now says how many specks were set aside.
On the raw .tif fields of the same plate (no masks for most of them) the
order is also right: 10 fields cell 145.9, nucleus 91.6, pathogen 27.4
(before 9.7 / 84.6 / 10.8).

Tests: tests/test_measured_diameters_come_out_in_order.py -- a synthetic
plate of whole objects plus specks (old code: cell 5.6, pathogen 5.6 against
150/90/30; new: 150/90/30), the speck rule keeping a genuine two-size
