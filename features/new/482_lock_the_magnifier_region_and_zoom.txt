LOCK THE MAGNIFIER REGION AND ZOOM

Status: IMPLEMENTED — validated 2026-09-22; see verification notes below.
Owner: implementation; API/settings descriptions and translations coordinated with the documentation lane.

REQUEST
Hold Ctrl+L and right-click to lock the magnifier region and zoom; repeat to unlock.

ACCEPTANCE
The gesture takes precedence over right-click removal and pan. While locked, moving the mouse or scrolling cannot move the inspected image region or alter its zoom. Show a visible lock state, retain editing against the locked region, and clear stale locks when switching images.

Validate real UI interaction and affected pixel/mask behavior with focused tests.
