A SCALED PREVIEW NO LONGER SCALES ITS DIALOGS

Status: DONE, 2026-09-25.

THE PROBLEM
Item 521 found the backend install dialog's buttons 59 px tall squares when
opened from Plaque Assay with its preview at 150 %, and fixed that one
dialog by parenting it to the window. Every other dialog opened with a
preview as its parent had the same fault: Plaque's Paper, Settings and
Overlay dialogs, and Mask's Live Settings, had 59 px buttons at 150 % and
30 px at 75 %, against the 40 px of every other dialog.

CAUSE
A preview's own scale (item 471, spacr/qt/widgets/preview_scale.py)
re-states the application sheet at its scale on the panel
(PreviewScaler._scale_root_sheet), and Qt cascades an ancestor's sheet
into a child dialog although it is a window of its own. The scaler's walk
(PreviewScaler.apply, root.findChildren) also found the dialog and its
contents among the panel's children and scaled their minimum sizes,
margins and own sheets.

THE FIX (root, not per dialog)
spacr/qt/widgets/preview_scale.py:
* PreviewScaler._window_of (line 377): the outermost window between a
  widget and the preview. PreviewScaler.apply (line 454) walks a window
  and everything in it at 100 %, which also gives back the sizes of
  controls lent to a dialog (PlaqueSettingsDialog, Live Settings).
* PreviewScaler._unscale_window (line 415): the window's own sheet is the
  preview's cascade re-stated at 100 % (the inherited sheet, the panel's
  and every widget's between), then the window's own sheet, which still
  wins where they differ. At 100 % its own sheet is given back untouched.
* PreviewScaler.eventFilter / _window_polished (line 393): the parent
  reports the new window polished from inside show(), before the window
  takes its size, so it is never laid out once at the preview's scale.
521's re-parenting of the install dialog stays.

MEASURED (offscreen, theme applied; ordinary dialog button 40 px)
                       before 150 % / 75 %    after 150 % / 75 %
  Paper                59 / 30                40 / 40
  Plaque Settings      59 / 30                40 / 40
  Plaque Overlay       59 / 30                40 / 40
  Mask Live Settings   59 / 30                40 / 40
The previews' own buttons still scale: Plaque 40 -> 59 / 30 px.

TESTS
tests/qt/test_522_a_scaled_preview_no_longer_scales_its_dialogs.py
(9 tests; all 9 fail before the fix): each dialog at 150 % and 75 % has
buttons of the ordinary height, the panel's own buttons scale, and a
dialog already open follows a later scale change and gets its own sheet
back at 100 %. Also run, before and after: test_521, test_519,
test_gui_scale_recovery, test_the_gui_scale_fits_a_laptop, the plaque
preview/calibration/mode tests, the live settings and text-fit dialog
