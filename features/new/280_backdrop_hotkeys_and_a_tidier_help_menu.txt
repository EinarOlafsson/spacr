================================================================================
BACKDROP HOTKEYS, AND A HELP MENU WITHOUT BADGES
================================================================================

Status:    DONE, 2026-08-27. 11 tests. ONE DEVIATION, named below.
Owner:     spacr/qt/app.py, spacr/qt/i18n.py.
Requested: 2026-08-27, in two messages.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WAS ASKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

"in the hot keys there should be a hotkey to toggle the theme on and off if
 the user is looking at images ... like ctrl+t for theme or similar."

"ctrl + T now stops the background annimation. add a hotkey ctrl+b to pause
 the animation and make the scree dark gray (dark mode) or white (light
 mode) ... in the help menue there are two large blue circles with an i in
 the middle ... remove the circle with the i and remove (web)"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT SHIPPED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CTRL+T stops the animation and leaves the last frame up. It stops rather
than hides: a hidden backdrop still rendering spends the cores and shows
nothing for them. It reaches both the ambient engines and the spaceout
fractal through `set_animating`.

CTRL+SHIFT+B pauses AND hides, so what sits behind the work is the theme's
own ground -- dark grey in a dark theme, white in a light one, taken from
the palette rather than hard-coded. The animation stops BEFORE the hide, and
the two toggles are kept agreeing or Ctrl+T appears to do nothing while
blanked.

THE DEVIATION: Ctrl+Shift+B, not Ctrl+B as asked. Ctrl+B is the app drawer
and, by its own note, the ONLY keyboard route to it -- a panel otherwise
summoned by hovering a 6 px strip. Taking it would remove a panel from
keyboard users to gain a decoration toggle. Raised with the maintainer;
moving the drawer first is the alternative if Ctrl+B is wanted.

THE HELP MENU loses both blue circled i's and both "(web)" suffixes. The
icon was SP_MessageBoxInformation, the glyph a dialog uses for a notice,
which beside a menu label reads as a badge; the same one on both entries
made it noise twice. No other Help entry has an icon, so they now match.
Where the page opens moved to the status tip. Both i18n keys moved with the
labels, or nine languages would have lost the row.
