--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE STATE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

694 shows the photomosaic centred on the loading cover, holds it at least
0.6 s after the first paint (longer while loading), then blows it away in
about 1.85 s while the live window appears under the front. There is no way
to watch it again after start-up, and its timing follows loading, not a
fixed schedule.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The maintainer wants the start to read as a deliberate title sequence on the
whole screen, at a fixed and calm pace, and to be able to show it on demand
(for talks, demos and tutorials) without restarting spaCR.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. ONE window (maintainer clarification 2026-10-12): there is no separate
   splash window. The spaCR main window opens borderless (frameless, sized to
   the available geometry of its screen, NOT full-screen mode). The FIRST thing
   painted in it is a black container with the spaCR logo photomosaic in the
   centre. Everything else (screens, modules, preload) builds in the background
   underneath that container; then the logo and the black container blow away,
   revealing the window.
   - Logo size: the mosaic is drawn at HALF the screen height (width follows
     its aspect ratio), centred.
   - Resolution: use a higher-resolution mosaic than the current 1600 px
     resource (80 x 80 tiles of 20 px), so it stays sharp at half the screen
     height on 1440p and 4K screens (e.g. rebuild with tools/build_splash_mosaic.py
     from color_final_v1.png at the full 3280 px grid, or larger, and pick the
     decode size from the device pixels needed). Keep the tile grid aligned.
2. Fixed timeline, measured from the first painted frame of the logo:
   - 0.0 - 2.0 s: the logo holds still in the centre.
   - 2.0 - 6.0 s: the logo tiles and the black container blow away over 4.0 s
     (stretch the 694 motion: wind front, lift, flip, spin, fly, fade).
   - 3.0 s (1 s into the blow-away): the window content underneath starts to
     become visible (the black container starts to fade).
   - 5.0 s (3 s into the blow-away): the window content is fully visible (the
     black container is gone; fade 3.0 -> 5.0 s, e.g. smoothstep).
   - 6.0 s (4 s into the blow-away): the last logo tile has gone.
   If the background loading is not finished at 2.0 s, keep the logo holding
   until it is, then run the same 4 s sequence (the content must never appear
   half-built). A click or a key still skips to the end.
3. Help menu: add "Show startup animation" (wording to confirm) to the Help
   dropdown. It replays the same 6 s sequence inside the main window (the
   black container with the logo comes back over the content, then blows
   away), then returns the user exactly where
   they were (focus, open dialogs untouched). It plays even when the
   Startup animation preference is off, but Reduce motion shows the logo
   still for 2 s and then a plain 1 s cross-fade instead (or ask the
   maintainer).
4. Keep the existing switches: Preferences > Animation > Startup animation,
   Reduce motion, SPACR_NO_SPLASH=1 and offscreen disable the start-up run.
5. The borderless main window needs its own way to move, minimise, restore
   and close without a title bar (for example the existing top bar gains
   window controls and is draggable), and a way back to a normal framed window
   (a View or Preferences switch; remember the choice).
6. Strings through tr(); new public docstrings into an API arrival contract.
   WS translates and registers them.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- A test drives the timeline with a fake clock and asserts: one top-level
  window only; it is frameless at the screen available geometry and not
  full-screen (windowState has no WindowFullScreen); the logo is drawn at
  half the screen height, centred, from a resource sharp at that size; no
  tile moves before 2.0 s; the black container opacity starts falling at
  3.0 s, falls monotonically and is 0 at 5.0 s; no tile is drawn after 6.0 s.
- A test for the Help action: it exists in the Help menu, replays the
  sequence, and leaves the main window state unchanged afterwards.
- A real launch on WS, recorded on the GNOME capture host with SPACR_NO_SPLASH
  unset, measured with the capture gate: hold 2.0 s +/- 0.1, tiles gone at
  6.0 s +/- 0.1, distinct frames >= 28 fps through the sequence.
