THE BLACK BACKGROUND BEHIND CONSOLE TEXT -- CAUSE FOUND, FIXED
  Item 408's mechanism, on the console's own widgets. A scroll area's
  viewport fills itself with QPalette.Base unless a stylesheet turns that
  off; the output blocks (_StdoutBlock) and the console scroll viewport rely
  on their own "background: transparent" sheets for it. When a polish nested
  in another stylesheet style's call lets the unpolish through (408), the
  sheet is dropped and the viewport's autoFillBackground comes back True
  with Base = #0d0e10 -- the console text on black, healed only by a fresh
  polish (leave the module and come back). Staged offscreen on HEAD: healthy
  block pixel #ff0000 (its host), after unpolish #0d0e10, after re-polish
  #ff0000. Fix: both viewports have autoFillBackground switched off before
  any sheet sees them (console_panel.py:538 and :1093), so an unpolish has
  nothing to restore. Both tests below were red before the fix.
