(a) Slide 10. Re-segmented from the raw tutorial field W0107F0002T0001Z000
    (900 x 900 px crop, drawn 2x): nuclei and cells with Cellpose-SAM
    (cpsam_v2, CPU, float32; 300 nuclei, 310 cells in the padded crop,
    about 14 min each), lipid droplets with spaCR's own spot segmentation
    (LoG, threshold 0.03, sigma 1-3, watershed) on a 450 px close-up, since
    droplets are a few pixels across. The GPU turn was queued through
    tools/gpu_turn.sh and withdrawn before it started: CPU sufficed once
    bfloat16 was off (bfloat16 on CPU had not finished nuclei in 30 min).
    SPACR BUG FOUND AND FIXED on the way: spacr.object._blobs_to_labels's
    watershed (organelle_watershed_spots with LoG/DoG) was bounded only by
    the image's 20th intensity percentile, so every spot flooded out to its
    neighbours -- on this field 7,049 "droplets" with median area 91 px and
    a 2,708 px maximum, a mosaic over the whole field. Each spot now stops at
    half its own height above a local white top-hat background, and pieces
    not holding their seed are dropped (same field: median area ~10 px).
    Tests: tests/test_cov_object_classical_spots.py (the pin that asserted
    flooding is replaced; a noisy-cell droplet test added).
(b) Slide 2. Panel letters are no longer covered by coarse rectangles:
    scripts/crop_paper3.py finds each letter's ink inside its rough region
    at full resolution (9922 px source, Fig. 3 from its PDF at 1200 dpi) and
    sets only those pixels (plus a 2 px antialiasing halo) to the local
    background. The "A" and every "(i)".."(v)" fragment on slide 2 are gone.
(c) Resolution. Slides are 3200 x 1800 (were 1600 x 900), rendered from a
    lossless LibreOffice PDF (no image downsampling) and saved as JPEG q86
    with 4:4:4 colour. The published spacr_deck.pdf is now LibreOffice's own
    with vector text (fonts embedded) and images at up to 300 dpi; a version
    stamp swaps only its title page (replace_first_page). Icons are 512 px
    (were 256) and their strokes are grown by a 13 px max filter per 1024
    source px before downscaling (scripts/prep_assets.py), white throughout.
(d) Slide 3. 21 rows, general first: point-and-click, batch/headless runs,
    microscope formats, DL segmentation, training, live assisted
    segmentation, mask correction, per-cell measurements, 3-D (spaCR: beta),
    time-lapse, whole plates, plate maps/QC, image and measurement
    classifiers, UMAP/gating, plots without code, GPU, pooled CRISPR screens
    (one row for what were five niche rows: gRNA linking, OPS, gene-level
    calls, screen power, parasite assays), and the three rows where other
    tools lead. Chosen by the agent from features spaCR already has; the
    maintainer should confirm.
(e) Panel letters. Same erasure for every reused preprint panel (Fig. 1A,
    1D-E, 3B, 3C-D, 4B-D, 4E, 4F, 5B-C, S7). Where a letter sat alone in a
