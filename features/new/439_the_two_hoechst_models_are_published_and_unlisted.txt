--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Cell from hoechst is the strongest cross-channel result there is -- it nearly
triples stock cpsam_v2 (0.3012 -> 0.8697) -- and it answers a question users hit
constantly: a screen imaged only with a nuclear stain and no cell mask. It is
reachable today only by someone who already knows the repo name.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Add both rows to BUNDLED_REMOTE_MODELS with the sha256 values above, kind
   "cellpose", repo_type "model".
2. Mind the two layouts: cell-from-hoechst puts its weights under weights/ and
   ships a `_best` checkpoint as well as the final one -- point the row at
   `weights/cell_from_hoechst_best`, since the published table scores the BEST
   epoch. toxoplasma-from-hoechst has its weight at the REPO ROOT, named
   `toxoplasma_from_hoechst_pv`. If fetch() assumes one layout, it will fail on
   the other; check before writing the rows.
3. Quote both stock baselines. A cross-channel model's number means nothing
   without what stock scores on the same fields -- 0.8697 is impressive against
   0.3012, and 0.5689 is an honest result on a much harder task, not a failure.
4. Say in the toxoplasma-from-hoechst row that it predicts a PARASITE mask from
   a HOST-nucleus stain, which is why it sits far below the cell model.
