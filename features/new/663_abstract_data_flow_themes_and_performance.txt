2026-10-05 — ORIGINAL IMPLEMENTATION; VISUAL ACCEPTANCE WITHDRAWN
Appearance > Theme offers the eight families and their Pointer variants;
Animation can also select the individual flow behind another visual theme.
Continuously travelling highlights follow smoothed curves, with localized
cursor bending in the eight mouse variants. Seeded layouts reproduce their
frames; synapse hubs and chromatin clusters cover the field. Existing theme,
palette, animation speed/size/density/resolution and sound controls remain
compatible. Selecting a preset respects disabled animation and sound.

The new renderer shades in the existing one-worker publication path with
cached pens, at most 96 seeds and 49 points per path. Normal 1080p, 4K and
HiDPI frames shade a 960x540 buffer and scale the ready frame for display:
4,147,200 bytes for two shaded images, with no per-frame 4K simulation grid.
Hidden, minimized, paused and closed widgets retire their worker; switching
all sixteen themes leaves one timer and no prior workers. GUI timer ticks
never wait for shading; ordinary variants do not poll the cursor. The mouse
poll accepts only the active window's in-bounds location and takes no input.

The final scoped ambient/catalog/lifecycle cohort passes 774 cases under
4 GiB with CUDA hidden. Focused branch receipts cover every added executable
line and touching arc in the renderer and catalog/preferences integration.
The final focused renderer/catalog/lifecycle receipt passes 98 cases:
197 added ambient executable lines and 46 touching arcs are exercised, with
no new missing line or arc. The unchanged catalog integration receipt covers
all 25 added executable lines and its seven touching branch arcs.
All sixteen palettes pass contrast/page-separation checks; the eight base
palettes also pass sixty Spaceout drift offsets. Root reviewed all sixteen
actual 3840x2160 frames, the refined helix/chromatin output and styled QWidget
dark/light previews. Real cursor polling reached all eight mouse variants.

Independent local CPU/offscreen profiling after final smoothing/glow:
1080p shade medians 6.5-16.7 ms, 4K 7.3-18.6 ms, DPR2 8.0-19.9 ms;
4K ready-frame blits 4.6-7.8 ms. Benchmark process high-water RSS was
185.0 MiB across 23 themes and two resolutions, and 181.6 MiB at DPR2.
Those are development measurements, not a hosted timing guarantee or a
whole-Qt memory result. Existing seven themes, buffer/work ceilings, launch
recovery, Extra Performance and No Animation rules remain in place.
With a CPU worker and 200 console messages, all sixteen backdrops render
all messages; local 16 ms heartbeat medians are 15.87-16.02 ms and p95 is
20.97-24.09 ms. The corresponding no-backdrop sample is 15.98/17.98 ms.

2026-10-05 — MAINTAINER REJECTED THE ORIGINAL VISUAL DIRECTION
The maintainer strongly dislikes every new theme and explicitly requests
removal of all sixteen. Prior tests and measurements describe the rejected
implementation only; they do not establish the requested visual acceptance.
All sixteen presets, their renderers and feature-specific tests are removed.
The original seven ambient engines and ten night presets remain available.

Replacement request: 10-20 genuinely different professional generative data
artworks representing spatial organization, genetics, genetic flow and
perturbation. Each artwork must have its own visual language, fine detail
and higher rendering resolution; recoloured or pointer-paired versions do
not count as separate artworks. CPU-safe bounded rendering and UI lifecycle
rules still apply. Real renderer previews precede completion claims. Item
663 is reopened; generated API/docs/translations/tutorials and GPU work
remain workstation-owned. Do not regenerate captions for removed flow keys.
