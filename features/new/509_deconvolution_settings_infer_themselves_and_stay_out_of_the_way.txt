WHAT TO BUILD
  1. PICK A MICROSCOPE AND OBJECTIVE, get everything else: a button that
     either infers the optics from the images and their metadata (the naming
     convention already read, the file's own metadata, magnification, pixel
     size, image dimensions) or lets the user choose a microscope and
     objective from a list, and fills numerical aperture, wavelength,
     refractive index, pixel size and the rest. Defaults are common values, so
     it works out of the box; every inferred value stays editable and says
     where it came from.
  2. ONE VISIBLE ROW, THE REST COLLAPSED: deconvolution, PSF, Cellpose
     enhancement and CLAHE each show what a person must choose and hide the
     remainder behind a collapsed group (item 471's folds), so the default
     path is one click.

DONE WHEN
Someone who knows none of the optics can deconvolve a real plate from the
defaults, the inferred values are shown and editable, and none of the four
groups shows more than its essential rows until it is opened.
