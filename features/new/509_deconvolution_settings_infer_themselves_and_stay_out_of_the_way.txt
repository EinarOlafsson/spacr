_segment_multi) now calls spacr.point_spread.fill_psf_settings on the
request's settings snapshot before prepare_psf, the same call the plate run
makes in psf_pipeline._prepare_segmentation_psf. Its source is the file the
preview field was loaded from: PreviewRequest gained `source_path`, which
the panel's _build_request fills from the loaded path; empty falls back to
the settings' `src` and then to the defaults, as fill_psf_settings does.

before the model loads. What was inferred is kept in the preview's
provenance as `psf_calibration` (describe_optics lines, or 'as set'), and

entry says where the kernel came from. No new tr() caption.
