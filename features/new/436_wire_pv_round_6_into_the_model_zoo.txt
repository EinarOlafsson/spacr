--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Round 6 is the first PV model trained with a held-out validation set AND a
separate test set, so it is the first whose numbers were not produced by the
data it fitted. Until the row exists, spaCR users get round 5, whose published
figure comes from cross-validation over the same pool it trained on.
