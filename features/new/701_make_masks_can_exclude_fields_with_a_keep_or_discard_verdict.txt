================================================================================
MAKE MASKS CAN EXCLUDE FIELDS THAT ALREADY HAVE A KEEP OR DISCARD VERDICT
================================================================================

Status:    not started
Requested: maintainer, 2026-10-13: "in make mask there should be an option to
           exclude images that already have a keep or discrad flag"
Owner:     Home (Make Masks); WS (translations, tutorial capture)
Related:   Make Masks Keep / Discard buttons (csv/keep_discard.csv), "First
           unreviewed" button, spacr-make-masks curation queues
           (spacr.curation_queue.build_queue, curate_status.csv).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE STATE IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Keep and Discard write a verdict per field to csv/keep_discard.csv beside the
images (spacr/qt/screens/make_masks.py, _on_curate). "First unreviewed" jumps
to the first field without a verdict, but every field stays in the image list
and Prev/Next still walk through fields already judged. A spacr-make-masks
queue skips only fields with a done/skip row in curate_status.csv; a Keep or
Discard verdict does not take a field out of the session.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The maintainer is curating thousands of plaque fields (plaque v5). Fields
already judged Keep or Discard keep coming back, which wastes time and risks
judging the same field twice.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Make Masks: a switch "Hide judged fields" (wording to confirm) next to
   First unreviewed. On: the image list, Prev/Next and the counter skip every
   field with a Keep or Discard verdict; Off: all fields, as now. Remember the
   choice per folder. Changing a verdict while the switch is on moves on to the
   next unjudged field.
2. spacr-make-masks: a flag --exclude-verdicts (and the same switch in the
   session) so build_queue drops fields with a Keep or Discard verdict in
   <folder>/csv/keep_discard.csv, in addition to done/skip rows; --dry-run
   reports how many were excluded and why.
3. Strings through tr(); docstrings into an API arrival contract (WS
   translates).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KNOW IT WORKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Test: a folder of 10 fields, 3 Keep, 2 Discard: with the switch on, Next
  visits exactly the 5 unjudged fields and the counter says 5; off, all 10.
- Test: spacr-make-masks --exclude-verdicts --dry-run on that folder lists the
  5 unjudged fields and says 5 excluded by verdict.
