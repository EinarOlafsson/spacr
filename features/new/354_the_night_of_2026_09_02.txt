THE FOUR THINGS WORTH REMEMBERING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. FIVE OF TWENTY-THREE INSTRUCTION FILES WERE WRONG ABOUT THEMSELVES. The
   repository has warned about this since August and the rate has not fallen.
   `git log --grep="\\bNNN\\b"` per open number found four of the five in
   minutes and is the highest-yield audit step there is.

2. A TEST THAT FAKES AN IMPORT LEAVES TWO PIECES OF STATE. `monkeypatch`
   restores the import. It does not restore a cached boolean filled while the
   fake was installed, nor a module object deleted by a reload. Both poisoned
   an entire process from one file. Instruction 346 took 21 failures to 3 on
   that insight alone.

3. MEASURE TWICE, DIFFERENTLY. Two confident findings were published and
   retracted within the hour on instruction 350, both because a single
   measurement looked plausible: a clipping sweep read widget widths before
   the layout settled, and then compared a deliberately-eliding control's full
   caption against its capped width. What caught both was a SECOND
   measurement of a different shape disagreeing -- never a re-reading of the
   code.

4. A DOCSTRING EDIT IS A TRANSLATION EDIT. Adding `plateID` to one formula in
   `ml.py` staled nine reviewed locale catalogs and turned the docs job red.
   Nothing in the editing experience says so.
