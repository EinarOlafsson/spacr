--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHERE THE DATA COMES FROM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The NAS copy under /nas_mnt/data/ops/OpticalPooledScreens_data/screenA/ is the
primary fixed-cell screen of Funk et al., "The phenotypic landscape of
essential human genes", Cell 185(24), 2022 (PMID 36347254), deposited in the
BioImage Archive as S-BIAD394 (released 2022-03-31). The NAS files are
byte-identical to the archive's: SHA-256 of
10X_c1_A1_DAPI-CY3-A594-CY5-CY7_Site-165.tif is the same from both.

Licence: S-BIAD394 carries no licence attribute. The BioImage Archive's policy
page says directly submitted data are released under CC0 or CC-BY-4.0, and
that an accession under any other licence carries it as an attribute. Both
permit redistribution; CC-BY-4.0 requires attribution, so the dataset cards
attribute as if it were CC-BY-4.0. The guide library (pool10_design.csv) comes
from the authors' code repository, which is MIT-licensed.

DOWNLOADING FROM THE ORIGINAL HOST INSTEAD WAS MEASURED AND REJECTED. The
archive serves every file over HTTPS at
https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/fire/S-BIAD/394/S-BIAD394/Files/<path>, but
on 2026-09-21 it delivered 94 KB/s for one file and 5.7 KB/s for another,
which is over an hour for the OPS sample. A button that slow is not usable in
a demo.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE TWO SAMPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Align & Stitch  einarolafsson/spacr-example-stitch   spacr-example-stitch.tar
                  Nine cycle-1 10X tiles of plate 20200202_6W-LaC024A, well
                  A1, sites 165-167, 186-188 and 207-209: a 3 x 3 block from
                  the middle of the well, acquired in snake-column order,
                  213 px (14.4 %) overlap. Unmodified, about 197 MB.

  OPS             einarolafsson/spacr-example-ops      spacr-example-ops.tar
                  Sites 331 and 332 of the same well, all eleven sequencing
                  cycles (c1 five-plane stack, c2-c11 four single-channel
                  files each), plus the pool10 11-base prefix library derived
                  as the reference Snakefile derives it. Unmodified, about
                  394 MB.

WHY SITES 331-332 AND NOT THE MIDDLE OF THE WELL. The engine takes the well's
field count as the highest site number plus one and fits a round well to it
(ops_engine._stitch). A sample of sites 0-8 would fit a 9-field well that
does not exist; a sample that includes the well's last site, 332, keeps the
measured 333-field layout, and the engine places only the sites present.

WHY NO PHENOTYPE IMAGES. A 20X phenotype field is 280 MB on its own, and the
phenotype phase aligns anchors spread over the WHOLE well (at least three of
eighteen candidates), which no partial-well sample can supply. The OPS sample
runs the stitch, objects and decode phases; phenotype placement is skipped
and says so.
