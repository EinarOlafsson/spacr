597. Home screen sliders size the right-hand widgets and their text

Requested by the maintainer (2026-09-29), verbatim:
"add to new list, a slider on the home screen that lets the user change the size of the rwidgets on the right on the bottom of those widgest should be a slider that allows the user to change the size of the text."

Brief:
At the foot of Home's right-hand column, two compact sliders in the app's
slider style: Widget size (scales the column's panels) and Text size (scales
the text in them). Both apply live, are saved in QSettings and restore on
start. Reuse the existing scaling machinery; no overlap or clipping at the
minimum and maximum.

Done when: both sliders change their size live, persist and restore, and
nothing overlaps or clips at the extremes (Qt tests).

Status: IMPLEMENTED 2026-09-29

Correction from the maintainer (2026-09-29), verbatim:
"no the widgets should have the same gui elements as the settings container has in the moduals with the arrow and the font slider should be the only slider right bellow the lowest widget."

Notes (2026-09-29, after the correction): spacr/qt/widgets/home.py. The
Widget size slider is gone. The right-hand column is an EDGE pane
("Widgets") of a CollapsibleSplitter, the class and handle the module screens
use for their settings column: drag the arrow handle to resize, click it to
fold the column away or bring it back. The width is stored under the pane
extents key home::body and the fold under home/Widgets. One slider,
HomeAsideTextSlider (70-160 %), sits right below the lowest panel, saved as
prefs/home_aside_text through private helpers in spacr/qt/preferences.py.
Text reuses live_zoom.scaled_font_sheet for inherited sizes and rewrites each
label's own font-size from its built size after every refresh. The column's
minimum width follows its content, so large text widens it instead of
clipping. Tests: tests/qt/test_597_home_aside_handle_and_text_slider.py.

Left: nothing.
