Investigate Hit: STILL NO EXAMPLE. LINCS does not satisfy it. The screen
(spacr.hit_investigation.investigate_hit) needs: (1) a per-cell measurements
database over >= 6 wells, including both target-free and target-containing
wells so every cross-fit fold has both kinds; (2) per-cell cross-validated
predictions with a phenotype score column; (3) a sequencing-derived
guide-fraction table, one fraction per well and guide; (4) a Regression
results folder naming the hit gene and its guides. LINCS is an arrayed
compound screen: no guides, so (3) and (4) cannot exist; the published
profiles are per well, so (1) and (2) are missing too (single-cell SQLite
backends exist per plate, several GB each, but still without guides).
Candidate that does fit: the maintainer's own published TSG101 pooled screen
(einarolafsson/spacr-example-screen plateN-measurements.tar, ~0.5 GB each;
per-cell scores plateN_dv.csv and per-well guide counts
plate_N_unique_combinations.csv from the example-data release). A cut of
plate 1 (>= 6 wells with and without the hit's guides) plus a Regression run
on it would make a small Investigate Hit set; not built here.

wells.
Made by spaCR on the cut, not by hand: spacr.ml.perform_regression (OLS, well
level, mean pred, fraction threshold 0.02, control columns kept) wrote
results/ols/, which names gene 239740 (GRA14, the screen's positive
control; TSG101 is the host reporter, not a guide target) with guide
239740_1: effect 0.482, p 0.0062, BH-adjusted 0.040, 16 supporting wells.
Its regression_data.csv is the guide-fraction table (guide_fractions.csv;
guides under 2 % of a well's reads count as absent: 16 GRA14 wells, 14
GRA14-free). The scores are the screen's own spaCR CV predictions, cut to the
kept cells (re-scoring would need the 8.9 GB crops and a GPU). hit.json names
the hit and the files; build paths in settings/text outputs are replaced by
<dataset>, filled in on download.
