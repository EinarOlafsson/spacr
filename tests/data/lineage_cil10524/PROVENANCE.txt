Test data of tests/test_lineage_division_rules_f537.py (item F537, lineage
trees from tracks).

Source movie: Cell Image Library CIL:10524 (maximum projection of the
deconvolved CIL:10522), John Murray and Ke Hu. Toxoplasma gondii RH
expressing EGFP-TgIMC1 and mRFP-TgMORN1 in HFF cells, 60x/1.2 NA water,
0.2134 um/px, one frame every 10 min, 100 frames, 256 x 256 px.
https://doi.org/10.7295/W9CIL10522 (raw) and the CIL:10524 record.
Licence: public domain (Creative Commons public-domain mark on the record).
Please cite the DOI and credit Murray and Hu.

Derived files (spaCR's own processing, BSD-3-Clause; no pixels of the movie
except bud_excerpt.npz):

tracks.csv
    Parasites segmented with Cellpose cyto3 (CPU, diameter 22) on IMC1 +
    MORN1, stage drift removed by phase correlation, tracked with spaCR's
    _track_by_iou. Columns: frame, track_id, x, y (registered pixels),
    edge_distance (pixels from the parasite's centre to the edge of the
    imaged field in that frame) and bud_signal (IMC1 95th percentile over
    median inside the parasite, spacr._lineage_trees._lineage_bud_signal).

division_labels.csv
    Inferred divisions checked by eye on crops (IMC1 green, MORN1 red,
    mother and daughter outlined, frames -8 to +10), 2026-10-11:
    R real division (daughter buds in the mother, then daughters emerge),
    E real but called while the daughters were still buds, A artefact
    (segmentation reshuffle in a mature rosette, a basal or residual body
    segmented on its own, or an object entering at the field edge),
    U unclear.

bud_excerpt.npz
    Registered IMC1 intensities (imc1, uint16) and the matching parasite
    labels (labels, labelled with track ids) of a 48 x 48 px crop around
    the budding mother of track 8, frames 2-9 (first_frame = 2).
